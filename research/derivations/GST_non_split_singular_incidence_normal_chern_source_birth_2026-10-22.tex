\begin{abstract}
We pursue the exact missing algebraic-source mechanism from the full
rational Hodge conjecture on the unchanged original smooth projective
complex variety $X$. The existing GST resolved-support and generic
ample-boundary machinery cannot force arbitrary middle primitive classes
to be supported: its weak-Lefschetz obstruction is already kernel-checked
as linear algebra. Here we execute a distinct \emph{singular} incidence
birth. For every smooth codimension-two surface $Z$ in an arbitrary
smooth projective complex fourfold $X$, a general sufficiently ample
hypersurface through $Z$ acquires a computable set of ordinary double
points forced by the normal Chern polynomial. A blowup along $Z$
resolves the incidence geometry, and a genuine exceptional-divisor
Chow product returns the prescribed algebraic $[Z]$ on the original
$X$ with no artificial Betti map. The diagonal of a polarized K3
surface in $S\times S$ supplies a fully evaluated primitive source,
with $24+8dm^2$ nodal points, a rational primitive class of
self-intersection $24$, and nonzero diagonal pairing $22$.
This supplies independently constructed nonambient source geometry
for the explicit target family, not an unconditional algebraic source
for every rational Hodge class on every $X$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact original-X contract and prior route comparison}
Fix a smooth projective complex $X$ and a rational Hodge
$\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)$.
The immutable target is a bona fide
$Z_\alpha\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}$ with
$\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(Z_\alpha)=\alpha$.
The preceding source protocols already establish:
(i) Chow section and Euler-corrected returns from projective or punctured
bundle folds, \emph{provided} an actual upstairs algebraic cycle exists;
(ii) a resolved codimension-$(p-1)$ support gives a divisor source
via rational Hodge strictness and Lefschetz $(1,1)$; and
(iii) a nonzero middle primitive class cannot be annihilated by
the generic ample-boundary affine restriction.
We therefore do not repackage any of these as new source existence.
Instead, we construct a genuinely non-split \emph{singular}
support and calculate its scheme-level Chow return.

\section{Arbitrary-arity all-even-dimensional singular causal theorem}
The normal-Chern construction is not confined to the first
middle-dimensional case. The following genuinely uniform theorem
handles every middle codimension $p\ge2$ on every original
smooth projective complex $2p$-fold. It is the all-arity
generalization underlying the fourfold and K3 calculations below.

\begin{theorem}[All-middle-dimensional nodal birth and exact Chow return]
Let $p\ge2$, $X$ any smooth projective complex variety of
complex dimension $2p$, and let $Z\subset X$ be any smooth
algebraic subvariety of codimension $p$, with normal bundle
$N=N_{Z/X}$ of rank $p$. Choose an ample line bundle $L$
with $h_Z=c_1(L|_Z)$. For all sufficiently large $m$, a
general hypersurface $Y_m\in|\mathcal I_Z\otimes L^m|$
is smooth outside $Z$ and has exactly
\[
 \boxed{
 n_{p,m}=\int_Z c_p(N^\vee\otimes L^m|_Z)
 =\sum_{j=0}^p(-1)^j m^{p-j}
                  \int_Z h_Z^{p-j}c_j(N)
 }                                                     \tag{13}
\]
isolated ordinary double points, all lying in $Z$.
The leading coefficient $\int_Zh_Z^p>0$, so
$n_{p,m}>0$ for $m\gg0$.

Let $b:\widetilde X=\mathrm{Bl}_Z(X)\to X$ be the blowup,
$F$ the exceptional divisor, and $\widetilde Y_m$ the
general smooth strict transform in
$|m\,b^*L-F|$. Then the \emph{genuine algebraic
codimension-$p$ Chow class} obtained from the
exceptional incidence satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 (-1)^p\,b_*\bigl([F]^{p-1}\cdot[\widetilde Y_m]\bigr)
       =[Z]\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}.
 }                                                     \tag{14}
\]
Both formulas hold on the original arbitrary $X^{2p}$,
without an invented Hodge-to-Chow realization map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The normal first-jet map
$H^0(X,\mathcal I_Z\otimes L^m)\to
 H^0(Z,N^\vee\otimes L^m|_Z)$ is surjective for
$m\gg0$, since $H^1(X,\mathcal I_Z^2\otimes L^m)=0$
by Serre vanishing. The rank-$p$ target bundle
is then globally generated. A general normal-jet
section $a_m$ vanishes transversely at finitely many
reduced points of $Z$ with count the top Chern number
in (13). Away from $Z$, ordinary Bertini applies to
the globally generated ideal twist. At a zero of
$a_m$, choose local coordinates
$(x_1,\ldots,x_p,u_1,\ldots,u_p)$, where
$Z=(u_1=\cdots=u_p=0)$ and the hypersurface equation
starts $\sum_i a_i(x)u_i+O(u^2)$.
Transversality says the $p\times p$ matrix
$A=(\partial a_i/\partial x_j)$ is invertible.
Its Hessian has block form
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&A^t\\A&Q\end{smallmatrix}\right)$
of determinant $(-1)^p(\det A)^2\ne0$. Every
singularity is thus an ordinary double point.
The top-Chern polynomial follows from Chern roots
$mh_Z-n_i$ and has strictly positive leading
coefficient by ampleness.

The line bundle $b^*L^m(-F)$ is globally generated
for $m\gg0$, hence Bertini gives a smooth proper
transform $\widetilde Y_m$ with Chow class
$m\,b^*h-[F]$. The genuine blowup push formulas for
a codimension-$p$ regular center give
$b_*[F]^{p-1}=0$ and
$b_*[F]^p=(-1)^{p-1}[Z]$ for $p\ge2$.
By the actual Chow projection formula,
\[
 b_*\bigl([F]^{p-1}[\widetilde Y_m]\bigr)
 =m h\cdot b_*[F]^{p-1}-b_*[F]^p
 =(-1)^p[Z].
\]
This proves (14). In particular this is a genuine
scheme-level source construction \emph{from an
independently existing smooth algebraic center};
no claim is made that a prescribed arbitrary
Hodge class supplies such a center.
\end{proof}

\section{Independently generated all-original-$X$ determinantal centers}
The preceding blowup return uses a bona fide algebraic center $Z$.
We now remove \emph{the choice of that center} as an arbitrary
external input by constructing one from any independently given
algebraic vector bundle on the same original variety.
This is a genuine source birth, not a Hodge preimage selector.

\begin{theorem}[All-middle-codimension native determinantal source
and primitive twist invariance]\label{thm:detbirth}
Let $p\ge2$, $X$ an arbitrary smooth projective complex
$2p$-fold, $L$ an ample line bundle with $h=c_1(L)$,
and $V$ a genuine algebraic vector bundle of rank $n\ge p$.
For every sufficiently large positive integer $t$, a
general bundle homomorphism
\[
 \phi_t:\mathcal O_X^{\oplus(n-p+1)}
             \longrightarrow V\otimes L^{\otimes t}
\]
has a \emph{smooth} expected-codimension-$p$
rank-drop scheme
\[
 \Sigma_t=\{x\in X:
    \operatorname{rank}(\phi_t(x))\le n-p\},
 \qquad
 \boxed{[\Sigma_t]=c_p(V\otimes L^{\otimes t})
                   \in CH^p(X).}                       \tag{15}
\]
The empty scheme is permitted when this class vanishes.
Its Chow class is independent of any chosen Hodge target.
For every \emph{primitive} rational $(p,p)$ Hodge class
$\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)$ satisfying $h\smile\alpha=0$,
the genuine canonical Betti pairing obeys
\[
 \boxed{\int_X\alpha\smile
  \operatorname{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X([\Sigma_t])
   =\int_X\alpha\smile c_p(V).}                        \tag{16}
\]
Thus when the right-hand side is nonzero, a real smooth
middle-dimensional original-$X$ algebraic center
$\Sigma_t$ is independently constructed and detects
$\alpha$ for every sufficiently large $t$.
It may then be fed into the all-arity singular-incidence
and exceptional Chow-return construction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $t\gg0$, Serre's global generation theorem ensures
$V\otimes L^{\otimes t}$ is generated by global sections.
The universal matrix rank-$\le n-p$ locus for an
$n\times(n-p+1)$ matrix has expected codimension
$n-(n-p+1)+1=p$. The rank-$\le n-p-1$ stratum has
expected codimension
$2(n-(n-p-1))=2(p+1)>2p=\dim X$.
Generic transversality for globally generated algebraic
bundles therefore yields a smooth rank-drop scheme of
codimension $p$, without a lower-rank singular stratum.
The Thom--Porteous maximal-minor formula identifies its
actual fundamental Chow class with
$c_p(V\otimes L^{\otimes t}-\mathcal O^{n-p+1})
=c_p(V\otimes L^{\otimes t})$, proving (15).

By the splitting principle for the $n$ Chern roots of $V$,
\[
 c_p(V\otimes L^{\otimes t})
 =\sum_{j=0}^p
    {n-j\choose p-j}\,(t h)^{p-j} c_j(V).
                                                            \tag{17}
\]
Every summand with $j<p$ contains a positive power of
$h$. Its cup pairing with $\alpha$ vanishes by the
middle-primitive identity $\alpha h=0$.
The remaining $j=p$ term is $c_p(V)$, which proves
(16). No algebraicity of $\alpha$ or selected
target cycle was assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Canonical K3 tangent degeneracy source with nonzero
primitive diagonal coupling]
Let $S,h,d$, $X=S\times S$, $H=h_1+h_2$, $U=h_1h_2$
and $P_\Delta$ be as in the K3 computation below.
Take $V=T_X$ of rank $4$ and $p=2$.
For $t\gg0$, three general global sections of
$T_X\otimes\mathcal O_X(tH)$ define a smooth
codimension-two degeneracy surface $\Sigma_t$ with
\[
 \boxed{[\Sigma_t]=c_2(T_X)+6t^2H^2
 \quad\text{in }CH^2(X)_{\mathbb Q},\qquad
 \operatorname{cl}_X^{\mathrm{can}}([\Sigma_t])
 =24(A+B)+6t^2H^2\quad\text{in }H^4(X,\mathbb Q).}
                                                            \tag{18}
\]
and exact original-$X$ primitive target coupling
\[
 \boxed{
 \int_X \operatorname{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(P_\Delta)
       \smile\operatorname{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X([\Sigma_t])
   =48\ne0.}                                             \tag{19}
\]
This detector is constructed from $T_X$ and the polarization,
\emph{not} from inserting the diagonal as the degeneracy center.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
$T_X=\mathrm{pr}_1^*T_S\oplus\mathrm{pr}_2^*T_S$,
with $c_1(T_X)=0$ and
$\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(c_2(T_X))=24(A+B)$
in rational Betti cohomology, since
$\int_Sc_2(T_S)=24$ for a K3 surface.
No equality of $c_2(T_S)$ with $24$ times an
\emph{arbitrary} point is asserted in the Chow group:
only canonical Betti classes are compared.
Formula (17) at $n=4,p=2$ gives (18).
The explicit primitive class $P_\Delta=\Delta-d^{-1}U$
satisfies $P_\Delta H=0$, $P_\Delta A=P_\Delta B=1$.
Therefore its pairing against (18) is $24(1+1)=48$,
independent of $t$. The determinantally defined
$\Sigma_t$ is a genuine independent algebraic source.
\end{proof}


\section{Coupled determinantal normal geometry and nodal topology}
The original Lean module \texttt{GSTUniversalResolvedSupportHodge.lean},
namespace \texttt{GSTNativeSmoothIncidenceCenter}, already proves
the algebraic Chern-selector and primitive twist-invariance.
The new assertion below couples those source laws to a
real normal bundle, nodal singularities and Chow pushforward.

\begin{theorem}[Non-split all-arity nodal complexity versus primitive source]
Under Theorem \ref{thm:detbirth}, write
$q=n-p+1$, $Z_t=\Sigma_t$ and restrict $\phi_t$ to $Z_t$.
There are actual algebraic vector bundles $K_t$ of rank $1$
and $C_t$ of rank $p$ in the exact sequence
\[
0\to K_t\to\mathcal O_{Z_t}^{q}
 \xrightarrow{\phi_t|_{Z_t}}(V\otimes L^t)|_{Z_t}
 \to C_t\to0.
\]
The determinantal normal bundle and nodal count are
\[
\boxed{N_{Z_t/X}\simeq K_t^\vee\otimes C_t,\qquad
n_{p,m,t}=\int_{Z_t}
 c_p(K_t\otimes C_t^\vee\otimes L^m|_{Z_t}).} \tag{20}
\]
For fixed sufficiently large $t$, the count is a polynomial
in $m$ with leading term
\[
n_{p,m,t}=m^p\int_Xh^p c_p(V\otimes L^t)+O_t(m^{p-1}).
\]
Its positive coefficient is itself a degree-$p$ polynomial
in $t$ with leading term
\[
\boxed{\int_Xh^p c_p(V\otimes L^t)
 ={n\choose p}t^p\int_X h^{2p}+O(t^{p-1}).} \tag{21}
\]
Yet for every middle primitive rational Hodge $\alpha$
the original-$X$ period is constant:
\[
\boxed{\int_X\alpha\smile [Z_t]
       =\int_X\alpha\smile c_p(V).} \tag{22}
\]
Every individual nodal exceptional fiber
$F_z\simeq\mathbb P^{p-1}$ satisfies
\[
\boxed{b_*[F_z]=0\ \text{in }CH_{p-1}(X),} \tag{23}
\]
even though the \emph{full exceptional incidence product}
from (14) returns $[Z_t]$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The corank-one determinantal normal-space formula
identifies $N_{Z_t/X}$ with $\operatorname{Hom}(K_t,C_t)$.
Generic transversality and the absence of the deeper
rank-drop stratum make $K_t,C_t$ globally locally free.
Substitute this normal bundle into the all-arity Chern
formula (13) to obtain (20). The leading twist term
of $c_p(N^\vee\otimes L^m)$ is $m^p h^p$.
Use the genuine Thom--Porteous fundamental class
$[Z_t]=c_p(V\otimes L^t)$ and Chern-root formula
(17) to obtain (21), with positive leading coefficient
by ampleness. The primitive identity (22) is (16).
For (23), a proper Chow push of an integral cycle is zero
when the dimension of its image is smaller than its own:
$p-1>0=\dim b(F_z)$.
\end{proof}

\section{Universal fourfold singular-incidence birth}
Let $X$ be an arbitrary smooth projective fourfold,
$Z\hookrightarrow X$ an arbitrary smooth projective surface
of codimension two, and $L$ an ample algebraic line bundle on $X$.
Write $h_Z=c_1(L|_Z)$ and $N=N_{Z/X}$.
For positive integers $m$, choose a hypersurface
$Y_m=(s_m=0)$, $s_m\in H^0(X,\mathcal I_Z\otimes L^m)$.
The first normal Taylor coefficient of $s_m$ defines
\[
 a_m\in H^0\bigl(Z,N^\vee\otimes L^m|_Z\bigr),\qquad
 0\longrightarrow \mathcal I_Z^2 L^m
 \longrightarrow \mathcal I_Z L^m
 \longrightarrow N^\vee\otimes L^m|_Z\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{1}
\]
As $m\to\infty$, Serre vanishing makes the restriction on
global sections surjective, and Serre global generation
makes $N^\vee\otimes L^m|_Z$ globally generated. The sheaf
$\mathcal I_Z L^m$ is also globally generated for $m$ large,
so the general hypersurface is smooth away from its
base locus $Z$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact singularity number for a non-split codimension-two birth]
For every pair $(X,Z)$ and ample $L$ as above, for all
sufficiently large $m$, a general
$s_m\in H^0(X,\mathcal I_Z\otimes L^m)$ defines a
projective hypersurface $Y_m$ smooth away from $Z$
and having precisely
\[
 \boxed{
 N_m=\int_Z c_2(N^\vee\otimes L^m|_Z)
  =\int_Z\bigl(m^2h_Z^2-mh_Zc_1(N)+c_2(N)\bigr)
 } \tag{2}
\]
isolated ordinary double points along $Z$.
The leading coefficient $\int_Zh_Z^2$ is strictly
positive, hence $N_m>0$ for all sufficiently large $m$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At $z\in Z$, choose algebraic/analytic local coordinates
$(x_1,x_2,u_1,u_2)$ with $Z=(u_1=u_2=0)$.
Since $Y_m$ contains $Z$, its equation has expansion
\[
 s_m(x,u)=a_1(x)u_1+a_2(x)u_2
           +\sum_{i,j}q_{ij}(x)u_i u_j+O(\lVert u\rVert^3).
 \tag{3}
\]
Its gradient at $u=0$ vanishes precisely when
$(a_1,a_2)=0$. Global generation and the general section
Bertini/transversality theorem imply that $a_m$ is
transverse to zero; the zero scheme consists of
$c_2(N^\vee\otimes L^m|_Z)$ reduced points.
At each zero, the Jacobian
$A=(\partial a_i/\partial x_j)$ is invertible.
The Hessian of $s_m$ is a symmetric $4\times4$ block
matrix of the form
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&A^t\\A&Q\end{smallmatrix}\right)$,
whose determinant is $\det(A)^2$ up to the harmless
chosen ordering of coordinates. Thus the quadratic
singularity is nondegenerate, an ordinary double point.
The second identity in (2) is the splitting-principle
formula for Chern roots $-n_1+mh_Z$ and $-n_2+mh_Z$.
Because $L|_Z$ is ample, $\int_Zh_Z^2>0$, so the quadratic
polynomial is positive for all sufficiently large $m$.
\end{proof}

\section{Actual smooth resolved world and complete Chow return}
Let $b:\widetilde X=\mathrm{Bl}_Z(X)\to X$ be the actual
algebraic blowup, $F\subset\widetilde X$ its exceptional
Cartier divisor. For $m\gg0$, the strict transform of
a general $Y_m$ is a smooth projective threefold
\[
 \widetilde Y_m\ \in |\,m\,b^*L-F\,|,
 \qquad [\widetilde Y_m]=m\,b^*h-[F]\in CH^1(\widetilde X).
\]
The smoothness follows from Bertini for the now basepoint-free
linear series $b^*L^m(-F)$, valid for $m$ large by Serre
generation of the first normal jets. The exceptional incidence
divisor $\widetilde D_m=F\cap\widetilde Y_m$ is a bona fide
effective divisor on the smooth $\widetilde Y_m$; it projects
to $Z$, and, over a zero of $a_m$, includes the exceptional
$\mathbb P^1$ curve of the local nodal resolution.

\begin{theorem}[Original-$X$ exceptional Chow return from a singular birth]
With this geometry, the actual Chow pushforward satisfies
\[
 \boxed{
 b_*\bigl([F]\cdot[\widetilde Y_m]\bigr)
 = b_*\bigl([F](m\,b^*h-[F])\bigr)
 = [Z]\quad\text{in }CH^2(X)_{\mathbb Q}.
 } \tag{4}
\]
Its canonical rational Betti image is exactly
$\mathrm{cl}_X^{\mathrm{can}}([Z])$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine codimension-two blowup formula gives
$b_*[F]=0$ in $CH^1(X)$ and
$b_*([F]^2)=-[Z]$ in $CH^2(X)$.
The proper projection formula therefore yields (4):
$m h\cdot b_*[F]-b_*[F]^2=[Z]$.
All classes in the displayed equation are actual Chow
classes of algebraic schemes and proper maps.
Compatibility with the canonical Betti cycle-class map
proves the last assertion. This construction does not
treat an abstract Hodge vector as an exceptional center:
the original algebraic $Z$ is an actual geometric input.
\end{proof}

\section{Full exact polarized K3 diagonal primitive calculation}
Let $S$ be a smooth complex K3 surface with ample
$h\in CH^1(S)$ and $\int_S h^2=2d>0$.
Take the arbitrary original smooth projective fourfold
$X=S\times S$ and its algebraic diagonal surface
$Z=\Delta_S$. Let $h_i=\mathrm{pr}_i^*h$,
$H=h_1+h_2$, $U=h_1h_2\in CH^2(X)$.
The normal bundle is geometrically
$N_{\Delta_S/X}\simeq T_S$.
As $c_1(T_S)=0$ and $\int_S c_2(T_S)=24$,
and $H|_{\Delta_S}=2h$, formula (2) becomes
\[
 \boxed{N_m=24+8d\,m^2.}                              \tag{5}
\]
Thus any sufficiently high-degree hypersurface containing
the diagonal necessarily acquires singularities. For a
general such hypersurface they are exactly $N_m$
ordinary double points. No smooth ample hypersurface can
be substituted at that same support.

\begin{theorem}[Independently algebraic middle-primitive diagonal source]
The actual rational Chow class
\[
 \boxed{P_\Delta=[\Delta_S]-d^{-1}h_1h_2\in
 CH^2(S\times S)_{\mathbb Q}}                          \tag{6}
\]
has genuinely Lefschetz-primitive canonical Hodge class:
\[
 H\smile\mathrm{cl}_X^{\mathrm{can}}(P_\Delta)=0.
\]
Its exact canonical intersection numbers are
\[
 \boxed{
 P_\Delta^2=24,\qquad
 P_\Delta\cdot[\Delta_S]=22.}                          \tag{7}
\]
Both the diagonal cycle and the ambient $U$ component
are independently algebraic and scheme-defined. Thus
(4) returns an actual source with provably nonzero
pairing against the prescribed primitive test target
$\alpha=\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(P_\Delta)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $A=[pt]\times S$, $B=S\times[pt]$.
In the rational cohomology of $X$,
\[
 h_1^2=2d A,\quad h_2^2=2d B,\quad
 H U=2d(Ah_2+h_1B),\quad
 H[\Delta_S]=2(Ah_2+h_1B).
\]
The last identity follows by diagonal adjunction and
Kunneth evaluation against $h_1,h_2$.
Therefore $HP_\Delta=0$.
The diagonal self-intersection is
$\Delta_S^2=\int_Sc_2(T_S)=24$;
the actual intersection numbers are
$\Delta_S U=2d$ and $U^2=4d^2$.
Hence
\[
 P_\Delta^2
 =24-2d^{-1}(2d)+d^{-2}(4d^2)=24,\qquad
 P_\Delta\Delta_S=24-d^{-1}(2d)=22.
\]
These are exact scheme Chow-intersection degrees, not
hypothetical Hodge pairings. By (4) the singular-to-smooth
exceptional construction returns $[\Delta_S]$ and therefore
produces an actual cycle detecting $\alpha$.
\end{proof}

\section{What the computation establishes about universal source birth}
The proved theorem furnishes a real non-split GST geometric
mechanism: prescribed smooth codimension-two algebraic
incidence $Z$ forces nodal topology and births an effective
exceptional divisor on a \emph{smooth resolved algebraic world}
whose pushforward is $[Z]$ on the same original $X$.
Unlike a split projective-bundle projector, the source
may be nonambient, as the K3 diagonal demonstrates.
It also reveals an exact obstruction to the common attempted
smooth-support shortcut: hypersurfaces containing a given
nonambient source can be \emph{forced singular}, with a
positive Chern singularity count. Blowup resolution rather
than an unverified smooth-support assumption is essential.

To convert this into the \emph{full} rational Hodge conjecture
for arbitrary $X,p,\alpha$, one must construct an incidence
center or singular causal degeneration \emph{from the
prescribed $\alpha$ without assuming it is already algebraic},
and prove its canonical original-$X$ return detects that
target. No such universally independent input is obtained
here. The K3 diagonal input is already an actual algebraic
subvariety, so it is not evidence that every rational
Hodge class has one. This route has a valid new geometric
output, while the universal source-forcing route remains active.
