\section{Original world, arbitrary fold arity, and actual geometry}

an ample algebraic line bundle L, and the nonzero-or-zero (depending on degree)
class h=c_1(L). Fix arbitrary integers r>=2 and 0<=k<=r.
Define E_{r,k}=L^{\oplus k}\oplus\mathcal O_X^{\oplus(r-k)}.
The index r is causal fold arity, whereas k is the number of independently
line-twisted directions; neither is assumed to equal d.
For each real 0<q<1, define the following actual holomorphic constructions:
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal P_{r,k}(X)=\mathbb P(\mathcal O_X\oplus E_{r,k}),
 \qquad
 \mathcal H_{r,k}(X)=
 (E_{r,k}\setminus 0_X)/\langle v\mapsto qv\rangle.} \tag{1}
\]
Both are smooth compact complex spaces of complex dimension d+r.
The first is smooth projective; the second is a holomorphic
fiber bundle with compact Hopf manifold fiber
$(\mathbb C^r\setminus\{0\})/q^{\mathbb Z}$.
The contracting action is free and properly discontinuous on the
nonzero vector bundle; a radial fundamental annulus and compactness
of X establish quotient compactness. This is not a nonholomorphic
change of coordinates on an unchanged X.

\section{The requested precise Hodge/non-Hodge bifurcation}
Choose any smooth Hermitian norm on E. The map taking a nonzero v to
its unit direction and logarithmic radius identifies the quotient
topologically and smoothly with
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal H_{r,k}(X)\cong S(E_{r,k})\times S^1.}\tag{2}
\]
The original complex rank r gives sphere fiber S^{2r-1};
it is simply connected for every r>=2. The homotopy sequence of this
sphere bundle therefore gives
$\pi_1(S(E_{r,k}))\cong\pi_1(X)$.
Consequently, with rational Betti numbers:
\[
 \boxed{b_1(\mathcal P_{r,k}(X))=b_1(X),\qquad
 b_1(\mathcal H_{r,k}(X))=b_1(X)+1.}\tag{3}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Genuine all-X non-Hodge world birth]
For every connected smooth projective complex X, every integer r>=2,
and every 0<=k<=r, the world $\mathcal P_{r,k}(X)$ admits its canonical
pure rational Hodge structure in each degree, whereas the smooth
compact complex world $\mathcal H_{r,k}(X)$ admits NO pure rational
weight-one Hodge structure on its first rational cohomology.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Smooth projective complex X is Kahler, so $b_1(X)=2h^{1,0}(X)$ is even.
The projective bundle is smooth projective and the projective bundle
formula preserves $H^1(X)$.
By (2), $b_1$ in the Hopf quotient is odd. A pure rational Hodge
structure of weight one has conjugate summands $H^{1,0}$ and $H^{0,1}$
of equal dimension, forcing even rank. Thus the odd-dimensional
rational cohomology of the Hopf quotient cannot carry such a structure.
In particular, it is non-Kahler.
\end{proof}
The Hopf outcome is a genuine geometric failure of the pure-Hodge
weight-one condition, NOT merely a positive or negative MHS weight gap:
a compact nonalgebraic complex manifold is not automatically equipped
with Deligne's algebraic mixed-Hodge filtration. Nor can the unchanged
original smooth projective X cease to be Hodge. It is the constructed
new world that fails the Hodge condition.

\section{The full-arity causal Chern threshold and arbitrary targets}
By the Whitney product formula:
\[
 c(E_{r,k})=(1+h)^k,\qquad
 \boxed{c_r(E_{r,k})=
 \begin{cases}0 & k<r,\\h^r & k=r.\end{cases}}\tag{4}
\]
Let $\pi:\mathcal H_{r,k}(X)\to X$ be the holomorphic bundle map.
The oriented real rank-2r unit-sphere bundle has Euler class
$e(E_{\mathbb R})=c_r(E)$, and its exact rational Gysin segment is
\[
 H^{j-2r}(X)\xrightarrow{c_r(E)\smile-}H^j(X)
  \xrightarrow{\pi_S^*}H^j(S(E)).
\]
The second pullback $H^j(S(E))\to H^j(S(E)\times S^1)$ is injective
by restricting along a chosen circle point. Thus in every degree:
\[
 \boxed{\ker[\pi^*:H^j(X,\mathbb Q)\to
 H^j(\mathcal H_{r,k}(X),\mathbb Q)]
 =c_r(E_{r,k})\smile H^{j-2r}(X,\mathbb Q).}\tag{5}
\]
This has TWO exact regimes. When $k<r$, the bundle E has a trivial
direct summand and thus an actual nowhere-zero holomorphic section;
its q-orbit defines $s:X\to\mathcal H$, with $\pi s=id_X$.
Hence $s^*\pi^*=id$ for EVERY degree and EVERY original rational
Hodge input $\alpha$, including nonalgebraic ones (without assuming any).
For $k=r$, the kernel is precisely the full image of $h^r$ cup product.
If $2\le r\le d$, ampleness ensures $h^r\ne0$ since
$\int_Xh^d>0$, so the genuine original-X algebraic Hodge class
$h^r$ is destroyed by this topology-changing pullback, but remains
nonzero under projective-bundle pullback:
\[
 \boxed{\pi_{\mathcal H_{r,r}}^*h^r=0,\qquad
 \pi_{\mathcal P_{r,r}}^*h^r\ne0.}\tag{6}
\]
The constructed compact Hopf world is not falsely declared a
smooth-projective counterexample to the rational Hodge conjecture.
For $r>d$ the top Chern class vanishes for dimension reasons;
Hodge failure persists by (3), but no base class is annihilated.

\section{Uniform FULL Betti-spectrum equation for arbitrary original X}
Write $b_j=\dim_{\mathbb Q}H^j(X,\mathbb Q)$, setting negative-index
groups to zero, and introduce the true original-X multiplication ranks
\[
 a_j^{r,k}=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}
 [c_r(E_{r,k})\smile-:H^{j-2r}(X)\to H^j(X)],\quad
 R_{r,k}(t)=\sum_j a_j^{r,k}t^j,\quad B_X(t)=\sum_j b_jt^j.
\]
The Gysin long exact sequence splits as short exact sequences of
rational vector spaces, and gives
$b_j(S(E))=b_j+b_{j-2r+1}-a_j^{r,k}-a_{j+1}^{r,k}$.
Therefore the two entire-world, all-dimensional realization equations are:
\[
 \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 B_{\mathrm{pure}}(t)
   &=B_X(t)\sum_{i=0}^rt^{2i},\\
 B_{\mathrm{break}}(t)
   &=(1+t)\left[(1+t^{2r-1})B_X(t)
        -(1+t^{-1})R_{r,k}(t)\right],\\
 R_{r,k}(t)&=0\quad(k<r),\\
 R_{r,r}(t)&=\sum_j\operatorname{rank}(h^r\smile-:
 H^{j-2r}(X)\to H^j(X))t^j .
 \end{aligned}}\tag{7}
\]
The apparent t^{-1} causes no negative powers because a_j=0
for j<2r. These are actual cohomology results, not guessed
Hodge polynomials on a non-Kahler manifold.
