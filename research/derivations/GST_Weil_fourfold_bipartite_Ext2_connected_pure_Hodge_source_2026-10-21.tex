\begin{abstract}
We attack the actual source-birth gap, rather than adding another
abstract source-completion equivalence. For the authentic split
Weil-type abelian fourfold E^4 with E having complex multiplication
by i, we construct a completely explicit nonzero coupled
dg-perfect-complex object whose rational Chern character is
four times a nonzero primitive rational exceptional Weil Hodge class,
with no other Chern grades. The construction uses eight genuine
abelian graph surfaces with signed K-classes, one geometrically
neutral positive/negative auxiliary pair, and actual degree-two
crossing Ext classes. Exact signed graph expansions, crossing
multiplicities and connectivity are independently checked with
Wolfram. This repairs the degree-three shift-parity failure of the
older six-dimensional bare graph sheaf architecture in the
four-dimensional case. The Weil character has zero first-order
Hodge-type leakage along balanced K-linear deformations, while
the derived Atiyah--Kodaira--Spencer class is not proved zero.
Accordingly, this is an actual coupled central-fiber geometric
source, NOT an unearned nonsplit deformation or an all-X proof.
\end{abstract}
\section{Original smooth projective variety and explicit algebraic surfaces}
Let E=C/(Z+i Z) be the genuine CM elliptic curve and
A=E_{x_1}\times E_{x_2}\times E_{y_1}\times E_{y_2}.
The K=Q(i) action is i on the two x factors and -i on
the two y factors, giving balanced Weil Hodge signature (2,2).
For \lambda_1,\lambda_2 in Z[i] define an ACTUAL smooth
embedded abelian surface in this original projective fourfold:
\[
 Z_{\lambda_1,\lambda_2}
 =\{\,y_1=\lambda_1 x_1,\quad y_2=\lambda_2 x_2\,\}
             \subset A.                            \tag{1.1}
\]
Its normal bundle is algebraically trivial of rank two
(the quotient of translation-invariant tangent bundles).
By scheme GRR on an abelian variety (Todd classes equal one),
\[
 \boxed{\ch[\mathcal O_{Z_{\lambda_1,\lambda_2}}]
          =[Z_{\lambda_1,\lambda_2}]
          \in\CH^2(A)_\Q,\quad
          \ch_j=0\ (j\ne2).}                        \tag{1.2}
\]
No class map on A is freely specified: all sources are actual
coherent sheaves of explicitly defined algebraic subvarieties.

For a single paired surface E_{x_j}\times E_{y_j} denote by
G_{\lambda,j} the actual graph divisor y_j=\lambda x_j,
and let e_x,f_x,e_y,f_y be the integral H^1 basis
compatible with [i]. Define the ACTUAL rational Chow divisor classes FIRST:
\[
 U_j:=\tfrac12([G_{i,j}]-[G_{-i,j}]),\qquad
 V_j:=-\tfrac12([G_{1,j}]-[G_{-1,j}])
               \in\CH^1(E_{x_j}\times E_{y_j})_\Q .
\]                                                     \tag{1.3}
Under the GENUINE canonical Betti class map and a compatible
integral cohomology basis, their exterior expressions are
\[
 \cl(U_j)=e_{x_j}e_{y_j}+f_{x_j}f_{y_j},\qquad
 \cl(V_j)=e_{x_j}f_{y_j}-f_{x_j}e_{y_j}.
\]
Thus every equality involving products of U and V
before Betti realization is an actual CHOW equality,
not a false identification of cohomology with rational equivalence.
They follow by expanding the pullback of the point class
under the actual homomorphism (x,y)\mapsto y-\lambda x.

\section{Exact codimension-two crossing Ext and parity repair}
Take two graph surfaces \(Z_\lambda,Z_\mu\) such that

  N_{\Q(i)/\Q}(\lambda_1-\mu_1)\,
  N_{\Q(i)/\Q}(\lambda_2-\mu_2).}                  \tag{3.1}
\]
This follows from the two independent elliptic isogenies
\([\lambda_j-\mu_j]\) of the true CM elliptic curve.

(2+i,2+i), the difference is \(-1-i\),
of norm 2 on each factor, hence intersection length 4.
For the pair (1,1) and (2+i,2+i) with a different
normalization used in older sixfold records,
degrees must be computed from the exact displayed
graph equations, not borrowed from other dimensions.
In particular for slopes (1,1) and (2+i,2+i)
the intersection length is 4; for (1,1) and

At each transverse crossing, the completed local ring and

\[
 R=\mathbb C[[x_1,x_2,y_1,y_2]],\qquad
 I_{Z_\lambda}=(x_1,x_2),\quad
 I_{Z_\mu}=(y_1,y_2).
\]
Applying the x-Koszul resolution to the ring R/(y_1,y_2)

\]
Its support is finite, hence the local-to-global Ext spectral

\[

         \Ext_A^q=0\ (q\ne2).}                     \tag{3.3}
\]
These are genuine coherent Ext groups, not assumed

A degree-one morphism of dg components
\(\mathcal O_{Z_\lambda}[0]\to

has type
\(\mathrm{Hom}^1=\Ext^2\).
The shift difference one gives opposite K0 signs, so unlike
