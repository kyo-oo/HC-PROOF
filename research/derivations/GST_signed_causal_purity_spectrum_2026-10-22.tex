\begin{abstract}
The previous GST causal graph and holonomy theorems construct arbitrarily
negative purity gaps on projective singular worlds. Here a DIFFERENT
geometric causal realization, a free Kummer quotient of an algebraic
torus twisted by cyclic permutations of $X^m$, yields all positive
purity gaps on genuinely smooth quasi-projective worlds. We obtain
an entire three-branch spectrum for the SAME arbitrary smooth
projective $X$ and any prescribed rational $(p,p)$ Hodge class:
negative on nodal-projective, zero on smooth-projective, and positive
on smooth-open worlds. A uniform weight argument proves that every
nonzero positive-gap target is not the usual Betti cycle class of a
Chow cycle of the cohomologically matching codimension on the open
world. The smooth projective middle branch preserves and reflects
algebraicity on the original $X$. This classifies a concrete
dimension/causality phenomenon without silently modifying the
rational Hodge conjecture's smooth-projective domain.
\end{abstract}

\section{Immutable target, exact inputs and previous GST laws}
Fix any nonempty connected smooth projective complex variety $X$
of dimension $d$, an integer $p\geq0$, and a nonzero
$\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)$.
The classical all-$X$ statement seeks an actual cycle on X with
canonical rational Betti class $\alpha$; \emph{no such source is
assumed here}. Fix any $m\geq2$ and let
$B=X^m$, $G=C_m$, with $\rho$ the cyclic permutation of $m$
factors.  The GST source is dimensionless General Space and its
arbitrary higher-dimensional causal birth relations; the geometric
realization below is where the complex dimension, cohomology, weight
and types are actually derived.

Write $V_k=H^k(B,\mathbb Q)$ and
$K_k=V_k^G$. The previous cyclic and all-graph GST equations
give the \emph{actual} projective nodal cyclic quotient $Y_m$
with
\[
 P_{Y_m}=(1+t)I_m+A P_B,\quad
 P_B=P_X^m,\quad A=t^2s^2uv,\quad
 I_m=P_{K_*}.
 \tag{1}
\]
They also give its actual smooth projective torsion-elliptic
companion $S_m$ with
\[
 P_{S_m}=(1+ts(u+v)+A)I_m.
 \tag{2}
\]
These are geometrically distinct realizations, not a claimed
interpolating flat family. The Hodge-weight generating polynomial
$P_Z(t,s,u,v)$ records ordinary Betti degree $k$, mixed-Hodge
weight $w$, and graded type $(p,q)$ by $t^ks^wu^pv^q$.
Set $\chi=p-q$, $\delta=w-k$.

\section{New open causal holonomy realization}
Let $\zeta$ be a primitive complex $m$th root of unity.
On the smooth quasi-projective product
$B\times\mathbb G_m$ let $G$ act algebraically by
\[
    \sigma(b,z)=(\rho b,\zeta z).
\]
The action is \emph{free}, since $\zeta^jz=z$ with $z\ne0$
forces $m\mid j$, independently of fixed points of $\rho$.
The quotient
\[
 \boxed{\ U_m(X)=(B\times\mathbb G_m)/C_m\ }
 \tag{3}
\]
is an actual smooth quasi-projective complex variety of
dimension $md+1$; its finite covering is algebraic and etale.
The map $z\mapsto z^m$ exhibits an open causal loop whose
monodromy permutes the complete $X^m$ cohomology, not merely
a rank-one graph label.

\begin{theorem}[Open causal Galois-Hodge genesis]
For every integer $k$, with negative-index groups zero, the
rational mixed Hodge structure is
\[
 \boxed{H^k(U_m(X),\mathbb Q)
    \cong K_k\oplus K_{k-1}(-1).}
 \tag{4}
\]
Here $K_k$ has pure weight $k$, whereas
$K_{k-1}(-1)$ has pure weight $k+1$.
In particular the open branch has the EXACT generating polynomial
\[
 \boxed{P_{U_m}=(1+ts^2uv)\, I_m.}
 \tag{5}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The Galois cover $B\times\mathbb G_m\to U_m$ is finite
etale of degree $m$; rational transfer yields a mixed-Hodge
isomorphism to its $G$-invariant cohomology.
$H^0(\mathbb G_m,\mathbb Q)=\mathbb Q(0)$ and
$H^1(\mathbb G_m,\mathbb Q)=\mathbb Q(-1)$, of
cohomological degree one but weight two and type $(1,1)$.
Multiplication by a complex root of unity is homotopic
to identity and induces the identity on these groups.
Rational Kunneth therefore gives (4)--(5); all summands
are genuine MHS summands of a geometric open realization.
\end{proof}

\section{Full GST signed purity-gap spectrum: arbitrary depths}
Let $D_1$ be the genuine rational projective curve with one
ordinary node. It has degree/weight polynomial $1+t+A$,
and $H^1(D_1,\mathbb Q)=\mathbb Q(0)$.

For integers $a\geq1$ and $b\geq0$ define the actual
finite-type complex algebraic world
\[
 U_{m;a,b}(X)=U_m(X)\times(\mathbb G_m)^{a-1}\times D_1^b.
 \tag{6}
\]
Its complex dimension is $md+a+b$, it is smooth when
$b=0$, and it is quasi-projective (not proper).
Its full mixed-Hodge polynomial is
\[
 \boxed{P_{U_{m;a,b}}=
       I_m(1+ts^2uv)^a(1+t+A)^b.}
 \tag{7}
\]
For pure negative gaps one instead uses the existing genuinely
projective, generally singular world
\[
 N_{m;b}(X)=Y_m(X)\times D_1^{b-1}\quad(b\geq1),
 \qquad
 \boxed{P_{N_{m;b}}=((1+t)I_m+A P_B)(1+t+A)^{b-1}.}
 \tag{8}
\]

Define the cyclic Hodge lift
\[
 \Theta_m(\alpha)=\sum_{i=1}^m\operatorname{pr}_i^*\alpha
                 \ \in K_{2p}.
 \tag{9}
\]
For $p>0$ the point insertion
$s:X\to B$, $x\mapsto(x,x_0,\ldots,x_0)$
satisfies $s^*\Theta_m(\alpha)=\alpha$.
For $p=0$ it returns $m\alpha$. Thus
$\Theta_m$ is injective in ALL codimensions, and the
recovery uses an actual algebraic map.

\begin{theorem}[Signed GST causal Hodge spectrum]
For EVERY nonzero rational Hodge $\alpha$ on EVERY original $X$,
EVERY $m\ge2$, and EVERY $a\ge1,b\ge0$, there is an
explicit nonzero rational class in
\[
  \operatorname{Gr}^{W}_{2p+2a}
       H^{2p+a+b}(U_{m;a,b}(X),\mathbb Q)
 \quad\text{of type }(p+a,p+a).
 \tag{10}
\]
It is the tensor of $\Theta_m(\alpha)$ with the unique
top exterior product of the $a$ open-torus $H^1$ generators
and with the $b$ nodal-graph $H^1$ generators.
Its precise GST signed invariants are
\[
 \boxed{\chi=0,\qquad \delta=a-b.}
 \tag{11}
\]
For EVERY $b\ge1$, the projective nodal world $N_{m;b}$
instead contains the nonzero rational pure weight-graded sector
\[
 \operatorname{Gr}^{W}_{2p}
 H^{2p+b}(N_{m;b},\mathbb Q)\supset K_{2p},
 \qquad(\chi,\delta)=(0,-b).
 \tag{12}
\]
These facts hold for every Hodge $\alpha$, whether
algebraic or not, and do not require a nonzero
primitive or CM-specific source.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The $G$-invariant Kunneth summand $\Theta_m(\alpha)$
is nonzero by (9). The highest cohomological degree of
the open torus $(\mathbb G_m)^a$ is one-dimensional
$\mathbb Q(-a)$ in degree $a$ (weight $2a$);
all $b$ nodal graph factors contribute
$\mathbb Q(0)$ in degree $b$ (weight zero).
Finite etale transfer, Kunneth and strictness of MHS
prove the nonzero claimed weight-graded summand.
The source has degree $2p+a+b$, bidegree
$(p+a,p+a)$ and weight $2p+2a$. Direct subtraction
proves (11).  The negative branch is the $K_{2p}$
weight-$2p$ summand of the first fold (1), tensored
with graph lines on $D_1^{b-1}$, giving (12).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Every integer gap, including exact cancellation]
Varying the numbers of causal open births $a$ and nodal births
$b$ realizes \emph{every integer value of $\delta$}
using an actual geometric world carrying any fixed $\alpha$.
Moreover $a=b>0$ gives $(\chi,\delta)=(0,0)$ in a
nonproper/singular world: even zero charge AND zero gap
do not logically guarantee the smooth-projective
hypotheses of the rational Hodge conjecture.
\end{corollary}

\section{Same complex dimension, three radically different outcomes}
For any $\ell\ge1$ define three concrete algebraic
worlds of the \emph{same} complex dimension $md+2\ell$:
\[
 \begin{array}{lll}
 Y^-_\ell=Y_m(X)\times D_1^{2\ell-1}
   &\text{projective, singular},\\
 Y^0_\ell=S_m(X)\times(\mathbb P^1)^{2\ell-1}
   &\text{smooth, projective},\\
 Y^+_\ell=U_m(X)\times(\mathbb G_m)^{2\ell-1}
   &\text{smooth, quasi-projective}.
 \end{array}                                             \tag{13}
\]
Their exact entire cohomological generating laws are
\[
 \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 P_-&=((1+t)I_m+A P_B)(1+t+A)^{2\ell-1},\\
 P_0&=(1+ts(u+v)+A)I_m(1+A)^{2\ell-1},\\
 P_+&=I_m(1+ts^2uv)^{2\ell}.
 \end{aligned}}                                          \tag{14}
\]
The SAME nonzero original-$X$ target $\alpha$ injects
into nonzero associated-graded sectors of cohomological
degree $2p+2\ell$ with
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
    \text{world}&(p',q')&w&\delta\\ \hline
    Y^-_\ell&(p,p)&2p&-2\ell\\
    Y^0_\ell&(p+\ell,p+\ell)&2p+2\ell&0\\
    Y^+_\ell&(p+2\ell,p+2\ell)&2p+4\ell&+2\ell.
 \end{array}}                                             \tag{15}
\]
The middle class is the finite-etale descent
$\widehat\alpha$ on $S_m$, cup-product with
$\ell$ chosen algebraic hyperplane classes on
$(\mathbb P^1)^{2\ell-1}$. This is a genuine rational
pure Hodge class and its algebraicity on $Y^0_\ell$
is EQUIVALENT to algebraicity of $\alpha$ on X:
forward direction uses averaged Chow transfer and
hyperplane products; reverse applies projective
pushforward along the chosen $\ell$ factors and
point restriction on the remaining factors, then
the finite-etale pullback and original-X point
insertion of the previous cyclic-fold theorem.

No implicit comparison between cohomologies of the three
different worlds is asserted: the injections are three
independent functorial realizations of the same original-X
Hodge vector.

\section{Uniform positive-gap NONALGEBRAIC class on smooth varieties}
\begin{theorem}[Causal topological obstruction to a native Chow source]
For $\ell\ge1$ and $\alpha\ne0$, the rational class
$\beta_+=\mathfrak B^+_{\ell}(\alpha)$ constructed in
$H^{2p+2\ell}(Y^+_\ell,\mathbb Q)$ is NOT in the image
of the ordinary canonical rational Betti cycle-class map
\[
 \mathrm{cl}^{\rm can}:
 CH^{p+\ell}(Y^+_\ell)_{\mathbb Q}
       \longrightarrow H^{2p+2\ell}(Y^+_\ell,\mathbb Q).
 \tag{16}
\]
This gives for EVERY original $X$ a smooth complex
quasi-projective realization with a concrete nonzero,
rational, diagonal but weight-mismatched cohomology class
not representable by any algebraic cycle of the matching
cohomological codimension.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The fold class $\beta_+$ lies in the pure MHS summand
$\Theta_m(\alpha)\otimes\mathbb Q(-2\ell)$ of weight
$2p+4\ell$, with cohomological degree $2p+2\ell$.
Because $Y^+_\ell$ is smooth quasi-projective,
the canonical Betti class of every algebraic
codimension-$(p+\ell)$ cycle lies in the
weight-$2p+2\ell$, type-$(p+\ell,p+\ell)$
Hodge part of cohomological degree $2p+2\ell$.
Equivalently the rational Chow class map after
Tate twisting is a morphism from pure weight-zero
classes into the weight-zero part of
$H^{2p+2\ell}(Y^+_\ell,\mathbb Q(p+\ell))$.
A nonzero class of weight $2p+4\ell$ cannot
be equal to such a cycle class because distinct
MHS weight-graded summands intersect trivially.
Our class is nonzero by the injective cyclic
lift and Kunneth, hence is not algebraic.
\end{proof}
This is NOT a counterexample to the rational Hodge
conjecture: the new world is smooth but NONPROPER.
The result is instead a precise positive-branch
purity obstruction, directly born from the causal
topological operation rather than fabricated by
changing the definition of Hodge structure.

\section{A cohomological causal-residue decoder for EVERY target}
The positive open birth is not merely an injection: its complete
original-X Hodge data can be recovered \emph{cohomologically} by an
explicit residual operation. This sharpens the source/target
interpretation of GST N-cohomology independent hole readouts.

Write $a\ge1$ and initially $b=0$.
The degree-$m$ Kummer map on the first torus coordinate
is $w=z^m$, and the invariant rational cohomology class
\[
 \lambda_1={1\over m}\left[{1\over2\pi i}{dw\over w}\right]
 \in H^1(U_m,\mathbb Q)
\]
pulls back to the normalized rational class
$[(2\pi i)^{-1}dz/z]$ on the covering product.
For $i=2,\ldots,a$, take the usual normalized
$\lambda_i=[(2\pi i)^{-1}dz_i/z_i]$ on the other
torus coordinates. Their exterior product
$\Lambda_a=\lambda_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\lambda_a$
is nonzero, of cohomological degree $a$, weight $2a$
and type $(a,a)$.

The invariant class $\Theta_m(\alpha)$ descends uniquely
to a rational class $\alpha_U$ along the finite etale
$B\times\mathbb G_m\to U_m$, by rational transfer.
Thus the \emph{actual} born topological class can be
written without a noncanonical basis as
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 \beta^+_a(\alpha)=\alpha_U\smile\Lambda_a
    \ \in H^{2p+a}\bigl(U_m\times(\mathbb G_m)^{a-1},
                   \mathbb Q\bigr).
 \qquad}                                                \tag{18}
\]
Let $\pi_a:B\times(\mathbb G_m)^a\to
U_m\times(\mathbb G_m)^{a-1}$ be the finite cover.
Projection to the last $H^a((\mathbb G_m)^a)$ Kunneth
coefficient defines a rational weight-graded map
\[
 \operatorname{Res}_a:
 H^{2p+a}\bigl(U_m\times(\mathbb G_m)^{a-1}\bigr)
 \xrightarrow{\pi_a^*}
 H^{2p+a}\bigl(B\times(\mathbb G_m)^a\bigr)
 \longrightarrow H^{2p}(B)(-a).
\]
It obeys the exact \emph{source-address recovery law}
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \operatorname{Res}_a\beta^+_a(\alpha)
         =\Theta_m(\alpha)\otimes\mathbf1(-a).
 \quad}                                                \tag{19}
\]
Untwisting by the specified normalized torus class,
then pulling back along the genuine point insertion
$s:X\hookrightarrow B$, returns precisely
$\alpha$ for $p>0$ and $m\alpha$ for $p=0$.
This is a full-$\alpha$ cohomological left inverse.

The Tate twist $(-a)$ is essential: the residue lowers
ordinary cohomological degree by $a$ and MHS weight by
$2a$.  Erasing it would create a spurious contradiction
with purity. Even though the recovered cohomology equals
$\alpha$, the residue of a \emph{non-Chow} positive-gap
class cannot by itself provide a cycle of $CH^p(X)$.
The theorem supplies a verified cohomological decoder,
not an unproved motivic or Chow realization map.

\section{The strict weight/causality firewall}
For a pure weight-$w$ rational MHS summand and a
smooth projective target with pure cohomological
degree $k$, every degree-preserving MHS morphism
from the former to the latter is zero when $w\ne k$.
Indeed morphisms respect the Hodge and weight
filtrations, and pure MHS of unequal weights
have no nonzero morphisms. Hence neither an
unshifted negative-fold class of weight $k-r$
nor an unshifted positive-fold class of weight
$k+r$ can be converted into a nonzero pure
smooth-projective degree-$k$ class by a
degree-preserving MHS morphism.
Correspondences with explicit degree shifts,
Gysin maps and Tate twists must be typed
separately; this theorem does NOT forbid them.

At the GRADING level the two elementary GST births are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 O:(k,p,q)&\mapsto(k+1,p+1,q+1),
    &\Delta(\chi,\delta)&=(0,+1),\\
 D:(k,p,q)&\mapsto(k+1,p,q),
    &\Delta(\chi,\delta)&=(0,-1).
 \end{aligned}
\]
They commute, yielding for every $a,b\in\mathbb N$
\[
 \boxed{F_{a,b}(k,p,q)
  =(k+a+b,p+a,q+a),\quad
  \chi(F)=\chi,\quad
  \delta(F)=\delta+a-b.}                               \tag{17}
\]
The composition law is
$F_{a,b}\circ F_{c,d}=F_{a+c,b+d}$.
This is a typed all-arity causal signature law in the
existing \texttt{GSTGradedWorldAlgebra.lean} ontology.
The preceding algebraic quotient constructions supply
its true geometric meaning and specify which versions
are smooth/projective/open/singular; equation (17)
alone does NOT assert algebraic Hodge existence.
