\begin{abstract}
This is an attempt to close the exact geometric arrow of the
owner's dimensionless-General-Space to arbitrarily high-dimensional
actual-realization argument, without replacing the target by
an elliptic test family or by a Chow-source compiler.
The existing all-original-$X$ Jouanolou incidence space has
nontrivial geometry beyond its homotopy type: it is an affine
TORSOR under an algebraic vector bundle $V$.
For every rational cohomology class, arbitrary original
smooth projective $X$, and every admissible degree, we construct
actual holomorphic bundles on the Stein total space and prove
that (1) holomorphic vertical first-order connections ALWAYS exist,
(2) holomorphic causal transports along EVERY individual translation
always exist, and (3) a precise missing HIGHER-CAUSAL coherence
equation is flatness of their two-direction vertical curvature.
For every original $X$, a holomorphic bundle on the fold has a
flat vertical holomorphic connection if and only if it is the
pullback of an actual holomorphic bundle on compact $X$;
projective GAGA then identifies the required algebraic
geometry. For any rational Hodge class, existence of TWO such
flat original-X compatible bundles with the prescribed entire
Chern-character difference is equivalent to a genuine original-X
canonical rational Chow representative.
We prove that adjoining any number of TRIVIAL extra fold
dimensions to a fixed holomorphic bundle does not remove this
curvature-descent obstruction.
The theorem isolates the genuine higher causal 2-cell relation;
a first-order Oka connection is not itself the all-original-$X$
integrability theorem. No conclusion about universal flatness
is presumed.
\end{abstract}
