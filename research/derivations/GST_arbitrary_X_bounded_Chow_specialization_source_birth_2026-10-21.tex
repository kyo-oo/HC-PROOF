\section{Immutable original-variety target, source types, and route difference}
Take any smooth projective complex \(d\)-fold \(X\), every
\(0\le p\le d\), and every
\(
\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X).
\)
The required output is an ACTUAL rational algebraic
\(Z_\alpha\in\CH^p(X)_\Q\) with the GENUINE
analytic fundamental-class comparison
\[
                 \cl_X^{\mathrm{can}} Z_\alpha=\alpha.
                                                               \tag{1}
\]
The earlier GST K0/Euler, finite-degree return, and
Chern--Segre results compile already-constructed actual
algebraic source columns, but cannot manufacture an
output outside the full image of the canonical class map.
This attempt therefore changes the INPUT geometry:
algebraic cycles are first constructed on OTHER
nearby fibers of a genuinely smooth projective
DEFORMATION FAMILY, not selected inside the
pre-existing original-\(X\) Chow group.
The new mathematical question is whether such
geometrically born cycles can be transported to
the original target without prior Chow preimage.

\section{Actual original-X source from projective Chow limits}
Let
\[
 \pi:\mathfrak X\to\Delta
\]
be any smooth projective analytic family over
a sufficiently small complex disk, with
\(\mathfrak X_0=X\), equipped with an honest
relative projective embedding
\(\mathfrak X\subset \mathbf P^N\times\Delta\).
Choose the genuine Ehresmann/Gauss--Manin
identification of the rational local systems
near \(0\). Denote by \(\alpha_t\) the
horizontal rational continuation of the
given original class \(\alpha_0=\alpha\);
it is not assumed algebraic on the central fiber.

\begin{theorem}[Universal bounded Chow specialization as an actual original-X source constructor]
Fix a codimension \(p\), a positive integer
\(N_0\) and a nonnegative integer \(D\).
Suppose there is a sequence \(t_j\to0\)
of noncentral smooth fibers, and on EACH
of those genuinely different original
fibers two INDEPENDENTLY CONSTRUCTED
effective algebraic codimension-\(p\)
cycles \(A_j^+,A_j^-\), each of relative
projective degree at most \(D\), such that
\[
 \boxed{
 \cl_{\mathfrak X_{t_j}}^{\mathrm{can}}
          \left(\frac{A_j^+-A_j^-}{N_0}\right)
                         =\alpha_{t_j}.}          \tag{2}
\]
No rational Chow representative of \(\alpha_0\)
on \(X\) is assumed.
Then a pair of ACTUAL effective
cycles \(A_0^\pm\) is CONSTRUCTED on
the ORIGINAL \(X\), with
degrees at most \(D\), and
\[
 \boxed{
 Z_\alpha=\frac{A_0^+-A_0^-}{N_0}
        \in\CH^p(X)_\Q,\qquad
 \cl_X^{\mathrm{can}}(Z_\alpha)=\alpha.}           \tag{3}
\]
The construction is the genuine
relative-Chow specialization of the
two effective cycle sequences, not
a supplied Hodge-to-Chow selection on \(X\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Relative Chow spaces of effective
codimension-\(p\) cycles of each FIXED
projective degree \(e\) are proper over
the projective-family base. Their finite
union over \(0\le e\le D\) remains proper.
Take the fiber product of TWO such
degree-bounded relative Chow spaces
over \(\Delta\); this also is proper.
The points \((t_j,A_j^+,A_j^-)\)
therefore have an accumulation point
\((0,A_0^+,A_0^-)\) in the central
Chow fiber, after passing to a
subsequence. Both \(A_0^\pm\) are
genuine effective projective algebraic
cycles on \(\mathfrak X_0=X\), of the
claimed dimension and degree.

In a smooth proper family, canonical
Betti cycle classes of a
continuous family of algebraic cycles
are locally constant in the
horizontal rational cohomology local
system. Equivalently, under an
Ehresmann trivialization,
integration of closed smooth
differential forms over the
specializing algebraic cycle currents
converges to integration over the
limiting effective cycle.
Consequently, under the local system
identification,
\(
 \cl(A_j^\pm)\to\cl(A_0^\pm).
\)
But each signed combination in (2)
has the same constant transported
Betti class \(\alpha\).
The rational cohomology lattice is
finite-dimensional and its
cohomology pairing with the
limiting cycle is continuous.
Thus
\[
 \cl_X^{can}(A_0^+-A_0^-)
     =N_0\alpha.
\]
Dividing by \(N_0\) proves (3).
Every map used in the construction
is the ACTUAL Chow, analytic-cycle
or Gauss--Manin comparison map of the
stated original family.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Necessary algebraic-degree escape for a hypothetical missing target]
If a given rational Hodge
\(\alpha\in\Hdg^p(X)_\Q\)
is NOT in
\(\im(\cl_X^{can})\),
then for every smooth projective
family through \(X\), every positive
integer \(N_0\), and every finite
effective degree bound \(D\),
there CANNOT exist a sequence
of noncentral fibers approaching
the original \(X\) satisfying
(2) with those bounds.
Thus along any genuine family of
nearby algebraic realizations of
the same horizontal Hodge class,
at least the effective degree
bound or the denominator bound
must eventually escape ALL
fixed finite bounds.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Contraposition of the theorem,
quantified over \(N_0,D\).
This is a criterion for a
hypothetical defect and does
not assert the existence of
any such defective variety.
\end{proof}

\section{STRONG bounded-complexity GST source locus theorem on arbitrary deformation bases}
The preceding specialization
constructs a cycle on
the ORIGINAL \(X\)

For fixed

 \boxed{
  t_j\to0,\quad
  t_j\in S_{D,N_0}(\alpha)
   \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  S_{D,N_0}(\alpha)=\Delta .
 }                                                       \tag{3b}

\section{GST arbitrary-X primitive residual has a STRICT quantitative exclusion radius}
Now suppose \(d=2p\), choose a genuine
ample divisor \(h\) and the primitive
rational \((p,p)\) Hodge sector
\[
 V=\Hdg_{\mathrm{prim}}^p(X)_\Q,\qquad
 Q(r,s)=(-1)^p\int_Xr\,s.
\]
The genuine Hodge--Riemann pairing \(Q\)
is positive definite on \(V_\R\).
Let
\(A_{\mathrm{alg}}\subset V\)
be the ACTUAL rational
primitive algebraic image
\[
 A_{\mathrm{alg}}=
  \im(\cl^{can}_{X,p})\cap V.
\]
Importantly this is an INTERSECTION
with the canonical Chow image,
not the orthogonal projection of
that image: the algebraicity of an
arbitrary primitive cohomological
projector is NOT assumed.
Thus every \(z\in A_{\mathrm{alg}}\)
is itself the genuine canonical
Betti class of some ACTUAL
primitive algebraic rational Chow
combination on the original \(X\).

For any \(r\in V\) let
\(\pi_A r\) be its rational
\(Q\)-orthogonal projection
to \(A_{\mathrm{alg}}\), and
set \(r_\perp=r-\pi_A r\).

\begin{theorem}[Quantitative universal GST native-image exclusion radius]
For every arbitrary original
\(X\), every \(p\), every \(r\)
and every algebraic native
primitive class
\(z\in A_{\mathrm{alg}}\),
\[
 \boxed{
 Q(r-z,r-z)
 =Q(r_\perp,r_\perp)
       +Q(\pi_A r-z,\pi_A r-z)
 \ \ge Q(r_\perp,r_\perp).}                       \tag{4}
\]
Thus if the full rational
native residual \(r_\perp\ne0\),
not even an INFINITE SEQUENCE
of finite source combinations
from the ORIGINAL algebraic
Chow image can converge to
\(r\) in polarized Hodge norm.
Allowing infinitely many
GST native Gram, Euler,
Segre, Adams, kernel or
finite-degree descent
operations that retain
the same original-X
algebraic image cannot
remove this strict
positive exclusion radius.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Orthogonality
\(r_\perp\perp A_{\mathrm{alg}}\)
kills the cross term in
the expansion of
\(r-z=r_\perp+(\pi_A r-z)\).
Both remaining terms are
nonnegative by positive
definiteness. If
\(r_\perp\ne0\), then
\(Q(r_\perp,r_\perp)>0\),
independently of how many
algebraic sources are used,
their denominators, or any
limit of their finite rational
linear combinations.
This is the exact GST
native-residual polarization
law applied to the COMPLETE
canonical image, not merely
a chosen finite Gram frame.
\end{proof}
