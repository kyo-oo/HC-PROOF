\section{An actual extension-sensitive two-handle fold}
Let $E_\tau=\C/(\mathbb Z+\tau\mathbb Z)$, $\operatorname{Im}\tau>0$.
Use marked rational cohomology $a,b$ with $\int_Ea\wedge b=1$ and
$H^{1,0}=\C(a+\tau b)$. Set
\[
 v=Aa_1a_2+Ba_1b_2+Cb_1a_2+Db_1b_2,\qquad A,B,C,D\in\Q,
 \quad \operatorname{vol}=a_1b_1a_2b_2.
\]
Juxtaposition denotes exterior cup product, in this fixed factor order.
\begin{theorem}[Exact period equation, not a signature surrogate]
On the actual projective surface $E_{\tau_1}\times E_{\tau_2}$,
\[
 v\in H^{1,1}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \Phi_v(\tau_1,\tau_2):=A\tau_1\tau_2-B\tau_1-C\tau_2+D=0. \tag{1}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Expansion gives $v(a_1+\tau_1b_1)(a_2+\tau_2b_2)=-\Phi_v\operatorname{vol}$.
The $(2,0)$ form detects the $(0,2)$ coefficient. Since $v$ is rational,
complex conjugation also kills its $(2,0)$ coefficient. These are the only
non-$(1,1)$ summands in this cross Kunneth block.
\end{proof}

Fix positive integers $n_1,n_2$ and complex constants $u_1,u_2$ and put
$\tau_i=n_i z+u_i$ on an upper half-plane where both imaginary parts
are positive. Equivalently $q_i=e^{2\pi i u_i}t^{n_i}$ for
$t=e^{2\pi i z}$: these are genuine elliptic punctured-disk families.
The parameter family here is analytic over the punctured disk; each fiber
is an algebraic projective elliptic curve. No algebraic finite-type model
of this entire period-parameter base is being asserted.
The marked transformation under $z\mapsto z+1$ is $a_i\mapsto a_i+n_i b_i$,
$b_i\mapsto b_i$. Consequently
\[
 T(A,B,C,D)=(A,B+n_2A,C+n_1A,D+n_1B+n_2C+n_1n_2A),\tag{2}
\]
and $N=\log T$ sends this vector to $(0,n_2A,n_1A,n_1B+n_2C)$.
The sign of geometric monodromy can reverse if marking is transported in
the opposite direction; its kernel and the following conclusions do not.
The rank-two nilpotent weight filtration on each handle is
$W_0=W_1=\Q b$, $W_2=\Q a+\Q b$. In the nilpotent orbit,
$e^{-zN}F^1=\C(a+u b)$, so its graded types are $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$.
They have purity gaps $-1$ and $+1$ in degree one. Their product has a
balanced grade, while the extension $u$ remains in the filtration.

\begin{theorem}[Full-family Hodge equation and its extension obstruction]
The flat rational vector $v$ has type $(1,1)$ for every $z$ in a nonempty
open set if and only if
\[
 A=0,\qquad n_1B+n_2C=0,\qquad D=Bu_1+Cu_2.\tag{3}
\]
There is a nonzero such rational vector if and only if
\[
 \delta_{12}:=n_2u_1-n_1u_2\in\Q.\tag{4}
\]
When (4) holds its full-family space is the rational line spanned by
\[
 w_{12}=n_2a_1b_2-n_1b_1a_2+\delta_{12}b_1b_2.\tag{5}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Substitution in (1) gives coefficients
$An_1n_2$, $A(n_1u_2+n_2u_1)-n_1B-n_2C$,
$Au_1u_2-Bu_1-Cu_2+D$. A polynomial vanishing on an open set has
all three coefficients zero. Positivity of $n_i$ gives (3).
If $B=0$, then $C=D=0$; otherwise (3) says
$C=-n_1B/n_2$, $D=B\delta_{12}/n_2$, giving (4)--(5).
The converse is direct substitution. A condition at a single $z$ can be
weaker, for example at a CM point; the all-family quantifier is essential.
\end{proof}

The nonzero vector $a_1b_2-b_1a_2$ has identical balanced weight and
trivial monodromy for $n_1=n_2=1$ regardless of the constants. But for
$u_1=0,u_2=\sqrt2$ its period defect is $\sqrt2$, whereas for $u_2=0$
it is zero. At $z=i$ in the former case there is in fact NO nonzero
rational cross $(1,1)$ vector: the real and imaginary parts of (1) are
$D-A-C\sqrt2=0$, $A\sqrt2-B-C=0$; irrationality forces all four
coefficients to vanish. This is a test on actual smooth projective
surfaces, not a hypothetical failure of a Hodge structure.

\section{The successful fold supplies an explicit actual graph divisor}
Assume (4), set $a=n_2/n_1>0$ and $b=u_2-a u_1\in\Q$. Choose a positive
integer $L$ with $La,Lb\in\mathbb Z$. The complex-linear map
$s\mapsto La\,s$ takes the lattice of $E_{\tau_1}$ into that of
$E_{\tau_2}$: its two generator images are $La$ and
$L\tau_2-Lb$. It therefore gives an actual algebraic isogeny $f$ of
degree $d=L^2a$. Its graph is an actual divisor, not an assumed Chow
preimage. Let $\eta_1=[\{0\}\times E_2]$ and
$\eta_2=[E_1\times\{0\}]$. Then
\[
 G_f=[\Gamma_f]-d\eta_1-\eta_2,\qquad
 \operatorname{cl}^{can}(G_f)=-\frac L{n_1}w_{12}.\tag{6}
\]
\begin{proof}
Pullback of the marking is $f^*a_2=La\,a_1-Lb\,b_1$,
$f^*b_2=L b_1$. Pair these with $a_1,b_1$ on the graph to obtain
the four cross coefficients $A=0,B=-La,C=L,D=Lb$.
The two factor coefficients are $d,1$, removed in (6). This proves
the canonical Betti formula. Algebraicity of the lattice map is the
checked complex-torus/elliptic-curve comparison, not Hodge conjecture.
\end{proof}
In particular $W=-n_1G_f/L$ is an explicitly constructed rational
divisor with class $w_{12}$ and
\[
 \int w_{12}^2=-2n_1n_2\ne0.\tag{7}
\]
The extension condition can be made causal: put $s_i=u_i/n_i$.
Then $s_i-s_k=(s_i-s_j)+(s_j-s_k)$, so rational compatible edges
compose. Changing $z$ to $z+c$ changes $u_i$ to $u_i-n_i c$ and
does not change $\delta_{ij}$. Integer marking shears only change the
displayed coordinates of the same criterion. Thus topology/monodromy
and Hodge-extension compatibility are distinct parts of the fold process.

\section{An all-arity equation that always gives Hodge tensors}
Now synchronize $m$ handles to the SAME varying $E_\tau$. In
$V^{\otimes m}$ define $E,F,H$ by summing the usual $\mathfrak{sl}_2$
operators over factors. For $\tau=x+iy$ the weight-one Hodge operator is
\[
 J_\tau=\frac1y\begin{pmatrix}-x&1\\-(x^2+y^2)&x\end{pmatrix},
 \quad J_\tau(a+\tau b)=i(a+\tau b).
\]
Its induced tensor operator is a SUM over factors, not the tensor power
of $J_\tau$. Three rational test operators are
\[
 Q_0=E-F,\quad Q_1=\tfrac12E-2F,\quad Q_2=-H+E-2F,
 \tag{8}
\]
coming from $\tau=i,2i,1+i$. They span $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ since
\[
 E=(4Q_0-2Q_1)/3,\quad F=(Q_0-2Q_1)/3,
 \quad H=2(Q_0+Q_1)/3-Q_2.\tag{9}
\]
\begin{theorem}[Three-test law, for every finite arity]
A rational tensor is of charge zero for every $\tau$ if and only if
all three operators (8) kill it. For $m=2r$ this is exactly the
flat $(r,r)$ tensor space throughout the synchronized family. It is
spanned by products of determinant pairings
$[ij]=a_i b_j-b_i a_j$ over perfect matchings. For $m$ odd it is zero.
For $m=2r$ its dimension is
\[
 C_r=\binom{2r}{r}-\binom{2r}{r-1}=\frac1{r+1}\binom{2r}{r}.\tag{10}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The tensor charge has eigenvalue $i(s-t)$ on type $(s,t)$.
Equations (8)--(9) show that the three kernels intersect in the
$\mathfrak{sl}_2$-invariants, equivalently $\mathrm{SL}_2$-invariants.
Every $J_\tau$ is a linear combination of $E,F,H$, proving the iff.
The first fundamental theorem for $\mathrm{SL}_2$ gives determinant
pairings as generators; restricting to multilinear degree forces a
perfect matching. The center $-1$ rules out odd $m$. For even $m$,
complete reducibility of finite-dimensional $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ modules
gives the multiplicity of the trivial representation as the difference
between weight-zero and weight-two multiplicities. In the tensor power
these are the two binomial coefficients in (10).
Noncrossing matchings give $C_r$ independent vectors: orient every arc
from its smaller endpoint, order words lexicographically with $a<b$,
and take the leading word of the product. Its $a/b$ word is Dyck, and
the stack rule recovers the unique noncrossing matching. All other
monomials are later, so distinct leading words give independence.
This proves they are a basis. The empty matching at $r=0$ is the unit.
\end{proof}

For actual cycles use the cross diagonal divisor on factors $i,j$.
When identifying its product with a tensor in factor order, retain the
Koszul sign of the permutation from arc order to factor order. Ordinary
commutative bracket coordinates obey
$[12][34]-[13][24]+[14][23]=0$.
The actual even-degree cross-divisor products instead obey the signed
translation with PLUS between all three terms. A bracket polynomial
identity must not be copied into the Chow ring without this sign dictionary.
These constructed classes give every flat Hodge tensor of this block,
not every Hodge class at an isolated CM fiber and not every arbitrary $X$.
The determinant invariant theorem and elliptic-power phenomena are known;
no global novelty claim is made for them or this synthesis.

\section{Execute the prescribed original-$X$ source test}
For the exact input $(X,p,\alpha)$, choose the actual synchronized
two-handle fold $A=E\times E$, its constructed divisor $W$ with class
$w=a_1b_2-b_1a_2$, and form the target-dependent rational Betti class
$\beta_\alpha=\operatorname{pr}_X^*\alpha\,\operatorname{pr}_A^*w$.
It is nonzero for $\alpha\ne0$, has type $(p+1,p+1)$ on the ACTUAL
smooth projective space $X\times A$, and returns by the explicit equation
\[
 -\tfrac12(\operatorname{pr}_X)_*
 (\beta_\alpha\,\operatorname{pr}_A^*w)=\alpha.\tag{11}
\]
For any actual $Z\in\CH^{p+1}(X\times A)_\Q$ representing this class,
$-\tfrac12(\operatorname{pr}_X)_*(Z\cdot\operatorname{pr}_A^*W)$
is an actual requested cycle on original $X$, by canonical pull--cup--push.
Conversely an actual cycle for $\alpha$ external-products with $W$ to
represent $\beta_\alpha$. Thus this candidate source is algebraic exactly
when $\alpha$ is: tensoring does not lower the unknown codimension to one.
For $r$ independent successful paired folds the return coefficient is
$\prod_j(-2n_{2j-1}n_{2j})$, explicitly invertible in $\Q$.

Using instead the irrational-shift pair above gives a non-Hodge
$\alpha\boxtimes(a_1b_2-b_1a_2)$ for EVERY nonzero original Hodge
$\alpha$, by direct Kunneth decomposition and (1). Both the preserving
and breaking equations requested by the owner therefore occur in actual
folded realizations over every original $X$. A class leaving Hodge type
cannot falsify Hodge algebraicity, whose premise requires that type.
Assuming universal Hodge makes the successful $\beta_\alpha$ algebraic
and (11) returns the assumed conclusion; it is not an independent proof.
