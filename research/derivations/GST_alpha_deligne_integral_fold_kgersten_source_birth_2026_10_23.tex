\section{Exact source and target}

\[
 \alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q(p))\cap F^pH^{2p}(X,\mathbb C).

\[

  \operatorname{cl}^{can}_X(Z_\alpha)=\alpha.}

\section{A new alpha-selected geometric descent object, without a supplied cycle}
Let $C_B^\bullet(X,\mathbb Q(p))$ be a rational Betti cochain model,
$C^\bullet_{\mathrm{dR}}(X,\mathbb C)$ a de Rham model, and
$F^pC^\bullet_{\mathrm{dR}}$ a filtered model computing Hodge $F^p$
(for algebraic projective X, use finite Cech algebraic differential
forms with the stupid Hodge filtration).
Choose comparison quasi-isomorphisms into a common complex cochain model.
The rational Deligne homotopy-pullback complex is
\[
 \boxed{
 C^\bullet_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Q(p))
 =
 \operatorname{Cone}\!\left[
 C_B^\bullet(X,\mathbb Q(p))
 \oplus F^p C^\bullet_{\mathrm{dR}}(X,\mathbb C)
 \xrightarrow{\;\iota_B-\iota_{\mathrm{dR}}\;}
 C^\bullet_{\mathrm{dR}}(X,\mathbb C)
 \right][-1].
 }                                                       \tag{2}
\]
Use the usual mapping-cone signs. In degree 2p a cycle is
represented by a finite triple $(b,\omega,u)$ with
\[
 \boxed{
 db=0,\qquad d\omega=0,\qquad
 du=\iota_{\mathrm{dR}}\omega-\iota_B b .
 }                                                       \tag{3}
\]
All maps and signs here are ordinary genuine cohomology
comparison maps; no user-defined 'Hodge-to-Chow' source is inserted.

\begin{theorem}[Universal actual alpha-selected rational Deligne descent]
For EVERY prescribed rational Hodge class alpha on EVERY original
smooth projective X and EVERY p, there is a finite triple
\[
 \boxed{\mathfrak d_\alpha=[b,\omega,u]
  \in H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Q(p))}
                                                               \tag{4}
\]
of type (3), with the actual rational Betti projection
equal to alpha. The set of possible such lifts is a torsor
under the rational intermediate-Jacobian group
\[
 \boxed{
 J^{2p-1}(X)_\mathbb Q=
 \frac{H^{2p-1}(X,\mathbb C)}
 {F^p H^{2p-1}(X,\mathbb C)+
                       H^{2p-1}(X,\mathbb Q(p))}.
 }                                                       \tag{5}
\]
There is an exact sequence
\[
 \boxed{
 0\longrightarrow J^{2p-1}(X)_\mathbb Q
 \longrightarrow H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Q(p))
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{Hdg}^p(X)_\mathbb Q
 \longrightarrow0 .
 }                                                       \tag{6}
\]
For p=1, the integral version of the same construction is
the usual holomorphic line-bundle exponential sequence:
$H^2_{\mathcal D}(X,\mathbb Z(1))\cong\operatorname{Pic}(X)$,
with projective GAGA providing algebraicity.
For p>=2, (4) is a genuine higher geometric differential
descent object but its existence does not alone construct
an algebraic codimension-p Chow cycle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take a rational Betti cocycle b representing alpha.
Hodge purity gives an algebraic filtered Cech--de Rham
closed cocycle omega representing the SAME class in complex
de Rham cohomology. Their comparison difference is thus
a coboundary in the common model, so solve du=omega-iota_B b.
This constructs the finite triple (3) FROM alpha, without
a cycle or K0 source in the input.
The cone long exact sequence identifies the image as
the intersection of rational Betti classes and F^p,
and the kernel as exactly (5), giving (6).
For p=1 the Deligne complex
$\mathbb Z(1)\to\mathcal O_X$ is quasi-isomorphic
to $\mathcal O_X^\times[-1]$ by the analytic exponential
sequence; H^2 is the holomorphic Picard group.
Projective GAGA identifies that Picard group
with actual algebraic line bundles.
\end{proof}

  &\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{cl}^{can}_X\ }&

 \end{array}                                             \tag{10}

Consequently a genuinely CONSTRUCTED K-source z

\begin{proof}

\section{The dimensional fold is an ACTUAL residue in both geometry and K-theory}
Let a smooth regular algebraic fold center of codimension c

$z_1,\ldots,z_c$.

$\{t,u_1,\ldots,u_{p-1}\}$ has the algebraic
$t$-valuation residue
\[
 \boxed{
 \partial_t\{t,u_1,\ldots,u_{p-1}\}
        =\{\bar u_1,\ldots,\bar u_{p-1}\}
        \in K^M_{p-1}(\kappa(t)).
 }                                                       \tag{11}
\]
Its actual differential logarithm obeys
\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Res}_{t=0}
 (d\log t\wedge d\log u_1\wedge\cdots
             \wedge d\log u_{p-1})
 =d\log\bar u_1\wedge\cdots\wedge
                   d\log\bar u_{p-1}.
 }                                                       \tag{12}
\]
The agreement is exact, in every p.
It is not necessary to call a de Rham 2p-form a K_p-symbol:
the K-symbol differential has exterior degree p, and

\begin{lemma}[Exact source degree bookkeeping prevents a false algebraicity inference]
A generic $K_p$-symbol generates a logarithmic
degree-p differential form, not a global
degree-2p differential form. In the codimension-p
Gersten/Deligne regulator its coniveau/Cech degree

K_p-symbol merely by taking its wedge or by

\begin{proof}
$\bigwedge^p d\log$ has cohomological de Rham

a=p,b=p gives total 2p, while a=0,b=2p

Identifying these two different cochain

\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Any alpha-dependent perfect differential with FIXED bundle terms leaves algebraic K0 unchanged]
Let E^a, for a in a finite interval, be ACTUAL

$d^a_\alpha:E^a\to E^{a+1}$
be ANY alpha-dependent algebraic differentials

Then every resulting actual strictly perfect complex

\[
 \boxed{
 [E^\bullet_\alpha]
    =\sum_a(-1)^a[E^a]\in K_0^{alg}(X),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{ch}_p^{CH}([E^\bullet_\alpha])
    =\sum_a(-1)^a\operatorname{ch}_p^{CH}(E^a).
 }                                                       \tag{14}
\]
In particular no choice of alpha-dependent
differential or changes to only its matrix coefficients
can make its TOTAL algebraic K0 source image vary
with alpha while the bundle terms are held fixed.
This includes derived graph/zero-section folds
where the localized total Chern/K class
is eventually pushed forward to original X.
The theorem does not exclude truly new

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The Grothendieck K0 class of a strictly perfect

Applying the additive algebraic Chern character
in the true rational Chow ring proves (14).
For a derived local zero-locus with fixed ambient

fundamental K-theory class is governed by the

\end{proof}

\section{Direct countercheck: algebraic de Rham need NOT be generated by logs of units}
Even at differential degree one, the tempting
universal primitive-to-algebraic-symbol step is false.

\begin{theorem}[A smooth affine actual counterexample to dlog spanning]
Let E be an ACTUAL smooth projective complex
elliptic curve and P a point.
On the smooth affine curve $U=E\setminus\{P\}$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)^\times=\mathbb C^\times,
 \qquad H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(U,\mathbb C)
                      \cong\mathbb C^2\ne0.
 }                                                       \tag{15}
\]
Thus all logarithmic differentials
$d\log f$ of REGULAR ALGEBRAIC UNITS on U vanish,
while genuinely nonzero global algebraic de Rham
classes exist. A global regular differential
cannot be universally reconstructed from
$d\log$ of regular units; allowing rational
functions with poles introduces actual divisor
boundary data that must be tracked rather than
assumed to cancel.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A nowhere-zero regular function on U can have
zeros and poles only at P on the compact E.
Its principal divisor has degree zero, forcing
the coefficient of P to be zero. Hence it is
a nowhere-zero regular function on the
compact projective curve E and therefore
constant. Its logarithmic differential is zero.
The cohomology long exact sequence for removal
of one point, or the topological first Betti
number of a once-punctured complex torus,
gives dimension two for H^1(U,C).
Algebraic de Rham comparison for the smooth
affine U identifies this group with
$H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(U)$.
\end{proof}

\section{ALL-DIMENSION true countertest: algebraic differential source is larger than regular-symbol logarithms}
The preceding punctured-elliptic obstruction is NOT
a low-dimensional accident. It scales to any
fold dimension, and precisely tests the naive
claim that increasing the dimensional fold can
turn all algebraic differential source data into
wedges of logarithms of GLOBAL regular units.

\begin{theorem}[For every p, a genuine p-dimensional affine world has rich de Rham cohomology but NO nonzero global-unit logarithmic p-source]
Let E be ANY smooth projective complex elliptic
curve, fix a point P, put U=E\setminus\{P\},
and for every integer p>=1 set
\[
 \boxed{U_p=\underbrace{U\times\cdots\times U}_{p\text{ factors}}.}
                                                               \tag{16}
\]
Then $U_p$ is an ACTUAL smooth affine algebraic
complex p-fold and
\[
 \boxed{
 \Gamma(U_p,\mathcal O_{U_p})^\times=\mathbb C^\times,
 \quad
 \dim_{\mathbb C}H_{\mathrm{dR}}^p(U_p)\ge2^p,
 \quad
 \operatorname{span}\{
 d\log f_1\wedge\cdots\wedge d\log f_p:
     f_i\in\mathcal O(U_p)^\times\}=0.
 }                                                     \tag{17}
\]
In fact the Kunneth component
$\bigotimes_{i=1}^p H_{\mathrm{dR}}^1(U)$
has dimension EXACTLY $2^p$
and injects into $H_{\mathrm{dR}}^p(U_p)$.
Thus in every dimension a nontrivial
algebraic de Rham source can be invisible to
the subalgebra generated by differential
logarithms of globally REGULAR units.

This result does NOT prohibit proper
Quillen Gersten residues with rational
functions, since poles on codimension-one
strata and their cancellations carry
additional local algebraic data.
It excludes only the shortcut that tries to
construct every target's algebraic K-cycle
from global regular units without any
Gersten boundaries.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The product of affine smooth varieties is
affine and smooth, proving the first claim.
Let $f\in\Gamma(U_p,\mathcal O)^\times$.
Hold p-1 coordinates fixed at arbitrary
complex points. Its restriction to the
remaining factor U is an ACTUAL regular
algebraic unit, therefore CONSTANT by
the one-factor theorem (15). This holds
independently for each coordinate and for
every choice of the other coordinates.
Hence f is constant on the complex points
of the irreducible reduced variety U_p,
and therefore is a scalar in the coordinate
ring. Its logarithmic differential vanishes,
proving the third identity.
The algebraic de Rham Kunneth theorem
identifies cohomology of the product with
the graded tensor product of the
factor cohomologies. In degree p its
component obtained by taking degree one
from every factor is precisely
$(H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(U))^{\otimes p}$.
The previous elliptic computation gives
$\dim H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(U)=2$, so the
tensor component has dimension $2^p$
and injects into degree p of the
product cohomology, proving (17).
\end{proof}
