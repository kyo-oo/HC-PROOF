\begin{abstract}
We separate GST's abstract sheet algebra, genuine Chow-cycle source
algebra, and canonical Hodge target. We derive precise constraints on
the missing universal geometric realization, and a full-rank
native-word theorem that would close the gap if its independently
geometric hypotheses were established. No universal proof of the
Hodge conjecture is asserted.
\end{abstract}
\section{Exact target and the unproved geometry}
For arbitrary smooth projective complex $X$ and $p\ge0$ define
\[
 V=\Hdg^p(X)_{\Q}
 =H^{2p}(X,\Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X),\qquad
 C=\CH^p(X)_{\Q},\qquad
 A=\im(c:C\to V),\quad D=V/A.
\]
Here $c$ must be the canonical Betti cycle-class map, not
the freely supplied linear map of the Stage2G interface.
The exact conjecture is $D=0$ for all $X,p$.
The repository's geometric-spine module explicitly admits
a zero-cycle-class countermodel, hence its current
naturality axioms alone do not characterize the canonical map.

\section{The universal native-image invariant}
GST source-specific programs have a true native/Hodge
commuting square
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
 C&\stackrel{U}{\longrightarrow}&C\\
 \downarrow c&&\downarrow c\\
 V&\stackrel{T}{\longrightarrow}&V
\end{array}
\qquad cU=Tc .
\]
\begin{theorem}[Native conservation]
Every actual geometric GST operator $T$ with an actual native
partner $U$ preserves the full algebraic Hodge image $A$.
Any rational sum or finite composition of such operators does so.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $a=c(z)$ then $Ta=Tc(z)=c(Uz)\in A$.
The compatibility equation survives finite sums and products.
\end{proof}
Thus a new native source may enlarge a finite known subspace
$A_0\subset A$, but no composition of algebraic operations
on already algebraic sources can escape the full $A$.
This is the first constraint on a universal cosmic birth.

\section{The genuine correspondence irreducibility route}
Let $\mathscr G\subseteq\mathrm{End}_{\Q}(V)$ be generated
only by genuine Hodge-compatible closed correspondences whose
Chow and Betti actions commute with $c$.
\begin{theorem}[Conditional irreducibility]
If $A\ne0$ and the representation of $\mathscr G$ on $V$
has no proper nonzero invariant $\Q$-subspace, then $A=V$.
If $0\ne A\ne V$, this actual geometric action is reducible.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The previous theorem proves $A$ invariant. Nonzero
irreducibility forces $A=V$. The converse follows immediately.
\end{proof}
The source module
\texttt{GSTClassicalHodgeActualGeometricIrreducibility.lean}
proves precisely such a conditional implication.
It does not establish actual geometric irreducibility on
arbitrary $X$. Abstract GST sheet matrix-unit irreducibility
cannot be substituted for actual geometric irreducibility.

\section{The complete native-source controllability criterion}
Fix genuine algebraic $z\in C$ with $v=c(z)\ne0$.
Choose actual geometric words $W_1,\ldots,W_m$,
where $m=\dim_{\Q}V$, each with a canonical commuting
native/Hodge square. Choose a rational basis $e_1,\ldots,e_m$
for $V$ and form the concrete source-period matrix
\[
 M_{ji}=e_j^*(W_{i,\Hdg}v).
\]
\begin{theorem}[Explicit GST source-word construction]
If the genuinely computed rational matrix satisfies
$\det(M)\ne0$, then for every $\alpha\in V$ the actual
original-$X$ rational cycle
\[
 Z_\alpha=\sum_{i=1}^m
 (M^{-1}[\alpha])_i W_{i,\mathrm{native}}(z)
 \in\CH^p(X)_{\Q}
\]
has canonical class $c(Z_\alpha)=\alpha$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Naturality gives
$c(W_{i,\mathrm{native}}z)=W_{i,\Hdg}v$.
The coordinate column of this class is the $i$th
column of $M$. The displayed linear combination
therefore has coordinate vector
$M M^{-1}[\alpha]=[\alpha]$.
\end{proof}
The conditional theorem is fully derived. The
unconditional theorem would require independent construction
of the words with $\det M\ne0$ on every original $X$;
providing the determinant as an abstract hypothesis
does not solve Hodge.

\begin{theorem}[Dual orbit test]
Let $T_1,\ldots,T_s$ be actual native-compatible
operators on finite-dimensional $V$. Their word orbit
$U=\mathrm{span}_{\Q}\{T_{i_k}\cdots T_{i_1}v:k\ge0\}$
equals $V$ if and only if no nonzero rational linear
functional $\ell\in V^*$ vanishes on every word value.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The vanishing functionals form $U^\perp$.
Finite-dimensional duality gives $U=V$ exactly if
$U^\perp=0$.
\end{proof}
A functional annihilating the orbit shows only that
the \emph{chosen} geometric source family is incomplete,
not that a Hodge class is nonalgebraic.

\section{GST primitive Gram projector test}
Let $P$ project onto a finite algebraic source frame
$A_0$, with $P^2=P$ verified by the native rational
Gram dual. For a genuine geometric operator $T$,
\[
 (I-P)TP\ne0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 A_0+T(A_0)\supsetneq A_0 .
\]
The new direction is algebraic, but may have been in
the full $A$ already.
For the entire $A$ and a nondegenerate rational
polarization restriction, every genuine $T$ satisfies
$(I-P_A)TP_A=0$. This follows from native-image stability.

The Wolfram exact symbolic experiment used
\[
 P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
 T=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}.
\]
It returned $P^2=P$ and
\[
 [T,P]=\begin{pmatrix}0&-b\\c&0\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 (I-P)TP=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\c&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus $c\ne0$ detects growth beyond the selected
frame; the calculation does not construct the
correspondence $T$ on arbitrary $X$.

\section{Exceptional cosmology cannot bypass the defect}
Embed $X\subset\mathbb P^N$ with normal codimension
$c\ge2$; take $Y=\mathrm{Bl}_X\mathbb P^N$,
exceptional divisor $j:E\to Y$, $q:E\to X$,
and $\xi=c_1(\mathcal O_E(1))$.
The actual Chow return is
\[
 R_p(W)=-q_*(\xi^{c-2}j^*W)
 :\CH^{p+1}(Y)_{\Q}\to\CH^p(X)_{\Q}.
\]
Using $j^*j_*q^*Z=-\xi q^*Z$
and $q_*(\xi^{c-1}q^*Z)=Z$,
we derive $R_p(j_*q^*Z)=Z$.
However, automatically
$\im(cR_p)\subseteq A$. Native source
surjectivity onto existing Chow classes is
not the unknown Hodge surjectivity.

For smooth projective $B$ and smooth center
$Z\subset B$ of codimension $c$,
the canonical Chow/Betti blowup splittings give
\[
 \coker(c^p_{\mathrm{Bl}_ZB})
 \cong \coker(c_B^p)
 \oplus\bigoplus_{i=1}^{c-1}\coker(c_Z^{p-i}).
\]
Thus blowups of Hodge-complete bases along
Hodge-complete centers cannot create missing
Hodge classes. They also cannot independently
remove a pre-existing defect. A counterexample
center, if it exists, survives upstairs.

\section{The missing theorem, in explicit geometric form}
\begin{problem}[Universal original-$X$ realized sources]
For every smooth projective complex $X$ and $p$
construct a finite packet of actual original-$X$
Chow seed cycles and finitely many bona fide
GST source-to-target operators built from its
algebraic incidence, perfect-$K_0$, correspondence
and cosmological mechanisms, with:
\begin{enumerate}
\item actual canonical Betti--Chow commuting squares;
\item all source objects independently geometrically
constructed without assuming target algebraicity;
\item a full-rank rational matrix of resulting
canonical Hodge classes, covering every Hodge direction.
\end{enumerate}
\end{problem}
The exact source-to-target program compiler,
actual-correspondence irreducibility route,
and native Gram dual become a global proof
only if the existence requirements above
are proved, not simply inserted as fields.
\section{Status and evidence classification}
Native conservation, conditional irreducibility,
matrix reconstruction, and orbit duality are
proved above. The $2\times2$ Wolfram test is exact.
The blowup formulas are valid for genuine
smooth projective blowups with proper canonical
cycle comparisons; their full scheme-level Lean
instantiation remains separate.
The missing universal geometric realization
and canonical Stage2G Betti identification
are \emph{not proved}. This manuscript is a
rigorous mathematical derivation and an explicit
new research contract, not a completed proof
or counterexample to the rational Hodge conjecture.
