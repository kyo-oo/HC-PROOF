\begin{abstract}
We execute a new source-existence route downstream of the compact causal
Hopf bifurcation: use the \emph{genuine smooth quasi-projective algebraic
precursor} $U=E\setminus0_X$ of the compact Hopf fold, rather than
postulating a Chow theory on the nonalgebraic Hopf quotient.
For arbitrary smooth projective complex original $X$, arbitrary algebraic
bundle $E$ and every codimension $p$, the canonical algebraic Chow
localization sequence and rational Betti Gysin sequence combine into an
exact Euler-defect sequence for the true rational Hodge algebraicity
obstruction. We construct an explicit original-$X$ correction from any
genuinely algebraic prescribed class upstairs when the lower-codimension
defect vanishes; in rank $r=p$ the correction is purely scalar and always
algebraic. We prove that the first possible nonzero rational Hodge defect
cannot be hidden by a punctured causal fold. This is a completed
all-original-$X$ structural theorem and a direct geometric source test,
not a surjectivity assumption or a universal Hodge-conjecture proof.
\end{abstract}

\section{Geometry and exact source/target contract}
Let $X$ be a connected smooth projective complex variety of dimension
$d$, $p\ge0$, and let $\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)$.
Let $E$ be any algebraic vector bundle of rank $r\ge1$. Write
\[
 V=\mathrm{Tot}(E),\quad i:X\hookrightarrow V\text{ zero section},
 \quad U=V\setminus i(X),\quad
 \rho:U\longrightarrow X,\quad
 e=c_r(E)\in CH^r(X)_{\mathbb Q}.
\]
For the compact Hopf realization, require $r\ge2$ and choose
$0<q<1$; then $\mathcal H_E=U/q^{\mathbb Z}$
is compact complex and smoothly $S(E)\times S^1$.
The open $U$ itself is a real smooth quasi-projective algebraic
variety with a canonical Chow group and a canonical rational
Betti cycle-class map. Its analytic deformation retract is
$S(E)$. All statements below use actual algebraic sources on
$X$ or $U$, not Chow classes on the nonalgebraic Hopf manifold.

Define the rational Hodge space, actual algebraic image, and defect
\[
 H_X^p=H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X),\qquad
 A_X^p=\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}),
 \qquad D_X^p=H_X^p/A_X^p,
\]
with groups in negative codimension equal to zero. Define the
\emph{inherited} open-world defect
\[
 D^p_{\mathrm{inh}}(U)
 =
 \frac{\rho^*H_X^p}
      {\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_U(CH^p(U)_{\mathbb Q})}
 \subseteq
 \frac{\mathrm{im}\rho^*:H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\to
                 H^{2p}(U,\mathbb Q)}
      {\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_U(CH^p(U)_{\mathbb Q})}.
 \tag{1}
\]
The quotient is well-defined because every algebraic codimension-$p$
cycle on $U$ lifts from $X$ by Chow localization and because
every actual class on $X$ is Hodge of type $(p,p)$.
No pure Hodge structure is asserted on the entire open $U$.

\section{The full arbitrary-$X$ algebraic-topological paired localization}
\begin{theorem}[GST paired causal localization]
For every $X,E,p$ as above there are exact sequences
\[
 CH^{p-r}(X)_{\mathbb Q}
   \xrightarrow{\, e\cdot\,}
 CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}
   \xrightarrow{\rho^*}
 CH^p(U)_{\mathbb Q}\longrightarrow0,                    \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 H^{2p-2r}(X,\mathbb Q)
   \xrightarrow{\,\mathrm{cl}(e)\smile\,}
 H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)
   \xrightarrow{\rho^*}
 H^{2p}(U,\mathbb Q).                                     \tag{3}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \boxed{\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_U(CH^p(U)_{\mathbb Q})
       =\rho^*A_X^p\subseteq\rho^*H_X^p.}                 \tag{4}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The closed-open localization sequence for the zero section
$i:X\hookrightarrow V$ and $j:U\hookrightarrow V$ is
$CH^{p-r}(X)\xrightarrow{i_*}CH^p(V)
 \xrightarrow{j^*}CH^p(U)\to0$.
By homotopy invariance, $\pi_V^*:CH^p(X)\simeq CH^p(V)$,
and the self-intersection identity $i^*i_*\beta=e\cdot\beta$
identifies $i_*$ with multiplication by $e$, giving (2).
Topologically, $U$ retracts onto the oriented unit-sphere
bundle $S(E)$; its real oriented Euler class equals the Betti
top Chern class $\mathrm{cl}(e)$. Its sphere-bundle Gysin
long exact sequence supplies (3). Canonical cycle-class naturality
and the surjectivity of $CH^p(X)\to CH^p(U)$ prove (4).
\end{proof}

\section{Exact algebraicity-defect causal sequence}
\begin{theorem}[Universal GST Euler-defect sequence]\label{thm:defect}
The actual top Chern class $e$ induces a well-defined rational map
$D_X^{p-r}\xrightarrow{e\cdot}D_X^p$, and the sequence
\[
 \boxed{
 D_X^{p-r}\xrightarrow{e\cdot}D_X^p
 \xrightarrow{\rho^*}D_{\mathrm{inh}}^p(U)
 \longrightarrow0
 }                                                          \tag{5}
\]
is exact for every connected smooth projective complex $X$,
every algebraic rank-$r$ bundle $E$ and every integer $p\ge0$.
Equivalently,
\[
 \boxed{D_{\mathrm{inh}}^p(U)\cong
              D_X^p/(c_r(E)\cdot D_X^{p-r}).}                \tag{6}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
As $e$ is algebraic of Hodge type $(r,r)$, multiplication by
$\mathrm{cl}(e)$ sends $H_X^{p-r}$ into $H_X^p$ and
$A_X^{p-r}$ into $A_X^p$, inducing the left map on defect quotients.
Equation (4) makes the second map surjective by definition.

For exactness in the middle, suppose a rational Hodge class
$\alpha\in H_X^p$ maps into the algebraic image upstairs.
Equation (4) furnishes an actual $Z\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}$
such that
$\rho^*\alpha=\rho^*\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(Z)$.
By the true Betti Gysin kernel (3) there is some rational
$\gamma\in H^{2p-2r}(X,\mathbb Q)$ with
\[
 \alpha-\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(Z)
         =\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(e)\smile\gamma.       \tag{7}
\]
The left side is a rational Hodge class of type $(p,p)$.
The map $\mathrm{cl}(e)\smile-$ is a morphism of polarizable
pure rational Hodge structures after the Tate twist $(-r)$.
Its image is a rational Hodge substructure, and the category of
polarizable pure rational Hodge structures is semisimple.
Thus every rational Hodge class in this image has a
\emph{rational Hodge preimage}
$\gamma_{\mathrm{Hdg}}\in H_X^{p-r}$ with the same image
as $\gamma$. Equation (7) now gives
$[\alpha]=e\cdot[\gamma_{\mathrm{Hdg}}]$ in $D_X^p$.
The converse is immediate by Gysin exactness and (2).
This proves (5) and (6) without positing a cycle for $\alpha$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[First-defect survival under every punctured fold]
Fix an original $X$. Suppose $p$ is the least codimension
with $D_X^p\ne0$. Then for every algebraic bundle $E$ of
rank $r\ge1$,
\[
  \boxed{D_X^p\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }D_{\mathrm{inh}}^p(U).}
                                                               \tag{8}
\]
More generally the same isomorphism holds whenever
$D_X^{p-r}=0$, including all $r>p$ and $r=p$ since
$D_X^0=0$ on connected $X$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For $r>0$, the index $p-r<p$. By minimality the left
defect in (5) vanishes, and surjectivity becomes an
isomorphism. When $r=p$, $D_X^0=0$ because the rational
degree-zero fundamental cycle represents the degree-zero
rational Hodge class. The convention for negative indices
handles $r>p$.
\end{proof}

\section{Constructive original-$X$ cycle return from an open source}
\begin{theorem}[Exact defect-zero algebraic correction]\label{thm:return}
Assume $D_X^{p-r}=0$. Suppose a bona fide algebraic
$\Gamma_U\in CH^p(U)_{\mathbb Q}$ has been constructed
\emph{independently} and satisfies
\[
 \mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_U(\Gamma_U)=\rho^*\alpha.
                                                               \tag{9}
\]
Then one can construct an actual
$Z_\alpha\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}$
with $\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(Z_\alpha)=\alpha$
by the following explicit correction.
Choose any algebraic Chow lift $W\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}$
of $\Gamma_U$ using (2).
Find a rational Hodge class $\gamma_{\mathrm{Hdg}}
 \in H_X^{p-r}$ with
\[
 \alpha-\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(W)
  =\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(e)\smile\gamma_{\mathrm{Hdg}}.
                                                               \tag{10}
\]
Since $D_X^{p-r}=0$, choose any actual
$B\in CH^{p-r}(X)_{\mathbb Q}$ with
$\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(B)=\gamma_{\mathrm{Hdg}}$.
Then
\[
 \boxed{Z_\alpha=W+e\cdot B,\qquad
          \mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{can}}_X(Z_\alpha)=\alpha.}   \tag{11}
\]
In the particularly important rank-matched case $r=p\ge1$,
the correction is purely rational:
$Z_\alpha=W+\lambda\,c_p(E)$, with $\lambda\in\mathbb Q$.
If $c_p(E)\ne0$ in Betti cohomology, any rational dual functional
$\ell$ with $\ell(\mathrm{cl}(c_p(E)))\ne0$ yields
\[
 \lambda=\frac{\ell(\alpha-\mathrm{cl}(W))}
                {\ell(\mathrm{cl}(c_p(E)))}.                \tag{12}
\]
If the top Chern class vanishes, $W$ already represents $\alpha$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Surjectivity in Chow localization (2) produces $W$.
Betti Gysin exactness and the Hodge-preimage argument in the proof
of Theorem \ref{thm:defect} give (10). Since the assumed lower
defect is zero, $\gamma_{\mathrm{Hdg}}$ is a canonical Betti cycle
class of some algebraic $B$. The Chow product $eB$ is an
actual original-$X$ codimension-$p$ cycle and naturality gives
(11). For $r=p$, $H_X^0=\mathbb Q$, so $\gamma_{\mathrm{Hdg}}$
is the scalar multiple $\lambda$ of the unit fundamental class.
The rational functional formula (12) determines it exactly.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Anti-circularity acceptance check.}
The existence of $\Gamma_U$ in (9) is the \emph{source-birth}
input to be constructed; it is NOT produced by the Gysin sequence.
When $D_X^{p-r}=0$, Theorem \ref{thm:return} proves
that a universally available construction of such $\Gamma_U$
would itself establish universal algebraicity on $X$ in
codimension $p$. The exact correction is new usable geometry,
but it cannot supply an absent algebraic $\Gamma_U$ by
topological injectivity or the absence of pure weight-one
Hodge structure on the compact Hopf quotient.

\section{Direct attempted source factory and decisive result}
Attempt: start from arbitrary rational Hodge $\alpha$, use the
actual algebraic punctured causal bundle $U$, then invoke
the existence of a pure Hodge class or a rational topological
$K$-theory representative to obtain a Chow cycle upstairs.
The latter implication fails at its required \emph{algebraic
source type}. Homotopy invariance/localization explicitly
shows that \emph{every} algebraic cycle on $U$ comes from a
Chow class on $X$; the only ambiguity is an Euler multiple
from lower codimension. Thus puncturing and subsequent
$S^1$-topology of the Hopf quotient supply no unconditional
new algebraic cycle in the first missing Hodge codimension.
This is a theorem about this punctured-bundle construction,
not an impossibility result for other GST geometric births.

The original all-$X$, all-$p$ target remains the independent
algebraic birth of $\Gamma_U$ in (9), or of an equivalent
non-split geometric source on the original $X$. Genuine
scheme-level incidence, Hilbert, moduli or specialization
geometry would have to furnish its canonical Betti class
without selecting an original algebraic preimage.
