\begin{abstract}
The existing GSTK3TranscendentalProjector.lean verifies rational-linear
projector algebra. We now derive the strengthened ACTUAL rational Chow
correspondence on the true smooth projective fourfold S times S:
idempotence holds in the Chow ring under correspondence convolution,
not merely on abstract Betti linear maps. Its canonical Betti class is
primitive and has strictly positive middle self-intersection
22-rho(S). For any fixed-point-free automorphism of S, the invariant
cycle descends to a smooth projective quotient fourfold
without assuming an abstract product decomposition with exact norm (22-rho)/|G|. Rational algebraic K0 perfect
sources with pure middle Chern character and the same Euler norm
are constructed from actual coherent structure sheaves and finite
Adams projection. These results introduce independent geometric
source inputs, without postulating universal Hodge-to-Chow
surjectivity; the arbitrary-variety original Hodge conjecture is not
asserted settled.
\end{abstract}

\section{Actual geometry and Chow correspondence convention}
Let S be an arbitrary complex projective K3 surface, so
H^1(S,Q)=H^3(S,Q)=0, b_2(S)=22,
and int_S c_2(T_S)=24.
Let rho be the rational Neron-Severi rank (1<=rho<=20).
Choose algebraic rational divisor classes D_1,...,D_rho
forming a Q-basis of NS(S)_Q, and set
G_ij=int_S D_i D_j.
By the Hodge index theorem G is a nonsingular symmetric
rational matrix. Write g^{ij}=(G^{-1})_ij.
Choose an actual rational zero-cycle o in CH^2(S)_Q
of degree one (a closed point suffices).

On X=S times S define ACTUAL codimension-two cycles
\[
 p_0=o\times S,\quad p_4=S\times o,\quad
 p_{\rm NS}=\sum_{i,j=1}^{\rho}g^{ij}D_i\times D_j,
 \quad
 \boxed{p_T=[\Delta_S]-p_0-p_4-p_{\rm NS}.}        \tag{1.1}
\]
All these are cycles in CH^2(X)_Q, and Delta_S is
the actual scheme-theoretic diagonal, not an abstract
identity operator.

For alpha,beta in CH^2(S times S)_Q define their
correspondence convolution by
\[
 \alpha\circ\beta
  =(p_{13})_*(p_{12}^*\beta\cdot p_{23}^*\alpha)
                  \in CH^2(S\times S)_Q.          \tag{1.2}
\]
The genuine diagonal is the two-sided convolution identity.
Convolution and ordinary intersection product
are distinct operations: the subscript in
int_X p_T^2 below ALWAYS refers to ordinary Chow
intersection, whereas p_T circ p_T is composition.

\section{Genuine scheme-level transcendental projector}
\begin{theorem}[Chow-idempotent transcendental K3 projector]
The actual algebraic correspondences p_0,p_4,p_NS
are pairwise convolution-orthogonal idempotents:
\[
 p_a\circ p_a=p_a,\qquad
 p_a\circ p_b=0\quad(a\ne b,\ a,b\in\{0,4,\mathrm{NS}\}).
                                                               \tag{2.1}
\]
Consequently their genuine Chow-complement p_T is an
ACTUAL CHOW IDEMPOTENT,
\[
 \boxed{p_T\circ p_T=p_T\quad\mathrm{in}\ CH^2(S\times S)_Q.}
                                                               \tag{2.2}
\]
Its canonical Betti correspondence action is identity
on T(S)_Q=NS(S)_Q^\perp\subset H^2(S,Q)
and zero on H^0,H^4, and NS(S)_Q.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The product o has degree one; in S cubed, the
pushforward formula for composing o times S with
itself integrates the middle factor o, giving p_0.
Likewise p_4 squared is p_4. All mixed
compositions p_0 with p_4 vanish by codimension:
one either integrates the fundamental middle surface
with insufficient codimension or multiplies two
codimension-two zero-cycles on that surface.
For p_NS, the triple-product computation uses
\[
 (p_{13})_*\bigl((D_i\times D_j)_{12}\,
                    (D_k\times D_l)_{23}\bigr)
  =\left(\int_S D_jD_k\right)(D_i\times D_l)
  =G_{jk}(D_i\times D_l).
\]
Therefore the coefficient matrix for
p_NS squared is G^{-1} G G^{-1}=G^{-1}.
Mixed products of p_NS with p_0 or p_4
contain a product of a divisor with a point
in CH^3(S)=0 or a pushforward of a divisor
without enough middle degree; all vanish.
Thus P=p_0+p_4+p_NS satisfies P circ P=P.
Since Delta is the convolution identity,
(Delta-P) circ (Delta-P)=Delta-P, proving (2.2).

Under the genuine Betti cycle map, p_0 and
p_4 are respectively the H^0/H^4 projectors.
The p_NS correspondence on H^2 has the explicit
geometric action
\[
 v\longmapsto\sum_{i,j}g^{ij}
                   \left(\int_S vD_i\right)D_j,
\]
which is the intersection-orthogonal projection
onto NS(S)_Q. H^1 and H^3 vanish for K3,
so the stated decomposition is complete.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact positive primitive middle fourfold source}
\begin{theorem}[Geometric primitive Euler source of rank 22-rho]
Let h be the first Chern class of ANY ample algebraic line
bundle on S and H=pr_1^*h+pr_2^*h an ample class on X.
The ACTUAL algebraic codimension-two Chow cycle p_T
has a primitive canonical Hodge class
\[
 H\smile\cl(p_T)=0\quad\mathrm{in}\ H^6(X,Q)
\]
and its genuine signed middle Hodge intersection is
\[
 \boxed{\int_X\cl(p_T)^2=22-\rho(S)>0.}            \tag{3.1}
\]
This produces an actual independent primitive
algebraic source for every projective K3 surface S,
including transcendental cohomological directions
not manufactured by the NS divisor projector.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The Betti class of p_T lies in
T(S)_Q\otimes T(S)_Q\subset
H^2(S,Q)\otimes H^2(S,Q)
and encodes the inverse rational cup pairing on T(S).
Because h belongs to NS(S), any t in T(S) satisfies
h cup t=0 in H^4(S,Q). Multiplying p_T by
pr_1^*h or pr_2^*h therefore gives zero in Betti
cohomology. The cycle itself is algebraic and of
Hodge type (2,2), so primitiveness is genuine.

For the norm, the diagonal self-intersection
on S times S is int_S c_2(T_S)=24.
The explicit cycle intersection degrees are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \int_X \Delta_S p_0=\int_X\Delta_S p_4&=1,\\
 \int_X p_0p_4&=1,\quad
 \int_Xp_0^2=\int_Xp_4^2=0,\\
 \int_X\Delta_S p_{\rm NS}
  =\int_Xp_{\rm NS}^2&=\rho,\\
 \int_Xp_0p_{\rm NS}
  =\int_Xp_4p_{\rm NS}&=0 .
\end{aligned}
\]
For p_NS squared, contraction gives
sum g^{ij}g^{kl}G_{ik}G_{jl}=rho.
Expanding (Delta-p_0-p_4-p_NS)^2 yields
24+2+rho-2(2+rho)=22-rho.
Projective K3 surfaces have 1<=rho<=20,
so this integer is strictly positive.
\end{proof}

\section{Actual pure-perfect-complex source}
Let X=S times S; its dimension is four.
For any actual rational codimension-two Chow cycle Z,
choose a finite formal presentation Z=sum c_j[Z_j]
by integral codimension-two subvarieties.
Since X is regular, each coherent O_{Z_j} is
a perfect K0 object. The leading-cycle formula gives
ch_0(O_{Z_j})=ch_1(O_{Z_j})=0 and
ch_2(O_{Z_j})=[Z_j] in rational Chow.
Let
\[
 \Pi_2=\prod_{j\in\{0,1,3,4\}}
                  \frac{\psi^2-2^j}{4-2^j}.
                                                               \tag{4.1}
\]
This rational algebraic Adams polynomial is the
eigenprojector onto Chern-character codimension two.
The actual perfect K-class

 \boxed{C_T=\Pi_2\left(\sum_jc_j[O_{Z_j}]\right)
                  \in K_0^{alg}(X)_Q}              \tag{4.2}

satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\ch(C_T)=\cl_{\CH}(p_T)
       \text{ purely in codimension two},\quad
 \chi_X(C_T,C_T)=22-\rho.}                          \tag{4.3}
\]
The class is independent of the chosen finite

\section{Descent to a NONPRODUCT smooth projective fourfold}
Suppose a finite group G acts freely on S by algebraic
automorphisms. It acts diagonally and freely on
Y=S times S. Let
\[
 q:Y\longrightarrow X_G=Y/G
\]
be the finite etale quotient, of degree n=|G|.
Average any actual degree-one rational zero-cycle
over G to choose o invariant. The K3 diagonal is
invariant under g times g, and the full NS inverse
tensor p_NS is basis-independent and invariant
under simultaneous pullback by each automorphism.
Hence p_T is G-invariant as an ACTUAL rational Chow class.

\begin{theorem}[Actual nonsplit quotient source birth]
On the genuinely smooth projective finite-quotient
fourfold X_G define
\[
 \boxed{\zeta_G=\frac1n q_*p_T
                         \in CH^2(X_G)_Q.}         \tag{5.1}
\]
Then q^*zeta_G=p_T, and the canonical Hodge class
cl(zeta_G) is primitive for an actual rational
ample class H_G descended from a G-invariant ample
class on Y. Its actual intersection norm is
\[
 \boxed{\int_{X_G}\cl(\zeta_G)^2
                     =\frac{22-\rho(S)}{n}>0.}    \tag{5.2}
\]
The rational Adams-purified structure-sheaf source
C_G=Pi_2(sum c_j[O_{Z_j}]) for a finite rational
subvariety presentation of zeta_G is genuinely
algebraic on the original quotient X_G, with
pure ch(C_G)=zeta_G and
\[
 \boxed{\chi_{X_G}(C_G,C_G)
                =\frac{22-\rho(S)}n.}             \tag{5.3}
\]
No algebraic representative of a target Hodge class
on X_G is assumed to produce this source.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The finite etale Galois projection satisfies
q^*q_*p_T=\sum_{g\in G}g^*p_T=n p_T
in rational Chow, because p_T is invariant.
Consequently q^*zeta_G=p_T.
Choose any algebraic ample class h on S,
average h over G to obtain an invariant rational
ample class, and use its sum along the two factors
on Y. Some integral multiple linearizes and
descends along q; denote the resulting rational
ample class by H_G. Its pullback is this invariant
product polarization. Since p_T is primitive,
q^*(H_G cup cl(zeta_G))=0; injectivity of
finite-etale rational cohomology pullback gives
H_G cup cl(zeta_G)=0.

For the intersection formula, the projection
formula and degree n give
\[
 \int_{X_G}(\cl(\zeta_G))^2
 =\frac1n\int_Y q^*(\cl(\zeta_G))^2
 =\frac1n\int_Y\cl(p_T)^2
 =\frac{22-\rho(S)}n .
\]
The pure Adams source on X_G follows from the
same scheme-level coherent K0 argument as (4.2);
HRR yields the rational Euler self-index.
\end{proof}
