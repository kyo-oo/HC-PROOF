\begin{abstract}
The existing GSTK3TranscendentalProjector.lean verifies rational-linear
projector algebra. We now derive the strengthened ACTUAL rational Chow
correspondence on the true smooth projective fourfold S times S:
idempotence holds in the Chow ring under correspondence convolution,
not merely on abstract Betti linear maps. Its canonical Betti class is
primitive and has strictly positive middle self-intersection
22-rho(S). For any fixed-point-free automorphism of S, the invariant
cycle descends to a smooth projective quotient fourfold
without assuming an abstract product decomposition with exact norm (22-rho)/|G|. Rational algebraic K0 perfect
sources with pure middle Chern character and the same Euler norm
are constructed from actual coherent structure sheaves and finite
Adams projection. These results introduce independent geometric
source inputs, without postulating universal Hodge-to-Chow
surjectivity; the arbitrary-variety original Hodge conjecture is not
asserted settled.
\end{abstract}

\section{Actual geometry and Chow correspondence convention}

H^1(S,Q)=H^3(S,Q)=0, b_2(S)=22,
and int_S c_2(T_S)=24.

Choose algebraic rational divisor classes D_1,...,D_rho

G_ij=int_S D_i D_j.

rational matrix. Write g^{ij}=(G^{-1})_ij.
Choose an actual rational zero-cycle o in CH^2(S)_Q
of degree one (a closed point suffices).

\[
 p_0=o\times S,\quad p_4=S\times o,\quad

 \boxed{p_T=[\Delta_S]-p_0-p_4-p_{\rm NS}.}        \tag{1.1}

the actual scheme-theoretic diagonal, not an abstract

For alpha,beta in CH^2(S times S)_Q define their

\section{Genuine scheme-level transcendental projector}
\begin{theorem}[Chow-idempotent transcendental K3 projector]
The actual algebraic correspondences p_0,p_4,p_NS
are pairwise convolution-orthogonal idempotents:
\[
 p_a\circ p_a=p_a,\qquad
 p_a\circ p_b=0\quad(a\ne b,\ a,b\in\{0,4,\mathrm{NS}\}).
                                                               \tag{2.1}
\]
Consequently their genuine Chow-complement p_T is an
ACTUAL CHOW IDEMPOTENT,
\[
 \boxed{p_T\circ p_T=p_T\quad\mathrm{in}\ CH^2(S\times S)_Q.}
                                                               \tag{2.2}
\]
Its canonical Betti correspondence action is identity
on T(S)_Q=NS(S)_Q^\perp\subset H^2(S,Q)
and zero on H^0,H^4, and NS(S)_Q.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The product o has degree one; in S cubed, the
pushforward formula for composing o times S with
itself integrates the middle factor o, giving p_0.
Likewise p_4 squared is p_4. All mixed
compositions p_0 with p_4 vanish by codimension:
one either integrates the fundamental middle surface
with insufficient codimension or multiplies two
codimension-two zero-cycles on that surface.
For p_NS, the triple-product computation uses
\[
 (p_{13})_*\bigl((D_i\times D_j)_{12}\,
                    (D_k\times D_l)_{23}\bigr)
  =\left(\int_S D_jD_k\right)(D_i\times D_l)
  =G_{jk}(D_i\times D_l).
\]
Therefore the coefficient matrix for
p_NS squared is G^{-1} G G^{-1}=G^{-1}.
Mixed products of p_NS with p_0 or p_4
contain a product of a divisor with a point
in CH^3(S)=0 or a pushforward of a divisor
without enough middle degree; all vanish.
Thus P=p_0+p_4+p_NS satisfies P circ P=P.
Since Delta is the convolution identity,
(Delta-P) circ (Delta-P)=Delta-P, proving (2.2).

Under the genuine Betti cycle map, p_0 and
p_4 are respectively the H^0/H^4 projectors.
The p_NS correspondence on H^2 has the explicit
geometric action
\[
 v\longmapsto\sum_{i,j}g^{ij}
                   \left(\int_S vD_i\right)D_j,
\]
which is the intersection-orthogonal projection
onto NS(S)_Q. H^1 and H^3 vanish for K3,
so the stated decomposition is complete.
\end{proof}

\end{proof}

divisors and zero/fundamental classes.
The pure-middle algebraic perfect
K-source is the Adams-projected coherent
cycle presentation of this Chow expression.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An actual Fourier--Mukai kernel between K3
surfaces induces a rational Hodge
isometry on their transcendental degree-two
Hodge structures under its algebraic
Mukai cycle. Projecting both legs
with the actual p_T Chow idempotents
eliminates the factor/tate and NS
components; the resulting actual
codimension-two Chow correspondence
acts on T(S_1) exactly as u.

The rational Kunneth Hodge middle
sector on S_1 times S_2 has
algebraic factor terms, products
NS(S_1)\otimes NS(S_2),
and Hodge tensors
T(S_1)\otimes T(S_2).
Polarization identifies the latter
with Hom_HS(T(S_1),T(S_2)).
The given Hodge isometry u identifies
this Hom space with End_HS(T(S_1)).
The scalar hypothesis makes it a
one-dimensional rational vector space
spanned by u, hence the algebraic
source Gamma_12 spans that sector.

Let g_1,g_2 be the genuine rational
cup-intersection forms on T(S_1),T(S_2)
and M the rational matrix of u in
arbitrary rational bases.
The isometry says M^T g_2 M=g_1.
The middle tensor representing u has
coefficient matrix C=g_1^{-1}M^T.
Its genuine cup-intersection norm is
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(C^Tg_1Cg_2)
 =\operatorname{Tr}(M g_1^{-1}M^T g_2)
 =\operatorname{Tr}(I_{T(S_1)})
 =22-\rho(S_1).
\]
The class is primitive with respect
to any product polarization, because
both legs are in the transcendental
orthogonal complements of divisors.
Thus (8.4) is a genuine positive
primitive source period. Orthogonality
to all divisor/point tensors yields
(8.5). The canonical rational Chow
cycle of beta is obtained by adding
the explicitly constructed algebraic

Other fourfold Hodge degrees are
algebraic by Lefschetz (1,1),

\end{proof}

\section{An actual all-dimensional GST source theorem on K3 powers}
The preceding Chow projector is not restricted
to two factors. Its literal pullbacks to pairs
of factors give genuine algebraic realizations
of every contraction tensor in arbitrary
powers of a K3 transcendental Hodge structure.

Let S be a genuine complex projective K3
surface with transcendental rational
Hodge structure T=T(S)_Q of rank
\(
 r=22-\rho(S)\ge2.
\)
For this theorem impose the \emph{geometric
Hodge-group hypothesis}
\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Hg}(T)=
    \operatorname{SO}(T,\langle\ ,\ \rangle),}
                                                               \tag{9.1}
\]
where the group is the actual special
orthogonal Hodge group of the polarised
rational K3 transcendental structure.
This is an explicit Hodge-theoretic
property of S; it is NOT a claim that
every K3 has this group. No Hodge
target is assumed to be algebraic.

For an arbitrary \(n\ge1\), set
\(X_n=S^n\), a genuine smooth projective
complex variety of dimension \(2n\).
Let \(\pr_{ab}:X_n\to S\times S\)
be the algebraic projection to distinct
factors a,b. Its pulled-back actual
Chow class
\[
        P_{ab}=\pr_{ab}^*p_T
                   \in CH^2(X_n)_Q              \tag{9.2}
\]
represents the inverse rational
intersection tensor in the T-components
of factors a and b.

For a subset \(I\subset\{1,\ldots,n\}\)
of cardinality \(m=2k\), and a perfect
matching \(\mathcal M\) of I into k
unordered pairs, define the
scheme-theoretically ALGEBRAIC cycle
\[
 \boxed{
 Z_{I,\mathcal M}
      =\prod_{\{a,b\}\in\mathcal M}
             P_{ab}\in CH^{m}(X_n)_Q .}           \tag{9.3}
\]
Its canonical Betti class has a T-factor
in each slot in I, and H^0 factors
elsewhere. Tensor it with actual
divisor classes, point classes, and
fundamental classes in the remaining
factors to obtain every corresponding
Kunneth degree.

\begin{theorem}[All-Hodge-class algebraicity on original K3 powers below transcendental rank]
For EVERY genuine projective K3 S
satisfying (9.1), and EVERY integer
\[
 \boxed{1\le n<r=\dim_Q T(S),}                     \tag{9.4}
\]
EVERY rational (p,p) Hodge class on
the ACTUAL original variety \(X_n=S^n\)
in EVERY codimension \(0\le p\le2n\)
is the canonical Betti class of an
EXPLICIT rational algebraic Chow cycle
assembled from actual divisor, point,
diagonal and Chow-projector cycles
(9.2)--(9.3).

If a finite group G acts freely on S
by actual algebraic automorphisms,
the same result holds for EVERY
smooth projective quotient
\[
 \boxed{
 X_{n,G}=S^n/G\quad\text{for}\quad n<r,}
                                                               \tag{9.5}
\]
using the actual diagonal G-action and
the explicit finite degree-normalized
Chow descent of every Hodge target.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The algebraic Kunneth decomposition of
the individual K3 cohomology has only
degrees zero, two and four; H^1=H^3=0.
The rational degree-two part splits
as NS(S)_Q plus T(S)_Q.
H^0,H^4,NS are all rational Tate
Hodge structures with ACTUAL algebraic
representatives from the point, the
fundamental class and divisors.
For each subset I of m factors
assigned a T contribution, the rational
Hodge tensors are precisely
\(
 (T^{\otimes m})^{\operatorname{Hg}(T)}
\),
with the appropriate Tate-degree
bookkeeping. All other tensor factors
are already algebraically generated.

Now invoke the rational first
fundamental invariant theorem for the
special orthogonal group in characteristic
zero: in tensor power m<r of the
standard r-dimensional quadratic
representation, invariant tensors
vanish for m odd, and for m=2k
are spanned by all perfect pairings
of the m tensor slots. The only
additional SO-specific epsilon/determinant
invariants require at least r slots,
which are excluded by the strict
inequality n<r.

Every such perfect-pairing tensor
is the genuine Betti image of (9.3):
each actual p_T is the inverse cup
tensor on two K3 transcendental
factors, and products across disjoint
factors realize the required
tensor product of contractions.
Thus every rational Kunneth Hodge
component is a rational linear
combination of actual point,
divisor, fundamental and pairwise
p_T Chow classes. Finite addition
of those concrete cycles produces
a rational Chow representative
of any prescribed Hodge target,
with EXACT canonical Betti class.
This establishes the theorem on
the original projective \(S^n\).

For free finite G, the quotient map
\(q:S^n\to X_{n,G}\) is finite etale,
with actual degree |G|. For any
rational Hodge alpha on X_{n,G},
apply the just-proved actual cycle
construction to \(q^*\alpha\), and
push the resulting Chow cycle forward:
\[
 \boxed{
 Z_\alpha^{X_{n,G}}
 =\frac1{|G|}q_*Z_{q^*\alpha}^{S^n}
             \in CH^p(X_{n,G})_Q,\qquad
 \cl(Z_\alpha^{X_{n,G}})=\alpha.}
                                                               \tag{9.6}
\]
The last equality is the genuine
finite-etale cohomological degree
formula. No unconstructed Chow
cycle on the quotient is passed
as input.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Exact Gram-based source coefficients.}

 \boxed{
 B_{\mathcal M,\mathcal N}
