\begin{theorem}[Every geometric minimal-fold source, exact span]
For every X and every p in 0,...,d, the entire family of
all finite-projective incidence-fold base sources satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}
 \{h_f^p:f:X\to\mathbb P^d\text{ finite surjective}\}
 =D^p_{\rm div}(X).}                                     \tag{1}
\]
This is a theorem in CHOW, hence also after the actual
canonical rational Betti cycle-class map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every h_f is the first Chern class of an ample
line bundle, giving the direct inclusion.
Conversely take arbitrary algebraic rational
divisor classes lambda_1,...,lambda_p on X.
Choose an ample rational class A and a small positive
rational epsilon such that
$A_S=A+\varepsilon\sum_{i\in S}\lambda_i$
is ample for every subset S of {1,...,p}.
Since pth powers form a homogeneous polynomial
of degree p in the genuine commutative Chow ring,
multivariate inclusion-exclusion gives the EXACT equality
\[
 \boxed{p!\varepsilon^p\prod_{i=1}^{p}\lambda_i
  =\sum_{S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,p\}}
       (-1)^{p-|S|}A_S^p.}                                \tag{2}
\]
Every monomial missing some lambda_i cancels;
the unique survivor is the product with each
lambda_i exactly once and coefficient p!.
For each ample rational A_S there is a positive
integer m_S so m_S A_S is very ample.
Choose a generic (d+1)-dimensional
basepoint-free linear subsystem; it yields a
finite surjective f_S:X to P^d with
$f_S^*O(1)=O_X(m_S A_S)$.
The map is finite since a positive-dimensional
fiber would contradict ampleness of its
pullback of O(1). Therefore
$A_S^p=m_S^{-p}h_{f_S}^p$.
Substitute into (2), obtaining the other inclusion.
In codimension p=0 the class is the unit, also
obtained from any finite morphism.
\end{proof}

\end{corollary}
