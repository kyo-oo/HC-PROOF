\section{The reduction laws (LA1)}

\begin{lemma}[the cocycle, the transport, the norm]
Let $\gamma\in\Sp(8,\Z)$ (the symplectic law
$\gamma^t\Theta\gamma=\Theta$) and let $(\gamma\times\gamma)\circ
\iota=\iota\circ\gamma$ (the intertwining). Then:
(i) $c_{m,\alpha}$ is an inhomogeneous $2$-cocycle of the abelian arrow
lattice;
(ii) the transport identity holds:
$\gamma\cdot c_{m,\alpha}=c_{m,\gamma\alpha}$;
(iii) the deck norm vanishes at the two-cell level:
$N(c_{m,\alpha})=\theta\cdot\iota(m\,N_\gamma\alpha)=0$ on every
$\gamma^2=-1$ content module.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
(i) Alternating forms on a free abelian group with trivial coefficient action
are $2$-cocycles; certified on fixed instances.
(ii) $\gamma\cdot c(v,w)=(\gamma\times\gamma)\,c(\gamma^{-1}v,\gamma^{-1}w)
=\theta(\gamma^{-1}v,\gamma^{-1}w)\,\iota(m\gamma\alpha)
=\theta(v,w)\,\iota(m\gamma\alpha)$, by the intertwining and the symplectic
law. (iii) Summing the four transports and using (ii) inductively gives
$\theta\cdot\iota(m\,N_\gamma\alpha)$; on the all-odd sector
$N_\gamma=0$ (the R1 norm degeneration), so the orbit sum is zero. All three
steps are computed in the certificate, word-exact and matrix-exact. The
norm law is the \emph{absorption of the $(A,-A)$ witness at the two-cell
level}: the anti-invariant pair dies in the deck orbit sum with no
symmetrization step and no invariant-class hypothesis. BOUNDARY (on the
face): the norm law $(1+\gamma)(1+\gamma^2)=0$ holds for every
$\iota$-shaped content and does not by itself distinguish $(A,-A)$ from
$(x,+x)$; the $\iota$-specificity is the strip's traceless condition
(computed in the certificate); the pair-level residue is discharged only
through the remaining two-cell reduction.
\end{proof}
