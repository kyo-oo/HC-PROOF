\[

\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}

$H^2(\Cfour;A)=\ker(\gamma-1)/\im N=0$. (ii) is the exact computation:

at $H^2(\Gbul;A)$: the kernel of the mixed-cell map is the image of
$H^2(\Cfour;A^\GammaL)=0$.
\end{proof}
