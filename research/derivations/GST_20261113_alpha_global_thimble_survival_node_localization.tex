\section{Alpha-dependent projective primitive carry}
\begin{theorem}[Uniform projective carry and exact original-X return]
Let h be the rational Betti class of an ample divisor on X. Suppose alpha is nonzero, rational Hodge, of degree 2p with 2p<=d, and primitive, so h^(d-2p+1) alpha=0. Put r=d-2p, Y=X times P^r, xi=c1(O(1)), and pi:Y->X. Define
\[
\boxed{\Phi_r(\alpha)=\sum_{j=0}^{r}(-1)^j\pi^*(h^j\alpha)\xi^{r-j}.}
\]
This is a rational Hodge class in middle cohomology H^(2(p+r))(Y,Q), because dim_C Y=d+r=2(p+r). It obeys
\[
\boxed{(h+\xi)\Phi_r(\alpha)=0,\qquad \pi_*\Phi_r(\alpha)=\alpha\ne0.}
\]
Thus Phi is a nonzero primitive middle Hodge class relative to the ample h+xi. Whenever an actual rational Chow class Z on Y has canonical Betti class Phi, the ACTUAL proper Chow pushforward pi_*Z is a rational algebraic cycle on the SAME original X with canonical class alpha.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All factors h and xi are rational algebraic Hodge (1,1) classes. The ring identity, for every r>=0,
\[
(a+b)\sum_{j=0}^{r}(-1)^j a^j b^{r-j}
=b^{r+1}+(-1)^r a^{r+1}
\]
applied to a=h, b=xi and multiplied by alpha gives zero: xi^(r+1)=0 on P^r and h^(r+1)alpha=0 by original primitivity. Integration pi_* over P^r kills all powers xi^q for q<r and sends xi^r to one, so it returns exactly alpha. Proper pushforward commutes with canonical Betti cycle class.
\end{proof}
\begin{proposition}[Reduction of every original target to this construction]
For arbitrary original X,p,alpha, rational Hard Lefschetz and primitive Lefschetz decomposition express alpha as a finite rational combination of algebraic powers of h times primitive Hodge classes of even degree at most d. Apply the theorem separately to each primitive component. If actual algebraic middle-cycle sources are subsequently constructed on these auxiliary Y, their genuine Chow pushforwards and multiplication by powers of h construct the original Z_alpha. This is an unconditional reduction but does NOT construct those auxiliary Chow sources by definition.
\end{proposition}

\section{Real three-world geometric death, survival and carry}
Let Y now be ANY smooth projective variety of even complex dimension n>=2, and beta a nonzero primitive rational middle Hodge class. Such Y,beta arise from the preceding theorem on every original X. Choose a smooth sufficiently ample hyperplane D in Y, denote by i:D->Y the inclusion and j:U=Y\setminus D->Y the complementary smooth affine open.

\begin{theorem}[Exact divisor death and affine survival]
\[
\boxed{i^*\beta=0\ \text{in }H^n(D,Q),\qquad j^*\beta\ne0\ \text{in }H^n(U,Q).}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Weak Lefschetz gives H^(n-2)(Y) isomorphic to H^(n-2)(D). Hard Lefschetz on D gives multiplication by h_D an isomorphism H^(n-2)(D) to H^n(D). The composite i_* h_D i^* on H^(n-2)(Y) equals h_Y^2, an isomorphism by Hard Lefschetz on Y. Hence i_*:H^n(D)->H^(n+2)(Y) is injective. Since i_*i^* beta=h_Y beta=0 by primitivity, i^*beta=0. The localization Gysin sequence gives ker(j^*:H^n(Y)->H^n(U)) = i_* H^(n-2)(D)(-1)=h_Y H^(n-2)(Y). Rational primitive Lefschetz decomposition has zero intersection with this image, so j^*beta !=0.
\end{proof}

Choose a generic affine hyperplane F in U. The affine Lefschetz theorem of Hamm--Le supplies a relative CW cell decomposition attaching real n-dimensional thimbles tau_1,...,tau_m to F. Andreotti--Frankel gives real CW dimension <= n-1 for F.

\begin{theorem}[Nonzero global N-thimble carry from EVERY original primitive Hodge alpha]
There exist a rational relation k=(k_i) among the actual thimble boundaries and a rational relative lift of the retained beta, such that
\[
\boxed{\sum_i k_i\partial\tau_i=0\ \text{in }H_{n-1}(F,Q),\qquad
\sum_i k_i\langle\widetilde\beta,\tau_i\rangle\ne0.}
\]
The carrier is an actual nonzero homology class in H_n(U,Q); no artificial Hodge-to-Chow map or algebraic cycle is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
H_n(F,Q)=H^n(F,Q)=0. Long exact sequences for (U,F) yield H_n(U,Q)=ker[partial:H_n(U,F,Q)->H_(n-1)(F,Q)] and a surjection H^n(U,F,Q)->H^n(U,Q). Take a relative lift tilde-beta of nonzero j^*beta. The rational universal coefficient theorem provides b in H_n(U,Q) with nonzero pairing. Expand b in the relative thimble basis as sum k_i tau_i. It is a boundary relation because it comes from absolute homology, and evaluation against tilde-beta is nonzero. All data depend on the prescribed original alpha through beta=Phi_r(alpha).
\end{proof}

\section{All-arity simultaneous singular-world carry law}
Let (V,Q) be a finite-dimensional rational symplectic space and delta_1,...,delta_m pairwise Q-orthogonal possibly dependent nonzero vanishing cycles, as in a simultaneously nodal odd-dimensional fiber. Define D:Q^m->V by D e_i=delta_i and B:V->Q^m by (Bv)_i=Q(v,delta_i). Picard--Lefschetz nilpotents N_i(v)=Q(v,delta_i)delta_i are pairwise commuting and square zero.

\begin{theorem}[Exact simultaneous-node survival obstruction]
\[
\boxed{\Q^m/\im B\cong(\kerrel D)^*,\qquad
[c]\mapsto \bigl(k\mapsto\sum_i k_i c_i\bigr).}
\]
A packet c admits one simultaneous potential v satisfying Bv=c iff sum_i k_i c_i=0 for ALL node relations Dk=0. Individual local exactness does not imply simultaneous exactness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Via the nondegenerate pairing Q, B is the transpose of D, up to sign. Thus im B=(ker D) perpendicular, giving the quotient and the criterion. Orthogonality of the delta_i gives N_i N_j=0. Nothing in this linear theorem constructs a degeneration of an arbitrary original X.
\end{proof}
