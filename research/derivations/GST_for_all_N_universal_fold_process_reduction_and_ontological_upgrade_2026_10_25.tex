\subsection{Types}

Fix the category of smooth complex projective varieties.  The typed data:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathrm{WORLD}(d)$: a smooth projective $X$ with $\dim X=d$ and a
fixed ample class $H$;
\item $\mathrm{CLASS}(p)$: rational even cohomology classes with graded
components $\alpha=\alpha_p$ concentrated in degree $2p$ (the input sector
below is: $\alpha\in \Hdg^{p,p}(X)\cap H^{2p}(X,\Q)$);
\item $\mathrm{CHAR}(p,r,m)$: the \emph{pure character}
$c=r+m\alpha$ with $r\ge1$, $m\in\Z_{>0}$: the component in every grade
$\ne 0,p$ vanishes;
\item $\mathrm{CARRIER}$: an exact topological vector bundle realizing a pure
character (cycle-1 Theorem A: it exists for every input at scaled
characters, with rank floor $r_0=\max(p+1,d+1)$);
\item $\mathrm{PAIR}(p,r,m)$: a pair $E_\pm$ of exact bundles of equal rank
$r$ with $\ch(E_+)-\ch(E_-)=m\alpha$ and all off-grade-$p$ components zero;
\item $\mathrm{CYCLE}(p)$: rational algebraic cycles in $\CH^p(X)_\Q$.
\end{itemize}
A pair or carrier is \emph{integrable} when the bundle(s) carry holomorphic
structures.  Integrability is the classical Koszul--Malgrange condition
$(\bar\partial_E)^2=0$; it is a property of the bundle plus its operator,
and is unchanged by re-presentation.

\subsection{The upgrade: two totality meta-laws}
\label{sec:totality}

The owner's directive is that the calculus be a \emph{process that applies to

\begin{meta-law}[Input-uniform totality of each law]
\label{law:MU1}
Each law $\mathrm L\in\{\mathrm{L1},\dots,\mathrm{L5}\}$ is \emph{total on
its input type}: for \emph{every} input of the prescribed type the output is
defined, and the class-return identity of the law holds, with no side
condition beyond the type discipline.  Moreover each law is
\emph{grade-typed}: it either preserves the graded support of pure characters
(the grade-preserving laws: pullback, tensor by a line bundle, dual, direct
sum --- the transport edges of Theorem~\ref{thm:T}) or it is declared
grade-mixing (Gysin/pushforward with Todd correction), in which case its
output type is cycle-level, never carrier-level.
\end{meta-law}

\begin{proof}[Proof (backings)]
For L1: the projective-bundle/Schur formalism is defined for every smooth
base and every exact bundle; the Segre return identity is the projective
bundle theorem plus the Whitney sum, valid for all inputs of the type.  For
L2: Thom--Porteous applies to every general section system of the prescribed
ranks with expected codimension; the determinantal formula is then an
identity, valid for all inputs of the type (Fulton, \emph{Intersection
Theory}, \S14.4).  For L3: the Rees algebra of any bundle map exists for all
inputs of the type, and the central-fiber return follows from the deformation
to the normal cone, valid for all inputs.  For L4: this is Theorem C of
\cite{GST-gr25}, whose hypotheses (equal-rank integrable pure pair at scale
$m\ne0$, generating twists, general sections, expected codimension) are
exactly the type discipline of $\mathrm{PAIR}$ plus Serre generation
(Serre: on projective $X$ every holomorphic $E$ becomes globally generated
after a positive twist --- again input-uniform).  For L5: the signed
component return at a discriminant split is the Porteous residue
decomposition, valid for all inputs of the type.  The grade-typing is the
splitting principle: pullback, line-tensor, dual and direct sum act on
Chern roots by $e^{x_i}\mapsto e^{f^*x_i}$, $e^{x_i+\ell}$, $e^{-x_i}$,
$\sum e^{x_i}+e^{y_j}$, all of which preserve the vanishing of off-grade
components of a \emph{pure} character when applied to pure inputs of the same
grade $p$; Gysin maps compose with $\mathrm{td}$-correction and mix grades by
GRR, which is why they are declared cycle-typed.
\end{proof}

\begin{meta-law}[Typed composition]
\label{law:MU2}
Total typed laws compose: if $\mathrm L$ (total on type $A$, output type
$B$) is followed by $\mathrm L'$ (total on $B$), the composite is total on
$A$.  The composite fold-process is therefore defined for every input of the
initial type, and its class-return is the composite of the laws'
class-returns.  This is a closure property of the typed process algebra, not
a new geometric input.
\end{meta-law}

\begin{proof}
Totality on types means: the output of the first law lies in the input type
of the second for every input; the class-return identities compose by
substitution.  No input-specific condition appears at any stage, so the
composite is again input-uniform.
\end{proof}

\section{The universal pipeline (Theorem U)}
\label{sec:pipeline}

\begin{definition}[The typed bit; the scale set]
\label{def:bit}
For an input $(X,p,\alpha)$ define the \emph{integrable-pair scale set}
\[
\Pi_I(X,p,\alpha)\;:=\;\bigl\{\,m\in\Z\;:\;
\exists\ \text{an integrable pure pair } E_\pm \text{ at scale } m
\,\bigr\},
\]
where an integrable pure pair at scale $m$ means: $E_\pm$ holomorphic, equal
rank $r\ge1$, $\ch(E_+)-\ch(E_-)=m\alpha$ with all off-grade-$p$ components
zero.  The \emph{typed bit} of the input is the statement
$\Pi_I(X,p,\alpha)\ne\{0\}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Pipeline universality: for all $N$, the process applies]
\label{thm:U}
Let $X$ be smooth complex projective of dimension $d$, $1\le p\le d$, and
$\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\Q)$ rational (Hodge or not).  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}] \textup{(APPLIES, unconditional)} The fold pipeline
$\mathrm P(X,p,\alpha)$ is defined, with no input-specific side condition:
\begin{itemize}
\item[\textup{S1}] \emph{Topological pair}: there is an exact topological
pure pair at a nonzero scale: by Theorem A \cite{GST-forcing-site} the exact
carrier $E_\alpha$ with $\ch(E_\alpha)=r_0+m\alpha$ pure exists at rank
$r_0=\max(p+1,d+1)$ and every scale $m$ divisible by the integrality scale
$m_0$; pair it with the trivial side $\Oo^{r_0}$: the difference is $m\alpha$
pure.
\item[\textup{S2}] \emph{Arithmetic}: by Theorem B2 \cite{GST-forcing-site}
every additive lattice congruence (RR/Schwarzenberger type) on an integrable
realization is cleared along the scales $m\equiv0 \pmod N$ for an explicit
$N=N(X,p,\alpha)$; the scale sector of the pipeline is an arithmetic
progression, not an input-specific condition.
\item[\textup{S3}] \emph{Generation}: for any holomorphic realization,
Serre generation supplies twists $L=\Oo(kH)$ making both sides globally
generated; general sections $\sigma_\pm\colon\Oo^{s}\to E_\pm\tensor L$,
$s=r-p+1$, have expected-codimension degeneracy centers
$D_\pm=D_{s-1}(\sigma_\pm)$.
\item[\textup{S4}] \emph{Fold}: by Theorem C \cite{GST-gr25}, in
$\CH^p(X)_\Q$,
\[
[D_+]-[D_-]=(-1)^{p-1}(p-1)!\,m\,\alpha
\qquad\text{CORRECTION-FREE, for every } p;
\]
\item[\textup{S5}] \emph{Landing}: $W:=\dfrac{(-1)^{p-1}}{(p-1)!\,m}
\bigl([D_+]-[D_-]\bigr)\in\CH^p(X)_\Q$ satisfies $\cl(W)=\alpha$.
\end{itemize}
\item[\textup{(ii)}] \textup{(WORKS iff the bit)} the landing of
$\mathrm P(X,p,\alpha)$ holds iff the typed bit of
Definition~\ref{def:bit} fires: if $E_\pm$ is any integrable pure pair at
scale $m\ne0$, then S3--S5 apply to it and produce $W$ with $\cl(W)=\alpha$;
conversely, if the pipeline's landing is produced from integrable inputs, the
pair realizing S1 integrably is a witness of the bit.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i) S1 is Theorem A verbatim (the carrier is exact: pure character, actual
bundle; the trivial side is exact and pure).  S2 is Theorem B2(i) verbatim.
S3 is Serre's theorem plus genericity of sections (the standard Bertini-type
genericity inside the type; no condition on $\alpha$).  S4 is Theorem C
verbatim, whose hypotheses are exactly S3's setup plus purity and integrality
of the pair.  S5 is the definition of $W$ together with the cohomological
reading of S4, since $\cl$ is $\Q$-linear and
$\cl([D_\pm])$ are integral classes; the scalar
$(-1)^{p-1}/((p-1)!\,m)$ is rational because $m\ne0$.  Every stage is
$\forall$-quantified over its type with no input-specific branch.
(ii) Given a witness pair, S3--S5 produce the cycle --- this direction uses
nothing but the type discipline.  Conversely, if a landing $W$ with
$\cl(W)=\alpha$ is produced by the pipeline, the pipeline's S1 input was an
integrable pure pair at some nonzero scale, which is by definition a witness
of the bit.  (Note the exactness discipline: the converse clause is a reading of the
process, not a claim that algebraicity alone implies the bit; see
Theorem~\ref{thm:N}(ii).)
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Theorem~\ref{thm:U} is deliberately unconditional about existence of stages
and deliberately single-pointed about what can fail: only integrability of
the S1 pair.  This is the precise sense in which ``the process applies for
all inputs'': the process is one total typed pipeline; the only
input-indexed question left in the world is the bit of
Definition~\ref{def:bit}.
\end{remark}

\section{Transport-constancy of the bit (Theorem T)}
\label{sec:transport}

\begin{theorem}[Transport laws]
\label{thm:T}
Integrable pure pairs transport as follows, for all inputs, with no
side conditions beyond the types:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{T1}] \emph{(Pullback)} Let $f\colon X\to Y$ be holomorphic
between smooth projective varieties and let $(Y,p,\beta)$ admit an
integrable pure pair at scale $m$.  Then $(X,p,f^*\beta)$ admits an
integrable pure pair at the same scale $m$: the pair
$(f^*E_+,\ f^*E_-)$ is holomorphic (pullback of holomorphic is
holomorphic), has equal ranks, and
\[
\ch(f^*E_+)-\ch(f^*E_-)=f^*(\ch(E_+)-\ch(E_-))=m\,f^*\beta ,
\]
which is pure: $f^*$ is graded, annihilates the off-grade zeros, and
preserves rational $(p,p)$-type when $\beta$ is of $(p,p)$ type.
\item[\textup{T2}] \emph{(Pair-sum)} If $(X,p,\alpha)$ admits integrable
pure pairs at scales $m$ and $m'$, it admits one at scale $m+m'$: take
$E_+\oplus E'_+$ versus $E_-\oplus E'_-$; ranks add equally; characters add;
off-grade components add zero-to-zero; the scale adds.
\item[\textup{T3}] \emph{(Swap; dual-swap)} Swapping the pair transports
scale $m$ to $-m$.  The dual-swap $(E_-^\vee,\ E_+^\vee)$ is an integrable
pure pair at scale $(-1)^{p+1}m$, because
$\ch(E^\vee)_k=(-1)^k\ch_k(E)$ grade-wise.
\item[\textup{T4}] \emph{(Single-to-pair)} If $E$ is an integrable single
exact carrier with pure character $r+m\alpha$, then $(E,\Oo^{r})$ is an
integrable pure pair at scale $m$.
\item[\textup{T5}] \emph{(Scaling)} If a witness exists at scale $m$, a
witness exists at scale $km$ for every integer $k\ge0$: iterate T2 (and T3
for negatives).
\end{enumerate}
Consequently the scale set $\Pi_I$ is a transport-invariant of the
fold-type: along a pullback edge $f\colon X\to Y$,
\[
\Pi_I(Y,p,\beta)\subseteq\Pi_I(X,p,f^*\beta).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
T1: holomorphic pullback is standard (the pullback operator on
$\bar\partial$-structures composes; the pulled-back structure is integrable
because $(\bar\partial_{f^*E})^2=f^*(\bar\partial_E)^2=0$).  The
naturality $\ch(f^*E)=f^*\ch(E)$ is the functoriality of Chern classes.
Grading: $f^*\colon H^{2k}(Y,\Q)\to H^{2k}(X,\Q)$, so off-grade components
stay zero and grade $p$ maps to grade $p$; rational $(p,p)$ type pulls back
to rational $(p,p)$ type for holomorphic $f$ (Hodge structures pull back).
T2: direct sums of holomorphic bundles are holomorphic; $\ch$ is additive;
the off-grade components of the summed difference are sums of zeros; the
grade-$p$ component is $(m+m')\alpha$.  T3: swap is bookkeeping; for the
dual-swap, the dual of a holomorphic bundle is holomorphic, and the graded
dual character is $\ch_k(E^\vee)=(-1)^k\ch_k(E)$ (Chern roots negate), so
the new difference has grade-$k$ component
$(-1)^k(\ch_k(E_-)-\ch_k(E_+))=(-1)^k(-m\alpha\,\delta_{k,p})
=(-1)^{p+1}m\alpha\,\delta_{k,p}$.
T4: $\ch(\Oo^r)=r$, so the difference is exactly the pure character of $E$;
both sides holomorphic.  T5: induction on $k$ with T2; T3 covers $k<0$.
The transport-invariance clause is T1 restated.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What is NOT claimed]
\label{rem:notwist}
Tensoring by a holomorphic line bundle is \emph{not} a scale-transport on
pairs: the twisted difference acquires higher-grade terms
$m\alpha\,c_1(L)^{j-p}$ (twist-mixing), so the twisted pair is generally not
pure.  This is exactly the twist-cancellation phenomenon isolated in the
cycle-1 lattice completion (Lemma T there): the pair \emph{form} is what
cancels twists, and the pair form is the one retained here.  No false
transport law is used anywhere below.
\end{remark}

\section{The fold-scale group and the invariant \texorpdfstring{$m^\star$}{m*}}
\label{sec:group}

\begin{theorem}[Fold-scale group; principality]
\label{thm:M}
For every input $(X,p,\alpha)$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}] $\Pi_I(X,p,\alpha)$ is an additive subgroup of $\Z$:
$0$ lies in it (the trivial pair $(E,E)$ for any holomorphic $E$, e.g.\
$E=\Oo$); it is closed under $+$ (T2) and under negation (T3).
\item[\textup{(ii)}] Hence either $\Pi_I=\{0\}$, or
$\Pi_I=m^\star\Z$ for a unique
\[
m^\star=m^\star(X,p,\alpha)\;:=\;\min\bigl(\Pi_I\cap\Z_{>0}\bigr)\in\Z_{\ge0},
\]
the least positive scale; in the first case set $m^\star:=0$.  Every
occurring scale is a multiple of $m^\star$, and $m^\star$ itself occurs.
\item[\textup{(iii)}] $m^\star$ is the complete per-type content of the bit:
the bit fires iff $m^\star>0$; and $m^\star$ is monotone along transport
edges: for $f\colon X\to Y$,
\[
m^\star(X,p,f^*\beta)\ \big|\ m^\star(Y,p,\beta)
\quad\text{whenever } m^\star(Y,p,\beta)>0 .
\]
\item[\textup{(iv)}] $\Pi_I(X,p,\alpha)\subseteq\Sigma(X,p,\alpha)$, the
committed character scale set of cycle 2 (strike 2): an integrable pure pair
is in particular a pure character difference, so the integrable scales are a
subgroup of the character scales; on the committed $\Gr(2,5)$ world the
certified completed pure pair gives
$m^\star(\Gr(2,5),2,\alpha)\in\{1,2,3,4,5,6,\dots\}$ with
$m^\star\mid 360$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i) The trivial pair has zero difference, is integrable, and has equal ranks;
closure laws are T2 and T3.  (ii) A nontrivial additive subgroup of $\Z$ is
cyclic: take the least positive element $m^\star$ (well-ordering; the set is
nonempty by T5 from any nonzero member); for any $s\in\Pi_I$, write
$s=qm^\star+r$ with $0\le r<m^\star$; then
$r=s-qm^\star\in\Pi_I$ by the group laws (T5 for the multiple, T2/T3 for the
difference); minimality forces $r=0$.  Uniqueness of $m^\star$ is immediate.
(iii) The bit is $\Pi_I\ne\{0\}$, i.e.\ $m^\star>0$ by (ii).  Monotonicity:
T1 gives $\Pi_I(Y)\subseteq\Pi_I(X)$, so the least positive element of the
right side divides every element of the left; with (ii),
$m^\star(X)\mid s$ for all $s\in\Pi_I(Y)$, in particular
$s=m^\star(Y)$.  (iv) An integrable pure pair is a pair of actual holomorphic
bundles whose character difference is the pure class $m\alpha$; membership in
$\Sigma$ is exactly the existence of a pure character difference at scale
$m$ (the cycle-2 strike-2 scale-monoid), so the inclusion is a relabeling of
the definition; the $\Gr(2,5)$ clause is the committed kernel-verified pure
pair certificate \cite{GST-gr25} at scale $360$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
$m^\star$ is a GST-native world quantity: it is defined purely by the owner's
fold data (which pure characters of the world are carried by actual
holomorphic fold-witnesses), it is transportable along the type-graph edges,
and it is arithmetic (a nonnegative integer, principal by a group law).  The
entire input-indexed content of the conjecture is henceforth carried by one
such integer per input.
\end{remark}

\section{The for-all-\texorpdfstring{$N$}{N} theorem (Theorem N)}
\label{sec:forallN}

\begin{theorem}[For all inputs: witness $\Rightarrow$ algebraic;
the collapse]
\label{thm:N}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}] \emph{(For every input, correction-free)} If
$m^\star(X,p,\alpha)>0$ then $\alpha$ is algebraic: take the witness pair at
scale $m^\star$, run S3--S5 of Theorem~\ref{thm:U}, and obtain
$W\in\CH^p(X)_\Q$ with
\[
\cl(W)=\frac{(-1)^{p-1}}{(p-1)!\,m^\star}
\bigl((-1)^{p-1}(p-1)!\,m^\star\,\alpha\bigr)=\alpha .
\]
\item[\textup{(ii)}] \emph{(Contrapositive reading)} If $\alpha$ is not
algebraic then $m^\star(X,p,\alpha)=0$.  So the bit is a
\emph{certificate}: a positive $m^\star$ is a stronger, explicit,
transportable proof of algebraicity than algebraicity itself.  The converse
implication (algebraicity $\Rightarrow m^\star>0$) is \emph{not} claimed
anywhere in this campaign: producing a holomorphic pure pair from an abstract
algebraic cycle is a descent-type question (the same higher-grade junk
phenomenon as the cycle-1 $[\Oo_Z]$ corrections), left as a labeled open
leaf~E15.
\item[\textup{(iii)}] \emph{(THE COLLAPSE)} Define the single universal law
\[
\boxed{\;\FS:\quad
\text{for every smooth projective } X,\ 1\le p\le d,\
\alpha\in\Hdg^{p,p}(X)\cap H^{2p}(X,\Q):\ \
m^\star(X,p,\alpha)>0 .\;}
\]
Then the Hodge conjecture (every rational $(p,p)$ Hodge class on every
smooth projective complex $X$ is a rational linear combination of classes of
algebraic subvarieties) follows from $\FS$ by (i), applied simultaneously to
all inputs.  Moreover, inside this reduction no per-input case structure
survives: by Theorem~\ref{thm:M}(iii) the law $\FS$ is a statement about the
fold-type graph (one integer per type, monotone along edges), and by
Theorem~\ref{thm:U}(i) the process realizing the implication is one total
typed pipeline with no input-specific branch.  The input space (all inputs,
``all $N$'') has been compressed into one universal law.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i) is Theorem~\ref{thm:U}(ii) specialized to the least positive scale,
using Theorem~\ref{thm:M}(ii) (that $m^\star$ itself occurs as a scale).
(ii) is the contrapositive of (i) plus the declared leaf.  (iii): given
$\FS$, fix any input; (i) yields the cycle; this holds for every input
uniformly, which is exactly the conjecture.  The structural clauses are
Theorem~\ref{thm:M}(iii) and Theorem~\ref{thm:U}(i) restated.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[M3 discipline]
The collapse is a one-way implication from a single, input-free, universal
law.  It is not a per-input conditional equivalence (the unproven direction
is isolated as leaf~E15 and never used); it is not a relabeling of the
conjecture ($\FS$ is a strictly structured law: arithmetic, transportable,
group-valued, decidable on presented worlds by bounded lattice computation);
and it is not a progress claim beyond its proof: $\FS$ is the exact
remainder, stated as one law.
\end{remark}

\section{The seed sector: the bit is certified true on explicit worlds}
\label{sec:seeds}

\subsection{The \texorpdfstring{$\Pp^2$}{P2} determination}

\begin{theorem}[$m^\star(\Pp^2,1,[\mathrm{line}])=2$; full determination]
\label{thm:P}
On $X=\Pp^2$ with $\alpha=h=c_1(\Oo(1))\in H^2(\Pp^2,\Q)$ and $p=1$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}] \emph{(Witness)} The pair
\[
E_+=\Oo(1)\oplus\Oo(1),\qquad
E_-=\Oo(1)\oplus\Oo(-1)
\]
is an integrable pure pair at scale $2$: ranks are equal ($2=2$) and
\[
\ch(E_+)-\ch(E_-)
=2e^{h}-e^{h}-e^{-h}=e^{h}-e^{-h}=2\sinh(h)
=2h+2\frac{h^3}{3!}+2\frac{h^5}{5!}+\cdots
=2h\quad\text{on } \Pp^2\ (h^3=0),
\]
pure at grade $1$; the grade-2 term cancels because $\sinh$ is odd.
\item[\textup{(ii)}] \emph{(Obstruction)} Every integrable pure pair on
$\Pp^2$ with difference $m h$ has $m$ even.  Indeed let
$c_1(E_\pm)=a_\pm h$ and $c_2(E_\pm)=k_\pm h^2$ with
$a_\pm,k_\pm\in\Z$ (integrality of the Chern lattice of $\Pp^2$).
Purity at grade $2$ means $\ch_2(E_+)=\ch_2(E_-)$, i.e.
\[
\frac{a_+^2-2k_+}{2}=\frac{a_-^2-2k_-}{2}
\;\Longleftrightarrow\;
(a_+-a_-)(a_++a_-)=2(k_+-k_-).
\]
The left side is $m\,(a_++a_-)$; the right side is even.  Since
$a_++a_-\equiv a_+-a_-=m \pmod 2$, if $m$ were odd then $a_++a_-$ would be
odd and the left side odd --- contradiction.  Hence $m$ is even.
\item[\textup{(iii)}] Consequently $m^\star$ divides $2$, is positive by
(i), and is even by (ii): $m^\star(\Pp^2,1,h)=2$ exactly.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
As stated: (i) is the displayed computation; the truncation is legitimate
because $H^{2j}(\Pp^2,\Q)=0$ for $j\ge3$.  (ii) is the Chern-lattice
argument; note it applies to \emph{arbitrary} holomorphic bundles, not only
sums of line bundles: only integrality of $c_1$-and-$c_2$ coefficients enters.
(iii) follows from Theorem~\ref{thm:M}(ii).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Parity is load-bearing here]
The parity obstruction in (ii) is the rank-independent form of the
$x^2\equiv x\pmod 2$ argument: for sums of line bundles it reads
$\sum a_i^2-\sum b_j^2=0\Rightarrow\sum a_i-\sum b_j$ even, since
$x^2\equiv x \pmod 2$ termwise.  The Chern-lattice form above extends it to
all holomorphic pairs on $\Pp^2$.  This is the same parity phenomenon that
the cycle-1 m-congruence analysis flagged on $\Pp^2$ for single carriers at
$p=1$; the pair form pins it as an exact $m^\star$-determination.
\end{remark}
