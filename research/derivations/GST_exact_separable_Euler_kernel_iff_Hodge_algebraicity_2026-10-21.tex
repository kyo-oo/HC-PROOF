\section*{GST exact diagonal separability threshold}
Let Y be any smooth projective complex n-fold.
Under the rational topological Chern character define
\[
 W_Y=\operatorname{ch}^{-1}
    \left(\bigoplus_{p=0}^{n}
     (H^{2p}(Y,\Q)\cap H^{p,p}(Y))\right)
       \subset K^0_{\mathrm{top}}(Y)_\Q,
\]
and let A_Y be the image of rational
algebraic K_0(Y) inside W_Y.
The genuine rational Euler form is
\[
 e(U,V)=\int_Y \operatorname{ch}(U^\vee)
                \operatorname{ch}(V)\operatorname{td}(T_Y).
\]
This form is nondegenerate on W_Y.
Indeed the ordinary Poincare pairing is
nondegenerate on the rational Hodge
(whole-degree) sector by Hodge-Riemann and
hard Lefschetz, and dualizing together with
multiplication by the invertible algebraic
Todd character preserve that property.

\begin{theorem}[Full algebraicity iff finite algebraic Euler coevaluation]
The following statements are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every rational (p,p) Hodge class on Y in every
codimension p is the canonical Betti class of
a genuine rational algebraic cycle on Y.
\item There are finite genuine algebraic
K-classes E_1,...,E_s,F_1,...,F_s on Y such that
for every U in W_Y one has the actual
topological K-theory equality
\[
 \boxed{U=\sum_{i=1}^s e(E_i,U)F_i.}
\]
\item The algebraic diagonal kernel
[O_{\Delta_Y}] has the same action on
every U in W_Y as a finite sum of
genuine separable algebraic kernels
\(\sum_i E_i^\vee\boxtimes F_i\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof.
The rational algebraic Chern-character map identifies
algebraic K_0(Y)_Q with the direct sum CH^p(Y)_Q;
canonical Betti realization identifies its image
with the algebraic Hodge subspace A_Y.
Thus (1) is equivalent to A_Y=W_Y.

Assume A_Y=W_Y and choose genuine algebraic
classes F_j whose rational topological images
form a Q-basis of W_Y. Since e is nondegenerate
on W_Y, their Euler Gram matrix with a
possibly distinct algebraic basis is invertible.
Rational inversion provides algebraic
Euler-dual E_i satisfying
e(E_i,F_j)=KroneckerDelta(i,j).
The displayed finite coevaluation reconstructs
every U in W_Y, proving (2).

Conversely each F_i in (2) is algebraic,
so every U in W_Y belongs to A_Y.
Thus (2) implies A_Y=W_Y and hence (1).

The Fourier--Mukai action of the genuine
external algebraic kernel E_i^vee box F_i
is U -> e(E_i,U)F_i, by actual proper
projection and Euler multiplicativity.
The diagonal structure sheaf has identity
action, so (2) and (3) are equivalent.
No exactness statement at the level
of derived functors is claimed:
the theorem is rational K-theory action.
\hfill QED

\begin{corollary}[GST residual diagonal equivalence]
Suppose a GST finite-frame projector
P is a sum of genuinely separable
algebraic kernels, and put Q=O_Delta-P.
Then Q has the same action on the
rational Hodge K-sector W_Y as some
finite separable algebraic kernel
if and only if every rational Hodge
class on Y is algebraic.
\end{corollary}
\noindent Proof.
A separable replacement S of Q makes
S+P a separable replacement of the
diagonal identity. The theorem then
implies A_Y=W_Y. Conversely when
A_Y=W_Y use the finite coevaluation for
the diagonal and subtract P.

\section*{Quotient action and quadratic detector}
For the actual Hodge quotient D_Y=W_Y/A_Y,
each genuine separable algebraic
kernel acts as ZERO since its output is
a sum of algebraic F_i classes.
The diagonal kernel acts as IDENTITY.
Therefore the GST residual kernel Q
acts as identity on D_Y. If D_Y is
nonzero, no separable replacement can
agree with Q on all Hodge inputs.
This does not produce a nonalgebraic
Hodge class on any actual variety;
it identifies the exact algebraicity
input needed for the replacement.

For a rational primitive Hodge fiber with
positive Hodge-Riemann pairing (V,q)
and algebraic subspace A,
the statement that every nonzero r has
some algebraic z with q(r,z) nonzero
is equivalent to A=V.
If A is proper, a nonzero r in A-perp
has q(r,r)>0 while all its algebraic
one-legged readouts vanish.
Thus the positive quadratic GST Euler
detector and an algebraic one-legged
source are mathematically distinct
claims: asserting their universal
equivalence already proves the
primitive Hodge conjecture.

\section*{Exact Wolfram self-checks}
An exact rational symmetric Gram matrix
[[2,1,0],[1,3,1],[0,1,4]]
was inverted. Its determinant is 18,
and its Euler dual coevaluation was
exactly the 3x3 identity; the input
(2,-3,5) was reconstructed. A rational
two-dimensional source subspace of this
three-dimensional space cannot supply
the full coevaluation. A separate exact
example with Gram diag(2,3,5) and
algebraic source frame (e1,e2)
had residual e3 with positive quadratic
norm 5 but algebraic linear readouts
both zero. These tests concern
finite rational algebra, NOT actual
counterexamples to the Hodge conjecture.
The general mathematical proof above
uses rational Euler nondegeneracy.


\section*{Sharp finite-length GST residual-source theorem}
Let W_Y have rational dimension n.
Suppose an ACTUAL algebraic Euler frame
C_1,...,C_t in W_Y has invertible Euler Gram
matrix G_ij=e(C_i,C_j), t<=n.
Its genuine algebraic separable projector P
is idempotent, rank t on W_Y; hence the
GST residual diagonal operator
Q=O_Delta-P has rank n-t on W_Y.

\begin{theorem}[Exact minimal number of algebraic Euler legs]
An algebraic separable kernel with exactly
the same rational Hodge action as Q
exists if and only if the full rational
Hodge conjecture holds for Y.
If it exists, every such decomposition
contains at least n-t rank-one algebraic
external-kernel terms, and one with exactly
n-t terms can be constructed.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof.
An external kernel with two genuine
algebraic legs has action of rational
rank at most one, so a sum of s such
kernels has rank at most s.
The rank of Q is n-t, proving
s>=n-t. If Q has an actual separable
algebraic realization, adding the
known separable projector P gives
a separable realization of the identity,
so all W_Y is algebraic by the
preceding equivalence theorem.
Conversely if all W_Y is algebraic,
then the Euler nondegeneracy gives a
full algebraic basis adapted to the
image of P and a dual basis adapted to
the complementary image of Q. Exactly
n-t dual algebraic pairs reconstruct
the operator Q. This is a genuine
finite-rank sharp bound, not a claim
that the missing complementary
algebraic objects have been born.

An independent Wolfram exact rational
check used Euler matrix
[[2,1,0],[1,3,1],[0,1,4]]
with algebraic frames of dimensions
t=1,2,3. The corresponding residual
ranks were exactly 2,1,0;
P and Q were idempotent in each case.

\section*{Research verdict}
The categorical separability criterion
is proved under the genuine stated
rational Chow/K-theory comparison.
It is an equivalence, not an unconditional
proof of Hodge algebraicity. Consequently
the previously proposed universal
quadratic-Euler-to-one-leg law is
not justified by diagonal positivity
alone. This source-type issue is
now precisely formulated without
postulating an algebraic representative.
No Lean proof or independent refereeing
is claimed for this LaTeX-first work.
