\section{Universal original-X target, provenance and carrier}
Let $X$ be any smooth connected projective complex $d$-fold, $0\le p\le d$, and $\alpha$ any rational $(p,p)$ Hodge class. Put $A_p=\CH^p(X)_{\Q}$ and $W_p=H^{2p}(X,\Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X,\mathbb C)$, $c_p=\cl_X^{\mathrm{can}}:A_p\to W_p$. The existing Lean source \texttt{GSTHodgeSingleHypothesisConditional.lean} proves the single-fold orbit/determinant implication from the supplied Stage-2G data. This route tries to derive its antecedent from the existing \emph{native GST correspondence algebra and polarized primitive residual energy} without assuming an algebraic Hodge basis and without replacing the original X by a CM/K3 test variety.

The existing \texttt{GSTPolarizedHodgeResidualEnergy.lean} motivates a rational positive definite Hodge-Riemann quadratic form $Q_p$ on the rational $(p,p)$ space: take the signed primitive intersection form on each primitive Lefschetz summand. The rational Lefschetz decomposition and Hodge-Riemann bilinear relations make their orthogonal direct-sum form rational and positive on $W_p$. This rational form is \emph{not} asserted to be induced by an algebraic correspondence or a holomorphic mass functional.

Let $\mathcal A_p\subseteq\End_\Q(W_p)$ be the unital rational algebra generated by a specified family of **genuine original-X Chow correspondences of codimension d on $X\times X$**, restricted to Hodge weight $p$. Each generator $T$ has an actual native push-pull operator $N_T$ with the \emph{canonical} square $c_pN_T=T c_p$. We assume explicitly that for each generator the $Q_p$-adjoint $T^\dagger$ also belongs to the algebra generated by genuine such correspondences and that the adjoint property holds on W. We do NOT infer algebraicity of a polarization adjoint from a formal matrix transpose or from conjectural Lefschetz inverses.

\begin{theorem}[GST polarized scalar-commutant source birth]
If
\[
 \boxed{\Comm_{\End_\Q(W_p)}(\mathcal A_p)=\Q\,I_{W_p}}
 \tag{PC}
\]
and the actual correspondence algebra $\mathcal A_p$ is closed under $Q_p$-adjoints as above, then every prescribed $\alpha\in W_p$ is the \emph{canonical Betti class} of an actual rational codimension-p cycle on the original X.
More strongly, starting only from the universally available $z_*=h^p\in\CH^p(X)_{\Q}$ for any ample divisor $h$, finite genuine GST native words applied to $z_*$ supply a rational basis of $W_p$ through the canonical Betti class map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $B=\operatorname{im}(c_p)\subset W_p$. The projective seed $h^p$ has a nonzero canonical Betti class because $\int_Xh^d>0$. Thus $B\ne0$. By canonical Chow push-pull naturality, $B$ is stable under all generators in $\mathcal A_p$. Because the $Q_p$-adjoints are also generators/words of actual correspondences, $B$ is stable under adjoints. Consequently its rational orthogonal complement $B^\perp$ is stable under every $T\in\mathcal A_p$:
\[
 Q_p(Ty,b)=Q_p(y,T^\dagger b)=0 \quad
 (y\in B^\perp,\ b\in B).
\]
Positive definiteness ensures $W_p=B\oplus B^\perp$ over Q. Let $P_B$ be the $Q_p$-orthogonal projection onto $B$. Both summands are $\mathcal A_p$-stable, hence $P_BT=TP_B$ for every $T\in\mathcal A_p$. Condition (PC) makes $P_B=cI$. As $P_B$ is a nonzero idempotent (since $B\ne0$), $c^2=c$ and $c=1$. Hence $P_B=I$, $B=W_p$, proving Hodge-algebraicity at this original X and weight.

For constructive word-source generation, set $v=c_p(h^p)\ne0$ and define
\(\mathcal U=\operatorname{span}_{\Q}\{T_k\cdots T_1v:
 T_i\text{ chosen among the actual generators and their geometric adjoints}\}\).
It is a nonzero $\mathcal A_p$-stable subspace. The same orthogonal-projector argument applied to $\mathcal U$ gives $\mathcal U=W_p$. Thus among the explicitly listed finite native words there are $n=\dim_\Q W_p$ words whose classes are linearly independent. Choose the lexicographically first independent n-tuple under any fixed enumeration of the genuine generators and exact rational Hodge coordinates. For a prescribed $\alpha$, solve the rational $n\times n$ system to give its coefficients and return the same finite rational combination of genuine native word cycles. No target Chow representative was input, and their classes equal alpha by canonical naturality.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A missing primitive residual manufactures a non-scalar GST symmetry]

If a rational Hodge class $\alpha$ is outside the entire native algebraic image $B\ne0$, then the rational positive-definite polarization yields a nontrivial idempotent $P_B\in\Comm(\mathcal A_p)$ whenever the actual correspondence algebra is adjoint-closed. In particular,
\[
 \boxed{\dim_\Q\Comm(\mathcal A_p)\ge2.}
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The projection and $I-P_B$ are linearly independent rational commuting endomorphisms because both $B$ and $B^\perp$ are nonzero. Their existence uses only the rational finite-dimensional vector-space $B$, not an algebraic projector and not a chosen cycle for alpha.
\end{proof}

Its inverse gives algebraic \emph{complementary-codimension} dual cycles $\check z_{p,i}\in\CH^{d-p}(X)_\Q$ because Hodge classes in that complementary degree have just been proved algebraic by the same condition there. Define
\[
 \Gamma_p=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}
 \operatorname{pr}_1^*\check z_{p,i}\cdot

\[

 \mathbf1_{a+b\equiv0\pmod n},
\qquad
 \boxed{\det M=(-1)^{(n-1)(n-2)/2}\ne0}

for $n\ge2$ (for $n=1$, determinant 1). This constructs the \emph{full Chow} single-fold hypothesis, after the adjoint-commutant criterion has produced cycles independently.
