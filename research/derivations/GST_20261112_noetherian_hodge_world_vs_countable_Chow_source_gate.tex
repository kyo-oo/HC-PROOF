\section{The owner's exact infinity-control demand}
For arbitrary original smooth projective complex X, p,
and prescribed rational Hodge alpha, the desired output is
ACTUAL Z_alpha in CH^p(X)_Q with canonical class alpha.
The owner's cardinal worlds, topology phi_n, N-cohomology,
Navigation and U/J/M/[J,M] source laws may produce many
N-indexed data, but the required Hodge and Chow conditions
are DIFFERENT mathematical TYPES.

We study a genuine smooth projective family pi:Y->B
with original X at b0 and a LOCAL flat extension
of the specified rational class alpha.
A global single-valued class can be obtained after a suitable
finite covering in situations where its Hodge-class locus
has finite monodromy; otherwise statements remain local.

\section{The Hodge condition is locally Noetherian finite}
The Cattani--Deligne--Kaplan theorem proves that the
locus of Hodge classes in an algebraic variation of
polarized Hodge structure is ALGEBRAIC.
Write H_alpha for an irreducible component through
the original point in the appropriate Hodge-class locus.

\begin{theorem}[All-order Hodge constraint finite reduction]
Let B be a finite-type complex algebraic variety.
For any algebraic Hodge-locus germ H_alpha at b0,
there exist FINITELY many algebraic local functions
f1,...,fr in the Noetherian local ring
O_(B,b0) such that an analytic/formal parameter
belongs to the Hodge locus near b0 if and only if
all its pullbacks f1,...,fr vanish.
In particular an arbitrarily cardinal-indexed list
of defining Hodge conditions has a locally FINITE
algebraic generating set.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By the CDK algebraicity theorem, H_alpha is locally
the zero locus of an algebraic coherent ideal sheaf.
The local ring of a finite-type complex variety is
Noetherian, and every ideal in it is finitely generated.
These finitely many generators cut out precisely the
local Hodge-class locus, including any nilpotent
scheme structure chosen in the defining ideal.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Nonconstant complete GST formal Hodge arc gives REAL variation]
If a compatible infinite-order nonconstant formal
arc through b0 factors through the algebraic Hodge-locus
germ H_alpha, then H_alpha has positive local dimension.
Consequently there exists an ACTUAL nonconstant algebraic
curve through b0 contained in that Hodge-locus component
(after choosing a suitable reduced irreducible component).
Over the smooth part of this curve the transported
prescribed rational alpha remains of Hodge type (p,p).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If a finite-type algebraic germ has local dimension zero,
its Artinian completed local ring has nilpotent maximal
ideal. Any morphism from it to the reduced formal DVR
C[[t]] kills the maximal ideal and is CONSTANT.
Thus a nonconstant formal arc forces positive local
dimension. An irreducible positive-dimensional
finite-type algebraic subvariety through b0 contains
an algebraic curve through b0 by cutting with suitable
hyperplanes and choosing an irreducible component.
By definition that curve lies in the Hodge-class locus.
\end{proof}

The above theorem produces REAL geometrically varying
HODGE-PRESERVING worlds from infinite compatible
Hodge equations. It does NOT manufacture Chow cycles
on those worlds; that is a different existence problem.
If the target-sensitive infinitesimal map
O_alpha: H^1(T_X) -> H^{p-1,p+1}
is injective along an attempted deformation direction,
there is no such nonconstant formal Hodge arc in that
direction. The explicit original elliptic example
has exactly this obstruction.

\section{The algebraic-source condition is a COUNTABLE DISJUNCTION, not a finite ideal}
For a genuine relative Chow family, the source-bearing
locus of the flat input alpha is
\[
\boxed{
A_\alpha=\bigcup_{m,r,s\ge0}A_{\alpha;m,r,s},
}
\]
where each A_(alpha;m,r,s) is the Zariski-closed
image of GOOD components in a projective relative
Chow parameter space of positive degree r,
negative degree s and denominator m.
Every individual source profile is finite-type;
the UNION over profiles need NOT be a single closed
subvariety, and there is no universal bound on
m,r,s supplied by Hodge purity or by the
Noetherian property of H_alpha.

For example a countably infinite subset of
a complex algebraic curve is Zariski DENSE but
can fail to contain a chosen original point.
The genuinely algebraic Legendre elliptic family
provides an actual source-geometry illustration:
the set of fibers isogenous to one fixed elliptic
curve is COUNTABLY infinite and Zariski dense on
the Legendre base, but is not all of it.
The cross-divisor Hodge classes themselves
change with the fiber and DO NOT provide a
flat target-matching original alpha.
Thus arbitrary GST infinity-control density
cannot replace the prescribed-class condition.

\section{Exactly what WOULD close the native source birth}
The prior original-X very-general source theorem says
ONE actual algebraic source on a single very-general
fiber of the prescribed alpha-Hodge locus is sufficient
to return an ACTUAL original-X Chow representative.
The global source-existence arrow to prove is:
\[
\forall (X,p,\alpha)\quad
\exists\,\text{an alpha-preserving algebraic
Hodge-locus stratum and a GENERIC fiber cycle }
Z_\eta\in CH^p(X_\eta)_Q,\quad
cl(Z_\eta)=\alpha_\eta.
\]
When the Hodge-locus germ is only the original
point, the generic-fiber reduction collapses to
the original-X problem; cardinal-world variation
cannot manufacture a positive-dimensional
family in that case.

To certify a proposed native law one must supply
an ACTUAL algebraic cycle at the generic point
with a bounded, finite algebraic formula,
or prove a genuinely new uniform mechanism producing
these cycles from prescribed alpha.
The finite Hodge equations and the cardinal
topology alone do not yield that output.
