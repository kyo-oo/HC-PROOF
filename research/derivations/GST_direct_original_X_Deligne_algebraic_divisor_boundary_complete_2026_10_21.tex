\section{The fixed target and noncircular source type}
For every smooth projective complex variety \(X\), every
\(p\), and every rational Hodge class
\(\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)\),
the immutable target is an independently geometric
\[
 Z_\alpha\in \CH^p(X)_\Q,\qquad
       \cl_X^{\mathrm{canonical}}Z_\alpha=\alpha.
\]
The preceding GST routes supply Gram, K0, Chern, Chow-correspondence,
world-return and normalized primitive-source operations but never
a proof that an arbitrary middle Hodge residual is detected by
an ACTUAL algebraic Chow source. Here we attack the original
target through a different, genuinely target-dependent geometric
carrier constructed directly on \(X\), followed by a complete
algebraic source construction wherever the carrier algebraizes.

\section{Construct a target-dependent holomorphic geometric lift without a cycle premise}
Take any \(p\ge1\). Multiply the rational \(\alpha\) by an integer
\(N>0\) so that it is the image of
\(a\in H^{2p}(X,\Z(p))\) in rational Betti cohomology;
torsion choices do not affect the rational conclusion.
The analytic exponential sequence (with the customary Tate factors)
\[
 0\longrightarrow\Z(p)
 \longrightarrow\mathcal O_X
 \xrightarrow{\exp}\mathcal O_X^\times
 \longrightarrow1
\]
has connecting map
\[
 \delta:H^{2p-1}(X,\mathcal O_X^\times)
                 \longrightarrow H^{2p}(X,\Z(p)).
\]
Since \(a\) is a rational \((p,p)\) class, its image in
\(H^{2p}(X,\mathcal O_X)=H^{0,2p}(X)\) vanishes.
Exactness therefore CONSTRUCTS a lift
\[
 \boxed{g_\alpha\in
 H^{2p-1}(X,\mathcal O_X^\times),\qquad
           \delta(g_\alpha)=a.}                 \tag{1}
\]
This is a genuine holomorphic
\((2p-2)\)-gerbe cocycle on the ORIGINAL \(X\)
(in particular an ordinary holomorphic line bundle when \(p=1\)).
The construction does not choose a Chow cycle and does not assert
the holomorphic object is algebraic.

The ordinary exponential lift is TOO WEAK to encode the
entire Hodge condition for \(p>1\). For example on a
four-dimensional complex abelian variety the complex dimensions
of \(H^{r,4-r}\) are
\[
 (1,16,36,16,1).
\]
The kernel of projection \(H^4(X,\C)\to H^{0,4}(X)\)
has complex dimension \(69\), versus \(36\) for \(H^{2,2}\).
The Hodge condition uses the full filtration, not
the single \((0,4)\) projection.
Wolfram verified these binomial multiplicities exactly.
This computation DOES NOT assert the existence of
non-Hodge rational classes in either subspace for a given
specific \(X\).

\section{Full original-\(X\) Hodge-type carrier via Deligne geometry}
To retain all Hodge-filtration conditions, use the
true Deligne complex \(\Z(p)_{\mathcal D}\) and the
classical exact sequence for smooth projective complex \(X\),
\[
 0\to J^p(X)
 \longrightarrow H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\Z(p))
 \xrightarrow{\epsilon}
 \Hdg^{2p}(X,\Z(p))
 \to0.                                              \tag{2}
\]
Here \(J^p(X)\) is the standard generalized intermediate
Jacobian group and the right-hand term denotes integral
Betti classes of Hodge type \((p,p)\), including torsion
by the usual convention. In particular, for the integral
multiple \(a=N\alpha\), exactness supplies an actual
**Deligne-cohomology geometric lift**
\[
 \boxed{\widetilde a\in
 H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\Z(p)),
 \qquad \epsilon(\widetilde a)=a.}                  \tag{3}
\]
This is a genuine target-dependent holomorphic cocycle on
the specified original \(X\); its construction uses the
actual Hodge condition rather than an abstract chosen
cycleClass. But the Deligne cycle-class map
\[
 \CH^p(X)\longrightarrow
 H^{2p}_{\mathcal D}(X,\Z(p))                       \tag{4}
\]
has not thereby acquired an inverse or a lift of \(\widetilde a\).
The exact missing algebraic-geometric operation for \(p>1\) is
to construct an ACTUAL codimension-\(p\) cycle whose
Deligne class has the same image under \(\epsilon\).
Equation (3) alone is not that operation. This distinction
prevents calling a holomorphic higher-gerbe a Chow cycle.

\section{COMPLETED original-\(X\) algebraic construction in boundary codimensions}
\begin{theorem}[All original X: exact actual rational Chow source for codimensions 0,1,d-1,d]
Let \(X\) be ANY connected smooth projective complex variety
of dimension \(d\), and let
\(p\in\{0,1,d-1,d\}\cap\{0,\dots,d\}\).
Every rational \((p,p)\) Hodge class
\(\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\Q)\) has a
GENUINELY algebraic cycle
\[
 \boxed{
 Z_\alpha\in\CH^p(X)_\Q,\qquad
          \cl_X^{\rm can}(Z_\alpha)=\alpha.}      \tag{5}
\]
In particular the ENTIRE rational Hodge conjecture
holds in all codimensions for every smooth projective
complex variety of dimension \(d\le3\).
For disconnected X perform the same construction
independently on each connected component.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For \(p=0\), the relevant rational Betti group is
\(H^0(X,\Q)=\Q\), generated by the canonical class
of the ACTUAL whole X; set \(Z_\alpha=q[X]\).

For \(p=d\), the top Betti group is
\(H^{2d}(X,\Q)=\Q\), generated by the canonical
class of an ACTUAL algebraic closed point \(x\).
Set \(Z_\alpha=q[x]\) with \(q\) chosen by
the true canonical integration/degree pairing.

For \(p=1\), clear denominators \(a=N\alpha\)
and use (1) with \(p=1\):
\(g_\alpha\in H^1(X,\mathcal O_X^\times)
=\operatorname{Pic}^{an}(X)\), with
\(c_1(g_\alpha)=a\).
Projective GAGA algebraizes this actual holomorphic
line bundle to an algebraic line bundle \(L\).
On a smooth integral projective variety every
algebraic line bundle has an ACTUAL rational Cartier
divisor representative D; hence
\[
 Z_\alpha=\frac1N[D]\in\CH^1(X)_\Q,\qquad
 \cl_X^{can}Z_\alpha=\alpha.
\]
This is an input-free true Chow-source construction
from arbitrary original-X codimension-one Hodge data.

For \(p=d-1\) and \(d\ge2\), let \(H\) be an
ACTUAL algebraic ample hyperplane divisor
and \(h=\cl_X^{can}H\).
Genuine Hard Lefschetz gives a rational isomorphism
\[
 h^{d-2}: H^2(X,\Q)
              \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}H^{2d-2}(X,\Q).
                                                               \tag{6}
\]
It is a Hodge morphism with the indicated bidegree shift.
Thus the unique rational \(\beta=(h^{d-2})^{-1}\alpha\)
belongs to \(H^{1,1}(X)\).
The preceding REAL \(p=1\) construction supplies
a rational algebraic divisor D with
\(\cl_X^{can}D=\beta\).
Define the genuine original-X intersection cycle
\[
 \boxed{Z_\alpha=D\cdot H^{d-2}
                      \in\CH^{d-1}(X)_\Q.}         \tag{7}
\]
Exact canonical intersection compatibility gives
\(\cl_X^{can}Z_\alpha=
 \beta\smile h^{d-2}=\alpha\).
For \(d=1\), p=d-1=0 is already covered.
For \(d\le3\) every possible codimension
is among the four allowed values, finishing
the all-classes statement for those dimensions.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact all-dimension reduction to primitive lower-half codimension}

\[
 h^{2p-d}:H^{2(d-p)}(X,\Q)
                   \xrightarrow{\ \simeq\ }H^{2p}(X,\Q).
\]
For any rational (p,p) Hodge class alpha, the unique rational
preimage beta is type (d-p,d-p). An independently constructed
algebraic Chow representative B of beta yields the ACTUAL
original-X representative
\(Z_\alpha=B\cdot H^{2p-d}\in CH^p(X)_\Q\),

\(d-p\ge2\). Hence the actual universal missing range after

\[

and the first unknown middle case is (d,p)=(4,2).
This is an exact domain reduction by an actual geometric ample
intersection, NOT a claim the remaining range has been solved.
