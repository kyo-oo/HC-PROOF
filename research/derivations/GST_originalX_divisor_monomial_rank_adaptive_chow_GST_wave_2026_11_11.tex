Let $X$ be \emph{any} smooth connected projective variety over $\mathbb C$

$I_{d-p}=\{\nu\in\mathbb N^\rho:|\nu|=d-p\}$.

\[

\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q},
\qquad
Y_\nu^{\mathrm{raw}}=D_1^{\nu_1}\cdots D_\rho^{\nu_\rho}
\in CH^{d-p}(X)_{\mathbb Q}.
\]
Define the actual rational \emph{intersection matrix}
\[
B_{\nu\mu}:=\deg_X(Y_\nu^{\mathrm{raw}}\cdot Z_\mu)
=\int_X cl_X(Y_\nu^{\mathrm{raw}})\cup cl_X(Z_\mu)

linear algebra supplies selected rows $\nu_0,\ldots,\nu_{t-1}$

\[

Then $\deg_X(Y_iZ_j)=(C^{-1}C)_{ij}=\delta_{ij}$.
