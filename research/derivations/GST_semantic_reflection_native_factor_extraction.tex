\begin{abstract}
A full-text index of all 701 Lean files locates three precise GST laws
that avoid separately prescribing an input/output pair: impredicative
semantic reflection, reverse ontological existence, and automatic
native linear lifting. We audit their exact hypotheses and apply them
to the established native residual Euler kernel. We prove its action
on the true algebraicity quotient is the identity, and derive an
explicit one-sided factor-extraction formula turning a suitable
native factorization of its quadratic test into a linear native
detector. A global version reconstructs actual cycles on the original
variety. Universal existence of the factorization is not proved.
\end{abstract}

 \boxed{y\in\operatorname{Cl}_G(S)

\]

$A=\im(c)$, $D=H/A$, and let $\pi:H\to D$. Then
\[
 \boxed{\exists U:C\to C\text{ linear},\quad cU=Tc
             \iff \pi T|_A=0.}                               \tag{4}
\]
\begin{proof}
If $cU=Tc$, then $T(cz)=c(Uz)\in A$.
Conversely choose a rational linear section $s:A\to C$

\section{Extract an actual native factor}
For $\dim X=2p$, let $r$ be the primitive residual, $Q$ its
positive rational form, and $Jr=(-1)^p r$. The established
normalized topological packets satisfy
\[
 -\chi_{Y\times Y}(A_r\boxtimes A_{Jr},\mathscr Q)=Q(r,r).         \tag{6}
\]
Suppose actual algebraic pure-middle classes $E_i$ and rational
coefficients $a_i$ give a one-sided factorization of this test:
\[
 \chi_{Y\times Y}(A_r\boxtimes A_{Jr},\mathscr Q)
       =\sum_i a_i\,\chi_Y(A_r,\real E_i)\,\ell_i(Jr).            \tag{7}
\]
The $\ell_i$ are independently specified rational linear functionals.
An external-product decomposition gives
$\ell_i(y)=\chi_Y(A_y,F_i)$.
The second factors may be topological; algebraicity is required
on the output leg $E_i$.

\begin{theorem}[One-sided native factor extraction]
Under the actual factorization (7), construct
\[
 \boxed{E_r=-\sum_i a_i\ell_i(Jr)E_i
                    \in K_0^{\mathrm{alg}}(Y)_\Q.}              \tag{8}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\chi_Y(A_r,\real E_r)=Q(r,r)>0\quad(r\ne0).}              \tag{9}
\]
At least one actual output factor has a nonzero linear residual period.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Pair (8) with $A_r$ and use bilinearity, (7), and (6).
If all output-factor periods vanished, (7) would be zero,
contrary to $Q(r,r)>0$. Rational coefficients preserve the
actual rational algebraic $K$-group; no native lift of $r$
is used in the formula.
\end{proof}

For a complete alteration $f:X'\to X$ of degree $e>0$,
the same construction on $Y'$ gives index $eQ(r,r)$.
Let $\mathfrak a'$ be the established actual Chow return
and inverse native normalization. With
$\sigma=(-1)^{M+p+1}$, the original-$X$ detector is
\[
 \boxed{Z_r=\frac{\sigma}{e}
      f_*\left[\mathfrak a'(\ch_M E'_r)\right]_p,\qquad
          Q(r,\cl Z_r)=Q(r,r)>0.}                             \tag{10}
\]
This is substitution into the previously proved exact
original-$X$ index-return formula.

Existence of (7) with actual algebraic output factors remains
a geometric requirement. An arithmetic decomposition of the
number $Q(r,r)$ does not supply those factors.

\section{Global factorization and the prescribed cycle}
For arbitrary $d=\dim X$, use the established middle blowup
world, full rational Hodge packet $\mathcal H_X$, perfect
pairing $P_X$, and actual Chow return $\mathfrak a$.
Put $\epsilon=(-1)^{M+1}$ and
$z_i=\mathfrak a(\ch_M E_i)$.
The normal-free Euler and diagonal laws give
\[
 \chi_Y(A_x,\real E_i)=\epsilon P_X(x,\cl z_i),\qquad
 \chi_{Y\times Y}(A_x\boxtimes A_y,\mathcal O_\Delta)
       =-P_X(x,y).                                             \tag{11}
\]
Suppose a finite one-sided factorization holds for all
$x,y\in\mathcal H_X$:
\[
 -P_X(x,y)=\sum_i a_i\chi_Y(A_x,\real E_i)\ell_i(y).              \tag{12}
\]
\begin{theorem}[Exact original-$X$ cycle]
Under (12), every prescribed packet $y$ has the actual cycle
\[
 \boxed{Z_y=-\epsilon\sum_i a_i\ell_i(y)z_i,\qquad
                         \cl(Z_y)=y.}                         \tag{13}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equations (11)--(12) imply, for every $x$,
\[
 P_X(x,\cl Z_y)
   =-\sum_i a_i\ell_i(y)\chi_Y(A_x,\real E_i)=P_X(x,y).
\]
Perfectness of $P_X$ proves the equality of classes.
\end{proof}
Only equality on the Hodge testing block is required.
A decomposition of the entire diagonal into external products
in $K_0$ is not asserted.

Uniform existence of (12) is equivalent to algebraicity
of all of $\mathcal H_X$: (13) proves one direction.
Conversely, for a native basis $v_i$ with Gram
$p_{ij}=P_X(v_i,v_j)$ and actual normalized lifts,
the native two-sided coefficients $a_{ij}=-(p^{-1})_{ij}$
give (12). This is an exact geometric construction target,
not a weaker unconditional theorem already proved by (13).
