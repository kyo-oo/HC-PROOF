\section{The actual universal original-$X$ input and output}
Let $X$ be any smooth connected projective variety over $\mathbb C$ of complex dimension $d\ge 2$, with ample integral divisor class $h\in CH^1(X)_{\mathbb Q}$.
Let
\[
W_p(X)=H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X,\mathbb C),
\qquad cl_X^p:CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\longrightarrow W_p(X)
\]
be the genuine canonical Betti cycle class.
No algebraic lift of an arbitrary prescribed Hodge class is used as input.

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional original-$X$ GST orbit construction in the outer middle weights]
For every such $X$ and each $p\in\{1,d-1\}$ there exist an \emph{actual rational codimension-$d$ algebraic Chow correspondence}
$\Gamma_{X,p}\in CH^d(X\times X)_{\mathbb Q}$ and an actual
$Z_{X,p}\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\Gamma_{X,p}$ acts by ordinary Chow push-pull $N_{\Gamma}$ and its canonical Betti action $T_\Gamma$ satisfies
$cl_X^p(N_\Gamma z)=T_\Gamma(cl_X^p(z))$ for every $z$;
\item $T_\Gamma$ preserves $W_p(X)$;
\item the single orbit $\{cl_X^p(Z_{X,p}),T_\Gamma cl_X^p(Z_{X,p}),\ldots\}$ spans the entire rational Hodge fiber $W_p(X)$.
\end{enumerate}
In particular the \emph{actual original-$X$} GST
\texttt{NativeGSTSingleFoldOrbitLaw} holds for these two weights when the
abstract Stage-2G Hodge data are instantiated by canonical Betti/Hodge theory
and correspondences are interpreted in full Chow intersection theory.
This does not assert all weights of the hypothesis.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write $r=\rho(X)=\dim_{\mathbb Q}NS(X)_{\mathbb Q}\ge1$.
By the Lefschetz $(1,1)$ theorem,
\[
NS(X)_{\mathbb Q}\xrightarrow{\sim}W_1(X).
\]
Choose actual rational divisors $D_0,\ldots,D_{r-1}$ whose
canonical classes form a rational basis of $W_1(X)$.
The intersection form
\[
G_{ij}=\int_X cl_X(D_i)\cup cl_X(D_j)\cup cl_X(h)^{d-2}
\]
is a nondegenerate rational matrix by the Hodge index theorem.
Let $(g^{ij})=G^{-1}\in M_r(\mathbb Q)$.

For $p=1$, define genuine algebraic cycle classes
\[
Z_i=D_i\in CH^1(X)_{\mathbb Q},\qquad
Y_i=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}g^{ij}\,D_jh^{d-2}
  \in CH^{d-1}(X)_{\mathbb Q}.
\]
For $p=d-1$, instead define
\[
Z_i=D_ih^{d-2}\in CH^{d-1}(X)_{\mathbb Q},\qquad
Y_i=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}g^{ij}D_j\in CH^1(X)_{\mathbb Q}.
\]
In both cases the projection formula and the definition of $G$ give
\[
\deg_X(Y_i\cdot Z_j)
=\int_X cl_X(Y_i)\cup cl_X(Z_j)=\delta_{ij}.
\]
For $p=d-1$, rational hard Lefschetz yields a rational Hodge
isomorphism $L^{d-2}:W_1(X)\cong W_{d-1}(X)$;
therefore the $cl_X(Z_i)$ form a basis also in this weight.
The case $d=2$ has the same construction for both choices.

For all $i,j$ the genuine codimension-$d$ product correspondence
\[
E_{ij}:=pr_1^*Y_i\cdot pr_2^*Z_j
\in CH^d(X\times X)_{\mathbb Q}
\]
is defined \emph{scheme-theoretically}. Its rational Chow action is
\[
N_{E_{ij}}(z)
=pr_{2*}\bigl(pr_1^*z\cdot E_{ij}\bigr)
=\deg_X(z\cdot Y_i)\,Z_j.
\]
The corresponding canonical rational Betti action is
$T_{E_{ij}}(\beta)=
\bigl(\int_X\beta\cup cl_X(Y_i)\bigr)\,cl_X(Z_j)$.
Proper Gysin functoriality and multiplicativity of the canonical
cycle map give
$cl_X\circ N_{E_{ij}}=T_{E_{ij}}\circ cl_X$,
with \emph{no Hodge surjectivity assumption}.
Thus the $T_{E_{ij}}$ restrict to the full rational Hodge fiber
as the ordinary rational matrix units.

Let $\sigma(i)=i+1\pmod r$ (where $\sigma(0)=0$ when $r=1$) and form
\[
\boxed{\Gamma_{X,p}=
\sum_{i=0}^{r-1} E_{i,\sigma(i)}
\in CH^d(X\times X)_{\mathbb Q},\qquad
Z_{X,p}:=Z_0.}
\]
Then the EXACT original-$X$ Chow and Betti actions satisfy
\[
N_{\Gamma_{X,p}}(Z_j)=Z_{\sigma(j)},\qquad
T_{\Gamma_{X,p}}(cl_X(Z_j))=cl_X(Z_{\sigma(j)}).
\]
It follows inductively that for $0\le k<r$,
\[
T_{\Gamma_{X,p}}^k(cl_X(Z_0))=cl_X(Z_k).
\]
These $r$ vectors form a basis of $W_p(X)$, proving all assertions.
\end{proof}

\section{Why this is a real geometric advance, not a circular formal selector}
The input consists only of the \emph{actual algebraic divisors} whose
existence is independently guaranteed by Lefschetz $(1,1)$.
The complementary cycles $Y_i$ are explicit rational Chow expressions
in $D_i$, $h$ and the computable intersection-matrix inverse;
the correspondence $\Gamma$ is constructed directly in the original
$CH^d(X\times X)_{\mathbb Q}$, and the canonical Betti square is
proven by the projection formula.
No arbitrary weight-$p$ Hodge vector is assumed to have a cycle lift.

The proof crucially uses the \emph{special} fact that the Hodge fiber
in weights $1,d-1$ is already algebraic by classical geometry.
It does not supply new cycles for primitive $p$ with
$2\le p\le d-2$, and no identity here can be extended to those
weights without independent source geometry.

\section{A concrete formalization bottleneck: non-finite product fibers}
The repo's
\texttt{FiniteClosedCorrespondence} is restricted to finite
set-theoretic fibers of the left projection.
A product correspondence $Y_i\times Z_j$, for instance on
$X=\mathbb P^2$, $d=2,p=1$, and $Y_i=Z_j=$ a projective line,
has positive-dimensional left fibers over the points of $Y_i$:
each such fiber is the entire projective line $Z_j$.
It therefore fails the \emph{datatype's} finite-fiber field.
This does \emph{not} mean the codimension-$2$ algebraic cycle
$Y_i\times Z_j$ does not exist: it is a perfectly valid Chow
correspondence and is the correct operation for the theorem.
Nor does it prove no other finite correspondence realizes the
same cohomological action. It demonstrates that the existing
finite-fiber type is too narrow to directly encode this
explicit construction.

A full formal API must implement $CH^d(X\times X)_{\mathbb Q}$
and its refined pullback/intersection/pushforward on
$CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}$, then certify the canonical Betti
comparison. Until that layer is represented in Lean, the
accompanying Lean file proves the complete finite rational
matrix-unit and cyclic-action algebra with explicit native
intersection readouts, while this section supplies the genuine
scheme-theoretic construction.

\section{The owner's Part-1 two-birth seed: exact parity resonance and its universal rational repair}

The original user-supplied source formula is
$Z=Z_0+N(Z_0)$ followed by $N(Z)=NZ_0+N^2Z_0$.
This is a valid equality of genuine Chow cycles once $N$ is the
actual Chow correspondence constructed above. It does \emph{not}
automatically preserve the full cyclic rank of $Z_0$.

On the actual $r$-dimensional rational Hodge fiber of the preceding
theorem, $T=T_\Gamma$ is the cyclic shift of a genuine algebraic
basis $b_0,\ldots,b_{r-1}$. Thus $T^r=I$, and
$b_0$ has a full $r$-dimensional orbit.
For a rational coefficient $\lambda$ put
\[
Z_\lambda=Z_0+\lambda N Z_0\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q},
\quad b_\lambda=cl_X(Z_\lambda)=(I+\lambda T)b_0.
\]
The $r$ Krylov vectors of the new seed are the image under
$I+\lambda T$ of the old basis-Krylov vectors, so
\[
\boxed{
\det K_r(T,b_\lambda)
=
\bigl(1-(-\lambda)^r\bigr)\det K_r(T,b_0).
}
\]
Indeed $\det(I+\lambda T)=\prod_{\zeta^r=1}
(1+\lambda\zeta)=1-(-\lambda)^r$.

At $\lambda=1$, this multiplier vanishes exactly when $r$ is even.
For even $r$ the polynomial $1+x$ has
$\gcd(1+x,x^r-1)=1+x$ and the new orbit has rank exactly $r-1$;
for odd $r$ the orbit has rank exactly $r$.
Applying $T$ once again to $b_\lambda$ does not repair the
defect, because $T$ is invertible. This is the precise mathematical
parity obstruction for the initial $N(Z_0+NZ_0)$ construction.

At $\lambda=2$, by contrast,
\[
\det K_r(T,b_2)
=\bigl(1-(-2)^r\bigr)\det K_r(T,b_0)\ne0
\qquad (r\ge1).
\]
Thus the \emph{actual original-$X$ algebraic cycle}
\[
\boxed{Z_{\mathrm{safe}}=Z_0+2N Z_0}
\]
is a full native/Betti cyclic seed for \emph{every} Picard rank
and every $X,p$ in the theorem.
This correction is independent of unknown Hodge cycles or
postulated surjectivity.

There is also an explicit inverse witness in the genuine
rational correspondence algebra:
\[
(I+2T)\sum_{k=0}^{r-1}(-2T)^k
=I-(-2T)^r
=\bigl(1-(-2)^r\bigr)I.
\]
Since the final scalar is nonzero in $\mathbb Q$,
$I+2T$ is invertible by a finite rational word in the actual

\section{Every weight in dimensions at most three}
For a connected smooth projective $X$ of complex dimension $d$,
the original rational Hodge fibers in weights $p=0$ and $p=d$
have dimension one, with actual algebraic generators $[X]$
and a closed point $[x]$, respectively. The diagonal correspondence
$\Delta_X$ and either generator realize a one-dimensional cyclic orbit.
Weights $p>d$ have zero rational cohomology and the zero-cycle
and diagonal trivially span their zero Hodge fiber.

For $d=1$, every weight is outer. For $d=2$, the only middle
weight is $p=1$. For $d=3$, the only middle weights are
$p=1$ and $p=d-1=2$. Combining these elementary cases with
the actual correspondence of the main theorem gives:

\begin{theorem}[Full GST single-fold hypothesis in dimensions $d\le3$]
For every connected smooth projective complex variety $X$ with
$d\le3$ and every integer $p\ge0$, there exists a genuine
original-$X$ rational Chow correspondence $\Gamma\in
CH^d(X\times X)_{\mathbb Q}$ and genuine $Z\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}$
whose canonical rational Betti orbit spans $W_p(X)$.
This is an unconditional original-$X$ statement in every weight
for these dimensions. It makes no claim about the higher middle
weights of arbitrary-dimensional varieties.
\end{theorem}
