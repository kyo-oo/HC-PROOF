\[

\]
More sharply, writing
\(\chi_i(t)=\chi(t-\delta_i)\)
for the $i$th shifted characteristic polynomial and
\(\mathcal K_N=[v_N,T_Nv_N,\ldots,T_N^{12N-1}v_N]\), one has the \emph{exact rational determinant formula}
\[
 \boxed{

 \ne0.}\tag{4}
\]
In particular there is no missing N-hole information channel or original carry/trit cell in the full single-fold orbit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The single-block Krylov determinant (1) makes the map
\(q(t)\bmod\chi\longmapsto q(T_0)v_0\)
an isomorphism of rational cyclic modules $\Q[t]/\chi\to\Q^{12}$.
For the shifted block $T_0+\delta_i I$, the same seed is cyclic, and its $12\times12$ Krylov matrix has the same determinant $\Delta_{12}$ because each $(T_0+\delta_i I)^kv_0$ is a monic triangular rational combination of the vectors $T_0^jv_0$ for $j\le k$.
The full orbit map from polynomials of degree $<12N$ is the direct sum of evaluation modulo $\chi_i$, followed by the block cyclic isomorphisms.

The maximum absolute row-sum is $4$, so the standard spectral radius bound gives every complex eigenvalue $\lambda$ of $T_0$ the bound $|\lambda|\le4$. If two shifted spectra intersect, one has $\delta_i+\lambda=\delta_j+\mu$ with $|\lambda|,|\mu|\le4$, hence
\(10\le|\delta_i-\delta_j|=|\mu-\lambda|\le8\), contradiction.
Thus the shifted monic polynomials $\chi_i$ are pairwise coprime over $\Q$. Chinese remainder yields a rational isomorphism
\[
 \Q[t]/\prod_i\chi_i \;\simeq\;\bigoplus_i\Q[t]/\chi_i,
\]
so the orbit map is invertible. Its determinant is the product of the block Krylov determinants $\Delta_{12}^N$ and the determinant of the polynomial Chinese-remainder coefficient map. The latter equals the product of pairwise resultants with sign $(-1)^{12^2\binom N2}=+1$ under the stated ordered power and hole bases. This proves (4) and (3).
\end{proof}

\[
 \boxed{\det H=+1\quad\text{for EVERY }N\ge1.}\tag{6}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By definition of $\ell_N$ and $\mathcal K_N$,
\(\ell_N(T_N^k v_N)=\delta_{k,r-1}\) for $0\le k<r$.

\[
 \det H=(-1)^{r(r-1)/2}=1
\]
because $r=12N$ makes $r(r-1)/2=6N(12N-1)$ even.
\end{proof}
