\begin{abstract}
An unconditional construction is provided for the \emph{topological}
perfect-\(K\) source of every prescribed rational Hodge class on an
arbitrary smooth projective original variety. We combine it with the
previous GST all-channel rational ambient blowup and show that any
prescribed original rational Hodge class can be transported faithfully
to a middle Hodge class on a smooth projective rational variety
with \emph{arbitrarily large prescribed absolute geometric coniveau}.
Every middle rational Hodge class of the chosen rational world has
that coniveau. The topological source comes with an exact
Grothendieck--Riemann--Roch Todd correction, and its algebraizability
is equivalent to original Hodge algebraicity. These are genuine
geometric reductions and source objects; the required universal
algebraization theorem is not proved.
\end{abstract}

\section{Original target, parameters, and source provenance}
Let \(X\) be an arbitrary smooth projective complex variety of
dimension \(d\), let \(0\le p\le d\), and fix
\(\alpha\in\Hdg^p(X)_{\Q}\).
Choose any integer \(k\ge1\). Start with an actual
projective embedding \(X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^{N_0}\)
and select
\[
 M\ge \max\{d+k,\;N_0,\;p+1\}. \tag{1.1}
\]
Embed \(X\) by a linear inclusion into \(\mathbb P^{2M}\);
form the genuine rational smooth world
\[
 Y=\operatorname{Bl}_X\mathbb P^{2M},\quad
 c=2M-d,\quad
 E=\mathbb P(N_{X/\mathbb P^{2M}}),\quad
 j:E\hookrightarrow Y,\quad q:E\to X,
 \quad\xi=c_1(\mathcal O_E(1)).
\]
Its complex dimension is \(2M\); the normal rank \(c\)
is at least \(d+2k\). Define
\(r=M-p-1\ge0\), so that \(1\le r+1=M-p\le c-1\).
The canonical exceptional Hodge class is
\[
 \boxed{\beta_{\alpha}=j_*(\xi^r q^*\alpha)
       \in \Hdg^M(Y)_{\Q}.} \tag{1.2}
\]
The prescribed target remains the genuine canonical Betti
cycle-class statement on the \emph{same original} \(X\).

\section{A geometrically explicit high-coniveau support}
For a smooth projective variety \(Y\), let
\(N^kH^n(Y,\Q)\) be the geometric coniveau filtration:
the subgroup generated by classes obtained from
cohomology with supports in closed algebraic
subsets of codimension at least \(k\).
The tangent bundle of projective space is globally
generated. The exact normal sequence
\[
 0\to T_X\to T_{\mathbb P^{2M}}|_X
   \to N_{X/\mathbb P^{2M}}\to0
\]
therefore shows that \(N_{X/\mathbb P^{2M}}\)
is globally generated. With the projective bundle
of quotient lines convention, \(\mathcal O_E(1)\)
is consequently globally generated.
Take \(r\) general members of
\(|\mathcal O_E(1)|\) and let
\(i_r:T_r\hookrightarrow E\) be their complete intersection.
For \(r=0\), take \(T_0=E\).
By Bertini the complete intersection can be chosen
smooth of codimension \(r\) in \(E\), and
\([T_r]=\xi^r\). The composite map
\(u_r=j\circ i_r:T_r\to Y\) has image of
codimension \(r+1=M-p\).
Using the projection formula,
\[
 \boxed{\beta_\alpha=
   (u_r)_*((q\circ i_r)^*\alpha).} \tag{2.1}
\]
This gives \emph{actual geometric support} for
\(\beta_\alpha\), even when \(\alpha\) is not known
to be algebraic.

\begin{theorem}[Universal high-coniveau target realization]
For every \(X,p,\alpha,k\) as above, the genuine rational
middle Hodge class \(\beta_\alpha\) on the smooth
projective rational \(2M\)-fold \(Y\) satisfies
\[
 \beta_\alpha\in N^{M-p}H^{2M}(Y,\Q)
    \subseteq N^kH^{2M}(Y,\Q).
 \tag{2.2}
\]
Moreover \(\beta_\alpha\) is algebraic on \(Y\)
if and only if \(\alpha\) is algebraic on \(X\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equation (2.1) supplies a Gysin representative supported
on \(u_r(T_r)\) of codimension \(M-p\ge k\).
The two-way algebraicity statement follows from the
actual all-exceptional-channel Chern--Segre Chow/Betti
retraction proved in the preceding GST derivation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The entire middle Hodge fiber has arbitrarily high coniveau]
Fix \(X,k,M,Y\) as in (1.1). Then
\[
 \boxed{\Hdg^M(Y)_{\Q}\subseteq
        N^kH^{2M}(Y,\Q).} \tag{2.3}
\]
The algebraicity defect is nevertheless
\[
 \boxed{
  D_M(Y)\cong\bigoplus_{p=0}^{d}D_p(X),\qquad
  D_p(X)=\Hdg^p(X)_{\Q}/\im(\cl_X^p).}
 \tag{2.4}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The canonical rational Hodge blowup decomposition
at codimension \(M\) is one projective-space middle
component plus the exceptional components
\(j_*(\xi^{M-p-1}q^*\Hdg^p(X)_{\Q})\) for
\(p=0,\dots,d\). The middle projective-space
component is a multiple of the algebraic class
\(H^M\) and therefore has support in codimension
\(M\ge k\). Each exceptional component has
support in codimension \(M-p\ge M-d\ge k\)
by the previous theorem. This proves (2.3).
The Chow and Hodge blowup decompositions are
compatible with canonical cycle class, so taking
cokernels proves (2.4).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A strengthened but not solved universal Hodge reduction]
For \emph{every fixed} integer \(k\ge1\),
the full rational Hodge conjecture on all smooth
projective complex varieties is equivalent to
the algebraicity of all rational middle Hodge
classes on smooth projective rational even-dimensional
varieties \emph{which lie in} \(N^k\).
In fact it suffices to establish algebraicity for
the explicit exceptional middle classes \(\beta_\alpha\)
on the worlds constructed in (1.1).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
One implication is immediate. Conversely, given
any original \(\alpha\) choose its rational world
with \(M\ge d+k\). The class \(\beta_\alpha\)
has coniveau at least \(k\), hence is algebraic
under the restricted statement. The exact GST
Chern--Segre return gives an algebraic
representative of \(\alpha\) on \(X\).
\end{proof}
The result is not the generalized Hodge conjecture:
no assertion that coniveau alone forces algebraicity
is made. The support carries the \emph{same}
codimension-\(p\) Hodge source on a new geometry;
adding more exceptional hyperplane powers does
not lower its intrinsic Hodge complexity.

\section{An exact GST middle-world intersection detector}
There is a second completely geometric invariant on the
rational world \(Y\): the cup-product intersection matrix
of all the transplanted exceptional Hodge channels.
This is especially useful for GST's rational Gram-dual
primitive and source-detection calculations.

For \(\alpha\in\Hdg^p(X)_{\Q}\) and
\(\gamma\in\Hdg^q(X)_{\Q}\), write
\(\beta_{p,\alpha}=j_*(\xi^{M-p-1}q^*\alpha)\)
and \(\beta_{q,\gamma}=j_*(\xi^{M-q-1}q^*\gamma)\).
\begin{theorem}[Exact cross-channel GST intersection kernel]
With the Segre convention of the previous GST
all-channel return,
\[
 \boxed{
 \int_Y\beta_{p,\alpha}\smile\beta_{q,\gamma}
 =\begin{cases}
 -\int_X\alpha\smile\gamma\smile
       s_{d-p-q}(N_{X/\mathbb P^{2M}}),
       &p+q\le d,\\
 0,&p+q>d.
 \end{cases}}
 \tag{3.1}
\]
In particular, whenever \(p+q=d\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \int_Y\beta_{p,\alpha}\beta_{d-p,\gamma}
 =-\int_X\alpha\gamma.}
 \tag{3.2}
\]
If \(d=2p\), the original middle Hodge
intersection form is transported by an exact
\emph{anti-isometry} into the middle Hodge
intersection form of the smooth rational world.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The exceptional divisor self-intersection is
\(j^*j_*(v)=-\xi v\). By projection formula,
\[
 \int_Y\beta_{p,\alpha}\beta_{q,\gamma}
  =-\int_E
  \xi^{(M-p-1)+(M-q-1)+1}q^*(\alpha\gamma)
  =-\int_E\xi^{2M-p-q-1}q^*(\alpha\gamma).
\]
Since the projective fibers of \(q\) have
dimension \(c-1=2M-d-1\),
the pushforward is \(s_{d-p-q}(N)\)
if \(p+q\le d\). If \(p+q>d\),
\(\alpha\gamma\) has degree exceeding
the top cohomology of \(X\), so the
integral vanishes. In the complementary
case \(p+q=d\), \(s_0=1\). The
anti-isometry assertion follows immediately.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Global rational Gram structure.}
Order the exceptional middle summands by
\(p=0,\ldots,d\). The cross-channel matrix
has \emph{zero blocks} when \(p+q>d\)
and anti-diagonal blocks given by
the negative Poincaré cup pairings between
\(\Hdg^p(X)_{\Q}\) and
\(\Hdg^{d-p}(X)_{\Q}\).
These pairings are perfect on the
rational Hodge-class subspaces by
polarizability and semisimplicity of
pure rational Hodge structures.
Consequently the exceptional middle
intersection matrix is nondegenerate:
its determinant, after reversing block order,
depends only on the nondegenerate
anti-diagonal cup-pairing blocks.
All Segre correction terms occur
strictly on one side of this anti-diagonal
and cannot destroy its invertibility.
The ambient projective-space middle
hyperplane class is orthogonal to each
exceptional middle summand, since the
relative power of \(\xi\) stays below
the projective-bundle pushforward threshold.

\paragraph{Concrete K3--fourfold detector.}
For \(X=S\times S\), \(d=4\), \(p=2\),
take the actual primitive diagonal
\(D=\Delta-A-B-U/h^2\) from the

with \(\int_XD^2=21\).
Using \(M=5\) and
\(Y=\operatorname{Bl}_X(\mathbb P^{10})\),
the actual algebraic middle lift
\(\beta_D=j_*(\xi^2q^*D)\) satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\int_Y\beta_D^2=-21.}
 \tag{3.3}
\]
This is a genuine nonzero source-period
certificate inside a rational tenfold,
not an assumed original-\(X\) Hodge
preimage. It does not imply that
arbitrary middle Hodge classes of the
rational tenfold are algebraic.

\section{Unconditional topological \(K\)-theory source}
Write \(KU^0(E)_{\Q}=KU^0(E)\otimes\Q\).
Because \(E\) is smooth complex projective,
it is a finite CW complex, and the rational
topological Chern character is an isomorphism
\[
 \ch_{\mathrm{top}}:KU^0(E)_{\Q}
  \xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }
  \bigoplus_{a\ge0}H^{2a}(E,\Q).
 \tag{3.1}
\]
The exceptional normal line is
\(N_{E/Y}=\mathcal O_E(-1)\), with
\(c_1=-\xi\). Its Todd class is the exact
truncated rational series
\[
 \td(\mathcal O_E(-1))
  =\frac{\xi}{e^\xi-1}
  =1-\frac{\xi}{2}+\frac{\xi^2}{12}
   -\frac{\xi^4}{720}+\cdots .
 \tag{3.2}
\]
All power series are truncated at the
finite complex dimension of \(E\).
Define an \emph{explicitly determined}
rational topological virtual bundle class
\[
 \boxed{
 u_\alpha=
 \ch_{\mathrm{top}}^{-1}
 \Big(q^*\alpha\smile\xi^{M-p-1}
     \smile\td(\mathcal O_E(-1))\Big)
 \in KU^0(E)_{\Q}.} \tag{3.3}
\]
Unlike an alleged algebraic perfect complex,
the object (3.3) \emph{always exists}, with
no algebraic preimage of \(\alpha\) assumed.

Let \(j_!^{\mathrm{top}}:KU^0(E)_{\Q}
\to KU^0(Y)_{\Q}\) be the complex topological
Gysin pushforward. Topological
Grothendieck--Riemann--Roch says
\[
 \ch_Y(j_!^{\mathrm{top}}u)
  =j_*\left(\ch_E(u)
     \smile\td(N_{E/Y})^{-1}\right).
 \tag{3.4}
\]
Define \(v_\alpha=j_!^{\mathrm{top}}u_\alpha\).
Substituting (3.3) gives the \emph{exact
pure middle-degree formula}
\[
 \boxed{\ch_Y(v_\alpha)=
 j_*(q^*\alpha\,\xi^{M-p-1})
 =\beta_\alpha\quad
 \text{with all other Chern character
 degrees zero}.} \tag{3.5}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact algebraization criterion for the unconditional source]
The following statements are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item \(\alpha\) is the canonical Betti class
       of a rational algebraic codimension-\(p\)
       cycle on \(X\);
 \item \(u_\alpha\) belongs to the rational
       image of algebraic \(K_0(E)\) inside
       \(KU^0(E)_{\Q}\);
 \item \(v_\alpha\) belongs to the rational
       image of algebraic \(K_0(Y)\) inside
       \(KU^0(Y)_{\Q}\);
 \item the pure middle Hodge class
       \(\beta_\alpha\) is algebraic on \(Y\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The equivalence between (1) and (4) is
the previously proved Chern--Segre
exceptional return.
For (1) implies (2), if \(\alpha\)
has a rational Chow representative,
then \(q^*\alpha\,\xi^{M-p-1}
\td(\mathcal O_E(-1))\) is a finite sum
of rational algebraic Chow classes on \(E\).
The rational Chern character
\(K_0^{\mathrm{alg}}(E)_{\Q}
 \to\bigoplus_a\CH^a(E)_{\Q}\)
is an isomorphism for smooth projective
\(E\), so an actual rational algebraic
\(K_0\) class has the prescribed Chern
character, and by rational injectivity
of \(\ch_{\mathrm{top}}\) it equals \(u_\alpha\).
For (2) implies (1), take
the degree \(M-1\) component of its
Chern character, which is exactly
\(\xi^{M-p-1}q^*\alpha\) since
the Todd class starts in degree zero.
Multiply by \(\xi^{M-d+p}\) and push
forward along \(q\):
\[
 q_*\left(
  \xi^{M-d+p}\,
  [\ch_E(u_\alpha)]_{M-1}
  \right)
  =q_*(\xi^{2M-d-1}q^*\alpha)
  =\alpha.
 \tag{4.1}
\]
The left side is a canonical Betti class
of a rational Chow cycle because the
assumed \(K\)-class is algebraic.
The implication (2) to (3) follows
by algebraic regular-immersion pushforward
and its topological comparison.
The implication (3) to (4) follows
from (3.5): the degree-\(M\)
Chern character of an algebraic
\(K_0\) class is a rational algebraic
Chow class, and its cycle class is
\(\beta_\alpha\).
Finally (4) to (1) follows by
the original-\(X\) Chern--Segre return.
\end{proof}
The theorem gives a genuine topological
source for \emph{every} target without
circularity. The transition from that
source to an \emph{algebraic} perfect
complex is the still-unproved geometric
problem: it is precisely the Hodge
conjecture in the transported component.

\section{Exact symbolic and falsification checks}
With the formal variable \(x=\xi\), Wolfram
verified symbolically through \(x^8\)
\[
 \td(-x)=1-\tfrac{x}{2}
     +\tfrac{x^2}{12}-\tfrac{x^4}{720}
     +\tfrac{x^6}{30240}
     -\tfrac{x^8}{1209600},
\]
and the product of this series with
\((e^x-1)/x\) equals \(1\)
to the checked order. The \emph{uniform}
identity is the definition of the Todd
class, not a finite computational
extrapolation. All Chow and Betti
degrees in (3.3)--(4.1) are checked
at arbitrary \(M,d,p\).

An attempted proof asserting that every
topological Hodge \(K\)-source on a
smooth rational variety algebraizes
would \emph{already imply the full
rational Hodge conjecture} by the theorem;
rationality alone cannot be used
as a proof of that assertion. Likewise,
the high-coniveau construction is not
a shortcut to algebraicity: it
supplies support, but leaves a
degree-\(2p\) rational Hodge source
on that support.

\section{Next exact GST operation, with no unearned hypothesis}
The strongest constructive state we
now possess unconditionally is
\[
 (\alpha,X,p)\ \rightsquigarrow\
 (Y,E,T_r,\beta_\alpha,u_\alpha,v_\alpha)
\]
where \(Y\) is smooth projective rational,
\(\beta_\alpha\) is middle and supported
in arbitrarily high geometric coniveau,
and \(v_\alpha\) is a canonical
rational topological \(K\)-theory class
with \emph{pure} prescribed Chern
character \(\beta_\alpha\).
Its algebraization is the exact
required new geometry. A genuine GST
law capable of crossing the remaining
gap must build an \emph{algebraic}
perfect complex from the explicit
topological \(u_\alpha\) (or a
provably cohomologous algebraic
middle cycle) for \emph{every}
\(X,p,\alpha\), and verify the canonical
Betti comparison. Neither perfect
complex existence nor middle-class
algebraicity is asserted here.
