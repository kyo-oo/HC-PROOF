\section{Route A: the prescribed alpha may have an ISOLATED Hodge locus}
For tau=u+i v, v>0, let E_tau=C/(Z+tau Z) and
X_tau=E_tau x E_i. Every X_tau is a GENUINE smooth projective
abelian surface. Under the fixed integral T4 coordinates
(x1,y1,x2,y2), define the ORIGINAL prescribed class
\[
\boxed{\alpha=dx_1\wedge dy_2+dx_2\wedge dy_1
\in H^2(T^4,\mathbb Z).}
\]
On X_i this is, up to a sign, the cross component of the
ACTUAL algebraic diagonal divisor minus the two coordinate divisors.
It is a genuine original-X rational Hodge class.

The complex structure on the first real tangent pair is
\[
J_\tau=\begin{pmatrix}
-u/v & -(u^2+v^2)/v\\
1/v & u/v
\end{pmatrix};
\quad J_\tau^2=-I.
\]
The second factor has J_i=((0,-1),(1,0)).
For a real alternating 2-form matrix A, Hodge type (1,1)
is equivalent to J^t A J=A, J=J_tau directsum J_i.
For the EXACT prescribed alpha, two entries of the
J-invariance defect are -u/v and 1/v-1.
All entries vanish at u=0,v=1. Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\alpha\text{ remains type }(1,1)\text{ on }X_{u+iv}
\iff u=0,\ v=1.}\tag{1}
\]
The full matrix and all-real-parameter equivalence were independently
checked by exact Wolfram Reduce. The infinitesimal defect at the
original point has rank TWO on real (u,v) tangent directions.

This refutes automatic Hodge preservation by cardinally many
different projective complex structures, but does NOT refute Hodge:
alpha has a genuine diagonal-derived Chow representative on X_i.

\section{Route C: formal infinite algebraic-cycle lift fails at order ONE}
Take the genuine flat projective family
\[
\mathcal Y=\{xy=s z^2\}\subset\mathbb P^2\times A^1_s.
\]
At s=0 the fiber consists of two ACTUAL lines Lx and Ly,
each of ambient degree one. Every nonzero-s fiber is a
smooth irreducible conic of degree two.
To deform Lx as a degree-one component over the FIRST
infinitesimal base s=epsilon, epsilon^2=0,
one needs a factorization
\[
xy-\epsilon z^2
=(x+\epsilon L)(y+\epsilon M)\pmod{\epsilon^2}.
\]
Comparing first-order coefficients yields
\[
\boxed{yL+xM=-z^2.}\tag{5}
\]
This has no solution: yL+xM lies in (x,y), but z^2
does not. Evaluation at [x:y:z]=[0:0:1] gives
0=-1. An independent degree argument says
1-dimensional components of any smooth irreducible
conic are multiples of the whole degree-two curve,
so a flat degree-one component cannot persist.
Wolfram verified the exact six linear equations,
including the impossible z^2 coefficient 1.
There is no formal infinite lifting sequence because
the first link already fails. This concerns a
specific degeneration, not the universal Hodge conjecture.

\section{Positive route: ONE very-general target-bearing Hodge world suffices}
Fix any smooth projective family pi:Y->B over
irreducible finite-type complex algebraic B,
a GLOBAL FLAT rational class alpha in R^{2p}pi_*Q,
and any original fiber X_b0.

\begin{theorem}[Very-general single-source specialization]
There exists a countable union N_alpha of proper
Zariski-closed subsets of B such that if there
is ONE point b outside N_alpha and ONE
ACTUAL rational Chow cycle Z_b on X_b with
canonical class alpha_b, then the SAME
original X_b0 has an ACTUAL rational Chow
cycle representing alpha_b0. No uniform
degree or denominator bound and no uncountable
source-bearing set are required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An actual rational Chow cycle can be expressed as
(P-Q)/m for two effective cycles of fixed integral
projective degrees r,s and positive denominator m.
There are countably many triples (m,r,s).
For each triple, the projective finite-type relative
Chow pair parameter C_{r,s}->B has finitely many
connected components; the canonical Betti cycle
class is locally constant along each connected
component relative to the Gauss-Manin local system.
Its components whose difference class is m alpha
have Zariski-closed images in B by properness.
Thus the algebraicity locus of alpha is a
COUNTABLE union of closed subsets of B.
Take N_alpha to be the union of those images that
are PROPER subsets of B. If b avoids N_alpha
but is source-bearing, its good component's
image is ALL of B. A point over b0 supplies an
actual original-fiber pair (P0,Q0), whose
rational Chow difference has class alpha_b0.
\end{proof}

This is a classical spread/relative Chow consequence,
not a claim of historical novelty. One can instead
start with ONE actual rational Chow cycle on the
GEOMETRIC GENERIC fiber, spread its finite components
over an open base and use proper Chow specialization
to return to the original b0.
For the owner's cardinal programme, the correct base
is a chosen HODGE LOCUS of the prescribed alpha,
rather than an arbitrary collection of projective fibers.
The Cattani--Deligne--Kaplan theorem
(1995, arXiv:alg-geom/9402009) supplies the algebraicity
of the Hodge locus, but does not manufacture
an algebraic cycle over its generic point.
