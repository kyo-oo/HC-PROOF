\begin{abstract}
We attack the FIRST unproved arrow in the exact arbitrary-X,
prescribed-rational-Hodge-class GST construction rather than
relabel a fixed source or another descent equivalence as a proof.
We prove (A) an all-dimension actual affine-fold tower theorem:
a genuinely convergent sequence of CANONICAL Betti classes of
algebraic cycles on ANY varying GST affine worlds yields a
FINITE explicit rational Chow cycle on the original X,
with NO uniform complexity, degree, denominator, mass,
or ambient-dimensional bounds. Thus a mathematically
established infinite-dimensional spatial-source convergence
would suffice for the original rational Hodge target.
We prove a quantitative uniform rational-annihilator
separation theorem characterizing the precise obstruction
to such convergence. We then construct (B) an exactly
alpha-dependent high-positive-twist Chern geometry
on ORIGINAL X with precisely TWO nonzero
degree-p terms; all other Chern contributions cancel
by an algebraic identity for EVERY twist integer.
Its actual smooth determinantal cycle difference
has EXACT canonical Betti target alpha.
Finally we compute the sharp necessary Kähler-angle
error budget for an asymptotically holomorphic
source family to yield an integral calibrated return.
A usual square-root angular estimate with mass
growing as k^p only yields defect O(k^(p-1)),
which does NOT force zero defect.
The proved results identify the valid all-N
experimental-to-mathematical extraction mechanism
and a specific geometric estimate to improve,
without silently assuming algebraic source birth.
\end{abstract}

\section{The exact flat-period no-approximation barrier}

   \quad\forall Z\in CH^p(X)_\mathbb Q.

      \mathrm{cl}^{can}_{J_n}(W)

\begin{proof}

\section{New alpha-dependent amplified spatial geometry on original X}
Let L be an actual ample
line bundle on original X,
$h=c_1(L)$, and fix the
prescribed rational class
alpha. Rational topological
K-theory provides m>0,
r≥p and an honest
complex topological bundle
E_alpha of rank r with
\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{ch}(E_\alpha)
     =r+m\alpha
 \ \text{with all other
 positive Chern-character
 components zero.}
 }                                                     \tag{9}
\]
Consequently its rational
Chern classes obey
$c_j(E_\alpha)=0$ for 1≤j<p
and
$c_p(E_\alpha)=
  (-1)^{p-1}(p-1)!m\alpha$.

\begin{theorem}[EXACT two-term high-positive-twist Chern law for all X, alpha and twist k]
For EVERY integer k≥0,
let $E_{\alpha,k}=
E_\alpha\otimes L^{\otimes k}$,
and let
$B_k=(L^{\otimes k})^{\oplus r}$
be the honest algebraic
reference bundle.
Their true degree-p
Chern classes satisfy the
EXACT rational Betti identity
\[
 \boxed{
 c_p(E_{\alpha,k})
  ={r\choose p}k^p h^p
      +(-1)^{p-1}(p-1)!m\alpha,
 \qquad
 c_p(B_k)={r\choose p}k^p h^p .
 }                                                     \tag{10}
\]
Thus their difference is
TARGET alpha and is
independent of k:
\[
 \boxed{
  {(-1)^{p-1}\over m(p-1)!}
      \big(c_p(E_{\alpha,k})-
               c_p(B_k)\big)=\alpha
       \quad \forall k\ge0.
 }                                                     \tag{11}
\]
Actual generic smooth
rank-degeneracy loci of
the two genuine original-X
bundles provide signed
REAL integral geometric
cycles with exactly this
original-X class for EVERY k.
No original-X Chow preimage
of alpha enters (9)--(11).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By the splitting principle
write x_i for the roots of E.
The roots of E tensor L^k
are x_i+kh. Thus
\[
 c_p(E\otimes L^k)
  =\sum_{j=0}^p
    {r-j\choose p-j}
     (kh)^{p-j}c_j(E).
                                                               \tag{12}
\]
Since c_0(E)=1 and
all c_j(E)=0 for
1≤j<p rationally,
only j=0 and j=p
survive, giving (10).
The reference bundle
has all r roots kh,
giving binom(r,p)(kh)^p.
Subtract and normalize
to obtain (11).
Topological Thom--Porteous
gives smooth REAL
degeneracy representatives.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact nonabelian integrability invariance under arbitrary ample twist}
An arbitrarily large ample twist can
change geometry and make actual
SMOOTH determinant sections
approximately complex,
but cannot remove
the original integrability
obstruction of the bundle.

\begin{theorem}[ALL-X holomorphic-integrability iff under every ample twist]
Let E be ANY honest smooth
complex topological bundle
on projective X and L an
actual holomorphic ample
line bundle. For EVERY
integer k, positive,
zero or negative,
\[
 \boxed{
 E\text{ admits an integrable
 holomorphic bundle structure}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 E\otimes L^k\text{ admits one}.
 }                                                     \tag{15}
\]
For a specified smooth
(0,1)-connection D_E,
the natural tensor
(0,1)-connection satisfies
the IDENTICAL curvature law
\[
 \boxed{
 F^{0,2}_{D_E\otimes
             \bar\partial_{L^k}}
       =F^{0,2}_{D_E}
                   \otimes\mathrm{id}_{L^k}.
 }                                                     \tag{16}
\]
Thus mere large k does not
force an originally
nonintegrable fixed
D_E to become integrable,
even though its
positive ambient Chern
term in equation (10)
grows like k^p.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A genuine integrable
holomorphic structure
on E tensors with
the given holomorphic
structure on L^k.
Conversely an integrable
holomorphic structure
on the SMOOTH tensor
bundle E tensor L^k
tensors with the actual
holomorphic line L^{-k},
producing an integrable
holomorphic structure
on the original smooth E.
The (0,2)-curvature
of a tensor product
(0,1) connection is
the sum of the two
(0,2) curvatures,
and the holomorphic
line L^k has curvature
component F^{0,2}=0.
Equation (16) follows.
\end{proof}

\section{The needed ANGLE precision is much stronger than qualitative tangency}
Let D_k be any family
of integral real
codimension-p geometric
cycles on original X,
such as generic
determinantal loci from
the preceding theorem.
Let theta_k be a
measurable maximum
Kähler-angle distance

oriented tangent

Grassmannian, with
theta_k small.
The pointwise
Wirtinger deficit
satisfies
\[
 0\le 1-\varphi(T_xD_k)
        \le C\,\theta_k^2
 \qquad\text{for
 sufficiently small angles}.
\]
Thus
\[
 \boxed{
 \Delta_\omega(D_k)
 \le C\,\theta_k^2\,{\bf M}(D_k).
 }                                                     \tag{13}
\]
If a geometric
construction supplies
M(D_k)=O(k^p), then
theta_k=O(k^{-1/2})
only yields
$\Delta(D_k)=O(k^{p-1})$.
For p≥2 this does NOT
force Delta(D_k)→0;
for p=1 it gives at
best a bounded defect.
To guarantee
vanishing defect
by this estimate
alone one needs
\[
 \boxed{
 \theta_k=o(k^{-p/2})
   \text{ together with }
 {\bf M}(D_k)=O(k^p).
 }                                                     \tag{14}
\]
This is a deterministic
GEOMETRIC ERROR-BUDGET
calculation, not a claim
that a particular
approximately holomorphic
construction attains
the quoted scalings.
It identifies why
increasing the positive
twist/complex dimension
cannot be used as a
proof merely from
angles tending to zero:
an increasing
geometric mass can
outweigh the
pointwise improvement.
To recover the fixed
original-X signed alpha
in (11) through
two calibrated cycles,
one must construct
a genuinely stronger
joint bound than
the general estimate.
