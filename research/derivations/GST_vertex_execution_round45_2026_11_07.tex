\section{The Pairing-Support Wall}

\begin{definition}[Pairing-structured support]
A degree-4 class is \emph{pairing-supported} if its word support lies in the span of the
six double words (the unions of full conjugation pairs).
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[The character closure of the graph algebra]\label{lem:characters}
Let $x_i = \sigma_i(a)\bar\sigma_i(a)$ be the four embeddings of $a\bar a \in K^+$
(the totally real subfield).  The doubles' characters are the monomials
$\chi_{\{i,j\}} = x_i x_j$; the six monomials of degree 2 in four variables are
linearly independent functions on $K^+ \otimes \mathbb{Q}$, and the diagonal algebra's
action on the divisor ring closes on exactly the six-dimensional double invariant space:
\[
\mathrm{span}\bigl\{D_v\,c : v \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}^8,\ c \in \wedge^2\mathrm{NS}\bigr\}
\;=\; \wedge^2\mathrm{NS}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The double $\{i,j\}$ carries $\chi_{\{i,j\}}(a) = x_ix_j$ with $x$ ranging over
$K^+\otimes\mathbb{Q} \cong \mathbb{Q}^4$ (the four embeddings are independent characters).
The six degree-2 monomials $x_ix_j$ are linearly independent as functions on
$\mathbb{Q}^4$ (evaluate on the standard basis and its pairwise sums).  Hence the
reweightings $D_v$ generate, from any class with full double support, the whole
six-dimensional space of double-supported systems --- which is $\wedge^2\mathrm{NS}$
itself (the ring is closed).  Conversely every $D_vc$ is double-supported: the diagonal
operators preserve word support.
\end{proof}
