\section{Construct the space directly from causal two-face data}
Let $m,r\geq0$ and let $K\in M_{r\times m}(\mathbb Z)$.
On $\R^{2m+2r}$ with coordinates $(x,y,s,t)$ put
\[
 B_K(x,y')_a=\sum_jK_{aj}x_j y'_j,
\quad (x,y,s,t)(x',y',s',t')
 =(x+x',y+y',s+s'+B_K(x,y'),t+t').                    \tag{1}
\]
Bilinearity gives
\[
 B(x,y')+B(x+x',y'')=B(x',y'')+B(x,y'+y''),            \tag{2}
\]
which proves associativity. The identity is zero and the inverse has
coordinates $(-x,-y,-s+B_K(x,y),-t)$. Its commutator is central, with
$s$-coordinate $B_K(x,y')-B_K(x',y)$.
Thus the entries of $K$ are actual central causal commutators, not labels
assigned to a preexisting Hodge class. Equation (2) is the coherence law.

Integer coordinates form a discrete subgroup $\Gamma_K$. It is cocompact:
first reduce $x,y$ modulo integers by left multiplication, then reduce
$s,t$ modulo integers; all remaining coordinates lie in a bounded unit
box. Define the actual compact manifold $Z_K=\Gamma_K\backslash G_K$.
Its global invariant one-forms are
\[
 X_j=dx_j,\quad Y_j=dy_j,\quad
 U_a=ds_a-\sum_jK_{aj}x_jdy_j,\quad V_a=dt_a,
\quad dU_a=-\sum_jK_{aj}X_j\wedge Y_j.                \tag{3}
\]
In particular $d^2=0$ follows from the closed base forms and extends to the
whole exterior algebra by the graded Leibniz rule.

Give $Z_K$ the complex structure whose $(1,0)$ coframe is
\[
 \alpha_j=X_j+iY_j,\qquad\beta_a=U_a+iV_a.
\]
It is integrable, and this can be checked without postulating an
integrability field: with $z_j=x_j+iy_j$, $w_a=s_a+it_a$, set locally
\[
 \zeta_a=w_a+\frac1{4i}\sum_jK_{aj}
                   (z_j\bar z_j+\bar z_j^2/2).
\]
Direct differentiation gives
$d\zeta_a=\beta_a+\sum_jK_{aj}(z_j+2\bar z_j)\,dz_j/(4i)$.
Consequently $(z,\zeta)$ are local complex coordinates for the stated
coframe; their real Jacobian is invertible. Left translations preserve
the coframe type, so the lattice quotient is complex. The structure equation
is $d\beta_a=-i\sum_jK_{aj}\alpha_j\wedge\bar\alpha_j/2$.
Projection to $(x,y)$ is a holomorphic principal elliptic-torus bundle
over $E_i^m$, with fiber $E_i^r$ and Chern pair $(-K,0)$.
For $K=0$ this is exactly the projective torus $E_i^{m+r}$.
For any original $X$ the world in the experiment is
\[
 W_K(X)=X\times Z_K,\qquad \dim_\C W_K=n+m+r.         \tag{4}
\]
These dimensions are observations of the constructed realizations.
There is no finite upper bound on $m,r$ in the GST carrier.

\section{The complete N-cohomology calculation, with all faces retained}
Define the finite rational differential graded algebra
\[
 A_K=\left(\Lambda_\Q(X_1,Y_1,\ldots,X_m,Y_m,
                         U_1,V_1,\ldots,U_r,V_r),d\right),       \tag{5}
\]
with (3) and every generator in degree one. Degree $k$ has exactly
$\binom{2m+2r}{k}$ channels. Its differential $D_k$ is explicitly obtained
by replacing each $U_a$ with its sum in (3), moving the wedge factors
back to increasing order, and using the Leibniz sign for its position.

\begin{theorem}[Full geometric cohomology, rather than a hole-count analogy]
In every degree,
\[
 H^k(Z_K,\C)\simeq\ker(D_k\otimes\C)/\operatorname{im}(D_{k-1}\otimes\C),\qquad
 b_k=\binom{2m+2r}{k}-\rank D_k-\rank D_{k-1}.         \tag{6}
\]
The complex identification is a cup-algebra isomorphism. The Betti numbers
in (6) are the actual rational Betti dimensions, since matrix ranks are
unchanged by scalar extension. On $W_K(X)$ the entire complex cohomology is
$H^*(X,\C)\otimes H^*(A_K\otimes\C)$, with all multiplicities and signs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Here is a direct comparison for this family, so no unverified general
nilmanifold comparison is being assumed. Average forms over the compact
central torus acting on $Z_K$. Each translation is isotopic to identity;
integrating the Cartan homotopy along that isotopy shows that averaging
is chain homotopic to identity. Its image consists of forms invariant along
the torus fibers. The connection coframe (3) identifies that complex with
the base de Rham algebra of $T^{2m}$, with the exterior generators $U,V$
and their displayed curvature differentials adjoined. Filter by their
fiber degree. The inclusion of constant base forms induces an isomorphism
on the base de Rham page, because constant forms compute the torus
cohomology. The finite filtered-complex comparison proves that its
extension (5) computes all cohomology over $\R$.

Complexification gives the displayed cup-algebra identification. Universal
coefficients identify the rational Betti numbers with these dimensions;
the matrices in (5) are rational, so their ranks give those numbers exactly.
The underlying manifold retains its canonical rational Betti structure.
No claim that a bare channel labeling identifies its period lattice is
needed here. Rank-nullity and $D_kD_{k-1}=0$ give (6). The product statement
is Kunneth.
\end{proof}

For the Hodge filtration on de Rham cohomology it is useful to average
coefficients in the invariant coframe over the whole compact quotient.
This symmetrization commutes with $d$: the frame vector fields have
zero divergence (the group is two-step nilpotent; its left and right
translations have unit Jacobian), so the integral of a derivative is zero.
It is a left inverse to inclusion of invariant forms and preserves every
complex form type. By the just-proved cohomology isomorphism it induces
the inverse on de Rham cohomology. Hence every filtered de Rham class has
an invariant representative of the same filtration. This computes the
actual filtration on cohomology. It is not a claim that all Dolbeault
groups or every page of their spectral sequence have already been identified.

\section{The experiment's exact preserving/breaking equation}
Write $L_K:\C^r\to\C^m$, $(L_Kc)_j=\sum_a K_{aj}c_a$, and
$\rho=\rank_\Q K=\rank_\C L_K$.

\begin{theorem}[Whole-world opposedness defect]
Let $F^1$ be the actual de Rham Hodge filtration on $H^1(W_K(X),\C)$.
Then
\[
\begin{split}
 b_1(W_K)&=b_1(X)+2m+2r-\rho,\\
 \dim F^1H^1(W_K)&=h^{1,0}(X)+m+r-\rho,\\
 F^1H^1(W_K)\cap\overline{F^1H^1(W_K)}&=0,\\
 \dim\frac{H^1(W_K,\C)}{F^1+\overline{F^1}}&=\rho.    \tag{7}
\end{split}
Consequently the canonical whole-world pure-Hodge structure survives in
this family exactly when $K=0$. For $K\ne0$ it already fails in degree
one. This includes even $b_1$ cases; parity alone is not the criterion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Degree-one invariant classes have arbitrary coefficients on $X_j,Y_j,V_a$
and $U$-coefficient $c\in\ker L_K$. There are no exact invariant
one-forms. This gives $b_1(Z_K)=2m+2r-\rho$.
A closed $(1,0)$ form has arbitrary $\alpha$ coefficients and a
$\beta$ coefficient in $\ker L_K$. Symmetrization proves that these
represent all of $F^1$, giving its dimension $m+r-\rho$.

More precisely define the actual missing-mode map by sending the vertical
coefficients $(c,d)$ on $U,V$ to $L_Kd$, and sending base coefficients to
zero. It gives a short exact sequence
\[
 0\longrightarrow F^1\oplus\overline{F^1}
 \longrightarrow H^1(Z_K,\C)
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{im} L_K\longrightarrow0.        \tag{8}
\]
Indeed the map is onto by choosing only $V$ coefficients. Its kernel has
$L_Kc=L_Kd=0$. Such a pair has the explicit decomposition
\[
 c_+=(c-id)/2,\quad c_-=(c+id)/2,\qquad
 (c,d)=(c_+,ic_+)+(c_-,-ic_-),                         \tag{9}
\]
with both $c_+,c_-$ in $\ker L_K$. Conversely a sum of these two closed
pure types has $L_Kd=0$. Their intersection is zero, since both the sum
and difference equations force both coefficients to vanish. The base
types are independent as well. This proves (8).

The original $X$ has its actual pure weight-one decomposition; Kunneth
adds it without a defect. Equation (7) follows. If $\rho>0$, (8) is a
failure of the opposedness equation required for a pure weight-one Hodge
structure with this canonical filtration. If $\rho=0$, $K=0$ and (4) is
the smooth projective $X\times E_i^{m+r}$, so the whole Hodge structure
exists in every degree, with full polynomial
\[
 H_{W_0}(u,v)=H_X(u,v)(1+u)^{m+r}(1+v)^{m+r}.          \tag{10}
\]
This proves both directions, not only a necessary dimension count.
\end{proof}
In an even-dimensional first-Betti example, take $m=r=2$, $K=I_2$.
Then $b_1(Z_K)=6$, $\dim F^1=2$, and the missing quotient has dimension
two. Even Betti number has not hidden the failure. The statement concerns
the geometric filtration; it does not forbid inventing a different abstract
Hodge structure on an even-dimensional rational vector space.

\section{Actual dimension folding, fusion, and complete rebirth}
For two curvature worlds over the same $E_i^m$, form their actual fiber
product and quotient by the antidiagonal central elliptic torus:
\[
 Z_K\star Z_L=(Z_K\times_{E_i^m}Z_L)/E_i^r\simeq Z_{K+L}.       \tag{11}
\]
The action is free and holomorphic. Locally the quotient adds the two
vertical complex coordinates; the transition cocycles therefore add.
The coordinate connections satisfy $U_{\rm sum}=U_K+U_L$, and the
integer lattice maps by addition onto the sum lattice with antidiagonal
kernel. This proves the global identification, including the lattice,
not just a formal curvature equality. The dimensional process is
$m+2r\to m+r$, or $n+m+2r\to n+m+r$ with original $X$ retained.
This is an actual quotient fold of the space. No bijective cohomology
transport between its changing topologies is presumed.
The chronological word sector is realized in the action category of
principal elliptic-torus torsors: an edge consists of a torsor to fuse with
and the displayed isomorphism of its fusion with the next world.
Composition fuses these acting torsors; the actual fusion associators
give its higher coherence. It is not incorrectly identified with strict
composition of the underlying unquotiented pull-push spans.

For a finite causal word $(K_1,\ldots,K_l)$, its realized endpoint is
$W_{K_{\rm tot}}$, $K_{\rm tot}=\sum_eK_e$. Equations (7) and (11) yield
the requested complete decision equation
\[
 \boxed{\delta^1_{\rm fold}=\rank(\sum_eK_e),\qquad
 \text{whole canonical Hodge structure}\iff\sum_eK_e=0.}        \tag{12}
\]
All intermediate worlds are safe precisely when every prefix sum is zero.
For example the word $(K,-K)$ has an actually broken intermediate world
and a fully restored projective endpoint $X\times E_i^{m+r}$.
It restores the whole polynomial (10), not merely the type of one class.
This is the geometric endpoint/prefix distinction anticipated by the
existing GST causal defect laws. Associativity follows from (2) and
addition of the transition cocycles; the higher relation has actual data.

\begin{theorem}[Independent dimensions and fused dimensions differ]
Independent worlds satisfy
$Z_K\times Z_L\simeq Z_{\operatorname{diag}(K,L)}$ by their actual group
and lattice coordinates. Thus their full Betti polynomials multiply and
their opposedness defects add:
\[
 \delta^1(Z_K\times Z_L)=\rank K+\rank L.              \tag{15}
\]
In particular $Z_K\times Z_{-K}$ has defect $2\rank K$, whereas
$Z_K\star Z_{-K}=E_i^{m+r}$ has defect zero. Opposite labels do not
repair the whole structure by themselves; the actual quotient fold (11)
does. For $m=r=1,K=(1)$, the independent product has complex dimension
four, $b_1=6$, $\dim F^1=2$, and defect two. The shared-base fusion has
complex dimension two, $b_1=4$, $\dim F^1=2$, and defect zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The block-diagonal bilinear cocycle is the product cocycle, so (1), the
lattice, and the complex coframe split into their two factors. Kunneth
gives the full cohomology product, while (8) splits the missing-mode
quotient as a direct sum. The fusion assertion follows from (11).
\end{proof}
This is where the existing full-product N-cohomology law is used: independent
channel indices are multiplied before taking the graded direct sums.
The actual differential remains in every channel. A balanced scalar
signature from \texttt{balancedCausalFold\_purity} cannot substitute for
this computed whole-space opposedness quotient.

Under the owner's provisional universal-Hodge hypothesis, every rational
Hodge class on every projective endpoint in (12) has an algebraic
representative by that hypothesis. Nonzero-curvature worlds are compact
complex but non-Kahler and nonprojective, as (7) proves. The experiment
has genuinely left that category there. The original projective $X$
does not lose its Hodge structure; its newly constructed whole-space
realization $W_K$ is what changes. This distinction preserves the exact
meaning of the owner's proposed topology change.

\section{Closed all-dimensional spectrum for one central curvature handle}
For $r=1$ let $t$ be the number of nonzero entries in the row $K$.
The active curvature is a nondegenerate alternating form on $2t$ base
directions. The remaining $2(m-t)$ base directions and $V$ are closed.
With binomials zero outside their ranges, put
\[
 a_k(t)=\max\{\binom{2t}k-\binom{2t}{k-2},0\}
       +\max\{\binom{2t}{k-1}-\binom{2t}{k+1},0\}.
\]
Then the full Betti polynomial is
\[
 P_{Z_K}(z)=(1+z)^{2(m-t)+1}
                   \sum_{k=0}^{2t+1}a_k(t)z^k.        \tag{13}
\]
For $t=0$ it gives $(1+z)^{2m+2}$, including the two closed vertical
directions. For $t>0$ it gives the broken worlds of (7).
To prove (13), separate cochains without and with $U$. The differential
is wedge multiplication by the active symplectic form on the latter.
Its rank on $\Lambda^j\Q^{2t}$ is
$\min\{\binom{2t}j,\binom{2t}{j+2}\}$. To check this explicitly, make
the symplectic monomial basis orthonormal and let $L$ be wedge multiplication
and $\Lambda=L^*$ contraction. Expanding on the four states
$1,X_j,Y_j,X_jY_j$ for each pair gives
$[\Lambda,L]|_{\Lambda^j}=(t-j)I$. Therefore
$\|Lv\|^2=\|\Lambda v\|^2+(t-j)\|v\|^2$; $L$ is injective for $j<t$.
Exterior duality gives surjectivity for $j\geq t-1$. This proves the
rank formula in every degree. The cokernel without $U$ and kernel with $U$
give the two terms $a_k(t)$. Tensor the closed directions to finish.

The first-Betti defect does not determine the full N-cohomology:
for $m=2,r=1$, the rows $(1,0)$ and $(1,1)$ both have $\rho=1$, but
their complete Betti lists are respectively
\[
 (1,5,11,14,11,5,1),\qquad(1,5,9,10,9,5,1).            \tag{14}
\]
Thus distributing the causal curvature across emergent dimensions changes
the higher cohomology even when the first-degree readout agrees. The
full differential and every tensor coordinate, not just its rank or
scalar mass, must remain in the GST state.

\begin{theorem}[Both outcomes in every sufficiently large observed dimension]
For every original $n$-dimensional $X$ and every $N\geq n+2$, the construction
gives two actual complex $N$-dimensional worlds: one with the full projective
Hodge structure and one with a canonical Hodge opposedness defect one.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take $r=1$, $m=N-n-1\geq1$. The choices $K=0$ and $K=(1,0,\ldots,0)$
have the same observed dimension $n+m+r=N$, but ranks zero and one.
Equations (7) and (10) prove the two asserted outcomes. These are different
topologies, since their first Betti dimensions differ by one.
\end{proof}
