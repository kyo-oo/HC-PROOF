\section{The pure realization ladder}
\begin{theorem}[Pure realization ladder]\label{thm:A}
Let $X$ be a smooth projective complex variety of dimension $d\ge 1$,
let $1\le p\le d$, and let
$\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)$ be ARBITRARY (no Hodge condition).
Then there exist explicit positive integers $m_0$ and $r_0$, with
\[
  r_0:=\max(p+1,\;d+1),
\]
such that for every $m\equiv 0 \pmod {m_0}$ and every $r\ge r_0$
there exists an exact complex topological vector bundle $E$ of rank $r$
on $X$ with
\[
  \boxed{\;
  \ch_j(E)=0\quad\text{for all }j\notin\{0,p\},\; 1\le j\le d,
  \qquad
  \ch_p(E)=m\alpha.\;}
\]
Here $m_0$ is the integrality scale of the K-theoretic preimage of
$\alpha$ (Step 2 below), computable from any integral basis of
$\widetilde K^0(X)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The proof has four steps.

\smallskip
\emph{Step 1 (Rational K-realization).}
A smooth projective complex variety of dimension $d$ has the homotopy
type of a finite CW complex of dimension $\le 2d$.
By the theorem of Atiyah--Hirzebruch, for every finite CW complex the
rationalized Chern character
\[
  \ch\otimes\mathbb Q:\;K^0(X)\otimes\mathbb Q
  \;\longrightarrow\;
  H^{\mathrm{even}}(X,\mathbb Q)
\]
is an isomorphism of rings. The mechanism is the Atiyah--Hirzebruch
spectral sequence
$E_2^{s,t}=H^s(X,K^t(\mathrm{pt}))\Rightarrow K^{s+t}(X)$:
each differential is torsion-valued (the first one, $d_3$, is the
cohomology operation $Sq^3$ on the torsion-free integral lattice, and
all higher differentials are of finite order), so after tensoring with
$\mathbb Q$ the spectral sequence collapses at $E_2$, and all
extension problems vanish rationally because the target is a
$\mathbb Q$-vector space.
Let $u\in H^{\mathrm{even}}(X,\mathbb Q)$ be the class whose degree-$2p$
component is $\alpha$ and whose components in all other positive degrees
are zero, and let
\[
  v:=(\ch\otimes\mathbb Q)^{-1}(u)\;\in\;\widetilde K^0(X)\otimes\mathbb Q,
  \qquad \operatorname{rank}(v)=\ch_0(v)=0 .
\]
Then $\ch(v)=u$, i.e.\ $v$ is a rational reduced K-class with a single
prescribed Chern-character grade.

\smallskip
\emph{Step 2 (Integrality scale).}
$K^0(X)$ is a finitely generated abelian group, hence a lattice inside
$K^0(X)\otimes\mathbb Q$. Therefore $v=w/N_0$ for some integral class
$w\in\widetilde K^0(X)$ and some positive integer $N_0$; set
\[
  m_0:=N_0 .
\]
For every $m\equiv 0 \pmod {m_0}$ the class
\[
  z_m:=m\,v=(m/N_0)\,w\;\in\;\widetilde K^0(X)
\]
is an INTEGRAL rank-zero virtual class, and by construction
\[
  \ch(z_m)=m\,u,\qquad\text{i.e.}\quad
  \ch_j(z_m)=0\;(j\neq 0,p),\quad \ch_p(z_m)=m\alpha ,
\]
a pure class.
Note that NO further Newton-type integrality conditions on $m$ arise
here. Writing a virtual difference $z=E-F$ of exact bundles, its
total Chern class is
$c(z)=c(E)c(F)^{-1}=c(E)\,(1+x)^{-1}$ where $x=c(F)-1$ is the
positive-degree part of $c(F)$; the truncated geometric series
\[
  (1+x)^{-1}=1-x+x^2-x^3+\cdots
\]
is a finite integer polynomial in $x$ (it terminates in degrees $>2d$),
so every denominator that a naive componentwise inversion of Newton
identities would display is subsumed by the single fact that
$z_m$ lies in the K-lattice. All higher-Chern denominators are carried
by the K-lattice integrality, not by $m$.

\smallskip
\emph{Step 3 (Exactness in the stable range).}
By Bott periodicity, for every $r\ge 1$ the stabilization map
\[
  BU(r)\longrightarrow BU
\]
is $(2r-1)$-connected.
Suppose $r\ge d+1$. Then
\[
  \dim_{\mathbb R}X\le 2d<2r-1,
\]
so the homotopy fiber of $BU(r)\to BU$ is $(2r-2)$-connected and all
obstruction groups to lifting a map $X\to BU$ through $BU(r)$
(these live in $H^{i+1}(X;\pi_i(\mathrm{fiber}))$ for
$i\le 2d-1\le 2r-3$) vanish. Since
$\widetilde K^0(X)=[X,BU]$, every $z_m\in\widetilde K^0(X)$ lifts through
$BU(r)$: there exists an exact rank-$r$ complex topological bundle $E$
with
\[
  [E]-r[1]=z_m\quad\text{in }\widetilde K^0(X).
\]
The lift is available at every single rank $r\ge d+1$; in particular
at $r=r_0$. For every $r>r_0$ one may equivalently stabilize: writing
$E_{r_0}$ for the carrier at $r_0$, the exact bundle
$E_{r_0}\oplus \underline{\mathbb C}^{\oplus(r-r_0)}$ has rank $r$ and
the same reduced class $z_m$.

\smallskip
\emph{Step 4 (Purity).}
For the exact $E$ of Step 3,
\[
  \ch(E)=\ch(r[1]+z_m)=r+\ch(z_m)=r+m\alpha ,
\]
so $\ch_0(E)=r$, $\ch_p(E)=m\alpha$, and every other grade $j$ with
$1\le j\le d$, $j\neq p$, vanishes.
Grades $j>d$ vanish automatically because
$H^{2j}(X,\mathbb Q)=0$ for $2j>2d$ on a $d$-dimensional variety.
The carrier is exact, of prescribed rank $r\ge r_0$, and its Chern
character is exactly the prescribed pure one. \qed
\end{proof}

\section{The sign-conductor class-firewall}
The October 22/24 anti-deck mechanism assembles a signed sum of
component classes with freely chosen per-component signs. The
following exact lemma shows that this mechanism is a re-signing device
on a FIXED algebraic class skeleton, never a birth route.

\begin{lemma}[Sign-conductor class-firewall]\label{lem:B1}
Let $D_1,D_2$ be actual effective divisors on $X$ with line-bundle
classes $[L_1],[L_2]$, let
$D_1\cap D_2=\bigcup_j C_j$ be pure of expected codimension $2$ with
distinct components $C_j$ and fundamental-cycle multiplicities
$\nu_j\ge 1$ (so that, by purity with no excess contribution, the
refined Gysin product is the fundamental cycle
$[D_1]\cdot[D_2]=\sum_j\nu_j[C_j]$ in $\operatorname{CH}^2(X)$), and
let arbitrary per-component signs
$\epsilon_j\in\{+1,-1\}$ be given. Define the actual effective algebraic
class
\[
  \gamma:=\sum_{\epsilon_j=+1}\nu_j[C_j]\;\in\;\operatorname{CH}^2(X).
\]
Then every signed fundamental-component assembly satisfies
\[
  \boxed{\;
  \sum_j \epsilon_j\,\nu_j[C_j]\;=\;2\gamma-[L_1][L_2]
  \quad\text{in }\operatorname{CH}^2(X)_{\mathbb Q}.}
\]
Consequently, if a prescribed primitive rational Hodge class $\alpha$
is representable by such an assembly (with fixed algebraic $[L_1][L_2]$
and fixed auxiliary divisor factors), then $\alpha$ is algebraic.
The sign-conductor mechanism cannot BIRTH a primitive source; it can
only re-sign components whose classes already encode $\alpha$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Split the assembly by sign:
\[
  \sum_j\epsilon_j\nu_j[C_j]
  =\sum_{\epsilon_j=+1}\nu_j[C_j]-\sum_{\epsilon_j=-1}\nu_j[C_j]
  =\gamma-\Bigl(\sum_j\nu_j[C_j]-\gamma\Bigr)
  =2\gamma-\sum_j\nu_j[C_j].
\]
Because $D_1\cap D_2$ is pure of expected codimension $2$, the
total fundamental component sum IS the Chow intersection product
\[
  \sum_j\nu_j[C_j]=[D_1]\cdot[D_2]=[L_1][L_2]
\]
(refined Gysin product; purity makes the assignment additive on
fundamental-cycle components with no excess contribution, the
multiplicities $\nu_j$ being the local intersection multiplicities
--- e.g.\ a tangential double intersection contributes $2[C]$).
This proves the displayed identity.
Now assume the assembly represents the prescribed primitive Hodge
class, i.e.\ $\cl\bigl(\sum_j\epsilon_j\nu_j[C_j]\bigr)=\alpha$ in
$H^{4}(X,\mathbb Q)$. By the displayed identity (an identity of
rational Chow classes, applied before passing to cohomology),
\[
  \alpha=\cl\Bigl(2\gamma-[L_1][L_2]\Bigr)
  \quad\text{in }H^{4}(X,\mathbb Q),
\]
the canonical class of a rational combination (with integer
coefficients $2$ and $-1$) of classes of actual algebraic cycles,
with $\gamma$ actual effective algebraic. Hence $\alpha$ is
algebraic. \qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Reading.}
The October 22/24 anti-deck sign-conductor
$\epsilon_j=\operatorname{sign}(\lambda_j)$ with period
$2\sum_j|\lambda_j|$ is thereby classified exactly: it is a
positivity/period optimizer over a fixed class skeleton
$\{[C_j]\}$ with fixed algebraic background $[L_1][L_2]$. It is
RETAINED as a coupling CERTIFIER (it certifies which component
assemblies couple to the prescribed $\alpha$, and optimizes the sign
pattern and the period), and it is CLOSED as a birth route: if it ever
assembles the prescribed primitive $\alpha$, then $\alpha$ was
algebraic already, with witness the explicit effective algebraic class
$\gamma$ with $\cl(2\gamma-[L_1][L_2])=\alpha$. This sharpens the
caveat in the cited records into the PROVED one-directional firewall
(representation forces algebraicity); no converse is claimed.

\section{Congruence completeness and localization of the forcing site}
\begin{theorem}[Congruence completeness; forcing-site localization]
\label{thm:B2}
Let $\operatorname{CH}$-theoretic algebraic K-theory be
$K^0_{\mathrm{alg}}(X)\cong G_0(X)$, the Grothendieck group of coherent
sheaves, a finitely generated lattice generated by the structure
sheaves of subvarieties of $X$. Define the additive invariant
\[
  c(y):=\int_X \alpha\;\ch(y)\;\td(X),
  \qquad y\in K^0_{\mathrm{alg}}(X),
\]
a homomorphism from a finitely generated lattice to $\mathbb Q$; its
image is $\frac1N\mathbb Z$ for an explicit positive integer $N$
(the least common multiple of the denominators of
$c(y_i)$ over any finite generating set $\{y_i\}$).
Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}] For every $m\equiv 0\pmod N$ and every rank $r$,
the hypothetical algebraic carrier
$x_m=z_m+r[1]\in K^0_{\mathrm{alg}}(X)$ satisfies
\[
  \chi(x_m\otimes y)=r\,\chi(y)+m\,c(y)\;\in\;\mathbb Z
  \qquad\text{for all }y\in K^0_{\mathrm{alg}}(X),
\]
and the same clearing-by-$m$ applies to every additive arithmetic
lattice invariant (all Schur-twisted Riemann--Roch congruences,
Schwarzenberger congruences included): the arithmetic layer is
obstruction-free.
\item[\textup{(ii)}] Nevertheless, for $\alpha$ Hodge:
$x_m$ lies in the image of
$K^0_{\mathrm{alg}}(X)\otimes\mathbb Q$ if and only if $\alpha$ is
algebraic (Atiyah--Hirzebruch; the K-theoretic Hodge conjecture is
equivalent to the Hodge conjecture, since Chern classes of algebraic
bundles are algebraic by degeneracy-locus/Thom--Porteous arguments,
and conversely GRR identifies the rational K-images with the rational
span of algebraic cycle classes).
\item[\textup{(iii)}] Hence the conjecture reduces to exactly ONE typed
input with zero residual ambiguity: the existence of an integrable
holomorphic structure
\[
  \boxed{\;(\ddbar_{E_\alpha})^2=0\;}
\]
on the SAME exact carrier $E_\alpha$ of Theorem A ---
equivalently, $\mathrm{GL}_r$-descent two-cell coherence of the
Oka--Grauert realization over the Jouanolou torsor, equivalently the
Abel--Jacobi/Griffiths layer of the Deligne lift of $\alpha$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i) For algebraic $y$, $\chi(y)=\int_X\ch(y)\td(X)$ is the Euler
characteristic of a coherent sheaf, an integer, by Riemann--Roch for
algebraic sheaves. For the carrier,
\[
  \chi(x_m\otimes y)
  =\int_X \ch(x_m)\ch(y)\td(X)
  =\int_X (r+m\alpha)\ch(y)\td(X)
  =r\,\chi(y)+m\,c(y),
\]
and both summands are integers: $r\chi(y)\in\mathbb Z$ always, and
$m\,c(y)\in\mathbb Z$ because $c(y)\in\frac1N\mathbb Z$ and $N\mid m$.
Every Schur-twisted Riemann--Roch invariant
$y\mapsto\chi(x_m\otimes y\otimes\operatorname{Schur}_\lambda(E_{\mathrm{test}}))$
at fixed algebraic twisting data, and every Schwarzenberger-type
congruence, is again an additive homomorphism on the same finitely
generated lattice with values in $\frac1{N'}\mathbb Q$ for its own
explicit $N'$, and is cleared by taking
$m\equiv 0\pmod{\operatorname{lcm}(m_0,N,N')}$.
Since finitely many such congruences are ever invoked at a fixed
$(X,p,\alpha)$, a single scale clears the entire arithmetic layer.
This proves (i).

(ii) is the Atiyah--Hirzebruch equivalence between algebraicity of
rational cohomology classes and membership in the rationalized image of
algebraic K-theory, recalled in the statement.

(iii) By Theorem A the exact carrier exists unconditionally with
explicit parameters $(m_0,r_0)$; by (i) no arithmetic lattice
congruence can obstruct an algebraic realization;
by Lemma B1 no signed assembly route can bypass it. What remains is
exactly the analytic statement that the SAME carrier carries an
integrable holomorphic structure. The equivalences are standard
reformulations at fixed carrier: Koszul--Malgrange identifies integrable
holomorphic structures with holomorphic bundle structures on the
SAME topological bundle; Oka--Grauert realizes holomorphic bundles on
Stein/affine bases, and descent of the realization from the Jouanolou
torsor to projective $X$ is exactly the two-cell coherence of the
$\mathrm{GL}_r$-valued transition data; and on the Hodge-theoretic
side, integrability of the carrier realizing the $(p,p)$ cocycle is the
Abel--Jacobi/Griffiths layer of the Deligne lift of $\alpha$.
(Recall from Remark \ref{rem:typediscipline} that the carrier exists
with or without the Hodge hypothesis; (iii) asserts that the Hodge
constraint enters ONLY at this one point.) \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What ``forcing'' now means]
The boss assertion ``the conjecture can get forced'' is now a precise

\section{The $\mathrm{Gr}(2,5)$ calibration carrier}
This section fixes the calibration world and derives the explicit
carrier identity, verified line by line.

\paragraph{Setup.}
Fix
\[
  X=\mathrm{Gr}(2,5),\qquad d=6,\qquad p=2,
\]
with tautological rank-2 subbundle $S$, universal quotient bundle $Q$
of rank 3, Pl\"ucker hyperplane class
$h=c_1(Q)=c_1(O(1))$, and
\[
  s_2:=c_2(S^\vee)=\sigma_{1,1}\in H^4(X,\mathbb Z),\qquad
  \alpha:=\tfrac25 h^2-s_2 .
\]
The relevant verified periods (matching the existing
$\mathrm{Gr}(2,5)$ Rees record
\texttt{2026-10-23-mathbox-simultaneous-geometric-algebraic-rees-fold-native-chow.md})
are
\[
  \int_X h^{6}=5,\qquad
  \int_X s_2\,h^{4}=2,\qquad
  \int_X s_2^2\,h^{2}=1,
\]
\[
  \int_X \alpha\,h^{4}
  =\tfrac25\int h^{6}-\int s_2h^{4}
  =\tfrac25\cdot 5-2=0,
  \qquad
  \int_X \alpha^2 h^{2}
  =\tfrac{4}{25}\cdot 5-\tfrac45\cdot 2+1
  =\tfrac15 .
\]
So $\alpha$ is a nonzero primitive nondivisor Hodge class on
the calibration world, and the already-proven fold-born witness is
\[
  Z_\alpha=\tfrac25[H_1\cap H_2]+b_*E^2
  \quad\text{with } b_*E^2=-[Z]=-s_2,
\]
an actual algebraic cycle with canonical Betti class $\alpha$.

\paragraph{The carrier identity, line by line.}
For a rank-2 bundle with Chern classes $c_1,c_2$ the second Chern
character grade is
$\ch_2=\frac{c_1^2-2c_2}{2}$.
For the tautological bundle $S$ one has $c_1(S)=-h$ and
$c_2(S)=s_2$, hence
\[
  \ch_2(S)=\frac{c_1(S)^2-2c_2(S)}{2}
         =\frac{h^2-2s_2}{2},
\]
and this expression is invariant under dualizing: dualization sends
$c_1\mapsto -c_1$, which appears squared, and fixes $c_2$, so also
$c_1(S^\vee)=h$, $c_2(S^\vee)=s_2$ gives
\[
  \ch_2(S^\vee)=\frac{h^2-2s_2}{2}.
\]
Therefore
\[
  \ch_2(S\oplus S^\vee)=h^2-2s_2 .
\]
For line bundles $\ch_2(L)=c_1(L)^2/2$ (since $\ch(L)=\exp(c_1(L))$),
and $c_1(O(\pm 1))=\pm h$, so both Pl\"ucker lines contribute
$h^2/2$ each and
\[
  \ch_2\bigl(O(1)\oplus O(-1)\bigr)=h^2 .
\]
Now form the explicit integral algebraic VIRTUAL carrier
\[
  \boxed{\;
  x:=[S\oplus S^\vee]^{\oplus 5}
     \;\ominus\;[O(1)\oplus O(-1)]
     \;\ominus\;[O^{\oplus 8}]\;\in K^0_{\mathrm{alg}}(X).}
\]
Its rank is $5\cdot 4-2-8=0$; its first Chern grade is
$5\,(c_1(S)+c_1(S^\vee))-(h-h)=0$; and its second grade is
\[
  \ch_2(x)=5\,(h^2-2s_2)-h^2=4h^2-10s_2=10\,\alpha,
\]
the last equality because $10\cdot\frac25 h^2=4h^2$.
So the naive Pl\"ucker assembly carries the prescribed class tenfold,
integrally and algebraically.

\paragraph{Exactness note (grade 4).}
The assembly $x$ is NOT yet the pure carrier. Computing the fourth
grade with Chern roots $u,v$ of $S$ ($u+v=-h$, $uv=s_2$):
\[
