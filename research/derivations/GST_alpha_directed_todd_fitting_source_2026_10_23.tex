\section{Exact original-world contract}
Fix ANY smooth projective complex variety X of dimension d, integer p between 1 and d, and a prescribed rational (p,p) class alpha in H^{2p}(X,Q). The required result is a genuine original-X Chow class whose CANONICAL rational Betti class is alpha.

Suppose we are supplied with an independently verifiable geometric SUPPORT datum: smooth projective Y_i of dimension d-p+1 and proper algebraic maps f_i:Y_i to X whose Gysin images satisfy
\[
 \alpha\in\operatorname{im}\left(
 F=\sum_i(f_i)_*:
 \bigoplus_i H^2(Y_i,\mathbb Q)(-(p-1))
 \longrightarrow H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\right).       \tag{1}
\]
The hypothesis is a cohomological support statement, NOT a Chow preimage of alpha. It is not assumed for an arbitrary Hodge class.

\begin{lemma}[Actual alpha-directed rational Hodge lift]
Choose a rational polarization of V, the direct sum source of F. Then K=ker F is a rational Hodge substructure. Its polarization-orthogonal complement K^\perp is rational, Hodge-stable, and F maps it isomorphically to im F. The unique lift
\[
 \boxed{\beta=(F|_{K^\perp})^{-1}(\alpha)
         =(\beta_i)_i\in V}                        \tag{2}
\]
has type (p,p) after Tate twist, hence every beta_i in H^2(Y_i,Q) has type (1,1).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The source and target are polarizable PURE rational Hodge structures of identical weight, and F is a rational Hodge morphism. Polarizability gives a nondegenerate restriction to the kernel and a rational Hodge orthogonal splitting. F kills precisely K, giving the rational Hodge isomorphism on its complement. Apply it to the specified rational type-(p,p) alpha.
\end{proof}

By Lefschetz (1,1) and GAGA, for each i select an ACTUAL algebraic line bundle M_i with c1(M_i)=m_i beta_i in rational Betti cohomology for a positive integer m_i. Define the genuine Chow divisor beta_i^CH=c1^CH(M_i)/m_i.

\begin{theorem}[Exact all-X alpha-sensitive PURE Chow source]
The ENTIRE original-X algebraic Chern character of v_X satisfies the identity
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{ch}_X^{CH}(v_X)
       =\sum_i(f_i)_*\beta_i^{CH}\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q},
       \quad \operatorname{ch}^{CH}_j(v_X)=0
       \text{ for }j\ne p.}                      \tag{5}
\]
Consequently the actual original-X rational Chow cycle
\[
 \boxed{Z_\alpha=\sum_i(f_i)_*\beta_i^{CH},
       \qquad \mathrm{cl}^{can}_X(Z_\alpha)=\alpha} \tag{6}

is constructed from the prescribed alpha and genuine support maps, without supplying an original-X algebraic representative as input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Grothendieck--Riemann--Roch gives
\[
 \operatorname{ch}((f_i)_!v_i)\operatorname{td}(T_X)
 = (f_i)_*(\operatorname{ch}(v_i)\operatorname{td}(T_{Y_i})).
\]
Substitute the definition of v_i, cancel the Todd terms and apply the Chow projection formula. This gives precisely ch((f_i)_!v_i)=(f_i)_*beta_i^CH. The codimension shift of f_i is p-1, so beta_i^CH of grade one pushes to grade p, with NO other graded outputs. The canonical Betti map commutes with the genuine proper algebraic Gysin map. The chosen rational Hodge lift (2) therefore proves that cl(Z_alpha)=sum f_* beta_i=alpha.
\end{proof}
