\section*{GST: exact original-X Chow return and Hodge defect}
Let X be any smooth connected projective complex d-fold. Choose
a closed embedding X into P=P^{2M} with M>=d+1 and set
Y=Bl_X P, E=P_lines(N), j:E to Y, q:E to X, c=rank(N)=2M-d,
and xi=c_1(O_E(1)), with s(N)c(N)=1.

\begin{theorem}[Canonical Hodge-defect decomposition]
Put D^p(X)=Hdg^p(X)_Q / cl(CH^p(X)_Q).
Then, in genuine rational Chow and Hodge cohomology,
\[
 CH^k(Y)_Q \cong CH^k(P)_Q\oplus
          \bigoplus_{a=1}^{c-1}CH^{k-a}(X)_Q,
\quad
 (\gamma,(z_a))\mapsto b^*\gamma+
 \sum_{a=1}^{c-1} j_*(\xi^{a-1}q^*z_a).
\]
The same isomorphism holds for rational Hodge classes,
and the canonical cycle map commutes termwise. Hence
\[
 \boxed{D^M(Y)\cong\bigoplus_{p=0}^{d}D^p(X).}
\]
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof.
Both decompositions follow from the actual Chow and
rational Hodge blowup formulas for a regular immersion.
Projective-space Hodge classes are algebraic. For p in [0,d],
the exceptional index a=M-p satisfies
1<=a<=2M-d-1 because M>=d+1. All other indices vanish
by dimension. Taking cokernels proves the result.

\begin{theorem}[Actual explicit Chern--Segre inverse]
For Gamma in CH^k(Y)_Q and 1<=a<=c-1 put
\[
 v_a(\Gamma)=-q_*(\xi^{c-1-a}j^*\Gamma),\qquad
 \boxed{P_a(\Gamma)=\sum_{t=a}^{c-1}c_{t-a}(N)v_t(\Gamma).}
\]
Then P_a is a genuine Chow operation
with values in CH^{k-a}(X)_Q, and
\[
 \boxed{P_a(b^*\gamma+\sum_{t=1}^{c-1}
       j_*(\xi^{t-1}q^*z_t))=z_a.}
\]
Its rational Hodge analogue commutes with canonical cycle class.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof.
The Cartier self-intersection formula yields
j^*j_*(xi^{t-1}q^*z)=-xi^t q^*z.
The projective bundle identity gives
q_*(xi^{c-1+s})=s_s(N) and vanishing for smaller
nonnegative exponents. Ambient pullback terms vanish.
Thus v_a=z_a+sum_{t>a}s_{t-a}(N)z_t.
The unitriangular Segre matrix is inverted by the
unitriangular Chern matrix because c(N)s(N)=1.
This proves the exact formula in CH, and naturality
proves the cohomological statement.

\begin{corollary}[Explicit original-X descent of a genuine world source]
For alpha in Hdg^p(X)_Q, let a=M-p and
tildealpha=j_*(xi^{a-1}q^*alpha) in Hdg^M(Y)_Q.
If an INDEPENDENTLY CONSTRUCTED Chow cycle Gamma on Y
represents tildealpha, then the ACTUAL original-X cycle
Z=P_a(Gamma) satisfies cl_X(Z)=alpha.
Conversely cl_X(Z)=alpha produces Gamma via the
algebraic exceptional inclusion. Thus this middle-world
existence is EXACTLY EQUIVALENT to original-X algebraicity,
not a weaker full-rank matrix hypothesis.
\end{corollary}

\section*{Actual fourfold}
If d=4 and M=5 then c=6; since the standard
Lefschetz results give D^0=D^1=D^3=D^4=0,
\[
 \boxed{D^5(Bl_X P^{10})\cong D^2(X),\qquad
 P_3(\Gamma)=v_3+c_1(N)v_4+c_2(N)v_5.}
\]
This is a full actual-X Chow return, not a
chosen Hodge-to-Chow inverse.

\section*{Wolfram exact checks}
The embedding index 1<=M-p<=2M-d-1 was checked for
d=1,...,15, M=d+1,...,d+4, all p=0,...,d:
60 complete cases, all true. The symbolic unitriangular
Chern/Segre matrices were checked as exact inverses for
c=2,...,8; all seven polynomial tests returned true.
These tests validate arithmetic and signs but are
not a substitute for the general proofs above.

\section*{Scope}
This is a complete actual-Chow retraction law and
middle Hodge-defect identity. It does not construct an
algebraic Gamma for every prescribed tildealpha;
that assertion is equivalent to the original rational
Hodge conjecture and cannot be inferred from the
existence of the projective blowup itself.
