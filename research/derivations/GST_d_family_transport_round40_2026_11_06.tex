\begin{theorem}[S29, the phase classification and the exact collar]
\label{thm:s29}
Write $\zeta_\alpha = \alpha/\bar\alpha$ (a norm-one element of $K$).
The roots of unity of an imaginary quadratic field have order at most $6$,
and only $\mathbb{Q}(i)$ (order 4) and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ (orders 3
and 6) carry any beyond $\pm 1$. Consequently:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $\zeta_\alpha$ has finite order only for
$\alpha/\bar\alpha \in \mu(K)$, and the order lies in
$\{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$;
\item[(ii)] order 1 $\iff b = 0$; order 2 $\iff a = 0$;
order 3 $\iff d = 3$ and $a = \pm b$; order 4 $\iff d = 1$ and $a = \pm b$;
order 6 $\iff d = 3$ and $a = \pm 3b$;
\item[(iii)] the \textbf{exact exclusion collar} of the family is
\[
\{b = 0\} \;\cup\; \{a = 0\} \;\cup\; \{(d, a, b) : d = 3,\ a = \pm b\}
\quad\text{(the phase orders 1, 2, 3),}
\]
and the \textbf{order-4 and order-6 phase worlds are family members}: at
these worlds the spectral law (dims $2/36/32$, pairwise coprimality) still
holds -- only the Weil factor's \emph{degree} degenerates at order 4
($\mathrm{minpoly}(\alpha^4)$ linear, $\alpha^4 \in \mathbb{Q}$), which does not
affect any load-bearing receipt.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i) is the classical torsion theorem for imaginary quadratic unit groups.
For (ii): $\zeta_\alpha = 1$ forces $\alpha = \bar\alpha$, i.e.\ $b = 0$;
$\zeta_\alpha = -1$ forces $a = 0$. Order 3 or 6 requires
$\zeta_\alpha \in \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})^\times$, hence $d = 3$; there
$\zeta = \frac{(a^2 - 3b^2) + 2ab\sqrt{-3}}{a^2+3b^2}$ equals
$e^{\pm 2\pi i/3}$ exactly when $a = \pm b$ (order 3) and equals
$e^{\pm \pi i/3}$ exactly when $a = \pm 3b$ (order 6). Order 4 requires
$d = 1$ and $\zeta_\alpha = \pm i$, i.e.\ $a = \pm b$. For (iii): the
spectral factors are $\mathrm{minpoly}(\alpha^4)$, $\mathrm{minpoly}(\alpha^3\bar\alpha)$,
$t - N(\alpha)^2$; shared roots occur exactly when
$\alpha^4 \in \{\alpha^3\bar\alpha\text{-orbit}, N(\alpha)^2\}$, i.e.\
when $\zeta_\alpha^3 = 1$, $\zeta_\alpha^2 = 1$, or $\zeta_\alpha = 1$
(the orders 3, 2, 1). At order 4: $\alpha^4 = \bar\alpha^4 \in
\mathbb{Q}$, the Weil factor is linear with root distinct from
$N(\alpha)^2$ (which has order-2 phase) and from the mixed factor's roots
(which carry order 3); the three factors remain pairwise coprime, the
$\alpha^4$-primary part retains dimension $2 = \binom{4}{4} + \binom{4}{0}$,
and every receipt of Rounds 38/39 applies verbatim. At order 6 no sharing
condition fires at all. The certificate verifies the classification
computationally at a 160-instance $(d, a, b)$ grid, both directions, with
the collar worlds detected by the certificate's own coprime machinery.
\end{proof}
