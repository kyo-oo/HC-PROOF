\section{The extension, quantifiers, and changes of category}
The original input is an arbitrary smooth projective complex variety $X$,
$1\leq p\leq\dim X$, and a prescribed rational Hodge class
$\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X,\C)$. The final source target remains
an actual rational cycle on this original $X$ with canonical Betti class
$\alpha$. The starting checkpoint is
\texttt{b2de3bdecf4467211702f5d5118577a014170333}.
Work componentwise with connected $X$. For a disconnected smooth projective
input the constructions and ranks are taken on each connected component,
and the resulting cycles, when constructed, are summed on the original $X$.

We extend the previous independent-factor construction in three directions.
First, fold the spaces \emph{over $X$ itself}, using genuine holomorphic
line bundles. All higher cohomology then sees the cup algebra of $X$.
Second, allow non-square quotient folds and determine the largest dimension
that can survive with zero curvature. Third, make the curvature the
prescribed $\alpha$ in \emph{every codimension}, by an actual rational
topological homotopy fiber. The third construction is a rational space with
an explicitly specified filtered model, not a claim of a compact complex or
algebraic realization. This distinction is essential to its precise theorem.
No universal Hodge algebraicity premise is used in these constructions.

\section{Actual compact complex folds coupled to every original base}
Fix $q=e^{-2\pi}$ and $E=\C^*/q^{\mathbb Z}$, an elliptic curve with square
period lattice. Given actual holomorphic line bundles $L_1,\ldots,L_r$ on
$X$, define
\[
 P(\mathbf L)=\prod_X (L_a^\times/q^{\mathbb Z})\longrightarrow X.       \tag{1}
\]
The fiberwise action is free and properly discontinuous; local line-bundle
trivializations identify (1) with a holomorphic principal $E^r$ bundle.
It is compact, has complex dimension $n+r$, and is generally not a product.
Choose Chern metrics whose curvature forms $c_a$ are harmonic representatives
of $c_1(L_a)$. The $\partial\bar\partial$ lemma permits this choice.
The invariant vertical complex forms $\beta_a=u_a+i v_a$ can be normalized
so that $d u_a=c_a$ and $d v_a=0$, with $\beta_a$ of type $(1,0)$.
The sign and common nonzero real scale in identifying a unitary connection
with this coframe do not change the maps below; fix them by $d u_a=c_a$.
Write
\[
 C:\Q^r\longrightarrow H^2(X,\Q),\quad e_a\longmapsto c_1(L_a),
 \qquad\rho=\rk C.                                                   \tag{2}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Base-coupled whole-Hodge decision law]
For every $X$ and every finite list $\mathbf L$, the actual filtration obeys
\[
\begin{split}
 b_1(P)&=b_1(X)+2r-\rho,\\
 \dim F^1H^1(P,\C)&=h^{1,0}(X)+r-\rho,\\
 F^1H^1(P,\C)\cap\overline{F^1H^1(P,\C)}&=0,\\
 \dim H^1(P,\C)/(F^1+\overline{F^1})&=\rho.                         \tag{3}
\end{split}
The canonical de Rham filtration is pure of the correct weight in every
degree if and only if $C=0$. When $C=0$, $P$ is compact K\"ahler.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Average over the compact fiber torus. Translation is isotopic to identity,
so this computes de Rham cohomology and preserves type. The invariant complex
is $\Omega^*(X)[u_a,v_a]$ with exterior odd generators and the displayed
differentials. The low-degree Serre sequence, or its degree-one complex,
gives $H^1(X)$ and the vertical coefficients $(s,t)$ with $Cs=0$.
Closed holomorphic vertical coefficients are constant, since $X$ is compact.
Their equation is $Cs=0$: necessity follows from curvature classes;
sufficiency uses the harmonic representatives, whose zero class means their
linear combination is the zero form. Thus the holomorphic contribution is
$\ker C_\C$, in addition to base holomorphic one-forms. The pure parts are
$(s-it)/2,(s+it)/2$ exactly as in the previous GST split law. They are both
closed exactly when $Ct=0$. This proves (3) and zero intersection, including
the base summand.

If $C\ne0$, degree one already fails opposedness. If $C=0$, each line bundle
has zero real Chern class and admits a unitary flat Chern connection. All
$\beta_a$ are then closed and
$\pi^*\omega_X+(i/2)\sum_a\beta_a\wedge\bar\beta_a$ is a closed positive
$(1,1)$ form. Hence $P$ is compact K\"ahler and has its actual pure Hodge
structure in every degree. Torsion integral Chern classes cause no exception.
\end{proof}
Purity here means the canonical Hodge filtration of the resulting manifold,
not equality of its Betti polynomial with that of $X$ and not projectivity.
The line bundles retain holomorphic $\operatorname{Pic}^0$ data even when (2)
vanishes. It is therefore incorrect to identify every zero-curvature endpoint
with $X\times E^r$.

\section{Full coupled cohomology and its non-product ranks}
The complete real cup algebra is computed by
\[
 B_C=\left(H^*(X,\R)\otimes\Lambda(u_1,v_1,\ldots,u_r,v_r),d\right),
 \quad |u_a|=|v_a|=1,\quad du_a=c_a,\quad dv_a=0.                       \tag{4}
\]
This is a model statement, not just an equality of first Betti numbers.
For completeness the relative formality argument is as follows. Put
$d^c=i(\bar\partial-\partial)$. The $dd^c$ lemma makes both maps in
$\Omega^*(X)\leftarrow\ker d^c\to H_{d^c}(X)$ quasi-isomorphisms of real
DGAs, where the right-hand differential is zero: if $d^c a=0$, then
$da\in\im d\cap\ker d^c=\im dd^c\subset\im d^c$.
The harmonic $(1,1)$ forms lie in $\ker d^c$, and map to exactly $c_a$.
Adjoin the same finitely many exterior generators to both arrows and filter
by their number. On the first comparison page the original quasi-isomorphisms
remain isomorphisms. The finite comparison proves (4). Averaging on the
principal torus and its connection identifies the left model with all forms
on $P$, as above. This argument establishes real cup cohomology; it does not
claim a complete rational period-lattice or Dolbeault identification.

For a single line, let $b_j=\dim H^j(X,\R)$ and
$r_j=\rk(c\cup-:H^j(X,\R)\to H^{j+2}(X,\R))$, taking all out-of-range
terms as zero. The two-row complex gives
\[
0\to\operatorname{coker}\mu_{k-2}\to
H^k(H^*(X)\otimes\Lambda u)\to\ker\mu_{k-1}\to0.
\]
The remaining closed generator $v$ gives the complete formula
\[
 b_k(P(L))=b_k+2b_{k-1}+b_{k-2}-r_{k-1}-2r_{k-2}-r_{k-3}.             \tag{5}
\]
Thus a curvature rank by itself no longer determines the higher spectrum.
On $X=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$, the two curvatures $h_1$ and
$h_1+h_2$ both give $(1,1,1,2,1,1,1)$; on $\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^1$
the single classes $h_1$ and $h_2$ have different multiplication-rank lists,
and (5) distinguishes their full total-space spectra. These are diagnostics
of the uniform formula (4), not a restriction of its original-$X$ scope.

\section{Non-square dimensional folds and the universal restoring quotient}
An integer matrix $M\in M_{s\times r}(\mathbb Z)$ defines actual line bundles
$N_b=\bigotimes_a L_a^{M_{ba}}$. If $M:\mathbb Z^r\to\mathbb Z^s$ is
surjective, its fiber map $E^r\to E^s$ has connected kernel and produces an
actual holomorphic quotient
\[
 P(\mathbf L)\longrightarrow P(\mathbf N),\qquad n+r\longrightarrow n+s.
                                                                         \tag{6}
\]
The target curvature is $CM^t$, and (3) applies to it. This includes fusion:
$P(\mathbf L)\star P(\mathbf T)=P(\mathbf L\otimes\mathbf T)$, where the
fiber product has dimension $n+2r$ and the antidiagonal quotient $n+r$.
Chronological histories tensor the actual line bundles, not only their
Chern classes. Hence zero curvature restores purity, while complete
holomorphic restoration to a trivial principal bundle requires the resulting
\emph{elliptic torsor classes} to vanish. Trivial tensor product lines are
sufficient, but not necessary: the line-to-torsor functor has a kernel,
which we compute below. Triviality always means over $X$, with the specified
principal action; it is not unmarked biholomorphism of the total manifolds.

\begin{theorem}[Largest Hodge-restoring quotient in this fold category]
Let
$R=\ker(\mathbb Z^r\to H^2(X,\mathbb Z)/\mathrm{torsion})$.
It is saturated of rank $r-\rho$. Choose an integral basis for $R$ as the
rows of $M_R$. Then (6) with $M_R$ is a connected-fiber quotient of dimension
$n+r-\rho$, and has its full canonical pure Hodge structure. Every
zero-curvature integer-matrix quotient factors through it. In particular
the minimum number of emergent complex dimensions which must be collapsed
to restore purity in this category is exactly $\rho$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The target of the integral Chern map is torsion-free, so its kernel is
saturated. Smith normal form, or extending a primitive sublattice basis,
extends the rows of $M_R$ to a unimodular matrix. Thus $M_R$ is surjective
on integer lattices and its torus kernel is connected. Its curvature is
zero, so the first theorem applies. Every row of any zero-curvature $M$
lies in $R$ and has a unique integral expansion in its basis. Thus
$M=NM_R$ and the actual tensor-line construction factors accordingly.
A surjective matrix has independent rows, so $s\leq\rk R=r-\rho$.
Equality is achieved by $M_R$. The case $r=\rho$ has target $X$ and no
vertical handles. If $\rho=0$, the identity fold is maximal.
\end{proof}
The category here consists of these integer-matrix principal-$E$ quotients.
We do not assert an optimality theorem among all possible complex manifolds
or all possible GST folds. This restriction defines the operation, not $X$.

\section{A second restoration kernel invisible to curvature}
Let $\mathcal E_X$ be the sheaf of holomorphic maps to $E$.
The quotient defining $E$ gives the exact sequence of analytic sheaves
\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{n\mapsto q^n}
 \mathcal O_X^*\longrightarrow\mathcal E_X\longrightarrow0.           \tag{11}
\]
Local holomorphic lifting proves surjectivity, and the discrete kernel is
exactly $q^{\mathbb Z}$. The class $\theta(L)\in H^1(X,\mathcal E_X)$
is represented by the images of the line-bundle transition functions.
Consequently
\[
 P(L)\text{ is a trivial principal }E\text{ bundle}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 L\in\im\bigl(H^1(X,\mathbb Z)\xrightarrow{q^{(-)}}\operatorname{Pic}(X)\bigr).
                                                                         \tag{12}
\]
This proves precisely what holomorphic memory survives a curvature fold.
For a list define the genuine integral relation subgroup
\[
 R_\theta=\{m\in\mathbb Z^r:\sum_a m_a\theta(L_a)=0\}.
\]
It need not be saturated, and is contained in $R$ above. Indeed a line in
the image in (12) has transition functions in the contractible positive-real
group, and so has zero integral topological Chern class. An integer-matrix
quotient is a trivial principal bundle exactly when all its rows belong to
$R_\theta$. Allowing possibly disconnected fiber kernels, the largest
retained handle count for \emph{trivial principal} endpoints is
$\rk R_\theta$, by choosing any integral basis. Requiring connected kernels
is an extra primitive-lattice condition; do not replace $R_\theta$ by its
saturation. In contrast $R$ is saturated and always gives the maximal
\emph{Hodge-restoring} connected quotient proved above.

\begin{theorem}[Hodge-preserving folds with persistent torsor memory]
For every smooth projective $X$ with $h^{1,0}(X)>0$, there is a line
$L\in\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$ such that $P(L)$ has its full canonical pure
Hodge structure but no quotient induced by a nonzero integer $[m]:E\to E$
is a trivial principal bundle over $X$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$H^1(X,\mathbb Z)$ is finitely generated and hence its image in (12) is
countable. Each multiplication-by-$m\ne0$ map on the positive-dimensional
complex torus $\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$ has finite fibers. The union of the
preimages of that countable image, over nonzero integers $m$, is countable,
whereas $\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$ is uncountable. Choose $L$ outside it.
Then $\theta(L^m)\ne0$ for every $m\ne0$ by (12). Its real Chern class
is zero, so the explicit K\"ahler construction proves whole Hodge purity.
\end{proof}
This theorem concerns actual spaces over every such $X$, not a speculative
counterexample to rational Hodge algebraicity. Its role is to preserve the
correct holomorphic invariant in the extended folding method.

\section{Target-sensitive curvature in every codimension}
For $d=2p-1$, choose a classifying map
$f_\alpha:X\to K(\Q,2p)$ representing the given rational class, and take
its actual topological homotopy fiber $F_\alpha$. Adjoin an independent
$K(\Q,d)$ factor. One can use simplicial abelian-group models for these
Eilenberg--Mac Lane spaces; pullback of the contractible path object gives
the principal $K(\Q,d)$ torsor representing $\alpha$. This is an actual
space up to homotopy, determined by the prescribed input, even when no
algebraic cycle for $\alpha$ is known. It need not have finite geometric
dimension or be a compact manifold.

Its real cohomology has the model
\[
 A_\alpha=H^*(X,\R)\otimes\Lambda(u,v),\quad |u|=|v|=d,
 \quad du=\alpha,\quad dv=0.                                          \tag{7}
\]
The fiber has exterior cohomology on an odd generator; its transgression is
the classifying class. The relative $dd^c$ formality argument above applies
to the harmonic $(p,p)$ representative and proves (7), with the same finite
fiber filtration. Equivalently, for the single torsor the Serre sequence has
only two nonzero rows and its sole possible differential is cup with
$\alpha$. The latter observation proves the additive dimensions without a
multiplicative extension claim.

For an arbitrary finite list $\boldsymbol\alpha$ of degree-$2p$ rational
Hodge classes, use the product of their torsors over $X$ and one flat odd
factor for each. Then
\[
 A_{\boldsymbol\alpha}=H^*(X,\R)\otimes\Lambda(u_a,v_a),\quad
 |u_a|=|v_a|=d,\quad du_a=\alpha_a,\quad dv_a=0.                       \tag{8}
\]
This makes the prescribed higher Hodge classes themselves the curvature,
whereas line bundles only permit degree-two curvature.

Give (8) the explicitly defined conjugate bigrading with
$\beta_a=u_a+i v_a$ of type $(p,p-1)$ and $\bar\beta_a$ of type $(p-1,p)$,
and the usual Hodge bigrading on the base. The differential is the sum of
operators of types $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$, because $d\beta_a=d\bar\beta_a=
\alpha_a$ has type $(p,p)$. This defines an actual filtered DGA with real
conjugation. This filtration is \emph{specified by the model}; no canonical
complex-geometric filtration on the topological space is asserted.

\begin{theorem}[All-codimension first affected degree]
Let $C_p:\Q^r\to H^{2p}(X,\Q)$ send $e_a$ to $\alpha_a$, and set
$\rho_p=\rk C_p$. In degree $d=2p-1$, the filtered cohomology of (8) has
\[
 H^d(A_{\boldsymbol\alpha})/(F^p+\overline{F^p})
       \simeq \im (C_p\otimes\C),\qquad \dim=\rho_p.                   \tag{9}
\]
Its full model Hodge structure is pure of the cohomological weight in every
degree exactly when $C_p=0$. Quotient-fold relations and their maximal
retained handle count are $\ker C_p$ and $r-\rho_p$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Below degree $d$, no new generators occur. At degree $d$ the only new
terms are $u_a,v_a$ times base degree zero; boundaries are base boundaries,
which vanish in this model. The closed vertical pairs are $(s,t)$ with
$C_ps=0$. Since the base is pure of odd weight $d$, it is the direct sum
of its $F^p$ and conjugate. The vertical pure parts are the two explicit
halves $(s-it)/2,(s+it)/2$, closed precisely when $C_pt=0$.
The quotient is therefore exactly $\im C_p$, proving (9), including
zero or dependent input classes. Nonzero rank obstructs purity in degree
$d$. If all classes vanish, the differential is zero and the exterior
algebra on the displayed conjugate pure generators, tensored with the base,
is pure in every degree. Linear changes of odd generators transform the
classifying classes by the same matrix; principal abelian torsor addition
realizes this operation up to homotopy. Kernel factorization is ordinary
linear algebra over $\Q$. There is no claim of complex dimension $n+r$
for these higher topological handles.
\end{proof}

\section{Full higher spectra and the actual source attempt}
Put $r_j(\alpha)=\rk(\alpha\cup-:H^j(X)\to H^{j+2p}(X))$.
For one higher torsor and its flat companion, the exact all-degree formula is
\[
 b_k(A_\alpha)=b_k+2b_{k-d}+b_{k-2d}
 -r_{k-2p}(\alpha)-r_{k-d}(\alpha)
 -r_{k-d-2p}(\alpha)-r_{k-2d}(\alpha).                              \tag{10}
\]
Indeed the cohomology without $v$ has dimension
$b_k+b_{k-d}-r_{k-2p}-r_{k-d}$, by the same two-row kernel/cokernel
sequence, and $v$ adds the degree-$d$ shift. Formula (5) is the $p=1$ case.
No positive-dimensional space or missing generator is silently discarded.

\subsection{Cup-coefficient folds across different emergent degrees}
There is a further extension beyond scalar curvature matrices. Allow a
finite list of different codimensions $p_i$, put $d_i=2p_i-1$, and use
$du_i=\alpha_i$, $dv_i=0$. If $p_j\geq p_i$ and
$a\in H^{2(p_j-p_i)}(X,\Q)\cap H^{p_j-p_i,p_j-p_i}$, set
\[
 u_j'=u_j-a u_i,\quad v_j'=v_j-a v_i,\quad
 \alpha_j'=\alpha_j-a\alpha_i.                                       \tag{13}
\]
Both new generators have precisely degree $d_j$. The substitution is an
invertible triangular change of the entire exterior algebra, with inverse
given by adding $a$ times the unchanged $i$ generators. Since $a$ is closed
and even, $du_j'=\alpha_j-a\alpha_i$ and $dv_j'=0$, with no omitted sign.
Moreover $\beta_j'=\beta_j-a\beta_i$ retains type $(p_j,p_j-1)$.
Thus (13) is a conjugation-compatible filtered DGA isomorphism. Its
coefficient is a class on the original $X$, not a scalar or an independently
adjoined coordinate. Whenever $\alpha_j=a\alpha_i$, an entire higher pair
becomes closed and pure by this explicit invertible transformation.

For example on $\mathbb P^3$, take $(\alpha_1,\alpha_2)=(h,h^2)$ in
codimensions $(1,2)$. The degree-three generators become
$u_2-hu_1,v_2-hv_1$, with zero differentials; the remaining degree-one
curvature is $h$. This is a diagnostic of the all-$X$ graded substitution,
not its definition. The underlying fiber equivalence can also be constructed
directly, so no nilpotence assumption on $\pi_1(X)$ is needed. Choose pointed
cup-product maps between Eilenberg--Mac Lane spaces, a representative
$a(x)$, and set $f_j'=f_j-a\smile f_i$. In path-space models of the homotopy
fibers, replace the nullhomotopy $\gamma_j$ by
$\gamma_j-a(x)\smile\gamma_i$, retaining $\gamma_i$. Its endpoints are
exactly $0$ and $f_j'(x)$; adding that same cup path is the inverse. For the
independent flat odd factors use their corresponding cup maps. This gives
an actual equivalence over $X$ with the cochain substitution (13), up to
the chosen classifying homotopies. No claim of a dimension-reducing
holomorphic map between compact manifolds is made for this higher operation.

For this mixed-codimension model, whole model purity still holds exactly
when all $\alpha_i$ vanish. To prove necessity, take the least $p$ with a
nonzero curvature. All smaller-degree handles have zero differential and
form a pure graded factor with the base. In degree $2p-1$ only the handles
of that degree can introduce a new nonclosed term; higher ones have not
appeared. The base-factor inclusion preserves the linear independence of
the nonzero $\alpha_i$. The same two-part calculation gives defect equal
to their rank. Sufficiency is the zero-differential tensor decomposition.
This extends the decision law simultaneously across every finite list of
codimensions, with the first affected degree computed rather than assumed.

The construction starts from the actual arbitrary $(X,p,\alpha)$ and uses
$\alpha$ in its classifying map. It provides a genuine new target-sensitive
topological object with the exact transgression $du=\alpha$, and the
pullback of $\alpha$ to $F_\alpha$ is zero. Opposite fusion uses torsor
addition and returns the zero classifying map, so that the endpoint is
homotopy equivalent to a trivial higher torsor over $X$. The surviving
transgression history still records the original $\alpha$.

For $p=1$, choose a positive integer $m$ making $m\alpha$ integral.
The integral $(1,1)$ theorem constructs an actual holomorphic line bundle
$L_\alpha$ with $c_1(L_\alpha)=m\alpha$; on projective $X$ this is algebraic.
Thus (1) is an actual target-dependent complex fold, and
$Z_\alpha=c_1^{\mathrm{CH}}(L_\alpha)/m$ has canonical Betti class $\alpha$.
For $p>1$, (7) constructs a rational topological torsor, not a line bundle
or a Chow cycle. Its fusion and full model purity do not manufacture such
an object. The new calculation is (9)--(10), with actual target dependence;
it is not an independent proof of higher algebraicity. The first unchecked
source arrow is a cycle-bearing algebraic realization of this higher
transgression with its computed canonical comparison. It is not supplied
as a hypothesis in any claimed source-birth theorem here.

\section{Proof audit and attribution boundary}
Self-review checks: compactness and type of (1); torsion versus real Chern
classes; actual degree-one filtration; sufficient K\"ahler form at zero
curvature; finite relative-formality filtration; all cup-product degrees in
(5) and (10); saturated integral relations and connected kernels; retained
holomorphic gluing; degree $2p-1$ rather than degree one for higher handles;
nullary and dependent curvatures; and the distinction between real cohomology,
rational classifying data, model filtration, and actual algebraic cycles.

The bundle, $dd^c$, transgression, and lattice mechanisms are standard inputs
at their stated strengths, with their uses derived here. The combined
base-coupled quotient and higher target-transgression construction extends
the branch's previous central-fold implementation. No claim of worldwide
historical priority is made without a literature comparison. The universal
original-$X$ conjecture remains the owner; these theorems give general
quantifiers for the specified new operations, not an assumed classification
of every imaginable fold. Lean changes formalize their algebraic cores;
the analytic and topological comparisons remain the hand proofs above.
