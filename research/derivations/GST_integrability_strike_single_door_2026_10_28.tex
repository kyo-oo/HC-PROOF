\begin{theorem}[I1: the traceless two-cell reduction]\label{thm:i1}

\begin{proof}

a transport trivializing the two-cell. Write $C_T$ for the defect

class is well defined and trivial for Hodge $\alpha$; the residue $[C_T^0]$ is the

scalar, $F^{vert}=\eta\,\mathrm{Id}$, the correction
$\nabla'=\nabla-\frac1r\theta\,\mathrm{Id}$ with $d_{vert}\theta=r\eta$ --- which exists
and is exact precisely under this matching condition (certified both directions) ---
constructs the flat connection, consistent with (2) being the only content.
\end{proof}
