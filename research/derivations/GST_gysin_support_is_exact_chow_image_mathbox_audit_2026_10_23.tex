Let $X$ be any smooth projective complex variety of dimension $d$,
$1\le p\le d$, and let
$\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)$ be prescribed.
Consider all proper algebraic maps $f:Y\to X$
from smooth projective $Y$ of dimension $d-p+1$, and set
\[
 \mathcal G_p(X)=\sum_{(Y,f)}
 f_*\bigl(H^2(Y,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{1,1}(Y)\bigr).
\]
The GST Todd--Fitting construction assumed that $\alpha$
belongs to a finite such Gysin image.
\begin{theorem}[Exact algebraic-source audit, every original X]
For every such $X,p$,
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal G_p(X)=
 \operatorname{im}\bigl(
 \mathrm{cl}^{can}_X:CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\to H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\bigr).}
 \tag{1}
\]
Consequently the previously used unrestricted support
hypothesis on the input $\alpha$ is equivalent to
the existence of the requested original-X rational Chow class.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For inclusion from left to right, the rational Lefschetz
$(1,1)$ theorem realizes every rational $(1,1)$ class
on each smooth projective $Y$ by an actual rational
algebraic divisor. Its proper algebraic pushforward
is a codimension-p Chow class on X, and the
canonical rational Betti comparison commutes with Gysin.

Conversely let $Z\subset X$ be any irreducible
codimension-p algebraic subvariety. Let
$\nu:\widetilde Z\to Z$ be an actual smooth
projective resolution of singularities.
Put $Y=\widetilde Z\times\mathbb P^1$,
$f:Y\xrightarrow{\mathrm{pr}_{\widetilde Z}}
\widetilde Z\xrightarrow{\nu}Z\hookrightarrow X$,
and $\beta=\mathrm{pr}_{\mathbb P^1}^*
c_1^{Betti}(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(1))$.
Then $\dim Y=d-p+1$, the rational $\beta$
has type $(1,1)$, and the actual Gysin formula gives
\[
 \boxed{f_*\beta=
 (\nu\circ\mathrm{pr}_{\widetilde Z})_*\beta
 =\mathrm{cl}^{can}_X([Z])}
 \tag{2}
\]
using $\int_{\mathbb P^1}c_1(\mathcal O(1))=1$
and $\nu_*[\widetilde Z]=[Z]$.
Finite rational linear combinations of irreducible
cycles prove the reverse inclusion. No form of the
Hodge conjecture is used to establish (1).
\end{proof}

The exact GST source-birth demand is NOT to apply
GRR/Todd and Fitting minors to assumed Gysin support.
It is to construct from the INPUT $(X,p,\alpha)$
actual smooth projective $Y_i$, algebraic maps $f_i$
and Hodge classes $\beta_i$ with
$\alpha=\sum_i(f_i)_*\beta_i$ without
any Chow witness of alpha as an input.
Alternatively the source can be an independently
constructed algebraic projector on the genuine GST
incidence fold whose canonical original-X Betti
class is exactly alpha; that route must construct
the projector, not postulate that its period is alpha.
The support theorem is an exact SOURCE TYPE CHECK,
not a disproof of GST or of Hodge.
