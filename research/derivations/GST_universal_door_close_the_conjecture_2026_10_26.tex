\begin{lemma}[The universal swap algebra]\label{lem:swap}
Let $A$ be any abelian group and let $M = A \oplus A$ carry the exchange
action $\sigma(v,w) = (w,v)$ of $C_2$. Then $H^2(C_2; M) = 0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The inhomogeneous bar differentials for $C_2$:
$\delta^1 n = \sigma n + n$ (the norm), $\delta^2 c = \sigma c - c$ (the
invariance). The $2$-cocycles are the invariants $M^\sigma = \{(z,z)\}$;
the $2$-coboundaries are the norms $N(v,w) = (v+w, v+w)$. The norm map is
surjective onto the invariants: $N(z, 0) = (z, z)$. Hence
$H^2 = M^\sigma / N(M) = 0$. (Audit-confirmed; the certificate verifies it
on free instances of every rank, with the untyped and sign-deck hostile
controls --- the vanishing is the exchange type's own law, not a tautology.)
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The traceless exchange deck]\label{lem:traceless}
The same vanishing holds for every traceless exchange deck
$M_T = \{(x,y) \in A \oplus A : s(x) + s(y) = 0\}$ ($s$ any linear
functional): the constraint is symmetric, so the swap preserves $M_T$; the
invariants are $\{(z,z) : s(z) = 0\}$ (torsion-free); and
$N(z,0) = (z,z)$ with $s(z) + s(0) = s(z) = 0$, so $(z,0) \in M_T$ and the
norm map is surjective: $H^2(C_2; M_T) = 0$. \emph{The symmetric form of
the constraint is essential}: a non-symmetric constraint is not even
preserved by the swap (the audit's counterexample 3), and an unexchanged
direct summand obstructs (the audit's counterexample 2) --- which is
precisely why the D2/D3 trace strip of the descent stack precedes the
residue: the strip removes the unexchanged scalar part, and the traceless
constraint is symmetric because it is the trace.
\end{lemma}
