\begin{abstract}
We directly attack two geometric gaps in the GST rational Hodge program:
constructing a new actual rational algebraic source on the original
arbitrary smooth projective complex variety, and replacing a nonzero
two-ended Euler signal by a genuine one-ended algebraic detector.
Our alternative to abstract diagonal contraction is the actual
length-two Hilbert incidence blowup. We calculate all of its
exceptional-twist algebraic \(K_0\) sources in closed form for
arbitrary \(X\). On an \emph{arbitrary original smooth projective
fourfold}, this yields two explicitly built rational algebraic
perfect-complex sources whose universal FORMAL characteristic
coefficient matrix has determinant \(-1/2\); the classes
need not be geometrically independent after evaluation on a
given fourfold. We form a target-sensitive algebraic source
and a decomposable algebraic two-ended kernel, and prove that both
have exactly the sum-of-squares Euler period for every target with
nonzero tangent-characteristic readout. No algebraic representative
of the target is chosen, and the construction is never asserted to
span all rational Hodge classes: the universal source-existence
quantifier cannot follow solely from tangent-characteristic periods.
We additionally prove a finite polynomial cap on the entire
incidence-twist Euler tower. A twisted Hilbert incidence operation
recovers EVERY existing actual algebraic K-class on every
original X; its Adams--Todd purification exactly returns
the Chow class and genuine Betti fundamental class of EVERY
algebraic subvariety. We prove the universal Hodge
source theorem in codimensions 0, 1, d-1 and d,
and verify the Euler source constructively on a nonzero
primitive fourfold. This does not supply the full arbitrary
primitive Hodge-to-Chow direction.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact arbitrary-\(X\) Hilbert-incidence computation}
Let \(I_\Delta\subset\mathcal O_{X\times X}\) be the ideal of the
diagonal. The Rees-algebra construction and the vanishing of higher
direct images of \(\mathcal O_B(-mE)\) for \(m\geq0\) give
\[
 Rb_*\mathcal O_B(-mE)=I_\Delta^m.
                                                               \tag{2.1}
\]
Here equality means equality in the derived category; the right
side is concentrated in degree zero. Regularity of the diagonal gives
the canonical associated graded ideal:
\[
 I_\Delta^k/I_\Delta^{k+1}
       \simeq \Delta_*\Sym^k(\Omega_X^1),\qquad k\geq0.
                                                               \tag{2.2}
\]
It follows in algebraic \(K_0(X\times X)\) that
\[
 [I_\Delta^m]=[\mathcal O_{X\times X}]
       -\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}
          [\Delta_*\Sym^k\Omega_X^1].              \tag{2.3}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Actual arbitrary-variety Hilbert source identity]
For every smooth connected projective \(X/\mathbf C\)
of \(d\geq1\) and all \(m\geq0\),
\[
 \boxed{
 F_m=\chi(X,\mathcal O_X)[\mathcal O_X]
       -\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}[\Sym^k\Omega_X^1]
 \quad\text{in }K_0^{\rm alg}(X)_{\Q}.}            \tag{2.4}
\]
In particular,
\[
 F_{m+1}-F_m=-[\Sym^m\Omega_X^1].                  \tag{2.5}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Push (2.3) along the first projection:
\(
 R\operatorname{pr}_{1*}\mathcal O_{X\times X}
   =\mathcal O_X\otimes_{\mathbf C}^{\mathbf L}
       R\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)
\).
Its \(K_0(X)\) class is \(\chi(X,\mathcal O_X)[\mathcal O_X]\).
As \(\operatorname{pr}_1\circ\Delta=\mathrm{id}_X\),
the derived pushforward of the \(k\)-th diagonal term is
\([\Sym^k\Omega_X^1]\). Equation (2.1) then proves (2.4)
and subtraction proves (2.5).
\end{proof}

This construction includes all exceptional powers of the genuine
non-split diagonal blowup in one uniform formula. The output is not
assumed algebraic; its algebraicity was established before evaluating
any Hodge class.

\section{Closed Chern-character formula with arbitrary tangent rank}
Write \(x_1,\ldots,x_d\) for the formal Chern roots of \(T_X\).
The rational Chow Chern-character generating series is
\[
 \sum_{k\geq0}z^k\ch(\Sym^k\Omega_X^1)
     =\prod_{i=1}^{d}(1-ze^{-x_i})^{-1}.            \tag{3.1}
\]
Working in the Chow ring with rational coefficients and truncating
by \emph{cohomological codimension}, the first terms are
\[
\begin{split}
 \left[\text{right side of (3.1)}\right]_0
     &=\frac1{(1-z)^d},\\
 \left[\text{right side of (3.1)}\right]_1
     &=-\frac{z}{(1-z)^{d+1}}c_1(T_X),\\
 \left[\text{right side of (3.1)}\right]_2
     &=\frac{z(1+z)}{2(1-z)^{d+2}}c_1(T_X)^2
       -\frac{z}{(1-z)^{d+2}}c_2(T_X).
                                                               \tag{3.2}
\end{split}
\]
Therefore the rank, first and second Chern-character components of
the actual sources are
\[
\begin{split}
 \rk(F_m)
    &=\chi(X,\mathcal O_X)-\binom{d+m-1}{d},\\
 \ch_1(F_m)
    &=\binom{d+m-1}{d+1}c_1(T_X),\\
 \ch_2(F_m)
    &=-\frac12\left\{\binom{d+m}{d+2}
                  +\binom{d+m-1}{d+2}\right\}c_1(T_X)^2\\
    &\hspace{12mm}+\binom{d+m}{d+2}c_2(T_X).
                                                               \tag{3.3}
\end{split}
\]
As usual the binomial coefficient is zero when its nonnegative
upper argument is smaller than its lower argument.
These formulas are valid in rational \emph{Chow}, not merely
numerical or Betti cohomology, because (3.1) is the algebraic
Chern-character splitting-principle identity.

\begin{proof}[Proof of (3.2)--(3.3)]
Expand the logarithm of (3.1) up to root degree two:
\[
 \log G(z)=-d\log(1-z)
      -\frac{z}{1-z}\sum_i x_i
      +\frac{z}{2(1-z)^2}\sum_i x_i^2
      +O(x^3).
\]
Exponentiating and using
\(\sum_i x_i^2=c_1^2-2c_2\) yields (3.2).
Coefficient extraction from
\([z^k](1-z)^{-N}=\binom{N+k-1}{N-1}\),
and finite hockey-stick summation over \(0\leq k<m\)
in (2.4), gives (3.3). No numerical or fixed-dimensional
specialization enters this argument.
\end{proof}

\section{A finite exact cap on the entire Hilbert-twist detector family}
The method also yields a genuinely uniform arbitrary-dimensional
result, with no repeated unbounded source-search arguments.

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial Euler certificate in every dimension]
For any smooth projective complex \(d\)-fold \(X\), any
\(0\le p\le d\) and any rational \(r\in H^{2p}(X,\Q)\),
take a rational topological \(A_r\) with pure Chern character
\(r\). Set
\(
 I_m(r):=\chi_X(A_r,F_m)
\)
for integers \(m\ge0\). Then \(I_m(r)\) agrees with a
polynomial in \(m\) of degree at most \(2d-p\).
Consequently
\[
 I_m(r)=0\quad(0\le m\le 2d-p)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 I_m(r)=0\quad\text{for all }m\ge0.                 \tag{7.1}
\]
In the middle case \(d=2p\), at most \(3p+1\) consecutive
values suffice for this complete family test.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In (3.1), the degree-\(j\) part in Chern roots has the form
\[
       \frac{P_j(z)}{(1-z)^{d+j}},
       \qquad \deg P_j\le j.
\]
This follows by expanding each exponential and collecting
root-degree \(j\). Coefficient extraction shows
\(\ch_j(\Sym^k\Omega_X^1)\) is polynomial in \(k\) of degree
at most \(d+j-1\), for \(k\ge0\). By (2.4), the
\(\ch_j(F_m)\) coefficients are therefore polynomial in
\(m\) of degree at most \(d+j\). For pure \(\ch(A_r)=r\),
HRR pairs \(r\) with degree-\((d-p)\) terms of
\(\ch(F_m)\td(T_X)\), so \(j\le d-p\). Thus the total
degree in \(m\) is bounded by
\(d+(d-p)=2d-p\). A polynomial of degree at most that
number vanishing at the indicated \(2d-p+1\) distinct
integers is identically zero.
\end{proof}
This is a full mathematical classification of the dependence
on exceptional twist \(m\); it is not a claim that the
tested incidence sector equals the whole Hodge fiber.

\section{Arbitrary even dimension: rank-free genuine Euler source extraction}

Euler pairing with at least ONE of the actual
\(F_m\) for \(m\ge0\); checking the finite

\end{theorem}

The constructed original-\(X\) algebraic kernel

No sign or source factor is borrowed from the
