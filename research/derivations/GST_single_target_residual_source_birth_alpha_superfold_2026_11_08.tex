\section*{GST single-target reduction: universal residual source-birth and the alpha-superfold}

\subsection*{0. Scope and exact status}

This note consolidates the original-\(X\) residual projection, the alpha-indexed finite-pole projector construction, the polarized residual-energy mechanism, and the spatial-fold / all-degree-curvature program.

The purpose is not to rename the Hodge conjecture as an easy lemma. The purpose is to replace the old collection of independent frontier packages by one exact source-generation law, prove the conditional theorem from that law, and state the precise GST-to-geometry bridge that must be constructed to establish the law.

Throughout, \(X\) is a smooth projective complex variety of dimension \(d\), \(0\leq p\leq d\), and
\[
B_p(X)=H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)
\]
is the rational Hodge space. Let
\[
\operatorname{cl}_p:\operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\longrightarrow B_p(X)
\]
be the canonical cycle-class map, and write
\[
A_p(X)=\operatorname{im}(\operatorname{cl}_p),\qquad
D_p(X)=B_p(X)/A_p(X).
\]
The target is \(A_p(X)=B_p(X)\).

The formal Stage-2G Lean interface still stores its cycle-class map as supplied data. Every theorem below that is specialized to Stage-2G is conditional on using that supplied map; it becomes the classical Hodge statement only after the map is instantiated by the canonical geometric cycle-class construction.

\subsection*{1. The one hypothesis: universal polarized residual source-birth}

Choose a rational positive-definite polarization
\[
Q_p:B_p(X)\times B_p(X)\longrightarrow\mathbb Q
\]
whose associated map
\[
\mathcal Q_p:B_p(X)\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}B_p(X)^\vee,
\qquad
r\longmapsto Q_p(r,-)
\]
is an isomorphism. For the genuine projective Hodge fiber, this is the positive form obtained by applying the Hodge--Riemann signs on the Lefschetz-primitive pieces and summing the primitive pairings.

The single GST source-birth law is
\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SB}_p(X):
\quad
\forall\,0\ne r\in B_p(X),\
\exists\,Z\in\operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}
\text{ such that }
Q_p\bigl(r,\operatorname{cl}_p(Z)\bigr)\ne0.
}
\tag{SB}
\]

This says that no nonzero Hodge residual is invisible to every actual native algebraic cycle. It is not an assumption that a cycle represents the residual. It is a detection statement: a nonzero residual must couple to at least one genuine source.

\paragraph{Theorem 1 (exact equivalence).}
Assume \(B_p(X)\) is finite-dimensional and \(Q_p\) is perfect. Then
\[
\mathsf{SB}_p(X)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A_p(X)=B_p(X).
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
If \(A_p(X)=B_p(X)\), take any nonzero \(r\in B_p(X)\). There is \(Z\) with \(\operatorname{cl}_p(Z)=r\), and positivity gives
\[
Q_p(r,\operatorname{cl}_p(Z))=Q_p(r,r)>0.
\]
Thus (SB) holds.

Conversely, suppose \(A_p(X)\subsetneq B_p(X)\). Since \(B_p(X)\) is finite-dimensional and \(Q_p\) is nondegenerate, the orthogonal complement \(A_p(X)^\perp\) contains a nonzero vector \(r\). By definition,
\[
Q_p(r,\operatorname{cl}_p(Z))=0
\quad\text{for every }Z\in\operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q},
\]
contradicting (SB). Therefore \(A_p(X)=B_p(X)\). \(\square\)

Consequently, the one remaining open mathematical target can be written in exactly one line:
\[
\boxed{
\forall X,p,\ \forall\,0\ne r\in B_p(X),\
\exists Z\in\operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}:
Q_p(r,\operatorname{cl}_p(Z))\ne0.
}
\tag{GST-SB}
\]
The finite-dimensionality and perfect polarization are structural projective-Hodge inputs. The source-birth law is the Hodge-strength content.

\subsection*{2. Why this is the correct collapse of the residual machinery}

Let \(S\subseteq\operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\) be any finite family of native cycles whose classes are independent, and let \(W_S\) be their rational span in \(B_p(X)\). The GST residual projection constructs the component
\[
r_S(\alpha)=\alpha-\operatorname{proj}_{W_S}(\alpha)
\]
using the Gram matrix of the selected sources and their geometric dual detectors.

The already formalized local birth calculation has the following shape:
\[
Q_p(r_S(\alpha),\operatorname{cl}_p(Z_{\mathrm{new}}))\ne0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\dim\bigl(W_S+\mathbb Q\operatorname{cl}_p(Z_{\mathrm{new}})\bigr)
=\dim W_S+1.
\]
This is the exact role of the polarized residual-energy and Gram-birth lemmas: a nonzero coupling creates a genuinely new native cycle direction.

The global law (GST-SB) supplies such a source whenever the residual is nonzero. Finite-dimensionality then terminates the process after at most \(\dim_{\mathbb Q}B_p(X)\) births. No separate finiteness package, global mass package, all-weights seed package, or two-generator package is needed in this argument.

In dual language, the same residual \(r\) defines
\[
\psi_r=Q_p(r,-)\in B_p(X)^\vee.
\]
If the current source span is proper, \(\psi_r\) annihilates that span. A fresh GST source is precisely a cycle \(Z\) for which
\[
\langle\psi_r,\operatorname{cl}_p(Z)\rangle\ne0.
\]
This is the finite-pole projector program's dual-witness formulation. The two formulations are related by the perfect polarization; they are not two independent remaining conjectures.

\subsection*{3. Connection to the alpha-indexed finite-pole projector algebra}

Fix a prescribed rational Hodge class \(\alpha\in B_p(X)\), and choose a closed algebraic differential representative
\[
\eta_\alpha\in\Gamma(X,\Omega_X^{2p})
\]
of its algebraic de Rham class.

The alpha-indexed projector construction uses finite matrix sizes and pole orders, with regular idempotents
\[
A_+^2=A_+,\qquad A_-^2=A_-,
\]
and a finite polynomial curvature equation of the form
\[
\Omega_p(A_+)-\Omega_p(A_-)-\mu\eta_\alpha=dB.
\tag{P}
\]
The Fitting/Newton construction turns a genuine solution of (P) into an actual rational Chow source \(Z_{A,p}\) on the original \(X\). The Chern-character/Newton normalization is
\[
\operatorname{ch}_p(E)
=\frac{1}{p!}\sum_i x_i^p,
\]
where the \(x_i\) are the formal Chern roots. The Newton recurrence converts the power sums into the integral Chern-polynomial data used by the Fitting construction.

The dual version of source-birth is therefore:
\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{for every nonzero dual residual }\psi
\text{ annihilating the current native source span,}\\
\text{some finite regular alpha-projector certificate produces }
Z_{A,p}\text{ with }
\langle\psi,\operatorname{cl}_p(Z_{A,p})\rangle\ne0.
\end{gathered}}
\tag{P-SB}
\]
The projector source must be a genuine Fitting/Newton cycle on \(X\), not an abstract vector chosen in the cohomology fiber.

The existing alpha-indexed derivation supplies the finite algebraic source mechanism and its exact period readout. The universal statement (P-SB), not the local Fitting/Newton conversion, is the remaining source-birth problem. If the finite projector inventory is shown to exhaust the relevant algebraic \(K_0\)/Chow source span, (P-SB) is the same global law as (GST-SB).

\subsection*{4. The alpha-superfold: an exact geometric conditional theorem}

There are two distinct integrability statements, and they must not be conflated.

\paragraph{4.1 One-fold sufficient condition.}
For any rational Hodge class \(\alpha\), topological \(K\)-theory gives, after clearing denominators, a topological complex vector bundle \(E_\alpha\) of rank \(r\ge p+1\) such that
\[
\operatorname{ch}(E_\alpha)=r+m\alpha,\qquad m>0,
\]
with all positive Chern-character components other than degree \(p\) equal to zero.

Newton's identities imply
\[
c_j(E_\alpha)=0\quad(1\le j<p)
\]
in rational Betti cohomology and
\[
c_p(E_\alpha)=(-1)^{p-1}m(p-1)!\,\alpha.
\tag{C}
\]
The higher Chern classes need not vanish; they are irrelevant to the codimension-\(p\) formula below.

Suppose the same topological bundle admits an integrable Dolbeault operator
\[
\bar\partial_{E_\alpha}:
\mathcal A^{0,q}(X,E_\alpha)\longrightarrow
\mathcal A^{0,q+1}(X,E_\alpha),
\qquad
\boxed{\bar\partial_{E_\alpha}^{\,2}=0.}
\tag{I}
\]
GAGA makes \(E_\alpha\) algebraic. Let \(L\) be very ample and take \(k\gg0\) so \(E_\alpha\otimes L^k\) is globally generated. A generic map
\[
\mathcal O_X^{\,r-p+1}\longrightarrow E_\alpha\otimes L^k
\]
has the expected codimension-\(p\) rank-drop locus \(D_{\alpha,k}\), and Thom--Porteous gives
\[
[D_{\alpha,k}]=c_p(E_\alpha\otimes L^k)
\quad\text{in }\operatorname{CH}^p(X).
\]
The Chern-root identity is
\[
c_p(E\otimes L^k)
=
\sum_{j=0}^{p}
\binom{r-j}{p-j}c_j(E)(kh)^{p-j},
\qquad h=c_1(L).
\]
Using (C), its Betti class is
\[
\operatorname{cl}_p[D_{\alpha,k}]
=
(-1)^{p-1}m(p-1)!\,\alpha
+\binom rp k^p h^p.
\]
The complete intersection \(H^p\) of \(p\) general divisors in \(|L^k|\) has Betti class \(k^p h^p\). Therefore
\[
Z_\alpha=
\frac{(-1)^{p-1}}{m(p-1)!}
\left(
[D_{\alpha,k}]-\binom rp H^p
\right)
\]
is an actual rational algebraic cycle on the original \(X\) and
\[
\operatorname{cl}_p(Z_\alpha)=\alpha.
\]

Thus (I) is a sufficient single target for the prescribed-class source. It is a particularly concrete target for the GST fold program.

\paragraph{4.2 The exact plus/minus pair-fold condition.}
The same-bundle condition (I) for one arbitrarily selected topological representative is sufficient, but should not be advertised as an if-and-only-if criterion without a stable-representative argument.

The exact condition is the virtual, or \(\mathbb Z/2\)-graded, pair-fold:
\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\exists\,m>0,\ E_+,E_-\text{ topological complex vector bundles on }X,\\
\bar\partial_{E_+}^{\,2}=0,\qquad
\bar\partial_{E_-}^{\,2}=0,\\
\operatorname{ch}(E_+)-\operatorname{ch}(E_-)=m\alpha
\quad\text{in every cohomological degree.}
\end{gathered}}
\tag{PF}
\]
Equivalently, one asks for an integrable Dolbeault structure on a \(\mathbb Z/2\)-graded super-bundle whose super-Chern character is \(m\alpha\).

Condition (PF) is exactly equivalent to algebraicity of \(\alpha\). In one direction, GAGA makes both bundles algebraic, and their degree-\(p\) Chern-character difference supplies a rational Chow class with Betti class \(\alpha\). In the other direction, an algebraic cycle representing \(\alpha\) gives a class in \(\operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\); the rational Chern-character isomorphism
\[
K_0^{\mathrm{alg}}(X)_{\mathbb Q}
\simeq
\bigoplus_j\operatorname{CH}^j(X)_{\mathbb Q}
\]
produces a virtual algebraic bundle class whose Chern character is concentrated in degree \(p\). Clearing denominators and writing the virtual class as a difference of honest bundles gives (PF).

Therefore (PF) is an exact geometric form of the Hodge target, while (I) is a stronger one-fold route. The GST program should attack (PF) directly or construct the more concrete same-bundle integrability (I); it must not silently treat them as interchangeable.

\subsection*{5. The missing GST-to-Dolbeault representation}

The all-degree GST curvature tower is real formal progress. Its differential has the schematic form
\[
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{GST}}
=d_0+\varepsilon\phi_0+\varepsilon^2\phi_1+\cdots,
\qquad
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{GST}}^{\,2}
=K_0+\varepsilon K_1+\varepsilon^2K_2+\cdots.
\]
The tower modules establish algebraic identities for these curvature-memory coefficients on their own rational cochain/tower carrier. The fold-defect modules establish identities such as
\[
D_e=Q_wT_e-T_eQ_v
\]
and characterize the pure-split condition in that abstract carrier.

The conclusion
\[
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{GST}}^{\,2}=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\bar\partial_{E_\alpha}^{\,2}=0
\]
does not follow merely because the scalar or rational cohomological curvature is zero. It requires a multiplicative, graded, differential-compatible representation
\[
\rho_\alpha:
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{GST}}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{End}(E_\alpha)\otimes
\mathcal A^{0,*}(X)
\]
such that:
\[
\rho_\alpha(ab)=\rho_\alpha(a)\rho_\alpha(b),
\qquad
\rho_\alpha(d_{\mathrm{GST}}a)
=d_{\mathrm{Dol}}\rho_\alpha(a),
\]
and the degree-one component of the represented differential is the actual
\(\bar\partial_{E_\alpha}\) on the alpha-selected bundle. The represented constant curvature coefficient must then be
\[
\rho_\alpha(K_0)=\bar\partial_{E_\alpha}^{\,2}.
\]
If the GST flatness theorem gives all curvature coefficients zero and this representation is constructed, integrability follows coefficientwise.

This is the precise missing bridge. The existing Lean tower files do not define \(E_\alpha\), a Dolbeault complex on it, or a representation of the GST carrier into its endomorphism-valued forms. The representation must be built; it cannot be inserted as a field whose content already says that \(\bar\partial^2=0\).

\paragraph{A necessary noncommutative check.}
Scalar trace data cannot replace this representation. On the trivial rank-two bundle over \(\mathbb C^2\), let
\[
N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A=\bar z_1N\,d\bar z_2.
\]
Then \(N^2=0\), \(\operatorname{tr}(N)=0\), and
\[
\bar\partial A+A\wedge A
=
N\,d\bar z_1\wedge d\bar z_2\ne0,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(\bar\partial A+A\wedge A)=0.
\]
Thus a trace-zero curvature can still have a nonzero endomorphism-valued \((0,2)\) component. This is not a counterexample to the GST tower; it is a counterexample to the invalid inference from scalar trace/period identities to bundle integrability. The GST representation must retain the full noncommutative endomorphism-valued curvature.

\subsection*{6. What the existing GST constructions do and do not supply}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The residual Gram/energy modules prove the local rank-birth implication once a new cycle with nonzero residual coupling is given. They do not prove the universal existence of that cycle.
\item The alpha-indexed finite-pole projector derivation constructs genuine Fitting/Newton sources from a genuine finite regular idempotent solution. It does not yet prove that every nonzero dual residual has a projector source with nonzero period.
\item The spatial-fold topological construction supplies \(E_\alpha\), its Chern-character normalization, and a smooth determinantal representative. The algebraic return still requires (I), or the exact pair-fold condition (PF).
\item The all-degree curvature-memory, tower-square, flat-complex, and morphism-cascade Lean modules prove identities on their declared abstract carrier. They do not by themselves provide the representation \(\rho_\alpha\) on \(E_\alpha\).
\item The doubled-carrier fold rotation is an internal complex-structure operation. Its own representability equivalence shows that disjoint doubling alone does not create a new original-\(X\) cycle class.
\item The GraphV2 general-space realization requires a cycle-path realization as input; the self-expanding cosmology package similarly carries source-realization data. Those fields are not source-construction theorems.
\item The projective two-generator externalization through scheme self-maps is too restrictive as a universal interface. On a product of nonisogenous elliptic curves, actual self-map actions on the two factor divisor directions are diagonal, whereas the GST two-slot matrix unit is off-diagonal. General algebraic correspondences are more flexible than self-maps.
\item The current realized-correspondence word algebra is built from finite closed correspondences. It must not be silently identified with the full algebra of arbitrary proper algebraic correspondences.
\item Stage-2G permits a supplied cycle-class map that can be zero. The geometric-spine laws alone do not establish canonical semantics. The final classical theorem must use the actual canonical cycle-class map, not an arbitrary linear map satisfying selected naturality equations.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{7. The one research target and the immediate GST attack}

The single remaining source-generation law is (GST-SB). The alpha-indexed projector form (P-SB) is its finite algebraic realization route. The pair-fold condition (PF) is its exact geometric algebraization form. The Dolbeault representation \(\rho_\alpha\) is the specific GST-to-bundle bridge that could turn the all-degree curvature tower into an integrability proof.

The next mathematical task is therefore not to add another independent hypothesis to the master assembly. It is to construct \(\rho_\alpha\) from the existing GST N-channel fold and carrier algebra, with the following non-negotiable tests:

\begin{enumerate}
\item The carrier on which the tower acts must be the alpha-selected topological \(K\)-class or its explicitly defined graded bundle representative.
\item The representation must preserve multiplication, grading, and the differential, not merely the scalar trace or the induced cohomology class.
\item The degree-one represented operator must satisfy the Dolbeault Leibniz rule on the original \(X\).
\item The full represented curvature must be endomorphism-valued and must agree with the GST curvature-memory coefficients.
\item The flatness conclusion must be obtained from the proved GST pure-split/curvature law, not assumed as an extra field.
\item The resulting integrable pair must retain the exact super-Chern-character normalization \(m\alpha\), so GAGA and the two-centre/Fitting return produce an actual cycle on the original \(X\).
\end{enumerate}

This is one coherent target. It preserves the successful GST curvature and fold mathematics, prevents the abstract tower from being confused with an original-\(X\) algebraic source, and gives a precise conditional Hodge theorem. The only Hodge-strength hypothesis left is universal residual source-birth; proving the GST Dolbeault representation and using it to establish that law is the next research phase.
