\begin{abstract}
We continue the exact arbitrary-original-$X$ source-construction objective
past linear Hopf/projective/Gysin fold limitations by testing a genuinely
different operation: multiplying two independently born anti-invariant
algebraic divisors on a double-world cover before returning them to $X$.
Although each anti-divisor has zero pushforward individually, their
product is invariant and can carry a nonzero primitive original-$X$
cycle. An explicit sign-controlled finite-cover construction realizes
arbitrary signed decompositions of a reduced intersection of two
Cartier divisors; a full calculation on the smooth quadric fourfold
produces its nonambient primitive codimension-two class directly as
a transferred product of two anticharacter sources. This constructs
genuine algebraic source geometry and provides a cofinal cycle
factory under enumerated incidence inputs. It does not show that
every rational Hodge residual pairs nontrivially with that factory;
that separate assertion is the actual conjectural universal step.
\end{abstract}

\section{Original contract and why a nonlinear source is different}
For every smooth projective complex $X$, $p\ge0$ and
$\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)$, the fixed goal is
an actual $Z_\alpha\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}$ with
$\operatorname{cl}^{\rm can}_X(Z_\alpha)=\alpha$.
Let $f:\widetilde Y\to X$ be an actual degree-two generically
finite projective morphism from a smooth projective double-world
resolution, with involution $\tau$.
A $\tau$-anti-invariant algebraic class $\Delta$ satisfies
$f_*\Delta=0$ in rational Chow, as
$f_*\Delta=f_*\tau^*\Delta=-f_*\Delta$.
But for two anti-invariant classes $\Delta_1,\Delta_2$,
\[
 \tau^*(\Delta_1\cdot\Delta_2)
 =\Delta_1\cdot\Delta_2,\qquad
 f_*(\Delta_1\cdot\Delta_2)\in CH^2(X)_{\mathbb Q}. \tag{1}
\]
This quadratic source channel is not a linear deck-invariant
projection and does not presuppose a chosen Chow preimage of a
rational Hodge target. We now construct its source from real
scheme equations.

\section{Exact signed-incidence source theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Arbitrary original-$X$ double-world sign extraction]
Let $X$ be a smooth projective complex variety of dimension
$d\ge2$. Let $W_1,W_2\subset X$ be distinct integral effective
Cartier divisors with a proper, \emph{reduced} scheme intersection
\[
 C=W_1\cap W_2=C_1\cup\cdots\cup C_m,
 \qquad \operatorname{codim}_X C_j=2 .
\]
Fix \emph{any} signs $\varepsilon_j\in\{+1,-1\}$.
There exist an algebraic line bundle $L=\mathcal O_X(kH)$,
$k\gg0$, global sections $A_1,A_2\in H^0(X,L)$ and
$F\in H^0(X,L^{\otimes2})$ for which
\[
 F|_{W_i}=A_i^2|_{W_i},\quad
 A_2|_{\eta_{C_j}}=\varepsilon_j A_1|_{\eta_{C_j}}\ne0. \tag{2}
\]
Let $Y_0\to X$ be the actual algebraic double cover $t^2=F$,
and let $\widetilde Y$ be an equivariant smooth projective
resolution of its normalization. Above $W_i$ there are two
proper-transform divisors $W_{i,+},W_{i,-}$ mapping birationally
to $W_i$, exchanged by $\tau$. Put
$\Delta_i=[W_{i,+}]-[W_{i,-}]\in CH^1(\widetilde Y)_{\mathbb Q}$.
Then the actual \emph{original-$X$} Chow cycle
\[
 \boxed{
  f_*(\Delta_1\cdot\Delta_2)
  =2\sum_{j=1}^m\varepsilon_j[C_j]
  \quad\text{in }CH^2(X)_{\mathbb Q}.
 } \tag{3}
\]
Its canonical rational Betti class is exactly the cycle class
of the right-hand side. A cover whose branch is singular
is allowed: the resolution and original-$X$ proper Chow return
are genuine algebraic operations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $\nu:\coprod_j\widetilde C_j\to C$ for the finite
normalization. Since $C$ is reduced, its conductor
$\mathfrak c\subset\mathcal O_C$ is a coherent ideal whose
generic localization on every $C_j$ is the unit ideal.
Choose $k$ large enough that $\mathfrak c\otimes L|_C$
is globally generated and that the Serre restriction maps
$H^0(X,L)\to H^0(C,L|_C)$ and
$H^0(X,L^2)\to H^0(W_1\cup W_2,L^2)$ are surjective.
Choose $a\in H^0(C,\mathfrak c\otimes L)$ nonzero at the
generic point of every $C_j$. Multiplication on the
normalization by the locally constant sign function
$\varepsilon|_{\widetilde C_j}=\varepsilon_j$ preserves
the conductor; hence $b=\varepsilon a$ is a genuine global
section of $L|_C$ with $b^2=a^2$ \emph{scheme-theoretically}
on $C$ and the prescribed generic sign ratios.
Lift $a,b$ to global sections $A_1,A_2$ on $X$.
The squares $A_1^2|_{W_1}$ and $A_2^2|_{W_2}$ agree on
the full scheme intersection $C$. The exact gluing
sequence for $W_1\cup W_2$ and Serre surjectivity produce
a global $F$ with (2). This is a true algebraic
double-cover datum independent of any prescribed $\alpha$.

At each generic point $\eta_{C_j}$, $A_1$ is invertible
and the finite double cover is etale. The two lifts of
each $W_i$ are locally disjoint sheets. Because $C$ is
reduced and its divisors meet properly, the local
intersection multiplicity of the \emph{matching} sheets
is one and that of the mismatched sheets is zero.
For $\varepsilon_j=+1$, the pair intersections
$(W_{1,+},W_{2,+})$ and $(W_{1,-},W_{2,-})$
each contribute $+[C_j]$ to the pushforward of the
signed product. For $\varepsilon_j=-1$, the pairs
$(W_{1,+},W_{2,-})$ and $(W_{1,-},W_{2,+})$
each contribute $-[C_j]$. There are no additional
codimension-two components in the pushforward: the
intersection maps into the scheme support $W_1\cap W_2$,
and exceptional cycles over proper closed subsets of its
components map into codimension at least three on $X$.
Therefore the actual Chow intersection and proper
pushforward yield (3). Natural compatibility of genuine
rational Betti cycle classes with intersection and proper
pushforward gives the final assertion.
\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}[One-component signed nonlinear extractor]
Under the theorem's hypotheses, choose $\varepsilon_j=+1$
for one selected component $C_j$ and $-1$ for every
other component. Then the exact original-$X$ cycle identity
\[
 \boxed{
 [C_j]=\frac14 f_*(\Delta_1\cdot\Delta_2)
             +\frac12[W_1]\cdot[W_2]
 \quad\text{in } CH^2(X)_{\mathbb Q}
 } \tag{4}
\]
recovers that component from a quadratic double-world
anticharacter product and a known ambient intersection.
Enumerating all divisor pairs and all finite sign patterns
requires no prior choice of an $\alpha$-preimage.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The reduced complete intersection has
$[W_1][W_2]=\sum_i[C_i]$.
For the chosen signs (3) equals
$2([C_j]-\sum_{i\ne j}[C_i])
=4[C_j]-2[W_1][W_2]$. Rearrange.
\end{proof}


\begin{corollary}[Exact target-directed signed-cover source amplification]
Assume $\dim X=d\ge4$ and let
$r_\alpha\in H^4(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{2,2}(X)$
be any prescribed rational primitive target or genuine
GST polarized residual. With an ample algebraic class $h$ define
\[
  \ell_j=\int_X r_\alpha\cup
     \operatorname{cl}^{\rm can}_X([C_j])\cup h^{d-4}\in\mathbb Q .
\]
For the signed-cover output $Z_{\boldsymbol\varepsilon}=
f_*(\Delta_1\Delta_2)$,
\[
 \boxed{
  \max_{\varepsilon_j=\pm1}
  \int_X r_\alpha\cup\operatorname{cl}^{\rm can}_X
               (Z_{\boldsymbol\varepsilon})\cup h^{d-4}
    =2\sum_{j=1}^m|\ell_j|. }                         \tag{8}
\]
The maximizing cover is obtained explicitly by choosing
$\varepsilon_j=+1$ when $\ell_j\ge0$ and $-1$ otherwise,
without a target Chow preimage. Therefore a single
realizable reduced incidence tuple with at least one
$\ell_j\ne0$ gives an \emph{actual positive original-$X$
primitive period} and a genuine new native source.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Pair the canonical cycle class of equation (3) with
$r_\alpha h^{d-4}$. The result is
$2\sum_j\varepsilon_j\ell_j$.
Each summand is bounded above by $2|\ell_j|$ and the
stated independent sign choice attains every bound.
The sign theorem constructs the required double cover
for precisely this choice. Thus the supremum is a
finite attained rational maximum and (8) holds.
\end{proof}
This is an unconditional optimizer for any given
\emph{genuine incidence periods}, not an independent proof
that some incidence period is nonzero for every arbitrary
nonzero residual. If all $\ell_j$ vanish for every tuple,
(8) correctly returns zero. The full arbitrary-$X$
Hodge algebraicity question is exactly whether this
possible residual annihilator must vanish.


\section{Higher codimension nonlinear anti-deck source with target-directed optimization}
The original degree-two signed source is a codimension-two
CHOW class. Multiplying it with genuinely algebraic
Cartier divisors gives an ACTUAL arbitrary-codimension
nonlinear construction that is still computed in original
$X$ and is sensitive to a prescribed higher primitive
target.

\begin{theorem}[ALL original $X$, ALL codimensions $2\le p\le d$: true nonlinear geometric anti-character source return]
Let $X$ be ANY smooth projective complex $d$-fold,
let $2\le p\le d$, and choose two effective integral
Cartier divisors $W_1,W_2$ whose intersection is
proper, pure codimension two and reduced:
\[
 C=W_1\cap W_2=\bigcup_{j=1}^{m}C_j.
\]
Choose the finite algebraic double cover $f:\widetilde
Y\to X$ and its two true signed algebraic
divisor classes $\Delta_1,\Delta_2\in
CH^1(\widetilde Y)_{\mathbb Q}$ by the original
signed-incidence theorem, for ANY signs
$\varepsilon_j\in\{\pm1\}$.
For arbitrary actual algebraic Cartier divisors
$H_3,\ldots,H_p$ on $X$, assume the relevant
intersections are proper. Define
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal S_{\varepsilon;H_3,\ldots,H_p}
 =f_*\!\left(
    \Delta_1\cdot\Delta_2
    \cdot\prod_{i=3}^{p}f^*[H_i]
       \right)\in CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}.}       \tag{12}
\]
Then the equality in genuine original-$X$ Chow is
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal S_{\varepsilon;H_3,\ldots,H_p}
 =2\sum_{j=1}^{m}\varepsilon_j
       [C_j]\cdot\prod_{i=3}^{p}[H_i].
 }                                           \tag{13}
\]
For an arbitrary prescribed rational $(p,p)$
class $r_\alpha\in H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)$
with $d\ge2p$, put
\[
 \lambda_j=(-1)^p
  \int_X r_\alpha\cup\operatorname{cl}^{can}[C_j]
       \cup\bigcup_{i=3}^{p}
         \operatorname{cl}^{can}[H_i]
       \cup h^{d-2p}\in\mathbb Q .
\]
Then an ACTUAL algebraic cover chosen FROM
$r_\alpha$ by $\varepsilon_j=\operatorname{sgn}
\lambda_j$ (choose $+1$ if zero) realizes
\[
 \boxed{
 \max_\varepsilon (-1)^p
  \int_X r_\alpha\cup
       \operatorname{cl}^{can}
         (\mathcal S_{\varepsilon;H_3,\ldots,H_p})
       \cup h^{d-2p}
       =2\sum_j|\lambda_j|.}                 \tag{14}
\]
All objects in (12)--(14) are constructed
independently of a chosen original-$X$
Chow preimage of $r_\alpha$. The maximum may
be zero; nonzero coupling follows exactly
when one independently constructed incidence
component has nonzero period.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The cover, signed divisors and original
codimension-two CHOW identity are produced
by the original theorem with arbitrary
component signs. The proper-projection formula
for actual rational Chow groups gives
\[
 f_*\left(\Delta_1\Delta_2
              \prod_{i=3}^{p} f^*H_i\right)
 =f_*(\Delta_1\Delta_2)
                   \prod_{i=3}^{p} H_i.
\]
Substitute the signed source theorem
$f_*(\Delta_1\Delta_2)=
  2\sum_j\varepsilon_j[C_j]$.
This proves (13) in actual Chow,
and applying canonical Betti comparison proves
the stated period formula. Each
$\varepsilon_j$ may be set independently by
the algebraic conductor sign interpolation
of the first source theorem. Maximization
termwise proves (14), and its chosen cover
is an actual alpha-sensitive algebraic
construction rather than a hypothetical
source basis.
\end{proof}

\section{Multi-world character conservation and the obstruction to naive all-component interpolation}
The original double-world can only push
products with TRIVIAL total deck character
to the original variety. This has an exact
higher-codimension generalization and shows
when simply adding more folded worlds cannot
force arbitrary component coefficients.

\begin{theorem}[Finite abelian GST world-charge conservation in original Chow]
Let $X$ be any smooth projective complex variety,
and let $f:Y\to X$ be the proper generically
finite G-equivariant morphism from a SMOOTH
projective equivariant resolution $Y$ of
a finite normal Galois cover of $X$,
with finite abelian deck group $G$.
Let $\chi_i:G\to\mathbb C^\times$ be characters
and let $\Delta_i\in CH^{q_i}(Y)_{\mathbb C}$
be genuine algebraic eigenclasses:
\[
 g^*\Delta_i=\chi_i(g)\Delta_i
       \quad\text{for every }g\in G.
\]
Set $p=\sum_i q_i$. Then
\[
 \boxed{
  \left(\prod_i\chi_i\ne1\right)
  \Longrightarrow
  f_*\!\left(\prod_i\Delta_i\right)=0
       \quad\text{in }CH^p(X)_{\mathbb C}.}  \tag{15}
\]
For a double-world $G=\mathbb Z/2$,
an odd product of anti-deck eigenclasses
has identically zero pushforward.
For the biquadratic $(\mathbb Z/2)^2$
with independent nontrivial characters
$a,b$ and $ab$, a TRIPLE eigenproduct of
types $(a,b,ab)$ is character-neutral,
so (15) does not kill its original-$X$
algebraic Chow output.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $W=f_*\prod_i\Delta_i$.
Because $f\circ g=f$, the proper Chow
pushforward gives
$W=f_*g^*(\prod_i\Delta_i)$
for an equivariant finite cover of
degree one on its automorphisms.
The right side is
$(\prod_i\chi_i(g))W$.
If the total character is nontrivial,
choose $g$ on which it is not $1$;
over $\mathbb C$ this forces $W=0$.
For the double group the nontrivial
character has sign $-1$.
For $(\mathbb Z/2)^2$, $a\cdot b\cdot ab=1$;
the assertion of allowed parity does NOT
by itself assert a nonzero Chow period.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All-original-$X$ obstruction to arbitrary higher-component signs when pairwise incidence is integral]
Fix $p\ge3$ and actual effective integral
Cartier divisors $W_1,\ldots,W_p$ on a smooth
projective original $X$. Assume every
pairwise scheme intersection $W_i\cap W_k$
is reduced and INTEGRAL, and their full
scheme intersection $C$ is reduced and
pure codimension $p$ with irreducible
components $C_j$. Let
\[
 t_a^2=F_a\qquad(a=1,\ldots,r)
\]
define a simultaneous $(\mathbb Z/2)^r$
algebraic cover whose restriction to
every $W_i$ is GENERICALLY split via
chosen globally algebraic roots
$A_{i,a}\in H^0(W_i,L_a|_{W_i})$,
so $A_{i,a}^2=F_a|_{W_i}$ and every
$A_{i,a}$ is nonzero at the generic point
of every $C_j$.

For ANY characters $\chi_1,\ldots,\chi_p$
with $\prod_i\chi_i=1$,
and their actual corresponding
eigen-divisor classes
$\Delta_{i,\chi_i}$ on the resolved cover,
the pushforward's coefficient at
EVERY generic $C_j$ is THE SAME
character-dependent scalar $\kappa$:
\[
 \boxed{
  f_*\Bigl(\prod_{i=1}^{p}
                  \Delta_{i,\chi_i}\Bigr)
     =\kappa\sum_j[C_j]
     =\kappa[W_1]\cdots[W_p]
        \quad\text{in } CH^p(X)_{\mathbb C}.} \tag{16}
\]
In particular, in the stated integral-pairwise
class of covers, increasing the number of
split unramified sign-worlds cannot isolate
one higher-codimension component with
independently chosen signs; the total
original-$X$ class remains the FULL ambient
Cartier intersection.
This obstruction is specific to the
split-root, generically unramified,
pairwise-integral construction, not to
all nonlinear GST source births.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each a and pair i,k, the two roots
on the actual integral scheme $W_i\cap W_k$
obey
\[
 (A_{i,a}-A_{k,a})(A_{i,a}+A_{k,a})=0
\]
as sections of $L_a^{\otimes2}$.
Because the coordinate ring of any
nonempty affine trivialization of an
integral scheme has no zero divisors,
and two distinct nonzero global
sections cannot have zero product
on an integral scheme, one entire
factor vanishes: $A_{i,a}=
s_{ik,a}A_{k,a}$ with
$s_{ik,a}\in\{+1,-1\}$ CONSTANT
across this WHOLE pairwise intersection.
Choose $W_1$ as reference and use
$s_{i1,a}$ for each $i,a$.
These fixed relative root phases
hold along EVERY full component $C_j$,
since $C_j\subset W_i\cap W_1$.
At a generic point of each $C_j$
the cover is etale and completely split.
The chosen eigen-divisor has the local
orbit sum
$\Delta_{i,\chi_i}=
  \sum_{g\in G}\chi_i(g)^{-1}[D_{i,g}]$,
and the components of the geometric
intersection are indexed by
a common lift $g\in G$. The local
multiplicity is one because the
scheme intersection is reduced.
The root-phase of the $i$th chosen
sheet relative to $W_1$ is the
SAME fixed group element $s_i\in G$
on each $C_j$. Therefore its coefficient
at every C_j is
\[
 \left(\prod_i\chi_i(s_i)^{-1}\right)
     \sum_{g\in G}
           \left(\prod_i\chi_i(g)^{-1}\right)
 =|G|\prod_i\chi_i(s_i)^{-1},
\]
which is independent of $j$ when
$\prod_i\chi_i=1$.
All other contributions push to
codimension greater than p or vanish
in $CH^p(X)$, since every generic
$C_j$ is etale and regular in the
resolved world.
Finally the original reduced proper
complete intersection has fundamental
cycle $\sum_j[C_j]=[W_1]\cdots[W_p]$
in genuine Chow, proving (16).
\end{proof}

\section{An explicit nonambient source: the smooth quadric fourfold}
\begin{theorem}[Actual anti-times-anti birth of the quadric primitive]
Let
\[
 X=Q^4=\{x_0y_0+x_1y_1+x_2y_2=0\}\subset\mathbb P^5,
 \quad W_1=\{y_1=0\}|_X,\quad W_2=\{y_2=0\}|_X.
\]
Their true reduced codimension-two intersection is
$C=P_A\cup P_B$, with
$P_A=\{y_1=y_2=x_0=0\}\cap X$ and
$P_B=\{y_1=y_2=y_0=0\}\cap X$.
Set $L=\mathcal O_X(1)$ and use the actual quadratic branch
\[
 \boxed{
 F=(x_0+y_0)^2+4x_1y_1
   =(x_0-y_0)^2-4x_2y_2\quad\text{on }X.
 } \tag{5}
\]
The corresponding algebraic double cover $t^2=F$ is
generically degree two (it can be singular); take a
projective equivariant smooth resolution $\widetilde Y$.
Above $W_1,W_2$, choose sheet sections
$A_1=x_0+y_0$ and $A_2=x_0-y_0$, respectively.
They have relative sign $-1$ generically on $P_A$ and
$+1$ generically on $P_B$. The actual anti-divisors satisfy
\[
 \boxed{
  f_*(\Delta_1\cdot\Delta_2)
       =2([P_B]-[P_A])\in CH^2(Q^4)_{\mathbb Q}.
 } \tag{6}
\]
This is a nonzero algebraic primitive class on the
\emph{original} smooth fourfold. Setting
$r=[P_A]-[P_B]$, the true intersection identities give
\[
 \boxed{h^2\cdot r=0,\qquad r^2=2,\qquad
 (f_*(\Delta_1\Delta_2))^2=8.}                 \tag{7}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Modulo $y_1$ the branch becomes $A_1^2$.
Modulo $y_2$ the quadric equation gives
$x_1y_1=-x_0y_0$, hence $F=A_2^2$.
On $P_A$ one has $x_0=0$ and $A_2=-A_1$;
on $P_B$ one has $y_0=0$ and $A_2=A_1$.
Both are nonzero at generic points. Equation (3)
therefore yields (6), regardless of exceptional divisors
over singular points of the branch double cover.
The intersection matrix of the two ruling-plane classes
has $P_A^2=P_B^2=1$ and $P_A\cdot P_B=0$;
also $h^2=P_A+P_B$, so $r$ is perpendicular to $h^2$
and has square $2$. Equation (7) follows.
\end{proof}


\begin{corollary}[Complete exact middle-Hodge cycle compiler on $Q^4$]
Keep the entirely constructed original-$Q^4$ algebraic
source $S=f_*(\Delta_1\Delta_2)=2(P_B-P_A)$ and
$h=c_1(\mathcal O_{Q^4}(1))$. For \emph{every}
rational $(2,2)$ Hodge class
$\alpha\in H^4(Q^4,\mathbb Q)$, define the genuine rational
algebraic Chow cycle
\[
 \boxed{
 Z_\alpha=
 \frac{\int_{Q^4}\alpha\cup h^2}{2}\,h^2+
 \frac{\int_{Q^4}\alpha\cup\operatorname{cl}^{\rm can}(S)}{8}\,S
 \in CH^2(Q^4)_{\mathbb Q}.
 }                                                          \tag{11}
\]
Then $\operatorname{cl}^{\rm can}_{Q^4}(Z_\alpha)=\alpha$
exactly, with \emph{no} assumed Hodge-to-Chow representative.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The rational middle cohomology of the smooth even quadric
$Q^4$ is two-dimensional with basis the hyperplane square
and the difference of the two rulings. The explicit
nonlinear double-world source $S$ is twice the negative
ruling difference, hence an independent basis vector.
Their true intersection Gram matrix is
$\operatorname{diag}(2,8)$: $\int h^4=2$,
$\int h^2\operatorname{cl}(S)=0$, and $\int S^2=8$.
The coefficients in (11) are the rational Gram-dual
coordinates of $\alpha$ in this \emph{actual} geometric
basis. Both basis classes are independently algebraic,
so (11) is a bona fide Chow cycle and its canonical Betti
class is precisely $\alpha$.
\end{proof}
This is a complete end-to-end middle-codimension
source-to-target construction for the stated genuine
fourfold test, not a substitution for the same required
construction on an arbitrary original $X$.

\section{Full original-$X$ Chow-generation consequence and exact boundary}
Let $X$ be any smooth projective complex $d$-fold and
let $Z\subset X$ be a genuine integral codimension-two
subvariety. For large sufficiently ample twists, two
integral hypersurfaces containing $Z$ can be chosen whose
scheme-theoretic intersection is pure codimension two
and generically reduced on every component (standard
Serre generation, regular local parameters and generic
Bertini). A complete intersection in smooth $X$ is
Cohen--Macaulay, hence satisfies $S_1$; generic reducedness
therefore makes the entire intersection reduced. Our
one-component theorem then realizes $[Z]$ as a
double-world anti-product transfer plus a divisor product.
By linear extension, the family of all such signed-cover
sources spans the \emph{entire actual rational codimension-two
Chow cycle group} in every original $X$.

For $p>2$, one may first choose $(p-2)$ sufficiently ample
divisors through an actual codimension-$p$ subvariety $Z$,
take an integral resulting component $V$ smooth at the
generic point of $Z$, and resolve $V$ to a smooth projective
$\widetilde V$. The strict transform of $Z$ is a genuine
codimension-two integral subvariety of $\widetilde V$ mapping
birationally to $Z$. Apply the preceding codimension-two
signed-cover construction to $\widetilde V$ and push its
Chow output to $X$. This constructs \emph{all genuine}
codimension-$p$ algebraic source generators from a
finite tower of actual geometric operations, with
codimension zero and one supplied by fundamental classes
and divisors.

The assertion is cofinality for already \emph{algebraic}
Chow generators, NOT an algebraization theorem for arbitrary
rational $(p,p)$ Betti classes. Its input can be produced
without any target-Hodge-to-Chow selector by enumerating
hypersurface tuples, resolved components and all sign
patterns. It advances GST from linear anti-deck
pushforward zero to a true nonlinear primitive birth,
but an independent universal geometric forcing theorem
must still prove that the resulting original-$X$
classes detect every arbitrary primitive rational Hodge
residual. The quadric computation provides a falsifiable
positive primitive model for that mechanism, not a
proof by extrapolation to all varieties.


\section{Higher-arity GST Galois character selection: the next distinct operation}
The preceding construction uses a single involution, hence its
nonlinear quadratic anticharacter product is invariant. The
higher-dimensional GST causal world family has a general
\emph{necessary character conservation law} with real
algebraic Chow semantics.

\begin{proposition}[Uniform finite-two-group character conservation]
Let $G=(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^k$ act algebraically on a
smooth projective $\widetilde Y$, and let
$f:\widetilde Y\to X$ be a proper morphism invariant
under $G$. Suppose real algebraic rational Chow
classes $Z_i\in CH^{p_i}(\widetilde Y)_{\mathbb Q}$
satisfy $g^*Z_i=\chi_i(g)Z_i$ for true sign
characters $\chi_i:G\to\{\pm1\}$.
For their algebraic intersection,
\[
  \boxed{
  \left(\prod_i\chi_i\ne1\right)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  f_*\bigl(\prod_i Z_i\bigr)=0
  \quad\text{in }CH^{\sum p_i-\mathrm{reldim}(f)}(X)_{\mathbb Q}.
  } \tag{9}
\]
If $\prod_i\chi_i=1$, their product is $G$-invariant
and gives an admissible original-$X$ algebraic
proper-pushforward source, although nonvanishing is
not automatic.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The cycle intersection is multiplicative for automorphisms:
$g^*\prod_iZ_i=(\prod_i\chi_i(g))\prod_iZ_i$.
Invariance $f\circ g=f$ gives
$f_*g^*(\prod_iZ_i)=f_*(\prod_iZ_i)$
since $g$ is an automorphism. If the product character
is nontrivial, some $g$ gives a minus sign, hence
$2 f_*(\prod_iZ_i)=0$; rational Chow coefficients
force the asserted equality. For trivial product
the source is invariant and its pushforward is
an actual algebraic Chow class by properness.
\end{proof}

For one $\mathbb Z/2$ involution a product of an odd
number of anti-divisor sources always pushes to zero.
This is a \emph{specific linear/odd-arity obstruction},
not a universal obstruction to nonlinear GST world
births. Already for $G=(\mathbb Z/2)^2$ there
are three distinct nontrivial characters
\[
 \chi_a(u,v)=(-1)^u,\qquad
 \chi_b(u,v)=(-1)^v,\qquad
 \chi_{a+b}(u,v)=(-1)^{u+v},
 \quad \chi_a\chi_b\chi_{a+b}=1 .                 \tag{10}
\]
Therefore a genuinely \emph{cubic} invariant output can
be built, provided three independently algebraic
character-eigen sources exist. This character law
opens a different higher-codimension construction
without falsely claiming existence of those sources
on an arbitrary original $X$; the scheme-level
geometric realization of all three is the explicit
first test of the next route.
