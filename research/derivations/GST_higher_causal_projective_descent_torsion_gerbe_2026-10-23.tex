\begin{abstract}
We attack the all-original-X universal algebraic source goal on the
ACTUAL dimensionless-GST affine incidence spatial fold. The previous
native mathematics constructs a genuine holomorphic pure-Chern-character
bundle H on its Stein realization J but leaves the two-cell
causal descent relation unestablished.
Here we prove a stronger descent theorem without assuming that H itself
has any flat vertical connection: for any holomorphic rank-r bundle H on
the actual fold, if its full projective bundle descends to the original
compact X AND its determinant line descends, then H itself descends.
The proof uses the finite central extension
$1\to\mu_r\to GL_r\to PGL_r\times\mathbb G_m\to1$
(the determinant/projective map), the exact homotopy
invariance of cohomology with $\mu_r$ coefficients
under the genuine affine-space torsor, and the
Stein exponential sequence for residual r-torsion
line-bundle twists. No Hodge-to-Chow selector is inserted.
For any prescribed rational (p,p) Hodge class alpha,
determinant descent is established without assuming
alpha algebraic, so the ONLY independent source-bearing
higher-causal condition is descent of the true PGL_r
projective fold. We give the complete canonical original-X
cycle formula if that projective condition is constructed.
We also prove that unbounded affine spatial stabilization
does not force projective descent of a fixed Oka bundle;
the proof uses exact central obstruction invariance,
not finite numerical sampling.
\end{abstract}

\[
 \boxed{H\simeq q^*E}.
                                                             \tag{5}
\]

ALGEBRAIC vector bundle by GAGA.

\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}

\[

 \longrightarrow GL_r(\mathbb C)
 \overset{\Phi}{\longrightarrow}
 PGL_r(\mathbb C)\times\mathbb C^\times
 \longrightarrow1,\qquad
 \Phi(g)=([g],\det g).
                                                             \tag{6}
\]
The kernel consists of scalar
matrices $\zeta I_r$ with
$\zeta^r=1$; the map is
surjective as a morphism of
complex Lie groups (locally
lift a projective element and
choose an rth root of the
determinant ratio).
The obstruction to lifting the

a rank-r holomorphic vector
bundle is the nonabelian

\[
   o(Q,L)\in H^2(X,\mu_r)
                                                             \tag{7}
\]
formed from local holomorphic
lift matrices: their triple-overlap

group $\mu_r$ and defines a
Cech 2-cocycle independent of lifts
up to coboundary. The pair (Q,L)
lifts iff this class vanishes.
Pull the pair to J. By (4) and
the determinant hypothesis its
pullback is the pair of actual
projective bundle and determinant

$q^*o(Q,L)=0$ in $H^2(J,\mu_r)$.
But q is a homotopy equivalence,

$\mu_r$ is the constant local

$o(Q,L)=0$. Hence some genuine

The two holomorphic bundles H

\[
    H\simeq q^*E\otimes M.
                                                             \tag{8}
\]

q^*L\simeq q^*\det E$ gives
$M^{\otimes r}\simeq\mathcal O_J$.

$\operatorname{Pic}_{hol}(J)
\simeq H^2(J,\mathbb Z)$,

constructs a genuine holomorphic

$c_1(M_X)=c$. Oka on Stein J
identifies $M\simeq q^*M_X$.
Substituting into (8) gives
$H\simeq q^*(E\otimes M_X)$,
which is (5).
\end{proof}

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{pch}^{CH}_p(Y)
   =\frac1{p!}\sum_{i=1}^r

 }                                                       \tag{15}
\]

\begin{proof}

\[

          \frac1{1-(x_i-\mu)t}.
                                                             \tag{16}
\]

\[
 \log S_Y(t)=\sum_{p\ge1}
     \frac{t^p}{p}\sum_i(x_i-\mu)^p
  =\sum_{p\ge1}(p-1)!\,
     \left(\frac1{p!}\sum_i
                    (x_i-\mu)^p\right)t^p.
                                                             \tag{17}
\]
Under a line twist $E\mapsto E\otimes M$,
