\section{Exact conditional cycle construction}
\begin{theorem}
If the above native fold-Hankel package exists for every original X and every live p, the rational Hodge conjecture holds for all such X.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each j let \(Z_j=N^jZ_0\) and \(v_j=T^jv_0\).
Naturality inductively proves \(\cl_X(Z_j)=v_j\).
Define rational linear maps
\[
 C:\Q^n\to W,\quad C(e_j)=v_j,\qquad
 O:W\to\Q^n,\quad (O\beta)_i=\ell(T^i\beta).
\]
By construction \(M=OC\). Since \(\det M\neq0\) and
both the domain and codomain of C and O have dimension n,
C and O are isomorphisms. For ANY prescribed class alpha,
form the target-only moment vector and its coefficients:
\[
 b_i(\alpha)=\ell(T^i\alpha),\qquad c(\alpha)=M^{-1}b(\alpha).
\]
The EXPLICIT original-X native cycle is
\[
 \boxed{Z_\alpha=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}c_j(\alpha)N^j Z_0.}
\]
All coefficients are rational and all summands are actual algebraic
cycles. Apply O to the class:
\[
 O\cl_X(Z_\alpha)=OCM^{-1}b(\alpha)
 =MM^{-1}O\alpha=O\alpha.
\]
Injectivity of O proves \(\cl_X(Z_\alpha)=\alpha\).
\end{proof}

\section{Positive all-rank construction on actual original varieties}
There is a concrete successful geometric family of the single-fold
Hankel criterion; the calculation is not limited to formal rational
matrices. Fix any integer \(m\ge1\) and pairwise nonisogenous complex
elliptic curves \(E_1,\ldots,E_m\). Let
\[
 X=\prod_{r=1}^{m}E_r,\qquad
 d_r=\operatorname{pr}_r^*[\mathrm{point}]\in\CH^1(X)_\Q.
\]
The degree-two rational Hodge space equals
\(\bigoplus_{r=1}^m\Q\,\cl(d_r)\); the nonisogeny assumption
eliminates cross-factor divisor directions. The intersection rules
are exact: \(d_r^2=0\) and \(\int_Xd_1\cdots d_m=1\).

For every ordered pair \((j,i)\), construct the actual codimension-m
correspondence in the original \(X\times X\),
\[
 \Gamma_{ji}
 =\pi_1^*\!\left(\prod_{k\ne i}d_k\right)
    \cdot\pi_2^*d_j\in\CH^m(X\times X)_\Q.
\]
The genuine push--pull action on the divisor classes satisfies
\[
(\Gamma_{ji})_*(d_r)
=\left(\int_Xd_r\prod_{k\ne i}d_k\right)d_j
=\delta_{ir}d_j.
\]
Thus \(\Gamma_{ji}\) is precisely the rational matrix unit
\(E_{ji}\) on the FULL Hodge fiber in degree two. Now let
\[
\Gamma=\Gamma_{2,1}+\Gamma_{3,2}+\cdots+
\Gamma_{m,m-1}+\Gamma_{1,m},\quad
Z_0=d_1,\quad
\ell(\beta)=\int_X\beta\prod_{k\ne1}d_k.
\]
The associated operator \(T=\Gamma_*\) rotates the geometric
divisor basis: \(T(d_i)=d_{i+1}\) for \(i<m\) and
\(T(d_m)=d_1\). The detector reads the coefficient of \(d_1\).
Consequently the ENTIRE Hankel matrix has the literal integer form
\[
M_{ij}=\begin{cases}1&(i+j)\equiv0\pmod m,\\0&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\quad 0\le i,j<m.
\]
It is a permutation matrix; row zero fixes coordinate zero and
the remaining \(m-1\) coordinates are reversed. Therefore the exact
all-rank determinant is
\[
\boxed{\det M=(-1)^{(m-1)(m-2)/2}\ne0.}
\]
The single native orbit is \(d_1,d_2,\ldots,d_m\) and the
moment-only reconstruction supplies every rational divisor

There is no assumption of Hodge algebraicity in higher codimension.

\section{GST full indexed spatial combinations in every codimension}
The preceding product-family proof extends from the divisor sector
to an EXPLICIT native geometric sector at EVERY codimension.
Let \(X=\prod_{r=1}^{m}E_r\) and \(0\le p\le m\).
For every subset \(I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}\) with \(|I|=p\),
let \(D_I=\prod_{r\in I}d_r\in\CH^p(X)_\Q\).
There are \(n=\binom mp\) such indexed native cycles.
Their complement cycles \(D_{I^c}\) have codimension \(m-p\).
Their full rational intersection pairing is EXACTLY
\[
\int_X D_I D_{J^c}=\delta_{IJ},
\quad |I|=|J|=p.
\]
Indeed if \(I=J\) every factor occurs exactly once, giving
one; if \(I\ne J\) then at least one divisor appears twice,
so the product is zero because \(d_r^2=0\).
This proves that the \(D_I\) form an independent indexed
native subspace \(W_p^{\mathrm{native}}\), of rank \(\binom mp\).

Define the actual codimension-\(m\) product correspondence
\[
\Gamma_{JI}:=\pi_1^*D_{I^c}\cdot\pi_2^*D_J
\quad\in\CH^m(X\times X)_\Q.
\]
The genuine Chow push--pull formula is
\[
(\Gamma_{JI})_*D_K
=\left(\int_X D_KD_{I^c}\right)D_J
=\delta_{IK}D_J.
\]
Thus the ENTIRE \(\binom mp\times\binom mp\) rational
matrix-unit algebra is realized in the native codimension-\(p\)
