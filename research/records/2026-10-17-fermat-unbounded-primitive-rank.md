- **Central move:** Use the d distinct complex d-th roots of unity to construct **d pairwise disjoint maximal planes on one explicit smooth Fermat X**. The primitive geometry is certified by intersections rather than an assumed Hodge target preimage or abstract generator enumeration.
- **Explicit geometric proof:** The Fermat projective Jacobian has partials ±d*x_i^(d−1), ±d*y_i^(d−1). Since d≠0 over ℂ, they vanish simultaneously only at the forbidden all-zero homogeneous coordinate vector; hence X smooth. Each ζ^d=1 gives plane P_ζ⊂X. If ζ≠η and a point belonged to both P_ζ and P_η, then (ζ−η)x_i=0 for all i, forcing x_i=y_i=0; thus the planes are pairwise disjoint in projective space. ℂ has d distinct dth roots because t^d−1 has no repeated roots in characteristic zero.
- **Normal-bundle geometric input:** On P_ζ ≅ ℙ^m, the tangent/normal sequence yields 0→N_(P_ζ/X)→O(1)^(m+1)→O(d)→0 and q_(m,d)=c_m(N)=Σ binomial(m+1,k)(−d)^(m−k). The geometric pairing is H²=d, H·[P_ζ]=1, and [P_ζ]·[P_η]=δ_(ζη) q_(m,d). These are standard scheme- and Betti-level intersection facts, not yet constructed internally using Stage2G's canonical cycleClass.
- **Primitive source:** Z_ζ=d[P_ζ]−H is algebraic by construction and orthogonal to H. In degree 2m, cup h:H^(2m)→H^(2m+2) has one-dimensional target in the needed Hodge sector by weak Lefschetz/Poincaré duality; hence this orthogonality implies primitive cohomology. This is a source-level claim, not all-Hodge surjectivity.
- **Exact rank computation:** For any list a=(a_ζ)∈ℚ^d, the pairing of Σ a_ζ[Z_ζ] with Z_η equals `d² q_(m,d) a_η − d Σ a_ζ`. Thus the Gram matrix M=d² q I_d − d J_d has eigenvalues d²q (multiplicity d−1) and d²(q−1) (multiplicity 1). Existing normal-Chern recursion q_0=1, q_(m+1)=1+(1−d)q_m implies q_m≤−1 or q_m≥2 for m≥1,d≥3 (uniform induction). Hence q_m≠0,1, and all eigenvalues are nonzero. Therefore the d actual primitive algebraic classes are linearly independent in the standard geometric interpretation.
