# Mathbox Gate 3: primitive middle Hodge classes live in relative hyperplane cohomology

- **Date:** 2026-10-17.
- **Route:** Direct original-X hyperplane/relative construction, not a special-variety rank expansion.
- **Original unmodified target:** On *every* smooth projective complex X, every p and every rational (p,p) class alpha, construct a genuine native codimension-p rational algebraic cycle on that same X with its canonical Betti class alpha.
- **Active sub-obligation:** Produce primitive native geometric sources from X itself, without assuming the desired cycle or resolved Gysin support.
- **Scope of this mathematical experiment:** Arbitrary smooth projective X of **even** complex dimension 2p (p>=1), smooth hyperplane section i:Y->X, the standard rational Betti restriction, Gysin and hyperplane cup product.
- **Precise standard geometric inputs:** Weak Lefschetz identifies H^(2p-2)(X,Q) -> H^(2p-2)(Y,Q). By Poincare duality its Gysin adjoint i_*:H^(2p)(Y,Q) -> H^(2p+2)(X,Q) is an **isomorphism**. The self-intersection formula gives i_*i^*(alpha)=h∪alpha. The long exact cohomology sequence of (X,Y) gives im(H^(2p)(X,Y,Q)->H^(2p)(X,Q)) = ker(i^*). Since X is proper and U=X\Y is open, H^(2p)(X,Y,Q)=H_c^(2p)(U,Q).
- **Universal middle-primitive identity:**
  \[
   \boxed{\alpha\in\ker(h\cup:H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\to H^{2p+2}(X,\mathbb Q))
      \iff i^*\alpha=0
      \iff \alpha\in \operatorname{im}(H^{2p}_c(U,\mathbb Q)\to H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)).}
  \]
  The equivalences hold for all rational middle-cohomology classes, hence also for the rational primitive (p,p) Hodge classes. The relative lift is rational *Betti cohomology*, not an algebraic cycle and not necessarily a rational pure (p,p) lift in mixed-Hodge cohomology.
- **Exact all-X no-recovery implication:** For any nonzero primitive alpha and any Q-linear R:H^(2p)(Y,Q)->H^(2p)(X,Q), one has R(i^*alpha)=R(0)=0≠alpha. Thus ordinary hyperplane restriction followed by a linear reconstruction, however sophisticated, cannot produce the missing original primitive class. A successful hyperplane route needs **nontrivial relative information**.
- **Cubic fourfold discriminating example:** For a smooth cubic fourfold containing a plane P, let H=h². The intersection Gram in (H,P) is [[3,1],[1,3]]. Set alpha=3[P]-H. Then alpha·H=0 and alpha²=24. On a smooth hyperplane cubic threefold Y, H⁴(Y,Q)=Q h_Y², and the exact Gysin/pairing formula gives i^*H=h_Y² and i^*P=(1/3)h_Y². Therefore i^*alpha=0 although alpha is a nonzero **actually algebraic** primitive class.
- **Wolfram exact computation:** For the cubic, b4(X)=23, b4(Y)=1, the ambient restriction has rank1 and the primitive kernel has dimension22. A formal rank-one map R:Q^23->Q and injective Gysin g:Q->Q has i_* i^*=L, ker(R)=ker(L), dimension22. The true geometric cubic class calculation returned i^*P=1/3, i^*(3P-H)=0 and self-pairing24. This is finite rational algebra consistent with the general proof, not evidence that every primitive class is algebraic.
- **Additional geometric obstruction to naive relative realization:** Since Y is a very ample hyperplane section, U=X\Y is **affine**. A complete closed algebraic subvariety contained entirely in an affine complex variety is zero-dimensional. For middle codimension p on 2p-dimensional X with p>=1, a positive-dimensional complete algebraic codimension-p representative cannot lie entirely in U. Therefore the relative Betti lift should not be confused with a compact algebraic subvariety of U: any useful algebraic source must interact with the boundary Y, typically through rational signed combinations or controlled boundary data. This specifically rules out the proposed naive 'find a compact positive-dimensional algebraic cycle inside U' method; it does not rule out rational algebraic cycles on X.
- **Actual Lean construction:** \`GSTUniversalPrimitiveHyperplaneRelative.lean\` defines a typed \`SmoothMiddleHyperplaneBetti\` packet for genuine rational Betti restriction, injective Gysin, self-intersection and relative exactness. Its theorems prove \`primitive_iff_restriction_zero\`, \`primitive_has_relative_betti_lift\`, \`primitive_hodge_relative_lift\`, and \`no_recovery_from_restriction\`. The packet's fields are standard independent topology inputs (not a cycle preimage). A concrete analytification/pair cohomology instantiation is still needed before those Lean statements can be taken as a fully formalized Mathlib topology theorem.

- **First unresolved implication after this route:** Given an arbitrary rational primitive (p,p) target alpha on original X, construct an **algebraic signed source with boundary cancellation on Y** from independently specified relative geometry and prove its canonical class is alpha. A relative Betti lift alone is NOT that algebraic source; positing it is algebraic is equivalent to assuming the missing conjecture.
- **Candidate next mechanism:** Lefschetz pencils, variation/vanishing cycles and algebraic incidence with a comparison theorem that takes a relative topological class and produces an actual codimension-p rational algebraic cycle on X. Its first test must construct a class not supplied in advance by an algebraic projector or already-algebraic Gysin support.
- **Proof route verdict:** **Proved as a classical universal relative-topology statement**, valid for every X in the even middle range, with Lean proof conditional only on genuine hyperplane/relative topological maps. **Closed as invalid:** direct primitive reconstruction from hyperplane restriction or compact projective positive-dimensional cycles entirely in the affine complement. **Gate 3 remains active:** relative-to-algebraic source construction for every primitive Hodge class.
