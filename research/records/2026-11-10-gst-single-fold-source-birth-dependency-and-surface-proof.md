Polynomial words of the diagonal and degree/NS projectors act as scalars on T and cannot obtain non-scalar CM endomorphism directions. This fails the restricted diagonal-only algorithm, not the wider GST hypothesis. Existing K3 order-four automorphism graph modules already provide a different geometric mechanism in selected cases.

For any arbitrary original X, every genuine native cycle operator with canonical Betti naturality preserves the full algebraic image A=im(cl_X). Hence all word/curvature orbits from algebraic seeds remain in A and map to zero in W/A. A finite-source rank gain can enlarge a chosen proper subspace of A, but cannot remove a genuine quotient residual using only operations already preserving A. This is a precise existing-law limitation, not a counterexample to the Hodge conjecture.
