# GST Mathbox: primitive middle Hodge currents from the affine complement, and why adding a calibrated hyperplane does NOT remove their absolute defect

Date 2026-10-16. Branch base at 825ad84bb60953580427f02f90543ee565c6c4c9.
Original, unchanged research target: for every smooth projective complex X and every rational Hodge class alpha, construct integer m>0 and M>=0 with beta=m alpha+M h^p integral, and CLOSED INTEGRAL RECTIFIABLE cycles T_n all in the same PD(beta) with absolute Kähler calibration defect D(T_n)->0. This would prove rational Hodge for all X. No universal zero-defect conclusion is claimed here.

## Route I — an unconditional middle primitive integral geometric carrier supported OFF the hyperplane

This is a genuine geometric construction for EVERY smooth projective X of EVEN complex dimension d=2p and EVERY primitive rational middle-degree class alpha in H^{2p}(X,Q). The class need not be Hodge for this particular construction.

Choose a very ample line bundle L and a smooth hyperplane section i:Y↪X with h=c1(L). Let U=X\Y, a smooth affine complex variety.

Primitivity is h∪alpha=0 in H^{2p+2}(X,Q).

Weak Lefschetz: H^{2p-2}(X,Q)→H^{2p-2}(Y,Q) is an isomorphism since 2p-2<dim_C Y=2p-1. Let v in H^{2p-2}(Y,Q) and choose u in H^{2p-2}(X,Q) with i*u=v. By the projection formula:

  ∫_Y i*alpha∪v = ∫_X alpha∪u∪h = ∫_X (h∪alpha)∪u =0.

Poincaré duality on Y gives i*alpha=0 in H^{2p}(Y,Q).

Now the long exact pair sequence gives a relative lift
  gamma in H^{2p}(X,Y;Q),   gamma↦alpha in H^{2p}(X,Q).

By excision/compact-support cohomology and Poincaré duality of the oriented open manifold U,

  H^{2p}(X,Y;Q)≅ H_c^{2p}(U;Q)≅ H_{2p}(U;Q).

Choose an integer m>0 clearing the rational denominators of this *actual relative homology class*. Ordinary homology is generated by finite singular cycles with compact images. Smoothly approximate their finitely many singular simplices inside U, yielding a compactly supported closed INTEGER rectifiable 2p-current S in U such that its inclusion into X represents PD(m alpha) in rational homology.

This is an actual, unconditional, all-X **primitive integral current realization away from Y**; not an abstract N-cohomology vector or a Hodge conjecture assumption.

Because alpha h=0 and p>=1,
  S(omega^p/p!) = ∫_X m alpha∧h^p/p! =0.
Thus its absolute Kähler calibration defect is exactly
  D(S) = Mass(S) >=0.
If alpha≠0, S is nonzero, hence Mass(S)>0. This cannot be calibrated as a positive holomorphic p-cycle by itself.

The code GSTPrimitiveMiddleHyperplaneRelative.lean kernel-checks the algebraic Poincaré-duality/projection/weak-Lefschetz core (with the standard geometric identities typed explicitly). Its GitHub workflow is gst-primitive-hyperplane-relative.yml; verify CI separately before calling it green. The relative-to-integral-current construction above is NOT formalized in Lean.

## Route II — test the exact requested AMPLE SHIFT without changing the class

For fixed M>=0 choose a holomorphic complete intersection C_M representing M h^p, supported entirely inside Y: choose Y as the first of p very ample divisors, intersect p-1 further generic divisors, and give the resulting p-dimensional holomorphic cycle integer multiplicity M. If p=1 just take M copies of Y.

Then C_M is a CLOSED INTEGRAL Kähler-calibrated current
  D(C_M)=0,
and its class is PD(M h^p).

The actual integer current T_M=S+C_M has exact rational cohomology class
  m alpha+M h^p
and is CLOSED INTEGRAL and in a fixed class for that M.

But S is supported in U and C_M in Y, so the supports are DISJOINT compact sets. Mass is therefore additive, while the calibration period is additive:

  D(S+C_M)
   = [Mass(S)+Mass(C_M)]-[S(c_p)+C_M(c_p)]
   = Mass(S)+0
   >0 whenever alpha≠0.

In particular, taking M larger (or repeating T_M as a constant sequence) does NOT force defect to zero for THIS all-X explicit construction. A sequence in ONE fixed beta with D→0 would have to rearrange/surgery the primitive relative carrier and the holomorphic hyperplane carrier so that complex tangent-plane mass replaces the real primitive mass. Simple disjoint addition of more ample complex cycles cannot accomplish it.

This is a proved obstruction for THIS ROUTE only, not a positive lower bound for the infimum over all integral currents in beta.

## Route III — the smallest explicit projective example: P¹×P¹

X=P¹×P¹. Let A=π1*hyperplane class and B=π2*hyperplane class; A²=B²=0, ∫ A∪B=1. Choose the Kähler class h=A+B. Take the diagonal Y={(z,z)}, a smooth hyperplane of class h, and the antipodal anti-holomorphic isometry
   a([z0:z1])=[-conj(z1):conj(z0)]
of P¹ with no fixed point. Its graph Γ={(z,a(z))} is a smooth 2-sphere DISJOINT from Y.

The map a reverses Kähler area: a*ω_P¹=-ω_P¹. Hence Γ is a genuine LAGRANGIAN 2-cycle:
   Γ*(ω1+ω2)=ω1-ω1=0.
Normalize each P¹ factor to area 1. The graph induced metric is 2g, so
   Mass([Γ])=2,
   [Γ](ω)=0,
   D([Γ])=2.

Its rational Poincaré dual is B-A, because Γ has projection degrees (+1,-1). Set alpha=B-A; it is primitive since
   alpha h=(B-A)(A+B)=B²-A²=0.
It is a genuine rational (1,1) Hodge class, and it is ALGEBRAIC as a SIGNED difference of ruling curves.

Now add the diagonal Y to Γ. Their supports are disjoint. The integral current
  T_bad=[Γ]+[Y]
has class alpha+h = 2B, mass 2+2=4, Kähler period 0+2=2, and
   D(T_bad)=2>0.

But for two distinct points u,v∈P¹, the holomorphic integral current
  T_good=[P¹×{u}]+[P¹×{v}]
has SAME class 2B, mass 2, Kähler period 2 and
   D(T_good)=0.

Thus the exact fixed homology class beta=2B has both a canonical disjoint
relative+ample construction with defect 2 and an entirely different
globally rearranged HOLomorphic representative with defect 0.
The constant sequence T_n=T_good truly meets the requested goal in
this example.

Wolfram Language exact Chow-ring calculation returned
  alpha h =0, alpha²=-2AB, h²=2AB, alpha+h=2B,
and the (mass,period,defect) triple (4,2,2) for T_bad.

This verifies WHY the all-X argument must produce a NONLOCAL geometric
replacement of primitive relative cycles and hyperplane cycles by
holomorphic representatives. Calibration is not achieved by formal
subtraction, smoothing a diffuse form or increasing M without surgery.

## Additional exact scope: known outer codimensions

For any projective X of dimension d:
* p=1: clear denominators of alpha; the exponential sequence yields a
  holomorphic line bundle N with c1(N)=m alpha. For sufficiently ample
  M, N⊗L^M has a nonzero holomorphic section. Its effective divisor
  is a closed integer calibrated current of class m alpha+M h.
* p=d-1: Hard Lefschetz identifies alpha=h^(d-2)u for rational
  u in H²(X,Q) of type (1,1). Clear denominators and use the p=1
  divisor construction for u, then intersect its effective divisor
  with d-2 generic hyperplane sections. The resulting effective
  curve current has class m alpha+M h^(d-1) and D=0.
* p=0 and p=d: fundamental components and closed points provide
  calibrated currents after clearing denominators; these cases
  are elementary.

So the original requested fixed-class integral sequence is genuinely
constructed for these weights on EVERY X, simply by taking a constant
sequence at an explicit effective representative. In dimension d<=3
these cases exhaust the rational Hodge conjecture. For higher
middle codimensions the target remains unresolved.

## New all-X quantitative constraint: NO compact-support-calibrated primitive carrier in an affine complement

The open complement U=X\Y of a very ample divisor is a smooth
AFFINE variety; as a complex analytic space it has a proper
algebraic closed embedding into some C^N.

Claim: U has no nonempty compact positive-dimensional complex
analytic subvariety. On any connected compact positive-dimensional
complex analytic subvariety Z⊂U, all coordinate functions of
U↪C^N restrict to globally holomorphic functions on Z, hence
are constant by the maximum principle. The embedding sends Z
to one point, a contradiction.

Consequently every positive-dimensional Kähler-calibrated
INTEGRAL current with COMPACT SUPPORT inside U is zero:
by calibrated-current regularity the current is a positive
holomorphic chain, whose compact components are forbidden
by affineness.

This yields a genuine geometric MASS GAP WITH SUPPORT CONTROL.

Let K⊂U be a FIXED compact set, q=d-p>=1, and let beta be
a NONZERO integral homology class representable by compactly
supported integral closed 2q-currents with support in K.
Then there exists epsilon(K,beta)>0 such that every such
current T satisfies

     Mass(T)-T(omega^q/q!) >= epsilon(K,beta)>0.

Proof by contradiction: if T_n with supports in K, all in beta,
have defects→0, then the common calibration period P_beta
implies masses Mass(T_n)=P_beta+D(T_n) bounded. The compactness
theorem for integral currents gives an integral flat-limit T,
closed and in beta (homology class preservation under sufficiently
small flat deformations), with support in K. Lower semicontinuity
of mass plus calibration inequality forces D(T)=0. Thus T is
a nonzero compactly-supported calibrated positive holomorphic
cycle in U, impossible. If beta is nonzero, T≠0. Therefore the
strict uniform gap exists.

One need NOT invent a value for epsilon(K,beta); it follows
by the contradiction and compactness argument. This is a
uniform statement across ALL integer currents of this fixed
class whose supports are inside the chosen compact K.

For the PURE nonzero middle primitive class m alpha, we
can give an EXPLICIT lower-bound scheme on all X without
requiring supports in a common K. Pick a rational smooth
closed real 2p-form psi with
    c = ∫_X alpha ∧ [psi] ≠0
by Poincaré duality on X. Let C be its finite positive comass
supremum for the fixed Kähler metric. Any closed current S
representing PD(m alpha) satisfies

    S(omega^p/p!) = 0,
    |S(psi)|=m|c| <= C Mass(S).

Hence every integer representative of this nonzero PURE
primitive class has
    D(S)=Mass(S)>=m|c|/C >0.

Adding a calibrated complete-intersection C_M supported in Y
DISJOINTLY preserves this same lower bound, independently of M,
as proved above.

**Exact new proof implication:** a universal fixed-class
integral-current construction for beta=m alpha+M h^p must
nontrivially interact across the hyperplane. A deformation
remaining in a fixed compact subset of U cannot approach the
Kähler calibration cone. Merely increasing an algebraic
component on Y while retaining a disjoint primitive current
cannot shrink the absolute defect.

This does NOT exclude other integral cycles in the exact
shifted class beta that cross Y and are holomorphic. On
P¹×P¹ the holomorphic pair of ruling curves explicitly
shows those can exist. The original universal all-X goal
still requires finding them for arbitrary rational Hodge alpha.


## A NEW ALL-X NONCONCENTRATION THEOREM FOR EVERY HODGE AMPLE SHIFT

This is a further quantitative fact valid for every smooth
projective X of even dimension 2p and EVERY nonzero rational
primitive Hodge (p,p) alpha, independently of whether it is
algebraic.

By the earlier relative cohomology construction, alpha has
a compact-support smooth CLOSED real de Rham representative
  psi∈Ω_c^{2p}(U)
with class [psi]=alpha in H^{2p}(X,R), U=X\Y.

This is obtained by passing from H^{2p}(X,Y;Q)
to H_c^{2p}(U;Q) and the compact-support de Rham theorem.
Its compact support K=supp(psi) is a FIXED compact subset of U.

The primitive rational Hodge--Riemann positivity theorem gives
  (-1)^p ∫_X alpha∧alpha >0.
In particular, c_alpha=|∫_X alpha∧alpha|>0.

Choose any positive integers m,M and any closed integral
2p-current T in the shifted class
   PD(m alpha + M h^p).
The pairing with psi is HOMOLOGICAL and independent of the
representative T:

  T(psi)
   =∫_X (m alpha+M h^p)∧[psi]
   =m∫_X alpha² + M∫_X h^p∧alpha
   =m∫_X alpha²

since h∧alpha=0.

Let C_psi=sup_X comass(psi), finite and positive.
The current's mass measure ||T|| therefore satisfies:

  m c_alpha
    = |T(psi)|
    <= C_psi ||T||(K),

so the quantitative ALL-X bound is

    ** ||T||(K) >= m c_alpha / C_psi >0. **

This holds for EVERY integral (or even real normal)
representative T of ANY ample-shifted class
m alpha+M h^p, for ALL M, with the SAME K and lower
bound independent of M.

It establishes a genuinely geometric primitive-information
CONSERVATION law: no candidate Hodge current sequence,
calibrated or not, may send all its mass arbitrarily close
to the ample divisor Y or to infinity in U. A uniformly
positive amount of mass must persist inside one compact
region of the affine complement.

In particular, a proposal that tries to make D(T_n) small
by concentrating every T_n solely into a thin collar of Y,
without realizing holomorphic tangent planes through the
region K where the primitive class is detected, is impossible.
However, this mass lower bound DOES NOT bound D(T) away
from zero, since the mass inside K could itself be
Kähler-calibrated. The P¹×P¹ holomorphic example realizes
precisely that possibility.

This deduction materially sharpens the actual proof task:
construct a CALIBRATED portion crossing a FIXED compact
subset of U and carrying the nonzero primitive pairing,
not merely a high-degree hyperplane accumulation.

No universal zero-defect conclusion has yet been derived.
