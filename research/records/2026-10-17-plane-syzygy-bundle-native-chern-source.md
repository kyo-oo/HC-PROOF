## Exact all-degree geometric construction

Let \(d\ge2\) and \(X_d\subset\mathbb P^5_{\mathbb C}\) be a **smooth degree-\(d\) hypersurface fourfold** containing the plane \(P=\{\ell_0=\ell_1=\ell_2=0\}\). The equation \(F\) lies in the ideal generated by these three linear forms: \(F=\sum_i\ell_iq_i\) with homogeneous \(q_i\) of degree \(d-1\). Smooth Fermat hypersurfaces with \(F=\sum_i(y_i^d-x_i^d)\) and \(\ell_i=y_i-x_i\) provide an explicit genuine example in every degree, so the geometric family is nonempty.

On **the original \(X_d\)** form the ideal sheaf \(\mathcal I_P\). The three actual linear equations define a surjection of coherent algebraic sheaves
\[
 \varepsilon:\mathcal O_X(-1)^{\oplus3}\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal I_P.
\]
Set
\[
 \boxed{E=\ker\varepsilon.}
\]
This is a rank-**two algebraic vector bundle**, not merely a formal \(K_0\) class. Indeed, at a point of \(P\), \(P\subset X\) is a smooth regular immersion of codimension two, so its ideal has a local resolution of projective dimension one. The kernel of any surjection from a locally free sheaf onto that ideal is locally free of rank two (Schanuel/projective syzygy argument over the regular local rings of X). Away from \(P\), the ideal equals \(\mathcal O_X\) and the kernel of a surjection of free modules is locally free of rank two. Thus the construction globalizes, with exact sequences
\[
 0\to E\to\mathcal O_X(-1)^3\to\mathcal I_P\to0,\qquad
 0\to\mathcal I_P\to\mathcal O_X\to\mathcal O_P\to0.
\]

## Chern character and primitive detection — all d

The genuine algebraic \(K_0\) relation is
\[
 [E]=3[\mathcal O_X(-1)]-[\mathcal O_X]+[\mathcal O_P].
\]
The plane has algebraic codimension two, so \(\operatorname{ch}_0(\mathcal O_P)=\operatorname{ch}_1(\mathcal O_P)=0\) and \(\operatorname{ch}_2(\mathcal O_P)=[P]\). Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{rk}E=2,\qquad c_1(E)=-3h,\qquad
 \operatorname{ch}_2(E)=\tfrac32h^2+[P].
\]
Using \(2\operatorname{ch}_2(E)=c_1(E)^2-2c_2(E)\) yields the **exact native Chow identity**
\[
 \boxed{c_2(E)=3h^2-[P].}
\]
Consequently the geometric primitive middle cycle \(Z_d=d[P]-h^2\) is
\[
 \boxed{Z_d=(3d-1)h^2-d\,c_2(E).}
\]
Every term on the right is a genuine algebraic codimension-two cycle on **original \(X_d\)**. Unlike the abstract \(\xi_d=d[\mathcal O_P]-[\mathcal O_T]-\cdots\), this construction exhibits a single honest rank-two vector bundle whose second Chern class detects the nonambient plane.

The actual normal bundle of \(P\subset X_d\) has
\[
 c(N_{P/X_d})=(1+h)^3/(1+d h),\quad
 [P]^2=d^2-3d+3,\quad h^2[P]=1,\quad(h^2)^2=d.
\]
Thus
\[
 Z_d\cdot h^2=d-d=0,\qquad
 Z_d^2=d^2(d^2-3d+3)-d=d(d-1)^3>0.
\]
Hence this *bundle-created* rational \((2,2)\) class is genuinely primitive and nonzero for all \(d\ge2\).

## Affine projector over J(X)

The universal affine Jouanolou bundle \(\pi:J(X_d)\to X_d\) pulls back \(E\) to a rank-two algebraic vector bundle over the affine coordinate ring \(A=\Gamma(J(X_d),\mathcal O)\). Every vector bundle on an affine scheme comes from a finite projective \(A\)-module, hence is \(\operatorname{im}e\) for a genuine algebraic idempotent \(e\in M_N(A)\) after a finite projective complement. Its exact second Chern class obeys
\[
 \pi^*c_2(E)=3(\pi^*h)^2-\pi^*[P],
\]
and the primitive bundle-created class retains nonzero Betti norm by affine-bundle homotopy equivalence. This is an **equation-driven** higher-rank algebraic source on the affine replacement, not a holomorphic-to-algebraic assumption.
