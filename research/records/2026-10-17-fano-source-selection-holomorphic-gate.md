# Mathbox source-selection gate: holomorphic-form obstruction to universal Fano domination

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Kind:** decisive geometric obstruction / route-selection audit
- **Target:** Assess whether the existing solved Fermat cubic source varieties and their projective-space/projective-bundle enlargements can dominate *every* original smooth projective complex variety by generically finite morphisms, so that all-codimension Chow push-pull can transfer Hodge representatives.
- **Hypotheses and types:** Smooth projective complex source Y, target X of the same complex dimension; generically finite dominant morphism f:Y→X. For each k≥0, H^(k,0)(V)=H⁰(V,Ω_V^k) is a finite-dimensional C-vector space. A smooth Fermat cubic fourfold F⊂P⁵ has h^(1,0)(F)=h^(2,0)(F)=0; a quartic K3 surface S has h^(2,0)(S)=1 and an elliptic curve E has h^(1,0)(E)=1. Projective-space factors contribute no positive holomorphic forms.
- **Current dependency/evidence:** The original all-p geometric finite-degree transfer is Lean verified in GSTFiniteDominantAllCodimensionDescent.lean, run #39028790599. This route independently tests whether its geometric source-morphism premise can be instantiated for arbitrary X.
- **Nearest prior attempt:** Transfer from solved Fermat/Fano sources to arbitrary X using finite-dominant morphisms, blowups and projective product towers. The exact missing assumption is the existence of the morphism and suitable solved source geometry for every X.
- **Mechanism:** The pullback f* on holomorphic differential forms of a generically finite dominant morphism in characteristic zero is injective: on the dense étale/smooth locus, a nonzero target form has a nonzero pullback, and a form vanishing on an open set vanishes globally. Hence h^(k,0)(Y)≥h^(k,0)(X) for all k.
- **First decisive genuine-geometric test:** Let X=E×P³, a smooth projective complex fourfold. It has h^(1,0)(X)=h^(1,0)(E)=1. Let Y be the Fermat cubic fourfold F, or a four-dimensional projective bundle over a solved Fano source with h^(1,0)=0. These have h^(1,0)(Y)=0. Therefore no generically finite dominant morphism Y→X exists.
- **Second independent test:** Let X=S×P², with S a K3 surface. It has h^(2,0)(X)=1. Fermat cubic fourfold F has h^(2,0)(F)=0. Consequently no generically finite dominant map F→X exists.
- **Exact Wolfram computations:** Hodge polynomial for elliptic E is (1+u)(1+v), for P^k is Σ_(j=0)^k (uv)^j, and for K3 is 1+u²+v²+20uv+u²v². Therefore coefficient h^(1,0)(E×P³)=1 and h^(2,0)(K3×P²)=1. For a smooth cubic fourfold all h^(p,0)=0 with p=1,2; the Wolfram comparison returned 0 against both target values of 1. The computations verify Hodge-number arithmetic, while the obstruction is proved geometrically by injectivity of holomorphic pullback.
