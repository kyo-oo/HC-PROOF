## Hodge group and genuine algebraic source generators

For a generic simple QM surface B, its rational Hodge group is the norm-one quaternionic group \(\mathrm{SL}_1(D)\). After scalar extension to C, this becomes \(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb C)\); its action on \(H^1(B,\mathbb C)\) is two copies of the standard 2-dimensional representation. Hence
\[
 H^1(A,\mathbb C)\cong \mathbb C^2\otimes \mathbb C^4,
 \qquad
 H^{2q}(A,\mathbb C)\cong\bigwedge^{2q}(\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^4).
\]
The rational (q,q) Hodge classes are exactly the rational Hodge-group invariants in this exterior representation. The generic full Hodge-group identity is an *actual geometric hypothesis proved by the standard theory of generic QM abelian surfaces*; the finite algebra calculation alone does not establish it.

Crucially the degree-two invariants correspond to genuine **rational algebraic divisor classes** by the Lefschetz (1,1) theorem, and have rational dimension ten. They can be constructed from the actual QM endomorphisms and polarization as follows: \(\mathrm{NS}(A)_\mathbb Q\) identifies with the Rosati-symmetric matrices in \(\mathrm{End}^0(A)=M_2(D)\). Relative to the product polarization, these have shape
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b^\dagger&d\end{pmatrix},
 \quad a,d\in D^{\dagger=1}\;(\dim_\mathbb Q=3),\;
 b\in D\;(\dim_\mathbb Q=4).
\]
Thus **3+3+4=10** independent actual divisor classes are obtained without an assumed Hodge preimage in degree four.

## The exact rank calculation (all Hodge codimensions on the fourfold)

Over C, choose e_i,f_i (i=1,...,4) for four copies of the standard SL2 representation. The invariant degree-two generators are
\[
 \omega_{ij}=e_i\wedge f_j-f_i\wedge e_j,
 \qquad 1\le i\le j\le4.
\]
They are actual Hodge (1,1) classes and the 10-dimensional rational divisor space base-changed to C is exactly their span.

By the first fundamental theorem for SL2, the full exterior invariant algebra is generated by these degree-two pairings. Here the key middle-degree case is checked directly with exact sparse exterior algebra, without assuming the FFT's conclusion: wedge products \(\omega_{ij}\wedge\omega_{kl}\) span a rank-**20** subspace of the 70-dimensional \(H^4(A)\). On the other hand, in the standard \(\mathrm{SL}_2\) representation, the invariant multiplicity in \(\bigwedge^{2q}(\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^4)\) equals the weight-zero multiplicity minus the weight-two multiplicity
\[
 h_q=\binom4q^2-\binom4{q+1}\binom4{q-1}
 \quad (q=0,1,2,3,4),
\]
with out-of-range binomial coefficients zero. Exact dimensions are
\[
 \boxed{h_0,h_1,h_2,h_3,h_4=(1,10,20,10,1).}
\]
The products of the 10 actual rational divisor classes attain **every** one of these full dimensions, so they span the full rational Hodge subspace in each codimension on the same A.
