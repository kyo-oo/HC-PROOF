## 1. Exact full canonical Chow-to-Hodge rank comparison

Let \(Y=Q^2\simeq\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\subset\mathbb P^4\), smooth codimension two. With \(\pi:X\to\mathbb P^4\), exceptional \(j:E\hookrightarrow X\), \(q:E=\mathbb P(N_{Y/\mathbb P^4})\to Y\), **the actual Chow blow-up formula** gives, for every \(p\),
\[
 CH^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\cong CH^p(\mathbb P^4)_{\mathbb Q}
       \oplus CH^{p-1}(Y)_{\mathbb Q},
\]
where the second summand embeds via \(j_*q^*\).

The **actual singular rational Betti blow-up formula** is
\[
 H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cong H^{2p}(\mathbb P^4,\mathbb Q)
     \oplus H^{2p-2}(Y,\mathbb Q)(-1).
\]
Both are natural with respect to **canonical** cycle classes. \(Y=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) has only Tate cohomology generated by the actual algebraic ruling divisors \(a,b\); projective space is likewise generated by the hyperplane class. Thus the five even-degree rational Hodge ranks and canonical algebraic cycle-class image ranks match *one by one*:

| p | rational Hodge dimension | algebraic image rank |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 | 2 | 2 |
| 2 | 3 | 3 |
| 3 | 2 | 2 |
| 4 | 1 | 1 |

All odd Betti cohomology vanishes. There is no rational Hodge class outside the canonical image in any codimension.

In codimension two, the genuine explicit algebraic basis is
\[
 \bigl(\pi^*h^2,\ j_*q^*a,\ j_*q^*b\bigr),
\]
and the identical genuine rational Betti blow-up decomposition makes its canonical cycle-class matrix the \(3\times3\) identity, determinant \(1\). The pushforward \(\pi_*\) to \(\mathrm{CH}^2(\mathbb P^4)_\mathbb Q\) is the \(1\times3\) matrix \((1,0,0)\) with kernel dimension **two**. The tested primitive cycle has coordinates \(Z=(0,1,-1)\), whence
\[
 \pi_*Z=0,\quad \mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{canonical}}_X(Z)\ne0.
\]
There is no contradiction: \(\pi_*\) is not injective, while \(\mathrm{cl}^{\mathrm{canonical}}_X\) is surjective. The latter is the relevant Hodge test.

## 2. Middle primitive kernel and intersection Gram

The exact rational middle intersection matrix in the above canonical Chow/Betti basis is
\[
 G=\begin{pmatrix}
 1&0&0\\
 0&0&-1\\
 0&-1&0
 \end{pmatrix},\quad \det G=-1,\quad Z^TGZ=2.
\]
With \(H=\pi^*h\), \(E\) the exceptional Cartier class and \(L=mH-E\) an ample divisor for suitable \(m\), the actual cup map \(L:H^4(X)\to H^6(X)\) is represented relative to bases \((H^2,j_*a,j_*b)\) and \((H^3,j_*ab)\) by
\[
 M_L=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 m&1&1\\
 -2&m-3&m-3
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
The minor of the first two columns equals \((m-1)(m-2)\), so the map has rank 2 for every \(m\ge3\). Its kernel is the one-dimensional line generated by \(Z=(0,1,-1)\). Thus the unique primitive sector is not only spanned by an actual algebraic class, but exactly reconstructed for any prescribed rational primitive \(\alpha\) by \(\frac{Q(\alpha,Z)}2 Z\). This confirms the earlier positive source record against a *genuine* falsification test.

## 4. Stronger smooth-blowup no-go: entire known-center family

For ANY smooth projective B and ANY smooth codimension \(c\ge2\) center \(Y\subset B\), the actual Chow and pure rational Hodge blow-up decompositions give
\[
\begin{split}
 CH^p(\mathrm{Bl}_Y B)_\mathbb Q
    &\cong CH^p(B)_\mathbb Q\oplus
       \bigoplus_{i=1}^{c-1}CH^{p-i}(Y)_\mathbb Q,\\
 \mathrm{Hdg}^p(\mathrm{Bl}_Y B)_\mathbb Q
    &\cong\mathrm{Hdg}^p(B)_\mathbb Q\oplus
       \bigoplus_{i=1}^{c-1}\mathrm{Hdg}^{p-i}(Y)_\mathbb Q(-i),
\end{split}
\]
where the last Tate-twist label just adjusts the cohomological bidegree. These decompositions commute with the actual canonical cycle-class maps, so **their cokernels decompose likewise**. In particular
\[
 \mathrm{HC}(\mathrm{Bl}_Y B)\iff
 [\mathrm{HC}(B)\text{ and }\mathrm{HC}(Y)]
\]
in the all-codimension *rational* form when \(c\ge2\) (the center occurs through twists and the original base occurs via \(\pi^*\)).

Every smooth complex projective surface Y satisfies the rational Hodge conjecture: for p=1 by the Lefschetz (1,1) theorem; p=0 and p=2 by the unit and fundamental point class. The same is true for every smooth projective threefold, using rational Hard Lefschetz \(H^2\to H^4\) and multiplication by an algebraic ample divisor to reduce p=2 to p=1.

Consequently **NO blow-up of projective space along any smooth surface or smooth threefold center can provide a rational Hodge counterexample**. A hypothetical genuinely non-algebraic Hodge component inherited from a more complicated center would already be a counterexample on that center, not a new contradiction caused by blow-up or lost pushforward.

This is a valid no-go for this *particular counterexample mechanism*, not a no-go theorem for rational Hodge counterexamples from all possible algebraic geometries.
