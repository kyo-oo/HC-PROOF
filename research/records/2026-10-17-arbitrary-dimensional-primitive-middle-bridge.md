# Mathbox Gate 3: arbitrary-dimensional primitive classes reduce faithfully to middle relative cohomology

- **Date:** 2026-10-17.
- **Route:** Original-\(X\), all-dimensions, all-admissible-\(p\) complete-intersection reduction and relative geometric lift.
- **Pinned final target:** For all smooth projective complex X, all p, every rational (p,p) class α, construct an actual codimension-p rational algebraic cycle Z on original X with canonical Betti class α. This note verifies a necessary cohomological bridge, not a replacement for the final target.
- **Mathematical objects:** Let n=dim_C X, choose 1≤p and 2p≤n, let r=n−2p≥0. A general sequence of r hyperplanes (by Bertini) cuts out a smooth complete intersection \(j:Y^{2p}\hookrightarrow X^n\). Write \(h_X,h_Y=j^*h_X\) for the genuine ample classes. Let α∈H^(2p)(X,Q) be a rational primitive Hodge (p,p) class, so \(h_X^{r+1}\cup\alpha=0\).
- **Genuine weak Lefschetz:** The iterated restriction \(j^*:H^{2p}(X,Q)\to H^{2p}(Y,Q)\) is injective: for sections before the final cut it is an isomorphism in degree 2p, and in the final cut it is injective. Dually, \(j_*:H^{2p+2}(Y,Q)\to H^{2p+2+2r}(X,Q)\) is an isomorphism (the adjoint of restriction in degree 2p−2).
- **Exact primitive transformation:** The complete-intersection self-intersection formula gives \(j_*j^*\alpha=h_X^r\cup\alpha\). The Gysin projection formula gives
  \[
  j_*(h_Y\cup j^*\alpha)
   = h_X\cup j_*j^*\alpha
   =h_X^{r+1}\cup\alpha=0.
  \]
  Since \(j_*\) on the displayed degree is injective, **\(h_Y\cup j^*\alpha=0\)**. Thus \(j^*\alpha\) is a middle-primitive rational Hodge class on the smooth 2p-fold Y, and is nonzero whenever α≠0 by weak Lefschetz.
- **Relative continuation:** Choose a further smooth hyperplane section \(i:Z^{2p-1}\hookrightarrow Y^{2p}\). The preceding all-middle theorem gives \(i^*(j^*\alpha)=0\) and a rational relative Betti class \(\widetilde\alpha\in H^{2p}(Y,Z;\mathbb Q)\) mapping to \(j^*\alpha\). Hence **every original-X primitive Hodge target in the low cohomological half has a faithful middle-dimensional relative Betti avatar**.
- **Crucial reverse transfer check:** If an algebraic cycle \(\Gamma\in CH^p(Y)_\mathbb Q\) represents \(j^*\alpha\), then its genuine proper pushforward \(j_*\Gamma\in CH^{p+r}(X)_\mathbb Q\) represents **\(h_X^r\cup\alpha\)**, not α. To reconstruct α from that higher-codimension class using algebraic operations would require an independently algebraic inverse Lefschetz correspondence, the content of an additional nontrivial standard-conjecture-type obligation. Similarly an arbitrary cohomological inverse of the injective restriction \(j^*\) is not automatically a map of algebraic cycles.
- **Uniform exactness and boundaries:** This provides a valid reduction of the **topological/primitive source-location** question to a smooth \(2p\)-fold relative pair for each arbitrary original X. It does NOT identify an algebraic cycle back on X. The difference between a relative Betti witness and a native Chow witness is preserved as the remaining Gate 3 construction step.

- **Discriminating cases:** If r=0, j=id and the theorem reduces exactly to the middle-relative hyperplane result, testing the zero-cut boundary. For r>0, the Gysin projection formula controls the necessary power r+1 without confusing it with r. A genuine example is X=Q×P^r where Q is a smooth 4-fold with primitive H⁴ class β; \(\alpha=\pi_Q^*\beta\) is primitive for the product polarization when \(\beta h_Q=0\) and \(h_{\mathbb P^r}^{r+1}=0\), and its restriction to a smooth 4-fold complete intersection is primitive and nonzero.
- **Result and route impact:** Arbitrary-dimension cohomological reduction is mathematically proved from standard weak Lefschetz and Gysin geometry; the Lean implication is committed for CI. The **only** all-X mathematical gap remains producing and transporting actual canonical algebraic representatives for the primitive relative classes, without assuming preimage/surjectivity. No special-family or extra-rank project is needed.
