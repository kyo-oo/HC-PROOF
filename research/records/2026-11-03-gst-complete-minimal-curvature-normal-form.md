## Completed mathematical result

by codimension, and m = A in positive degree. This finite commutative graded
algebra has A/m = Q and m^(dim X+1) = 0. For the actual input list alpha_i
of weights p_i, let I be their ideal in A. Define

`mu_q = dim_Q (I / m I)_q`.

The allowed operations are homogeneous invertible matrices over A, applied
to both odd generator lists u_i and v_i. Entry (j,i) has codimension
p_j-p_i; negative degrees vanish. These operations preserve the specified
model filtration, real conjugation and the complete differential.

1. **An exact terminating normal form.** Process curvature weights in
   ascending order. At weight q, Gaussian elimination modulo the ideal
   generated by smaller weights selects exactly mu_q active curvatures.
   Subtract their cup combinations from every redundant row. This is a
   finite invertible operation, with no division by a nilpotent class.
2. **A sharp lower bound and uniqueness at the correct level.** Every
   allowed presentation needs at least mu_q active rows at weight q. Any
   two minimal generating lists for the same ideal are related by an
   allowed invertible matrix. An ordered basis is not claimed canonical.
3. **A complete all-degree split.** If r_q input pairs have weight q, the
   model is the minimal active extension tensored with r_q-mu_q closed
   exterior pairs of degree 2q-1. Consequently
   `P_B(t) = P_min(t) * product_q (1+t^(2q-1))^(2(r_q-mu_q))`.
   The active extension keeps all base cohomology, including odd and
   off-diagonal classes. This is not a first-Betti-number surrogate.
4. **Actual rational spaces over X.** The elementary substitutions act
   on the nullhomotopy paths defining the actual rational torsors, using
   base cup-product maps. Each has an inverse up to the specified
   homotopies. Every zero classifying row becomes a chosen product fiber.
   No nilpotence assumption on pi_1(X), compactness assertion for a higher
   torsor, or change to the original X is introduced.
5. **The prescribed target is conserved under all its cup births.** For
   every nonzero prescribed alpha, any finite list containing alpha and
   homogeneous multiples b_i alpha has exactly one active curvature after
   reduction. Its ideal has one minimal generator in alpha's own weight.
   Choose alpha as that generator: the exact final base transgression is
   still alpha. All other pairs split. If alpha is zero they all split.
