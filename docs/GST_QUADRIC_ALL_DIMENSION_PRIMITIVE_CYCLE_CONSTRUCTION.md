## Geometric construction (all dimensions, genuine cycles)

Fix \(m\ge1\), and let \(Q^{2m}\subset\mathbb P^{2m+1}_{\mathbb C}\) be a smooth complex quadric. The maximal \(m\)-dimensional projective linear subspaces of this quadric fall into **two spinor rulings**, both consisting of actual algebraic subvarieties \(\mathbb P^m\subset Q^{2m}\). Choose representatives \(A,B\) from the two rulings.

Standard quadric Schubert/cellular geometry proves:

\[
H^{2m}(Q^{2m},\mathbb Q)
 =\mathbb Q[A]\oplus\mathbb Q[B],
\qquad
h^m=[A]+[B],
\]
where \(h\) is the genuine hyperplane divisor class. These classes have Hodge type \((m,m)\), because they are actual projective algebraic subvarieties.

The integral intersection matrix on this **geometric** two-cycle lattice is

\[
J_m=\begin{cases}
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}, &m\text{ even},\\
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}, &m\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
\]

Thus \(Z_m=[A]-[B]\) satisfies the primitive orthogonality \(\langle h^m,Z_m\rangle=0\), and \(\langle Z_m,Z_m\rangle =2(-1)^m\neq0\). In middle degree, primitivity is equivalent to orthogonality to \(h^m\) by Hard Lefschetz and Poincare duality (equivalently, \(h\cup Z_m=0\) because the target \(H^{2m+2}\) has rank one).

Every primitive rational \((m,m)\)-class is uniquely \(c Z_m\) for \(c\in\mathbb Q\); **the explicit algebraic representative is** \(c(A-B)\), with no previously chosen target-cycle preimage. Every arbitrary middle rational Hodge class \(a[A]+b[B]\) is represented by that same actual algebraic combination, and decomposes into its ample and primitive components as

\[
a[A]+b[B]
 =\frac{a+b}{2}\big([A]+[B]\big)
 +\frac{a-b}{2}\big([A]-[B]\big).
\]

For \(p\ne m\) the rational cohomology of the quadric in even degree \(2p\) is rank one, generated by a Schubert linear section, hence by an actual algebraic cycle. Thus the **ordinary rational Hodge conjecture holds on every smooth complex quadric of any finite dimension**, not just in middle degree. This is a standard established geometric result, reproved here in a constructive way for the even-dimensional middle primitive sector.

- rational decomposition error \((a,b)-\frac{a+b}{2}(1,1)-\frac{a-b}{2}(1,-1)=(0,0)\).

These computations verify the finite, symbolic two-coordinate identities; they do **not** prove the geometric Schubert cell decomposition inside the Lean kernel.

## Why this matters to the original goal

This is a genuinely **non-hyperplane-only**, all-dimensional algebraic primitive construction. It does not require an imaginary CM eigenvalue, a speculative universal NM–period equality, or arbitrary CM covers. It works because the two actual algebraic ruling families exist and the cellular topology of the quadric supplies the target rank count.

It does not make a generic noncellular smooth projective variety into a quadric, nor produce new cycles on an arbitrary unknown primitive cohomology fiber. Therefore it is **not** an unconditional proof of the general Hodge conjecture.

The next genuinely new geometric step is to construct a comparable family of actual algebraic subvarieties/irreducible correspondences on a general smooth projective \(X\) and independently bound its rational primitive Hodge rank, without assuming either its cycle-class surjectivity or a CM-source dominant map.
