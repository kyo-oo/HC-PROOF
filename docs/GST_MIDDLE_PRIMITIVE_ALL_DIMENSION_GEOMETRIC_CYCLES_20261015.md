## I. All middle-primitive classes on X_m = P^m × P^m

Let \(a,b\) be the hyperplane classes pulled back from the two projective factors. Over Q,

\[
H^*(X_m,\mathbb Q)=\mathbb Q[a,b]/(a^{m+1},b^{m+1}),\qquad h=a+b.
\]

The middle Hodge group \(H^{m,m}\) has basis \(a^{m-j}b^j\), \(0\le j\le m\). A class
\(\alpha=\sum_{j=0}^m c_j a^{m-j}b^j\) is primitive precisely when \(h\cup\alpha=0\), i.e.

\[
c_j+c_{j+1}=0,\quad 0\le j<m.
\]

The recurrence gives \(c_j=(-1)^jc_0\) for all \(j\). Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\alpha=c_0 Z_m,\quad
Z_m=\sum_{j=0}^m(-1)^ja^{m-j}b^j
}
\]

where every monomial is the cycle class of an actual intersection of projective hyperplane divisors. There is no basis-cycle existence assumption and no target-dependent free correspondence. Every rational middle-primitive class of \(X_m\) has a concrete rational algebraic representative at any \(m\ge1\).

## II. Non-product smooth cubic fourfold containing a plane

Let \(X_3\subset P^5_{\mathbb C}\) be a smooth cubic fourfold containing a plane \(P=P^2\). The two actual algebraic surface classes are \(h^2\) (two hyperplanes) and \([P]\). Their intersection numbers are

\[
h^4=3,\qquad h^2[P]=1,\qquad [P]^2=3.
\]

**Important normal-bundle correction:** \(N_{P/P^5}\simeq O_P(1)^{\oplus3}\), *not four copies*. Exact sequence
\(0\to N_{P/X}\to O_P(1)^{\oplus3}\to O_P(3)\to0\) gives

\[
c_t(N_{P/X})=\frac{(1+t)^3}{1+3t}=1+3t^2\pmod{t^3}.
\]

The explicit algebraic cycle \(Z=3[P]-h^2\) pairs to zero with \(h^2\) and has \(Z^2=24\ne0\). Since \(H^6(X,\mathbb Q)\) is one-dimensional (by weak/Hard Lefschetz and Poincare duality), \(hZ=0\); \(Z\) is a genuine nonzero middle-primitive rational Hodge class.

## III. All even dimensions and all degrees: maximal linear planes in hypersurfaces

Let \(m\ge1,d\ge2\) and suppose \(X_d\subset\mathbb P^{2m+1}_{\mathbb C}\) is smooth of degree \(d\), containing an actual linear \(L=\mathbb P^m\). This is a two-parameter family of genuine projective hypersurfaces, not an assumption of Hodge algebraicity for an arbitrary target vector.

The normal exact sequence is

\[
0\to N_{L/X_d}\to O_L(1)^{\oplus(m+1)}
  \to O_L(d)\to0.
\]

The top self-intersection \(s_{m,d}=[L]^2\) is the coefficient of \(t^m\) in
\((1+t)^{m+1}/(1+dt)\). It obeys

\[
s_{0,d}=1,\qquad s_{m+1,d}=(1-d)s_{m,d}+1.
\]

Induction gives the **exact closed form**

\[
d s_{m,d}-1=-(1-d)^{m+1}=(-1)^m(d-1)^{m+1}.
\]

The actual algebraic cycle \(Z_{m,d}=d[L]-h^m\) is primitive. Indeed
\(H^{2m+2}(X_d,\mathbb Q)\) is one-dimensional, generated by \(h^{m+1}\); comparing its intersection with \(h^{m-1}\) yields
\(h[L]=h^{m+1}/d\), so \(h Z_{m,d}=0\).

Its self-pairing is

\[
\boxed{Z_{m,d}^2=-d(1-d)^{m+1}=(-1)^m d(d-1)^{m+1}\ne0.}
\]

Thus **every \(m\ge1\) and \(d\ge2\) admits an explicit nonzero rational middle-primitive algebraic class** on each smooth degree-\(d\) hypersurface containing a maximal linear plane.

## IV. Why this is *not yet* the user's original universal Hodge theorem

The constructed varieties are special. They have obvious rational algebraic hyperplanes, projective factors, or maximal linear planes supplying primitive classes. A general smooth projective \(X\) need not admit any of those constructions, and may have primitive rational Hodge classes inaccessible through the above geometric cycles. In the hypersurface family, \(Z_{m,d}\) generates one line but can leave a much larger primitive Hodge fiber untouched.

GST's internal matrix-unit and CM/NM coefficient theorems cannot by themselves turn arbitrary rational Hodge basis vectors into algebraic cycles: \`GSTClassicalHodgeBareLefschetzNonCircularity.lean\` already proves that general native-lift assertions of this kind are equivalent to the desired algebraicity. The repository's abstract \`HodgeBigradedBettiData\` also permits zero cycle-class maps, so an authentic universal theorem must use geometrically fixed cycle-class semantics.

**Remaining exact target:** construct additional genuine algebraic correspondences that hit every rational primitive Hodge direction on arbitrary \(X\), without assuming a comparison certificate or target-cycle preimage. No construction here establishes that. Continue evaluating CM/NM period operations only when they induce honest algebraic push/pull correspondences on target varieties and create demonstrably new primitive cycle-class directions.
