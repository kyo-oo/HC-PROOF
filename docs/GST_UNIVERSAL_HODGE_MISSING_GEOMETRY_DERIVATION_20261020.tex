\begin{abstract}
This derivation answers exactly what geometric existence step remains
after the verified GST native-cycle, Gram-residual, cosmological
blowup, CM, and Hodge-operator developments.  For arbitrary
smooth projective complex X of dimension d and arbitrary rational
Hodge class alpha of codimension p, we construct a canonical rank-one
rational Hodge correspondence kappa(alpha) on X times X.
We prove an exact two-sided algebraic Chow/Hodge retract:
kappa(alpha) is algebraic if and only if alpha is algebraic on the
same original X.  The induced Hodge defect of X is therefore a
split direct summand of the middle Hodge defect of X times X.
This supplies a concrete, type-correct kernel and perfect-complex
geometric construction target, proves an actual-native-image
obstruction to purely formal GST matrix-unit completion, and
identifies an alternative resolved-support Gysin construction.
Neither unconditional Hodge algebraicity nor a genuine
counterexample is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{GST native-image conservation: the fundamental firewall}
\begin{theorem}[Native-program image invariance]\label{th:native}
Let a genuine algebraic GST program act on actual native cycles
by T_C and on canonical rational Hodge classes by T_H,
with a geometrically established commuting square
\[
                 c_p T_C=T_Hc_p. \tag{2.1}
\]
Then T_H preserves A_p(X).  Every finite rational combination
and composition of such operators also preserves A_p(X).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a=c_p(z), one has T_H(a)=c_p(T_Cz) in A_p(X).
Composition, rational scaling and addition preserve the image.
For cross-carrier programs, use the matching source/target
algebraic images in place of a common A_p(X).
\end{proof}
Choose any rational complement V_p(X)=A_p(X)\oplus R_p(X).
Every realized endomorphism has the matrix shape
\[
 T_H=\begin{pmatrix}T_{AA}&T_{AR}\\0&T_{RR}\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{2.2}\label{eq:block}
\]
If A_p(X) is a nonzero proper subspace, a formal GST
matrix unit carrying an algebraic vector to a vector in R_p(X)
has nonzero lower-left block and CANNOT be the Hodge action
of any such genuine Chow-natural operator.  This explains,
without negating any verified GST formal laws, the distinction
between internal rank-free matrix algebra and the actual
geometric action.  It is the content of the repository's
native-image barriers including
\texttt{full\_native\_annihilator\_kills\_exceptionalGRR}.
For a detector ell vanishing on the entire A_p(X),
ell(c_p(T_C z))=0 for \emph{every} native input z.
A nonzero coupling can certify growth only beyond a
\emph{chosen incomplete} finite source frame, never beyond
the total algebraic image.

\section{New exact self-product Chow/Hodge retract}
Let delta=\int_X h^d>0, and let q_1,q_2:X\times X\to X
denote the projections.  Define actual Chow maps:
\begin{align}
 S^C_p(z)&=\frac1\delta\,q_1^*(h^{d-p})\cdot q_2^*z
    &&\in\CH^d(X\times X)_\Q, \tag{3.1}\\
 R^C_p(W)&=(q_2)_*\bigl(q_1^*h^p\cdot W\bigr)
    &&\in\CH^p(X)_\Q. \tag{3.2}
\end{align}
Here h^k in Chow means the product of actual hyperplane
divisor classes.  Define their canonical Hodge counterparts
\begin{align}
 S^H_p(\alpha)&=\frac1\delta\,q_1^*(h^{d-p})
      \smile q_2^*\alpha &&\in\Hdg^d(X\times X)_\Q,
      \tag{3.3}\\
 R^H_p(\beta)&=(q_2)_*(q_1^*h^p\smile\beta)
      &&\in V_p(X). \tag{3.4}
\end{align}
The Chow/Betti operations are product, flat pullback,
proper pushforward and fiber integration; they are not
arbitrary Hodge-to-Chow selectors.

\begin{theorem}[Two exact commuting retracts]\label{th:retract}
For all X and 0<=p<=d,
\[
 R^C_pS^C_p=\id_{C_p(X)},\qquad
 R^H_pS^H_p=\id_{V_p(X)}, \tag{3.5}
\]
and
\[
 \cl_{X\times X}S^C_p=S^H_pc_p,\qquad
 c_pR^C_p=R^H_p\cl_{X\times X}. \tag{3.6}
\]
The endomorphisms S^C_pR^C_p and S^H_pR^H_p are
idempotent and commute with cycle class.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By projection formula and fiber integration,
\begin{align*}
 R^C_pS^C_p(z)
 &=\delta^{-1}(q_2)_*
   (q_1^*h^p\cdot q_1^*h^{d-p}\cdot q_2^*z)\\
 &=\delta^{-1}\left(\int_X h^d\right)z=z.
\end{align*}
The same calculation in canonical Betti cohomology gives
R^H_pS^H_p=id.  Cycle classes are compatible with
the stated products, flat pullbacks, and proper pushforward.
Thus both squares commute.  If RS=id, then
(SR)^2=S(RS)R=SR.
\end{proof}
All codimensions are checked: on X times X, S^C_p has
codimension d-p+p=d; after multiplication by h^p
and pushing along a d-dimensional fiber, R^C_p lands
in codimension p.  At p=0 and p=d the formulas still
make sense.  No negative powers are used.

\section{Exactly what missing geometry has to be constructed}
For an arbitrary prescribed rational (p,p) Hodge target
alpha on the original X, form the explicit rational
middle Hodge correspondence
\[
 \boxed{\kappa_\alpha=
       \frac1\delta\,q_1^*h^{d-p}\smile q_2^*\alpha
       \in\Hdg^d(X\times X)_\Q.}\tag{4.1}
\]
This is a defined cohomology class of type (d,d), not
a claimed algebraic cycle.

\begin{theorem}[Exact rank-one kernel criterion]\label{th:equivalence}
For a fixed triple (X,p,alpha), the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item An actual original-X algebraic cycle z in CH^p(X)_Q
      satisfies c_p(z)=alpha.
\item There is an actual algebraic Chow correspondence
      W in CH^d(X times X)_Q whose canonical cycle class
      equals kappa_alpha.
\end{enumerate}
Given z, set W=S^C_p(z).  Given W, set z=R^C_p(W).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The forward direction follows from the first commuting
square of Theorem~\ref{th:retract}.  Conversely,
\[
 c_p(R^C_pW)=R^H_p\cl_{X\times X}(W)
            =R^H_p S^H_p(\alpha)=\alpha.
\]
The construction occurs on the \emph{same} original X
and needs no artificial target map.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Explicit target action on an algebraic seed]
The Hodge kernel kappa_alpha has the rank-one ambient
correspondence action
\[
 T_{\kappa_\alpha}(v)=
   (q_2)_*(q_1^*v\smile\kappa_\alpha)
   =\frac{\int_X v\smile h^{d-p}}{\delta}\alpha.
   \tag{4.2}
\]
Hence T_{\kappa_\alpha}(h^p)=alpha exactly.
If an algebraic W represents kappa_alpha, then the
genuine Chow action on h^p constructs z_alpha.
\end{corollary}

\begin{problem}[The precise missing GST geometric operation]
For each arbitrary original X and each rational Hodge
target alpha, independently construct a genuine rational
algebraic kernel W_alpha in CH^d(X times X) and prove
\[
             \cl(W_\alpha)=\kappa_\alpha. \tag{4.3}
\]
The construction may use native world births, projective
geometry, algebraic incidence, GRR-perfect complexes,
CM sources, and resolved supports, but may \emph{not}
take a previously selected algebraic preimage of alpha
or a formal matrix unit declared algebraic as input.
\end{problem}
Theorem~\ref{th:equivalence} establishes that this
kernel existence problem is exactly equivalent to the
original rational Hodge class problem.  That is the
mathematically sharp target, \emph{not} a claim that
the kernel already exists.

\section{A split injection of the actual Hodge defect}
The compatible Chow/Hodge maps induce rational maps
\[
 \overline S_p:D_p(X)\longrightarrow D_d(X\times X),
 \qquad
 \overline R_p:D_d(X\times X)\longrightarrow D_p(X).
\]
\begin{theorem}[Defect splitting]\label{th:defectsplit}
There is a canonical split injection with
\[
 \overline R_p\overline S_p=\id,\qquad
 D_d(X\times X)\cong
     \overline S_p(D_p(X))\oplus\ker\overline R_p.
 \tag{5.1}
\]
Moreover a given alpha is nonalgebraic on X if and only if
the explicitly specified kappa_alpha is nonalgebraic
on X times X.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The maps are defined on quotients because of the two
Chow/Betti commuting squares.  The composition equals
the identity by Theorem~\ref{th:retract}.  The induced
projector \(\overline S_p\overline R_p\) splits the target
vector space into image and kernel.  Since
\(\overline S_p([\alpha])=[\kappa_\alpha]\) and
\(\overline S_p\) is injective, their zero classes
are equivalent.
\end{proof}
Thus proving rational Hodge in middle codimension d
on all self-products X times X suffices to prove
rational Hodge for all smooth projective X in every
codimension.  A genuine counterexample downstairs
would be an explicit counterexample upstairs.
This is a genuine exact mathematical reduction,
but not an unconditional Hodge solution.

\section{GST primitive Gram and rank-free matrix-unit geometry}
In the primitive rational (p,p) Hodge fiber for
2p<=d, use the positive definite signed rational form
\[
 Q_p(u,v)=(-1)^p\int_X u\smile v\smile h^{d-2p}.
 \tag{6.1}
\]
The existing GST finite-native-frame Gram formula
for known algebraic primitive cycles Z_1,...,Z_m
with a_i=cl(Z_i), G_{ij}=Q_p(a_i,a_j), is
\[
 P_A(v)=\sum_{i,j}a_i(G^{-1})_{ij}Q_p(a_j,v),
 \qquad r_v=v-P_A(v). \tag{6.2}
\]
One derives Q_p(r_v,a_j)=0 and the exact energy
decomposition Q_p(v,v)=Q_p(P_Av,P_Av)+Q_p(r_v,r_v).
When the a_i span only a finite \emph{known} source
frame, r_v need not be outside the full algebraic image.
When they span the full algebraic primitive image,
all genuine algebraic sources pair trivially with r_v:
this follows from Theorem~\ref{th:native}, not from a
missing positivity estimate.  The GST integer Gram
threshold and rank-one residual laws remain valid
under their separately documented hypotheses.

\begin{theorem}[Algebraic primitive basis realizes actual GST units]
\label{th:units}
Assume the full primitive rational Hodge fiber has a
basis e_1,...,e_m of classes of genuine algebraic cycles
Z_1,...,Z_m on X.  Then the rational algebraic kernels
\[
 W_{ij}=\sum_k(G^{-1})_{jk}
 q_1^*\bigl((-1)^pZ_k\cdot h^{d-2p}\bigr)
 \cdot q_2^*Z_i\in\CH^d(X\times X)_\Q
 \tag{6.3}
\]
have genuine Betti actions
\[
                  T_{W_{ij}}(e_\ell)=\delta_{j\ell}e_i.
                  \tag{6.4}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The codimension of each kernel is
(p+d-2p)+p=d.  Its action on e_l gives
\(\sum_k(G^{-1})_{jk}G_{\ell k}e_i
=\delta_{j\ell}e_i\), using symmetry
and nonsingularity of the polarized primitive Gram.
Every displayed factor is an \emph{actual} algebraic
Chow class under the stated hypothesis.
\end{proof}
Conversely, given any nonzero algebraic seed in this
primitive fiber, if the FULL rank-free matrix-unit
family E_{ij} already has actual algebraic-kernel
realizations, applying its j-th column to that seed
constructs every primitive basis vector.
Thus global genuine unit realization with a nonzero
seed is essentially conjecture-level: it cannot be
inferred from the Hodge-vector-space matrix algebra
without a geometric theorem.

\section{Why blowup cosmology does not by itself finish the task}
For an actual smooth center Z of codimension c>=2
in smooth projective B, canonical Chow/Hodge
blowup decompositions give
\[
 \coker(\cl_{\operatorname{Bl}_ZB}^p)
 \cong\coker(\cl_B^p)\oplus
 \bigoplus_{i=1}^{c-1}\coker(\cl_Z^{p-i}).
 \tag{8.1}
\]
A blowup from Hodge-complete B and centers remains
complete; a missing class in a center survives.
For Y=Bl_X(P^N) with exceptional j:E->Y,
q:E->X, and xi=c_1(O_E(1)), the genuine maps
\[
 I(z)=j_*q^*z,\qquad
 R(W)=-q_*(\xi^{c-2}j^!W) \tag{8.2}
\]
satisfy RI=id, using j^!j_*=-xi and
q_*(xi^{c-1}q^*(-))=id.
Consequently the full image of algebraic
exceptional-return classes on original X is
\emph{exactly}
\[
             \im(\cl_X\circ R)=\im(\cl_X).
             \tag{8.3}
\]
The inclusion from left to right is tautological
from the Chow-valued target; reverse inclusion
uses RI=id.  This mathematically closes the
unproductive attempt to gain \emph{new classes
outside the entire algebraic image} merely by
ordinary native exceptional birth.  Additional
non-tautological objects may enlarge the \emph{known
library} but cannot bypass this full-image identity.
