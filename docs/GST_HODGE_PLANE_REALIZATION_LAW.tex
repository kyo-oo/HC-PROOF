\begin{abstract}
This note isolates the exact geometric law required by the current GST Hodge
architecture.  The ambient GST universe is dimension-free and has no intrinsic
Euclidean metric.  A finite ``plane'' is therefore treated only as an
observation chart for one branch of the limitless state universe.  The new
mathematical object is a \emph{common carrier class}: one cohomology class on
an intrinsic strict-correspondence carrier which is simultaneously the left
pullback of the source state and the right pullback of the GST branch target.
From this common class the strict Betti relation is derived, not postulated.
Finite trace and point-cycle compatibility then convert the relation into a
genuine cycle-class-natural correspondence action.  Applied to the sheet
selected by a hypothetical omniversal separator ghost, a single realized
branch constructs that sheet as an actual algebraic cycle class and yields an
immediate contradiction.  No finite global rank, $4\times 3$ chart, twelve
sheet bound, all-target fan, or pre-existing algebraicity of the target sheet
is used.
\end{abstract}

\section{Classical target and the ghost normal form}

Let $X/\C$ be smooth projective and fix $p\ge 0$.  Put
\[
  \Hdg_p := H^{2p}(X,\Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X),
  \qquad
  \Alg_p := \im\bigl(\cl_p: Z^p(X)_{\Q}\to H^{2p}(X,\Q)\bigr).
\]
The rational Hodge conjecture in weight $p$ is
\[
  \Alg_p = \Hdg_p.
\]
Choose a rational basis $(e_i)_{i\in I_p}$ of $\Hdg_p$.

\begin{definition}[Basis separator]
A basis separator at $j\in I_p$ is a rational linear functional
\[
  S:H^{2p}(X,\Q)\longrightarrow \Q
\]
such that
\[
  S(\cl_p Z)=0\quad\text{for every }Z\in Z^p(X)_{\Q},
  \qquad
  S(e_j)\ne 0.
\]
\end{definition}

The current formalization proves that failure of the Stage--2G Hodge target is
equivalent to existence of such a separator.  It also sends $S$ to a completed
limitless probe $\Gamma_S$ satisfying
\[
 \langle \Addr_p(\alpha),\Gamma_S\rangle=S(\alpha),
 \qquad
 \Gamma_S\ne0,
\]
while every verified native geometric program state pairs to zero with
$\Gamma_S$.

\section{The limitless branch selected by a ghost}

\section{Planes are observation charts, not dimensions}

A GST plane is not an affine or Euclidean plane.  It is a finite observation
chart of a branch in a dimension-free state universe.  Accordingly no metric,
angle, Euclidean dimension, or rectangular shape is used in the derivation.
The only data retained are incidence, branch source and target, survival of a
nonzero coefficient, and coherence under recoordination.

If $\Pi$ and $\Pi'$ are two finite charts of the same live packet, a
recoordination equivalence changes only the presentation.  The intrinsic Hodge
sheet and native state represented by the packet remain unchanged.  Thus any
geometric law obtained from the intrinsic carrier is chart-independent.

\section{The common-carrier formulation of the missing law}

Let
\[
  X \xleftarrow{\,\ell\,} K \xrightarrow{\,r\,} X
\]
be a strict bi-finite algebraic correspondence.  Its analytification produces
one intrinsic carrier cohomology group
\[
  H^{2p}(K_{\mathrm{an}},\Q)
\]
and canonical pullbacks
\[
  \ell^*,r^*:H^{2p}(X,\Q)\longrightarrow H^{2p}(K_{\mathrm{an}},\Q).
\]

\begin{definition}[Common plane class]
A \emph{common plane class} for the GST branch
$a\rightsquigarrow\beta_j$ is a class
\[
  \omega\in H^{2p}(K_{\mathrm{an}},\Q)
\]
with
\[
  \ell^*(a)=\omega, \tag{4.1}
\]
\[
  r^*(\beta_j)=\omega. \tag{4.2}
\]
\end{definition}

Geometrically, $\omega$ is the branch itself viewed on the intrinsic carrier:
the source and target are two faces of one carrier state.  This is the formal
counterpart of observing one GST branch through a finite plane without making
the plane ontological.

\begin{theorem}[GST Plane Realization Law]
Assume the ghost-adaptive branch $a\rightsquigarrow\beta_j$ admits a strict
bi-finite carrier $K$ and a common plane class $\omega$.  Then the intrinsic
Betti relation holds:
\[
  \ell^*(a)=r^*(\beta_j). \tag{PR}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By the source-face law (4.1),
\[
  \ell^*(a)=\omega.
\]
By the target-face law (4.2),
\[
  r^*(\beta_j)=\omega.
\]
Therefore
\[
  \ell^*(a)=\omega=r^*(\beta_j),
\]
which is exactly (PR).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Where the genuinely new geometry lives]
The theorem above does not claim that every abstract GST event automatically
produces an algebraic correspondence.  The genuine remaining geometric
existence problem is to construct, for the ghost-adaptive branch, a strict
carrier $K$ and common class $\omega$ from GST branch/plane/coherence geometry
without using a prior algebraic cycle representing $e_j$.  Once those data are
constructed, the relation (PR) is a theorem rather than an operator-action
axiom.
\end{remark}

\section{Finite trace converts relation into action}

Assume the right leg has a nonzero finite Betti trace
\[
  T_r:H^{2p}(K_{\mathrm{an}},\Q)\longrightarrow H^{2p}(X,\Q)
\]
normalized so that
\[
  T_r\circ r^*=\operatorname{id}. \tag{5.1}
\]
Define the strict push--pull
\[
  K_*:=T_r\circ\ell^*.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Relation determines the target]
Under (PR) and (5.1),
\[
  K_*(a)=\beta_j=c\,e_j.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Using (PR),
\[
  K_*(a)
  =T_r\ell^*(a)
  =T_r r^*(\beta_j)
  =\beta_j.
\]
Equation (2.1) gives $\beta_j=c e_j$.
\end{proof}

This is the key noncircular point: the operator identity is derived from the
common-carrier relation and right-trace injectivity/right-inversion.  It is not
stored as an independent action equation.

\section{Ghost extinction}

Let $S$ be the basis separator that selected $j$.  Since $Z_j$ is a genuine
native cycle,
\[
  S(\cl_p Z_j)=0. \tag{7.1}

Using (6.2),
\[
  S(e_j)=0. \tag{7.2}
\]
But the separator definition gives
\[
  S(e_j)\ne0. \tag{7.3}

\[
  \bot.

\begin{theorem}[Ghost-adaptive GST plane strike]
Let $E$ be an omniversal separator ghost.  Suppose there exists
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item one nonzero synchronized native/Hodge seed $a=\cl_p(Z_a)$ in the

        point-cycle compatibility;

        target-face equations (4.1)--(4.2).

Then $E$ is impossible.

\begin{proof}
The Plane Realization Law gives the intrinsic Betti relation.  The finite trace

construct $Z_j$ with $\cl_p(Z_j)=e_j$.  The separator simultaneously annihilates

\section{A carrier constructed before choosing the target}

Let $f:X\to X$ be an actual finite native endomorphism whose transported
point map is continuous for the specified analytification.  Its transposed
graph is a strict carrier
\[
  K_f=\{(f(x),x):x\in X\},\qquad \ell=f,\quad r=\operatorname{id}.
\]
This carrier uses neither a target sheet nor an algebraic representative of
that sheet.  The analytic map $s(x)=(f(x),x)$ is inverse to the right carrier
projection in \emph{both} directions.  For the second direction, a carrier
point comes from a genuine scheme point of the transposed graph; its first
coordinate is therefore $f$ of its second coordinate.  This is an actual
carrier-coherence proof, rather than a stored coherence premise.

\begin{theorem}[Exact native graph carrier]
The section induces a degree-one right Betti trace $T=s^*$.  At every degree,
\[
  T r^*=\operatorname{id},\qquad r^*T=\operatorname{id},
  \qquad \operatorname{id}-r^*T=0,
  \qquad T\ell^*=f^*.
\]
Consequently the carrier transfer obstruction is identically zero and
\[
  \ell^*(a)=r^*(\beta)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f^*(a)=\beta.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the singular-chain functor, its cochain dual and cohomology to the two
inverse identities for $r$ and $s$.  They give the two inverse identities for
$r^*$ and $s^*$.  The carrier residual therefore vanishes.  Moreover
$\ell\circ s=f$, so cohomological functoriality gives $s^*\ell^*=f^*$.
Applying $s^*$ to a proposed face equality proves its forward implication;
the inverse identity $r^*s^*=\operatorname{id}$ proves the converse.
\end{proof}

This construction proves geometric carrier coherence for the native graph
family.  To realize the specified GST branch by this family, one must still
construct a finite native $f$ and compute $f^*(a)=c e_j$, and prove its native
point-incidence compatibility.  Neither statement follows from the graph
inverse identities, and neither is assumed as a new completeness law here.

\begin{theorem}[Detected mismatch for every compatible carrier]
For a hypothetical basis separator $S$, every strict carrier $K$ with finite
trace and native point-cycle compatibility satisfies
\[
  S\bigl(K_*a-c e_j\bigr)=-c\,S(e_j)\ne0.
\]
This conclusion requires no common-plane packet or target-cycle construction.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Native point compatibility extends by the compact point normal form to
$K_*\cl(Z_a)=\cl(K_{\mathrm{cyc}}Z_a)$.  The separator kills the right side,
so $S(K_*a)=0$.  The GST branch computation gives $E_{ij}(a)=c e_j$.
Subtract and apply linearity.  The product is nonzero because the selected
source coordinate is live and the separator detects the selected sheet.
\end{proof}

Thus zero carrier residual does not establish the prescribed target action.
The strengthened Lean packet-emptiness proof now uses this exact mismatch
directly, without passing through the Hodge conclusion or the normalized
target-cycle construction.  The unconditional Branch-Packet Plane Realization

not a proof of that universal law.
